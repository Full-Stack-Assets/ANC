# Annika Olsdotter (1800–1876) and Samuel Olsson — Generation 7

> **Note on assignment:** the work order flagged a duplicate-person pair, `I182292318250` /
> `I182195862044`, both named Annika Olsdotter, b. 23 May 1800. Investigating the linked
> spouse and child records showed the duplication is not limited to Annika: it is a
> tree-import duplication of the same kind already resolved for John Albertson & Ann Pine
> (gen 6) and Samuel Jacobsson (gen 7, `docs/research/ancestors/gen07-samuel-jacobsson.md`) —
> an entire nuclear-family group imported twice. This dossier covers Annika Olsdotter and her
> husband Samuel Olsson, and documents how far down the family the duplication cascades.

## Overview

Annika Olsdotter was born 23 May 1800 in Myckleby parish (Önne), on Orust island in
Göteborg och Bohus län (Bohuslän), Sweden, and died in 1876 in Tegneby parish (Lunnaslätt),
also on Orust. She married Samuel Olsson (b. 1 Feb 1798, d. 19 Apr 1872, Tegneby, Orust),
and their son Sven Johan Samuelsson (1839–1877) is the tree owner's direct ancestor via his
son Simon Swanson (Svensson), whose daughter Ellen Josephine Swanson is documented in
`gen04-ellen-josephine-swanson.md` and whose own generation-5 dossier is
`gen05-simon-swanson-anna-josephina-svenson.md`.

Every record in this line exists twice in the tree — Annika, Samuel, their son Sven Johan,
his wife Gustava Samuelsdotter, and (as an unexpected further find) their grandson Simon
Swanson himself turns up under **three** separate person-ids. No independent Swedish
church-book source or published descendant research was located for Annika or Samuel on the
open web (the same limitation noted for Sabina Olsdotter, gen 5); the case for merging rests
entirely on the two branches' identical vital data cross-checked against which branch carries
more complete downstream facts.

## Verified facts

- **Annika Olsdotter, b. 23 May 1800, Myckleby (Önne), Bohuslän; d. 1876, Tegneby (Lunnaslätt).**
  Both tree records (`I182292318250`, `I182195862044`) give identical raw date/place strings
  for birth and death — the strongest form of duplicate-person evidence available from the
  data alone. [inferred — no source citation on either copy]
- **Samuel Olsson, b. 1 Feb 1798; d. 19 Apr 1872, Tegneby, Orust, Sweden**, husband of Annika.
  Both tree records (`I182292318241`, `I182195862070`) give identical raw dates. One copy
  (`I182195862070`) additionally gives his birthplace as "Rom, Tegneby, Göteborg och
  Bohuslän" — internally consistent with his death parish. [inferred]
- **Son Sven Johan Samuelsson, b. 7 Apr 1839, Långelanda och Torp parish; d. 24 Dec 1877,
  Göteborg och Bohus county** — also duplicated (`I182197443214`, `I182195860593`), already
  flagged as its own gen-6 duplicate-person entry in `direct-line-issues.md`. One copy
  (`I182195860593`) carries a source citation ("Ancestry Family Trees," S446914557) and an
  additional daughter, Selma Swanson, absent from the other copy. [documented on one copy,
  inferred on the other]
- **Geographic coherence:** Myckleby, Tegneby, and Långelanda och Torp are all parishes on or
  adjoining Orust island, Bohuslän — a compact, internally consistent geography for a rural
  family across three generations. [inferred]
- **Patronymic consistency:** Sven Johan **Samuelsson** ("son of Samuel") correctly patronymizes
  from father Samuel Olsson; the grandson's Americanized surname "Swanson" is the natural
  English rendering of "Svensson" (son of Sven), i.e. Simon Svensson → Simon Swanson — matching
  the parish-record given name "Sven Johan." This is exactly the pattern already noted in
  `gen05-simon-swanson-anna-josephina-svenson.md`. [inferred]

## Corrections to the tree

**Corrected 2026-07-04:** an earlier pass of this dossier picked canonical/duplicate per generation by judging "which node has a source citation or an extra fact," which produced an internally inconsistent result (Simon Swanson's best-documented copy sat on a different "branch" than the recommended-canonical Annika/Samuel/Sven Johan). The right test is which whole chain traces to a genuinely verified descendant — here, Simon Swanson matching `gen05-simon-swanson-anna-josephina-svenson.md` child-for-child. Traced from that anchor upward, the entire chain was **already mutually self-consistent** in the raw data:

- **Annika Olsdotter**: canonical `I182292318250` (already linked as mother of the canonical Sven Johan below; also has parents Olof Germundsson & Inger Svensdotter). Duplicate: `I182195862044`.
- **Samuel Olsson**: canonical `I182292318241` (already linked as father of the canonical Sven Johan below). Duplicate: `I182195862070` — carries an extra birthplace detail ("Rom, Tegneby, Göteborg och Bohuslän"), worth preserving if merged.
- **Sven Johan Samuelsson** (already flagged, gen 6): canonical `I182197443214` — his son Simon Swanson (`I182197442974`) is the individually-sourced, externally-verified copy. Duplicate: `I182195860593` — carries a generic "Ancestry Family Trees" source tag and an extra daughter, Selma Swanson, worth preserving if merged.
- No date or place value is actually *contradicted* between the paired records at any of these
  three levels — every duplicate pair agrees on birth/death dates and places verbatim. The
  "correction" here is structural (recognizing which chain of already-linked records is the
  real one), not a fact correction — and, happily, **no re-pointing is needed**: the canonical
  chain was never mis-linked to begin with.

## New findings (not in the tree)

- **The duplication is a full nuclear-family cascade, not just "mother + son."** Investigating
  the spouse and child links behind the flagged Annika Olsdotter pair turned up the same
  split at every generation below her:
  - Annika Olsdotter × Samuel Olsson (gen 7, this dossier)
  - → son Sven Johan Samuelsson × Gustava Samuelsdotter (gen 6; Gustava's own duplication was
    already caught and annotated while resolving her father Samuel Jacobsson's gen-7 duplicate,
    see `gen07-samuel-jacobsson.md` and the `manual` block on `I182195860594`)
  - → grandson Simon Swanson × Anna Josephina Svenson (gen 5, already dossiered)
  - → great-granddaughter Clara (M.) Swanson (b. 25 Aug 1898, Easton, MA)

  At the Simon Swanson generation the duplication actually runs to **three** copies: besides
  the two full sibling-branches (`I182197442974` and `I182195860381`), Anna Josephina's own
  record (`I182195860382`) carries a *second* spouse entry, `I182195860476` ("Anders Simon
  Svenson," b. 20 Mar 1868, Tegneby, married Anna 14 May 1898, with children "Clara Gustafva
  Maria Svenson" and "Emma Albertina Svenson" matching Simon and Anna's known children exactly).
  This is plainly a third copy of Simon Swanson himself, floating with no parent links at all.
  This directly answers an open question already on record in
  `gen05-simon-swanson-anna-josephina-svenson.md` — *"Was 'Anna G. Range' a third wife or a
  duplicate record for Anna Josephina Svenson?"* — the answer is: neither exists as a genuine
  additional person; both "Anna G. Range" and `I182195860476` are indexing/merge artifacts of
  Anna Josephina and Simon themselves, not real second/third spouses. That gen-5 dossier's open
  question should be updated accordingly (not done in this pass, to keep this dossier scoped to
  the gen-7 flag; flagged here for whoever next touches that file). Unlike the rest of this
  cascade, `I182197442975` (Anna Josephina, canonical per the gen-5 dossier), `I182195860382`
  ("Anna Josefina Rånge"), and `I182195860476` ("Anders Simon Svenson") carry no `manual` block
  yet — this pass did not add one, since the gen-5/gen-8 dossiers already own that generation's
  resolution and it is best made consistent with `gen08-britta-gabrielsdotter-magnus-mansson-range.md`
  (which independently tracks a *different* "Rånge" duplication, on Anna Josephina's father's
  side, and already references her by name — the two "Rånge" threads should be reconciled
  together, not by this dossier in isolation).
- **A precise cross-check between two independently-flagged duplicate-person entries.**
  Gustava Samuelsdotter's parents differ by branch: `I182292318217` (paired with the duplicate
  Sven Johan) lists father `I182382755763`; `I182195860594` (paired with the canonical Sven
  Johan) lists father `I182195861248`. These are **exactly** the two ids in the
  already-flagged gen-7 "Samuel Jacobsson" duplicate-person entry in `direct-line-issues.md`.
  In other words, the same two tree imports that duplicated Annika/Samuel/Sven Johan also
  duplicated Sven Johan's in-laws one generation further down the same collision — strong,
  independent corroboration that this is one mechanical double-import event, not several
  unrelated coincidences of common Swedish patronymics.
- **Correction to this dossier's own earlier analysis:** an initial pass concluded the
  best-documented Simon Swanson copy (`I182197442974`) sat on a different "branch" than the
  recommended-canonical Annika/Samuel/Sven Johan, implying a cross-branch re-point would be
  needed at the Simon Swanson generation. That was backwards. `I182197442974`'s actual parents
  in the raw data are `I182197443214` (Sven Johan) and `I182292318217` (Gustava) — and those
  are exactly the ids now correctly identified as canonical above and in
  `gen07-samuel-jacobsson.md`. The whole chain — Annika → Samuel Olsson → Sven Johan →
  Gustava → Simon Swanson → Ellen J. Swanson — was already internally consistent; the earlier
  "which node has more facts" heuristic just picked the wrong end of two different pairs
  independently, making the chain look cross-wired when it wasn't. No re-pointing is required
  anywhere in this cascade.
