# Sabina Olsdotter (1826–1909) and Anders Holst — Generation 5

> **Note on assignment data:** the work order named "Adam Crist & Susanna Fogel," but the attached tree data (item A20, person I182195859672) is for **Sabina Oldsdotter/Olsdotter**, wife of Anders Holst. This dossier covers the person in the tree data, at the dossier path the tree data specifies.

## Overview

Sabina Olsdotter (spelled "Oldsdotter" in the tree — almost certainly a typo, see Corrections) was born 24 June 1826 (per the tree, unsourced), married Anders Holst on 14 December 1855 in Torsby parish, Göteborg och Bohus län (Bohuslän), Sweden, and died 15 December 1909 in Lundby on Hisingen, by then part of greater Göteborg. Her own Swedish records are not reachable on the open web, but her two sons, John Oscar Holst and Axel Holst, emigrated to Boston (1879 and 1887) and are richly documented in Massachusetts vital records, naturalization files, and a descendant's published family research, which independently confirms the couple's names.

## Verified facts

- **Her name was Sabina Olsdotter, wife of Anders (recorded "Andres") Holst.** Documented on the American side: John Oscar Holst's Boston marriage record (15 Nov 1882, Boston, Suffolk co., MA, vol. 336, p. 204, no. 3665) and his Massachusetts death record (16 Dec 1938, Norfolk, MA) name his parents as Andres Holst and Sabina Olsdotter (one US record renders her "Subina Olsson"). Source: Kristin Hall's Holst family pages, kristinhall.org (archived), which cite the original Commonwealth of Massachusetts vital records. [documented]
- **Son John Oscar Holst** — born 14 Aug 1858 (Hall research; Geni and the death-record age calculation 79y:4m:2d imply 14 Aug **1859**) in Göteborg, Sweden; emigrated to Boston arriving 21 June 1879; declared intention 21 May 1885, naturalized 15 May 1891 (US Circuit Court, Boston; recorded 19 Sep 1891); machinist & locomotive repairman at the Readville car shops of the NY, NH & Hartford Railroad for ~50 years, forced to retire in 1936 at age ~75; married (1) Augusta Matilda Svensson 15 Nov 1882 in Boston (she died 23 May 1898), (2) Mina ——, (3) Emma ——; at least 12 children; died 16 December 1938 in Norfolk, Norfolk co., MA of prostate carcinoma; buried 18 Dec 1938 in Prospect Hill Cemetery, Millis, MA (Find A Grave memorial 68223024). [documented — MA vital records, naturalization file, Find A Grave]
- **Son Axel Holst** — born ~1867 in Göteborg (age 20 at immigration); emigrated aboard the **SS Scythia**, arriving Boston 22 September 1887, listed as "laborer"; became a fireman in East Boston; lived at 5 Union Place, East Boston; married Bertha —— (also Swedish-born); children Olaf Henry (b. 2 Jan 1895, Boston), Henry, George, Mabel, Lillian. [documented — ship manifest and Olaf Henry Holst 1895 Boston birth record, per Hall research]
- **Surname variants in US records:** Holst, Holz, Holtz; researchers advised to also check "Holt" in Soundex indexes. [documented — Hall research]
- **Geographic consistency of tree data:** marriage in Torsby (Bohuslän, ~20 km north of Göteborg) 1855 → sons born in Göteborg 1858/1867 → death in Lundby (Hisingen, incorporated into Göteborg 1906) 1909 forms a coherent rural-to-city trajectory typical for the period. [inferred]

## Corrections to the tree

- **"Sabina Oldsdotter" → "Sabina Olsdotter."** "Oldsdotter" is not a standard Swedish patronymic; the form throughout the independent American-side documentation (marriage and death records of her son, Hall family research, Geni, the archived kristinhall.org pages) is **Olsdotter** ("daughter of Ola/Olof"), with period spelling variants Saben/Sibina and an Americanized "Subina Olsson." The tree spelling is almost certainly a transcription slip.
- **Son "John Oscar Holst" birth year:** tree lists him only as a child; note that sources split between 14 Aug 1858 (Hall family research, WikiTree Holst-616) and 1859 (Find A Grave 68223024, FamilySearch LYYK-W32, Geni, and the death-record age of 79y:4m:2d, which back-computes to 14 Aug 1859). The death-record arithmetic favors **1859**.
- **Son "Axel Serenius Holst":** the middle name "Serenius" appears only in the tree; every located record calls him simply Axel Holst. Not necessarily wrong (Swedish birth record would settle it), but currently unsupported.

## New findings (not in the tree)

- **Husband Anders Holst — family legend:** per notes handed down in the Hall family (descendants of John Oscar), Anders "supposedly migrated to Hull, England, where he was a 'bonesetter' or surgeon." This is explicitly flagged as lore by the family researcher; no record found. If true, it could explain why Sabina apparently died in Lundby without a husband nearby. [legend]
- **Both sons emigrated to Boston** and settled in the Swedish community of East Boston — John Oscar in 1879, Axel in 1887 (SS Scythia). Sabina therefore likely spent her last decades in Sweden with both known sons abroad.
- **Full American careers, addresses and families of both sons** (see Verified facts): Readville railroad shops; East Boston addresses (8 Harmony Place 1891, 1 Union Place 1895, 98 Loudon Street 1898 for John Oscar; 5 Union Place for Axel); John Oscar's burial at Prospect Hill Cemetery, Millis, MA.
- **Grandchildren:** at least 12 by John Oscar (incl. Esther Gladys Holst 1895–1969, who married LaForrest Leroy Hall — the line the kristinhall.org research descends from) and 5 by Axel.
- **Family anecdotes preserved:** John Oscar called himself a "thick-headed Swede," brewed famously strong coffee, was a big, tall man, and at his forced retirement (age 75) did chin-ups on the office door frames to prove his fitness.

## Open questions

- **Sabina's own Swedish records.** Her birth (24 Jun 1826, parish unknown — plausibly Torsby or a neighboring Bohuslän parish given the 1855 marriage there), the 14 Dec 1855 Torsby marriage record, household-examination (husförhör) entries 1855–1909, and the 15 Dec 1909 Lundby death record should all exist in Swedish church books (Riksarkivet/ArkivDigital; Sveriges dödbok 1860–2017). These are captcha- or subscription-gated and could not be checked on the open web. Her patronymic implies a father named **Ola/Olof**, otherwise unknown.
- **What became of Anders Holst?** No Swedish or English record found; the Hull "bonesetter" story is untested. Did he die before Sabina? A Lundby/Göteborg household examination would show her marital status (änka = widow?).
- **Were there more children?** The 9-year gap between John Oscar (1858/59) and Axel (~1867) strongly suggests additional children, possibly ones who died young or stayed in Sweden.
- **John Oscar's exact birth date** (14 Aug 1858 vs 1859) — the Göteborg (probably Domkyrko, Kristine or Karl Johan parish) birth/christening record would settle it, and would also give Sabina's stated age, cross-checking her 1826 birth year.
- Did Sabina live near/with relatives in Lundby in 1909? The Swedish 1890/1900 censuses (folkräkningar, free at Riksarkivet but captcha-gated) should locate her household.
