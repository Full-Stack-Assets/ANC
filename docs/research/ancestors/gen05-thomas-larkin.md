# Thomas Larkin & Catherine Burton (Generation 5) — duplicate tree node (I182584905624)

**Data-mismatch flag**: the research brief for this item named the subject couple as "Patrick
Larkin & Winifred Burns," but the tree JSON actually supplied under this item ID
(`I182584905624`) is for **Thomas Larkin** (b. 1825, Co. Galway; d. 14 Nov 1898, Lancashire)
and his wife **Catherine Burton** (m. 23 Nov 1851, Liverpool) — the identical couple already
researched in `docs/research/ancestors/gen05-thomas-larkin-catherine-burton.md` under different
tree IDs (I182789737425 / I182625291737). No couple named "Patrick Larkin & Winifred Burns"
appears anywhere in the supplied data; "Winifred Burns" is phonetically close to both
"Winifreda Mulken" (this Thomas's putative mother) and "Catherine Burton" (his wife), and
"Patrick" appears in this record only as a child's given name ("Patrick James Larkin") and as
the name of an unrelated Irish-resident brother turned up in new research below. This dossier
follows the actual tree data supplied, per instructions, and treats the couple as Thomas
Larkin & Catherine Burton. The mismatch is reported to the user/orchestrator as an open
question rather than silently resolved.

## Overview

This tree node duplicates the couple already fully documented in
`gen05-thomas-larkin-catherine-burton.md`: Thomas Larkin (Ireland-born, ~1825/28) and Catherine
Burton, married in Liverpool in 1851, parents of at least nine children raised in the St
Alban's parish district (Vauxhall, Liverpool) and enumerated together in the 1861, 1871 and
1881 England censuses. This node, however, carries three additional events not present on the
other node — an 1860 Wisconsin, USA residence, an 1875 naturalization, and a 31 March 1901
residence at Muckanagh, Meelick, Co. Galway (paired with a 14 Jul 1839 Galway baptism) — that
are internally impossible for this man (he was continuously resident in Liverpool 1861–1881
and died in 1898) and that this dossier traces to **at least two other, unrelated men also
named Thomas Larkin**. The most important new finding is a full identification of the real
1901 Irish census household behind the Muckanagh event: it is headed by a *different*,
unmarried, Ireland-resident Thomas Larkin (b. ~1841) living with his brother Patrick Larkin's
family — not our Liverpool ancestor.

## Verified facts

- **Marriage (Thomas Larkin × Catherine Burton, 23 Nov 1851, Liverpool) and death (14 Nov 1898,
  Lancashire) are independently confirmed via the Lancashire BMD civil-registration index** —
  already established in the companion dossier `gen05-thomas-larkin-catherine-burton.md`
  (marriage ref. LL:REG_LP/40/194; death ref. LL:ISL/131/7, Islington sub-district, Liverpool,
  age 70). Not re-run this pass since it is the same real person; cross-referenced here rather
  than duplicated. [Lancashire BMD, cited in companion dossier]
- **1901 Census of Ireland, Galway / Meelick / Muckanagh, House 5: household headed by a
  Thomas Larkin, age 60, unmarried, farmer, Roman Catholic, born Co. Galway**, living with
  brother **Patrick Larkin** (45, married, farmer), sister-in-law **Anne Larkin** (40), niece
  **Ellen Larkin** (7), and nephews **Peter** (6), **Patrick** (5), **James** (3), **Michael**
  (1), and **Timothy** Larkin (age not given) — all born Co. Galway, all Roman Catholic.
  Retrieved directly from the National Archives of Ireland census database. [National Archives
  of Ireland, Census of Ireland 1901, Galway/Meelick/Muckanagh, house 5]
- **This Muckanagh household head cannot be the Liverpool Thomas Larkin.** The Liverpool
  Thomas died 14 Nov 1898 (already a sourced event on this same tree node) — two and a half
  years before the 1901 Irish census was taken (31 Mar 1901) — and he was married to Catherine
  Burton from 1851, whereas the Muckanagh head is recorded as unmarried. The Muckanagh man's
  age (60 in 1901 ⇒ b. ~1841) also does not match the Liverpool Thomas's age-based birth year
  of ~1827/28 (from the 1898 Lancashire BMD death index, age 70) nor the tree's own "1825"
  birth year for this node.
- **The baptism date on this node, "14 Jul 1839, Galway," is a closer age-fit for the Muckanagh
  farmer (b. ~1841 by census math) than for the Liverpool emigrant (b. ~1827/28 by the death
  index).** This supports the baptism event being misattached from the Muckanagh man's record
  onto the Liverpool man's tree profile, rather than being a genuine record of the Liverpool
  ancestor's own baptism.
- **The children list on this node (Patrick James, Margaret, Catherine, Frances Ann, Frances H,
  Thomas, William, "Clemon" [likely Clement], and Ellen Larkin) matches the Liverpool St
  Alban's-parish family already identified from the 1861/1871/1881 England censuses** in the
  companion gen-4 dossier (`gen04-thomas-larkin-mary-blackmore.md`), which independently listed
  "Patrick James, Margaret, Catherine, Frances Ann, William and Ellen Larkin" as siblings from
  the same censuses. This is internally consistent with the children belonging to the Liverpool
  couple, not the Muckanagh household.

## Corrections to the tree

1. **"residence: 1860 @ Barton, Barton, Washington, Wisconsin, USA" [unsourced] → remove; does
   not belong to this Thomas Larkin.** He is independently and continuously documented in
   Liverpool by the tree's own sourced 1861, 1871 and 1881 England censuses; a man cannot be
   resident in rural Wisconsin in 1860 and enumerated as a Liverpool household head one year
   later in 1861. This event belongs to a different, US-immigrant Thomas Larkin of the same
   common name (not independently identified in this pass — Wisconsin census/naturalization
   indexes are not freely searchable online; see Open Questions).
2. **"naturalization: 1 Nov 1875" [unsourced, no place given] → remove or reattach to the
   Wisconsin Thomas Larkin.** "Naturalization" is a U.S. legal process; Ireland was part of the
   United Kingdom throughout this period, so an Ireland-born man resident in Liverpool (as our
   Thomas was, per the sourced English censuses) was already a British subject by birth and had
   no naturalization to undergo. This event's presence strongly corroborates the Wisconsin
   conflation in correction #1 — both events likely belong to the same unrelated American
   Thomas Larkin.
3. **"residence: 31 Mar 1901 @ Muckanagh, Meelick, Galway, Ireland" [sourced] → remove from
   this node; belongs to a different Thomas Larkin.** This Thomas Larkin's own sourced death
   event on the same tree node is 14 Nov 1898, almost 2.5 years before the 1901 Irish census
   date. The actual household recorded at Muckanagh house 5 in 1901 is headed by a different,
   unmarried, Ireland-resident Thomas Larkin (age 60) — see Verified Facts above. The Ireland
   census citation is real and correctly retrieved, but attached to the wrong person.
4. **"baptism: 14 Jul 1839 @ Galway, Ireland" [sourced] → probably misattached from the
   Muckanagh Thomas Larkin's record, not this node's Liverpool emigrant.** Age math favors the
   Muckanagh man (b. ~1841) over the Liverpool man (b. ~1827/28 per the 1898 Lancashire BMD
   death-index age); flag pending direct inspection of the original Meelick/Fahy Catholic
   parish register.
5. **This entire tree node (I182584905624) is a duplicate of I182789737425 (Thomas) /
   I182625291737 (Catherine) already researched in `gen05-thomas-larkin-catherine-burton.md`.**
   Identical spouse, identical 1851 Liverpool marriage, identical 1898 Lancashire death, and
   matching 1861/1871/1881 England census citations. Recommend merging the two nodes in the
   family tree software to prevent future duplicate research and conflicting edits.
6. **Birth "1825 @ Co Galway" is a specific year within, but at the edge of, the previously
   established plausible range (~1823–1828) from the 1898 death-index age of 70** — see the
   companion dossier for the fuller discussion; no new correction beyond noting the two nodes
   should be reconciled to a single stated birth year (or range).

## New findings (not in the tree)

- **Full identification of the real 1901 Irish census household behind the misattached
  Muckanagh event**: Thomas Larkin (60, unmarried farmer, head) with brother Patrick Larkin
  (45, married farmer), sister-in-law Anne Larkin (40), niece Ellen (7), and nephews Peter (6),
  Patrick (5), James (3), Michael (1), and Timothy Larkin, all Roman Catholic and Co.
  Galway-born. This is a previously untraced record for this research thread and is very
  likely connected — as a brother's, uncle's, or cousin's household — to whatever Galway Larkin
  family produced the "Laurence Larkin & Winifreda Mulken" parentage claim carried on this tree
  node, though the exact relationship is not yet proven.
- The presence of a **Patrick Larkin, age 45 (b. ~1856), as brother of the Muckanagh household
  head** is notable given the research brief's mention of a "Patrick Larkin" as a possible
  subject — but this Patrick's age rules him out as the "Patrick James Larkin" child listed
  among the Liverpool couple's children (who would have been born after the 1851 marriage,
  making him at most ~44–49 in 1901, and in any case tied to the Liverpool household, not an
  Ireland-resident farm family). These appear to be two different Patrick Larkins.
- Cross-reference: the companion dossier (`gen05-thomas-larkin-catherine-burton.md`, Open
  Question 1) had already flagged that the "14 Jul 1839" baptism date and the "Laurence
  Larkin & Winifreda Mulken / Muckanagh" parentage claim were hard to reconcile with a
  Liverpool emigrant born ~1823–28, and recommended checking against a then-unwritten "A51"
  work item (Laurence Larkin & Winifreda Mulken). This item (A29) appears to be exactly that
  promised reconciliation data, arriving attached to a duplicate Thomas Larkin node rather than
  to a fresh Laurence Larkin/Winifreda Mulken node — which is itself useful confirmation that
  the tree's data entry for this family is scattered/duplicated across multiple person records.

## Open questions

1. **Subject-line mismatch**: this item's assignment described the subject as "Patrick Larkin
   & Winifred Burns," but no such couple exists in the supplied tree data. Flagging for the
   task owner in case a different item's data was intended for this dossier, or in case a
   genuinely distinct "Patrick Larkin & Winifred/Winifreda ... Burns/Burton" couple exists
   elsewhere in the tree and was not supplied here.
2. **How is the Muckanagh household (Thomas Larkin b. ~1841 and brother Patrick Larkin b.
   ~1856) related, if at all, to our Liverpool Thomas Larkin (b. ~1825/28)?** Possible
   relationships: younger brothers who stayed in Ireland while an elder brother emigrated to
   Liverpool; more distant cousins sharing common grandparents; or no relationship beyond
   sharing a common Galway surname. The "Laurence Larkin & Winifreda Mulken" parentage claim on
   this node needs to be tested against whichever generation the Muckanagh household actually
   belongs to.
3. **Who is the Thomas Larkin behind the 1860 Wisconsin residence / 1875 naturalization
   events?** Not identified in this pass; Wisconsin naturalization indexes are not freely
   searchable online (Wisconsin Historical Society holds originals by county, in-person or via
   Area Research Centers only) and FamilySearch's 1860 census search requires a login. Worth a
   dedicated look-up given how common the name "Thomas Larkin" is among Irish immigrants of
   this era.
4. Per the companion dossier: order the GRO certificates behind Lancashire BMD refs
   LL:REG_LP/40/194 (1851 marriage) and LL:ISL/131/7 (1898 death) — these would very likely
   name Thomas's actual parents and resolve the Laurence Larkin/Winifreda Mulken question
   directly.
5. **Merge this duplicate tree node (I182584905624) with I182789737425/I182625291737** to
   avoid future research and edits being split across two records of the same person.
