# Russell Morrow & Sarah Jane (Swallow) Morrow — Generation 4 Deep Dive

Research date: 2026-07-03. Subjects: Russell Morrow (b. abt 1871/72, Eglington, PEI – d. ?) and
Sarah Jane (Swallow) Morrow (b. abt 1872/73, Lot 56, Kings Co., PEI – d. ?), direct ancestors of
the tree owner (through daughter Abigail Elizabeth Morrow, who married Douglas Leon Holst in 1930).

## Overview

Both spouses came from adjoining communities in eastern Kings County, Prince Edward Island —
Russell from the East Point / North Lake / Eglington district, Sarah Jane from Little Pond (Lot 56) —
and ended up in Boston, Massachusetts by 1930. Web research confirmed and greatly extended both
parental lines (Morrow of East Point via the Island Register compiled genealogy; Swallow of Little
Pond, with a documented 1868 PEI marriage bond for Sarah's parents). It also surfaced two serious
problems with the tree: the 1940 Halifax/Nova Scotia death data almost certainly belongs to a
*different* Sarah Jane Morrow (proven by the register image), and the 31 Aug 1898 Dover NH marriage
conflicts with a documented 17 Sep 1892 PEI marriage of Sarah Jane Swallow to Charles Richard Palmer.

## Verified facts

- **Russell's parentage — John MacDonald and Elizabeth Morrow — confirmed** (compiled/secondary).
  The Island Register "Descendants of John Morrow" genealogy (submitted by Fulton Underhay) lists
  "Elizabeth Morrow #1421, b. about 1831, Occ.: Housemaid, and John MacDonald #3152 … d. Burnt at Sea.
  Elizabeth died about 1901, Mattapan Massachusetts USA, Religion: C. Presbyterian," with child
  "Russell William McDonald Morrow #3151 b. Abt. 1871." Russell bore his mother's surname
  (his full name preserves "McDonald"). Source: islandregister.com/morrow1.html.
- **Russell's birthplace Eglington, PEI confirmed** (compiled): the Island Register Swallow page
  gives "Russell William McDonald Morrow #3151, born Abt. 1871, Eglington Prince Edward Island
  (son of John MacDonald #3152 and Elizabeth Morrow #1421) … Religion: C. Presbyterian."
  Source: islandregister.com/swallow.html. (Tree says "abt 1872" — one year off, within tolerance.)
- **Sarah Jane's parentage — William "Curley The Sailor" Swallow and Abigail Jenkins — confirmed**
  (compiled + primary corroboration). Island Register "Descendants of Robert Swallow and Kate
  Blackett": "William 'Curley' 'The Sailor' Swallow, born 10 Jun 1846, Little Pond PEI, Occ.: Farmer,
  married 20 Jun 1868 Abigail Jenkins, born 9 Feb 1851 … William died 16 Jun 1903, Little Pond,
  Interred: Annandale United Baptist Cemetery, Religion: Baptist. Abigail died 24 Jul 1912, interred
  Annandale United Baptist Cemetery." Daughter "Sarah Jane Swallow #16907 born Abt. 1873."
- **Sarah's parents' 1868 marriage documented in a primary index**: PARO Collections Database
  (PEI Public Archives), Marriage Record — Groom: William Swallow, residence Lot 56; Bride: Abigail
  Jenkins, residence Lot 56; Source: RG19/s3/ss5 Marriage bonds, 1868 (record ID 296707).
- **Sarah's 1891 residence at Lot 56, Kings Co. confirmed**: PARO index of the 1891 Census of Canada
  (PEI) shows, in Lot 56, Baptist households: SWALLOW William 44, Abigail 40, Frederick 20,
  **Sarah 18**, Priscilla 15, Ulysses 11, Gertrude 9, Mable 7, Minnie 5, Fanny 4, Lilla 2 — matching
  the tree's sourced 1891 census event.
- **Sarah in the 1881 Census (new)**: PARO 1881 index, Lot 56 (Baptist): William 34, Abigail 29,
  Frederick 10, **Sarah F.[J.] 8**, Priscilla 5, Edith 3, Ulysius 1.
- **Russell's probable mother in the censuses (new, inferred)**: PARO 1881 index has MORROW,
  Elizabeth, age 50, C. Presbyterian, **Lot 56**; PARO 1891 index has MORROW, Elizabeth, 53,
  Presbyterian, Lot 56. Age fits b. ~1831, religion matches the compiled genealogy. Notably, no
  Russell Morrow (or given name Russell under any surname) appears in the PARO 1881, 1891, or 1901
  PEI census indexes — consistent with him working/living in the US by 1890 (tree's sourced
  arrival 1890, departure Charlottetown, arrival Boston from his Massachusetts naturalization file).
- **Children Abigail Elizabeth and Howard Russell confirmed with new detail** (compiled):
  "Abigail Elizabeth Morrow #69899 born 11 Apr 1910, Little Pond Prince Edward Island, … married
  --1930, Douglas Leon Holst #69898. Abigail died 1958" (cause of death: cancer). "Howard Russell
  Morrow #69941 born 28 Aug 1913, Milton Norfolk Massachusetts USA, married 24 Jun 1936, By/At:
  Dover Strafford [written 'Stratford'] New Hampshire USA, Drusilla Amelia Richie #69942."
  Source: islandregister.com/swallow.html.

## Corrections to the tree

1. **Sarah Jane's death "8 June 1940" (unsourced) is almost certainly a false match.**
   The only Sarah Jane Morrow death in the Nova Scotia index near that date (d. 7 June 1940,
   Waverley, Halifax County — NS Archives death registration 1940, book 193, no. 50) was checked
   against the original register image: that woman was **born 13 March 1914 at Pictou, NS**, aged 26,
   wife of Edward S. Morrow, daughter of Wm. O. Harris and Helen Mills. She cannot be our Sarah
   (b. 1872/73, PEI). The tree's date and Russell's "Halifax" death place appear to derive from this
   wrong record. **Correct value: unknown death date/place — needs research in Massachusetts records.**
2. **Russell's death "1940 @ Halifax, Nova Scotia" (unsourced) is unsupported.** The Nova Scotia
   Archives death index (online coverage through 1971) contains **no Russell Morrow at all** in
   1938–1971 (or any year). Likely the same false-match contamination as above. Correct value:
   unknown; last documented alive in Boston in the 1930 US census.
3. **Marriage "31 Aug 1898 @ Dover, Strafford, New Hampshire" is in conflict with primary PEI
   records.** The PARO marriage index documents **Sarah Jane Swallow marrying Charles Richard
   Palmer, license 16 Sep 1892, ceremony 17 Sep 1892** (Island Register adds: at Annandale, PEI),
   with a son LeRoy Palmer born abt 1899, and Palmer dying abt 1900. The compiled Swallow genealogy
   dates her marriage to Russell Morrow as "Abt. 1909," and their daughter Abigail was born at
   Little Pond, PEI in April 1910. Either (a) the 1898 Dover record belongs to a different couple
   (there were at least two contemporaneous "Sarah J. Swallow"s — another one married Hiram M.
   Blaisdelle on 24 Aug 1910 per the same PEI index), or (b) Russell married a *first* wife in Dover
   in 1898 (possibly the mother of Estella Myrtle and Laura M) and the tree has merged two wives.
   The original NH record needs to be re-read for the bride's name and parents.
4. **Sarah's "arrival 1890" immigration year is wrong for her.** She was enumerated in PEI in 1891,
   married there in 1892, and bore a child at Little Pond in April 1910; her real move to the US was
   between April 1910 and August 1913 (Howard born Milton, MA). The 1890 figure (1930 census
   immigration column) can apply at most to Russell, whose naturalization file supports a ~1890
   Charlottetown-to-Boston move.
5. Minor: Russell's birth year is given as abt 1871 (not 1872) in both Island Register genealogies.

## New findings (not in the tree)

- **Russell's full name: Russell William McDonald Morrow** — useful for record searches under both
  Morrow and McDonald/MacDonald spellings.
- **Russell's father John MacDonald died "Burnt at Sea"** (compiled; no date given).
- **Russell's mother Elizabeth Morrow emigrated too, dying abt 1901 in Mattapan (Boston), MA** —
  a lead for Massachusetts death registers, and consistent with Russell settling in Boston.
- **Morrow grandparents (new generation):** Elizabeth was daughter of **John Morrow (III), b. 17 Jul
  1792 at East Point, PEI, d. 20 Aug 1872 at North Lake, PEI**, and **Margaret Coffin** (m. 4 Jan 1815).
  Elizabeth's siblings: John (IV) (b. ~1819), James, David George (b. ~1827), Artemas (b. 27 Feb 1835,
  d. 1921 at Eglington — likely the household where Russell was born), Joseph, and William.
- **Swallow immigrant origin:** the Swallow line runs Sarah Jane → William "Curley" (b. 1846) →
  Robert Swallow Jr. → **Robert Swallow, b. abt 1790 in Yorkshire, England**, who married
  **Catherine "Kate" Blackett** (b. 1786).
- **Sarah Jane's siblings** (Island Register): Frederick Kitson (b. 1871), Priscilla (b. 1875),
  Ulysses (b. 1879), Edna, Gertrude (b. ~1882), Mabel (b. ~1884, m. Pius E. "Sailor" MacDonald),
  Lilla Belle (b. 1884), Frances (b. 1888), Ella (b. 1893); plus Edith (b. ~1878, in 1881 census).
- **Sarah Jane's first marriage and half-sibling to the Morrow children:** Charles Richard Palmer
  (m. 17 Sep 1892; d. abt 1900) and son **LeRoy Palmer** (b. abt 1899) — the tree omits this entire
  first family.
- **Grandparents' graves:** William and Abigail (Jenkins) Swallow are both interred at Annandale
  United Baptist Cemetery, Annandale, Kings Co., PEI (findagrave cemetery #2306973 exists; site
  blocked robotic access, memorials not individually verified).
- **Howard Russell Morrow's wife and marriage:** Drusilla Amelia Richie, m. 24 Jun 1936 at Dover, NH
  — an odd echo of the Dover connection, suggesting genuine family ties to Dover/Strafford Co.
- **Abigail Elizabeth (Morrow) Holst died 1958, of cancer** (compiled).

## Open questions

- Where and when did Russell and Sarah Jane actually die? Boston-area records (Massachusetts death
  index 1916–1970, Boston city directories, Mount Hope/other Boston cemeteries) are the obvious next
  vein; neither appears in the Nova Scotia death index.
- What exactly does the 31 Aug 1898 Dover, NH marriage record say (bride's full name, her parents,
  prior marital status)? This determines whether the tree has merged two different wives, or whether
  the Island Register's Palmer marriage/1909 remarriage chronology is off (e.g., Palmer could have
  died 1897-98 and "LeRoy b. abt 1899" could be earlier).
- Births of Estella Myrtle Morrow and Laura M Morrow: not found in the Island Register genealogies or
  the PARO baptismal index. If born 1899–1908 they were likely born in New Hampshire or
  Massachusetts — check NH/MA birth registers; if they were Sarah's children, their birthplaces will
  date the family's movements.
- Russell in 1881/1891 PEI censuses: not indexed under Morrow or "Russell" — check images for the
  Elizabeth Morrow household (Lot 56) for a boy indexed under MacDonald or a middle name, and check
  the 1900 US census (Sarah's tree birth "Mar 1872" is in the 1900-census month+year style,
  suggesting a 1900 US household exists somewhere — but whose wife was she then?).
- Russell's Massachusetts naturalization file (tree-sourced): obtain the petition — it should give
  exact birth date/place, arrival date/port, address, and witnesses.
- LeRoy Palmer's fate (b. ~1899): did he come to Boston with his mother?
