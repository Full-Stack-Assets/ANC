# Johanna Andersdotter (1793–1856)

## Overview
Johanna Andersdotter was born 13 August 1793 in the fishing village of Mollösund (Morlanda parish, Orust, Bohuslän — today Västra Götaland County), Sweden, and died 5 November 1856 in Torsby parish, Bohuslän. She was the daughter of Anders Månsson, a "lotsålderman" (senior harbor pilot/pilot-foreman) of Mollösund, and Margareta ("Greta") Nilsdotter. This dossier newly identifies her parents' vital dates, her full sibling set, and a notable coincidence — her father and one brother died on the same day — using a compiled parish-based family register for Morlanda socken.

## Verified facts
- Born 13 August 1793 in Mollösund, Morlanda socken, Bohuslän — tree flagged this as sourced, and it is independently corroborated by the parish-derived family register for Morlanda's fiskelägen (fishing villages), which records "Johanna Andersdotter 17930813 Mollösund" as a child of Anders Månsson and Margareta Nilsdotter. [morlanda.se, Familjeregister för fiskelägena i Morlanda socken]
- Died 5 November 1856 in Torsby, Bohuslän (per tree, flagged sourced). Torsby socken is a genuine, separate parish in Inlands Södre härad, Bohuslän (part of Kungälv municipality since 1971), with a documented medieval church — confirming the place-name is real and geographically plausible as a destination for a woman born in the Morlanda/Orust area (there was regular migration between Morlanda and Torsby socknar in this period, per the same family register). [Wikipedia: Torsby socken; Wikipedia: Torsby kyrka; morlanda.se register, multiple "flyttade till/från Torsby socken" entries]
- Parents per tree: "greta nilsdotter" and "Anders Månsson." This matches the parish register's Anders Månsson (b. 11 Jan 1752, Mollösund; d. 1 May 1796, Mollösund), "lotsålderman," married 8 Nov 1775 to Margareta Nilsdotter (b. 25 Nov 1755, Mollösund). "Greta" is the standard Swedish diminutive of "Margareta," so this is not a discrepancy — same person, informal-name form. [morlanda.se, Familjeregister för fiskelägena i Morlanda socken]

## New findings (not in the tree)
- **Father's occupation and dates:** Anders Månsson was recorded as "Lotsåldermannen" (senior/foreman harbor pilot) at Mollösund — a position of local standing in this seafaring village. Born 11 Jan 1752, died 1 May 1796, both in Mollösund. [morlanda.se]
- **Mother's dates:** Margareta (Greta) Nilsdotter, born 25 Nov 1755 in Mollösund; married Anders Månsson 8 Nov 1775 in Mollösund. No death date is given for her in this register, meaning she likely was still living when the register's data ends or died outside the tracked area/period.

  - Jakob Andersson, b. 6 Jun 1788, d. 26 Jun 1861
  - Johanna Andersdotter, b. 27 Jul 1791, d. 12 Jun 1792 (an elder sister who died in infancy — Johanna the ancestor was effectively a "replacement" name, common Swedish practice)
  - **Johanna Andersdotter** (the subject), b. 13 Aug 1793
  - Anna Andersdotter, b. 22 Mar 1796 (born roughly seven weeks before her father's death)
- **Notable coincidence flagged for follow-up:** father Anders Månsson and son Petter Andersson both died on 1 May 1796, the same day. Given the family's maritime occupation (pilot family in a fishing/pilot village), this strongly suggests a single fatal incident — most plausibly a drowning or boating accident — though the register gives no cause of death. This is an **inference**, not documented, and would benefit from checking the Morlanda dödbok (death register) for 1796 directly.
- **"Rönn" as a real Bohuslän soldier/place name:** the tree records a son of Johanna's, "Johannes Rönn fd. (Olsson) Soldat," i.e., a soldier who used the assumed military name "Rönn" but was born an Olsson. Web research confirms "Rönn" (also written Slussen/Rönn) was a genuine loading/place name in the Bohuslän coastal area, and Torsby socken's indelta (allotted) soldiers belonged to Bohusläns regemente, Södra kompaniet — consistent with, though not proof of, this son's recorded identity. This is circumstantial corroboration only.
- The tree's other listed children of Johanna — Anna Lovisa Olsdotter and Anders Holst — could not be independently verified in this session; see Open Questions. Notably, the surname "Holst" recurs elsewhere in this family tree (a later generation, gen03-douglas-leon-holst, exists in this same research repository), suggesting the Holst surname may be a real, continuing family line worth cross-checking against this Anders Holst.
