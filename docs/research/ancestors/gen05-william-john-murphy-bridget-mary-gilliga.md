## Overview

William John Murphy was born January 1859 in Rockland (at the time still the
Third/East Parish of Abington, not incorporated as its own town until 1874),
Plymouth County, Massachusetts, to Irish immigrant parents John Murphy and
Ellen Flynn Murphy. On 25 Dec 1880 in Weymouth, MA, he married Bridget Mary
Gilligan (b. 11 Aug 1861, Weymouth, daughter of James Gilligan and Julia
McCue). The couple raised at least seven children before Bridget died young,
in Brockton, on 19 Feb 1898, aged 36. William outlived her by 45 years, dying
in Avon, Norfolk County, MA, on 24 Jul 1943, aged 84. This deep dive found
strong internal corroboration of the couple's core facts via a companion
dossier already in this repo (their daughter Emeline's), but found the open
free web thin for independent verification of the couple's own records —
Ancestry, FamilySearch's record-image viewer, and Find a Grave's own search
are all gated against anonymous/automated access from this environment. The
most substantive new finding is a strong circumstantial case that William
remarried after 1898, based on internal inconsistency in the tree's own
children lists (see New findings).

## Corrections to the tree

- **Baptism event is almost certainly misattached.** The tree lists
  "baptism: 18 Jul 1842 @ Tipperary, Ireland" for William John Murphy — but
  William was born January 1859 in Massachusetts. A Tipperary baptism 17
  years *before* an 1859 Massachusetts birth cannot belong to the same
  individual. The event's own cited sources (*Ireland, Select Births and
  Baptisms, 1620-1911*; *Ireland, Catholic Parish Registers, 1655-1915*) are
  Irish-origin collections, consistent instead with an Irish-born adult —
  most plausibly William's own father, **John Murphy**, whose baptism this
  record likely represents (a Tipperary baptism in 1842 would make John
  roughly 17 at William's 1859 birth — young for a father but not impossible,
  or the baptism could be later than birth per period Catholic practice).
  Alternative explanation: an automated hint-matching error linked an
  unrelated Tipperary William Murphy to this profile. **Recommend:** detach
  this event from William John Murphy, or reassign it to father John Murphy,
  pending direct record review (login-gated from this environment).
- **"Residence: Kerry St. [sourced]" carries no date and no town.** Given the
  family's known towns (Rockland, Weymouth, Brockton), this is very likely a
  residence in one of those three, but the tree does not say which. Not
  necessarily a wrong fact, but incomplete as recorded — flag for a
  city-directory or census follow-up to supply town and date.
- **"Rockland" as the 1859 birthplace is an anachronistic but not incorrect
  label.** Rockland was still part of Abington (locally, the Third/East
  Parish) until incorporation in 1874; a record actually created in 1859
  would read "Abington," not "Rockland." Not a factual error, but worth
  annotating so future record searches also try "Abington."

## New findings (not in the tree)

- **Probable second marriage for William John Murphy (inferred, not
  documented).** The tree gives William 11 children but credits Bridget Mary
  Gilligan as mother of only 7 of them: Emeline Jane, Julia Maria, William
  Francis, Abbie Teresa, Robert Emmet, Charles Henry Sr, and Walter Joseph.
  Four more children appear only under William — a second **Walter Joseph
  Murphy** entry (see duplicate note below), **William Henry Murphy**,
  **Grace Agnes Murphy Sullivan**, and **Olive Catherine Murphy O'Malley** —
  and are not listed among Bridget's children. Since Bridget died in 1898 and
  William did not die until 1943, it is plausible some or all of these four
  were born to a second wife after 1898, whom the tree does not name. This is
  the most actionable open lead in this dossier: a Massachusetts marriage
  index search (1898–1910, "William J. Murphy," Plymouth/Norfolk/Bristol
  counties) and the 1900/1910/1920 federal censuses (which would show a
  post-1898 wife, if any, and the birth years of the four children in
  question) would resolve it.
- **Duplicate "Walter Joseph Murphy" entry.** The name appears twice in
  William's children list — once also under Bridget's children (implying
  birth before 1898), once listed only under William's (implying it could be
  a later, second child of the same name). This is either (a) a data-entry
  duplication of one real child, or (b) two distinct children sharing a name
  because an earlier Walter Joseph died young and the name was reused for a
  later child — a common 19th-century Catholic naming custom, and one that
  would also support the second-marriage theory above. Needs disambiguation
  via birth/death records.
- **Occupational context (inferred, not documented for this individual).**
  Rockland's economy in this era was dominated by boot-and-shoe
  manufacturing. An Irish Catholic laboring-class family like the Murphys is
  very likely to have been tied to the shoe trade, paralleling the
  independently-researched Regan/Reagan in-laws in nearby "Shoe City"
  Brockton (per the companion gen04 dossier). No direct record found ties
  William John Murphy to a specific shoe-industry job; flagged as a lead for
  a census or city-directory occupation column, not a documented fact.
- **Naming pattern.** Son Robert Emmet Murphy carries the name of the Irish
  patriot Robert Emmet, and several children carry saints'-name
  combinations (Abbie Teresa, Grace Agnes, Olive Catherine, Charles Henry) —
  consistent with, but not proof of, a devoutly Irish Catholic household.
  Contextual observation only, no record found.

## Open questions

1. Did William John Murphy remarry after Bridget's 1898 death, and who was
   the mother of the four children not attributed to Bridget? Check the MA
   marriage index 1898–1910 and the 1900/1910/1920 federal censuses.
2. Which town does "Kerry St." belong to — Rockland, Weymouth, or Brockton? A
   city directory or census address would settle this.
3. Whose baptism is the "18 Jul 1842, Tipperary" record actually for — father
   John Murphy, or an unrelated same-named Murphy incorrectly linked? Direct
   viewing of the Ireland, Catholic Parish Registers image is needed.
4. Where are William (d. 1943, Avon) and Bridget (d. 1898, Brockton) buried?
   Given that William died in Avon, and the Regan/Reagan in-law family (see
   gen03 dossier) is linked to St. Michael's Cemetery, Avon, that cemetery is
   a plausible candidate for William; Bridget more likely rests in a
   Brockton Catholic cemetery (Calvary or St. Patrick's). Find a Grave's own
   search function is bot-blocked from this environment — a manual visit, or
   a login-based Ancestry/FamilySearch search, is needed.
5. What became of Julia Maria, Abbie Teresa, William Francis, Charles Henry
   Sr, William Henry, Grace Agnes (Sullivan), and Olive Catherine (O'Malley)
   Murphy — marriage dates, spouses, and death information? None were found
   this pass; none appear in any freely searchable index (Find a Grave,
   WikiTree, Geni) under these specific name/date combinations.
6. Full identities, birth/death dates, and immigration details for parents
   John Murphy & Ellen Flynn Murphy, and for James Gilligan & Julia McCue —
   presumably Irish-born immigrants to Massachusetts. No immigration records
   located this pass.
7. Census confirmation across William's long life: he should appear in the
   1860 census as an infant, 1870 (as a boy), 1880 (pre-marriage), and then
   1900/1910/1920/1930/1940 as a Brockton-area widower or remarried head of
   household; Bridget should appear in 1870, 1880, and briefly 1900 records
   (deceased). None were retrievable from open web search this pass — the
   record images are login-gated.
