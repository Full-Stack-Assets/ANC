# The "Persdotter" Line — Lineage Survey

## Overview

This "surname line" is not a genuine multi-generation lineage but a **data-modeling artifact**. "Persdotter" is a Swedish patronymic ("daughter of Per/Peder/Pehr"), not a hereditary family surname, so the tree's surname-grouping has collected four biologically unrelated women who merely happened to have fathers named Per — born a century apart, in four different provinces of Sweden, with no parent-child relationship to one another. There is no continuous "generations 7-12" chain to reconstruct here: each woman appears as an isolated leaf with no parents recorded and exactly one child. None of the four has a documented immigrant crossing to America — all events are Swedish parish/place names — so the Coffin/Bunker/Swallow/Pond-style New England immigrant research does not apply to this particular slice of the tree (that pattern belongs to other surname lines in this family file). Web research turned up no confirmed matching individual records for any of the four; the strongest hits were same-named/same-parish women with different vital dates, more likely coincidental namesakes than the tree's subjects.

## Verified facts

- "Persdotter" (also spelled Pehrsdotter/Pedersdotter in the events) is a Swedish patronymic surname, formed from the father's given name + "-dotter" ("daughter of"), standard practice in Sweden until surnames were fixed in the 19th–20th centuries. This is general historical/genealogical background knowledge, not a tree-specific fact, but it explains why the four "Persdotter" people in this file are unrelated to each other. [Source: general knowledge of Swedish patronymic naming customs, corroborated by the disparate provinces/eras below]
- Tolsbo, Grufriset, and other place-names in Norrbärke parish, Kopparberg (Dalarna) are confirmed real historical locations in that parish. [Source: FamilySearch Research Wiki, "Norrbärke Parish, Kopparberg, Sweden Genealogy," https://www.familysearch.org/en/wiki/Norrb%C3%A4rke_Parish,_Kopparberg,_Sweden_Genealogy]
- Hökhult (Fröderyd parish) and Urås (Ramkvilla parish), Jönköping county, are confirmed real historical locations, and death/marriage register indexes for Ramkvilla and Fröderyd exist and are digitized. [Source: Mina Anor parish register indexes, https://minaanor.com/ramkvilla-dodbok/ and https://minaanor.com/register-till-froderyd-vigselbok-1688-1800/]
- A "Måns Månsson Rånge" appears in an online compiled tree (Geni) as born 3 Oct 1713, Skattegården Velinga, died 19 Apr 1762, Skattegården Hadäng, Velinga, with children including a "Magnus Månsson [Rånge]." This is consistent in name-pattern with the tree's "Måns Månsson Rånge," husband of Maria Persdotter (gen. 9), and their child "Magnus Månsson Rånge" — but the Geni record's dates (1713–1762) were not independently corroborated with a primary parish source, and no birth/death years are given for Maria Persdotter or Måns Månsson Rånge in the tree data itself, so this is only a plausible/inferred match. [Source: Geni.com, "Måns Månsson Rånge (1713–1762)," https://www.geni.com/people/M%C3%A5ns-M%C3%A5nsson-R%C3%A5nge/6000000084079789851 — compiled/secondary, unverified against primary records]

## Corrections to the tree

No corrections could be confirmed with primary-source confidence. One conflict worth flagging for tree maintainers to investigate further:

- **Possible date/identity conflict for Ingrid Persdotter (b. 1651, Hökhult; d. 1720, Urås, Ramkvilla) -> unresolved, needs primary-source check.** Web search surfaced two *different* women also named Ingrid Persdotter tied to the same two villages (Hökhult, Fröderyd and Urås, Ramkvilla) but with vital dates that do not match the tree: (a) a FamilySearch compiled profile "Ingrid Persdotter Bratt" dated c.1628–1651 (i.e., she died in 1651, the very year the tree's Ingrid was born); and (b) a parish-register-index hit describing an "Ingrid Persdotter" who died in Urås, Ramkvilla on 30 Mar 1706 at age 15 (implying birth c.1691), born in Hökhult, Fröderyd. Neither matches the tree's 1651–1720 Ingrid Persdotter. This is very plausibly a case of several different women sharing name + home village across generations (common with patronymics recurring in small parishes), and the tree's specific 1651/1720 dates could not be confirmed against any primary record found. Recommend tree owner check the original source of the 1651 birth / 1720 death claim (currently flagged `[unsourced]` in the tree itself) against the Fröderyd/Ramkvilla parish registers (via Mina Anor's indexes or ArkivDigital/Riksarkivet) before trusting it further. [Sources: FamilySearch, "Ingrid Persdotter Bratt (about 1628–1651)," https://ancestors.familysearch.org/en/93KT-49V/ingrid-persdotter-bratt-1628-1651; Mina Anor, "Ramkvilla Dödbok," https://minaanor.com/ramkvilla-dodbok/]

## Open questions / data-quality flags

1. **This is not a real lineage chain.** The tree data provided contains four *disconnected* women, not a continuous ancestor-to-ancestor chain across generations 7–12:
   - **Maria Persdotter** (generation 9) — no birth/death year given, no parents, spouse Måns Månsson Rånge, child Magnus Månsson Rånge.
   - **Ingrid Persdotter** (generation 12, b. 1651 Hökhult, Frödteryd/Fröderyd, Jönköping — d. 1720 Urås, Ramkvilla) — no parents, spouse Per Svensson, child Elin Pehrsdotter.
   - **Börta Persdotter** (generation 12, b. 1620 Västra Röra, Långelanda, Myckleby, Orust, Bohuslän — d. 1675 Backa, Myckleby) — no parents, spouse Börje Olsson, child Torborg Börjesdotter.
   - **Brita Persdotter** (generation 12, b. 1618 Tolsbo, Norrbärke, Kopparberg/Dalarna — d. 1679 Torberbo, Norrbärke) — no parents, spouse Per Olofsson, child Brita Pedersdotter.
   Two of these are generation 12 pairs from entirely different provinces (Jönköping vs. Bohuslän vs. Dalarna) with overlapping birth years (1618, 1620, 1651) — they cannot be the same lineage rung; they are, at best, three unrelated generation-12 ancestors of three unrelated tree branches that all happen to be tagged with the patronymic "Persdotter." **The documented line ends immediately at each of these four women — there are no sourced parents for any of them, so genealogically the paper trail stops here on all four branches.**
2. **No immigrant ancestor / no American crossing** appears anywhere in this data slice. All locales are Swedish parishes (Jönköping, Bohuslän/Västra Götaland, Kopparberg/Dalarna). The general instruction to look for a colonial New England/New Jersey/Nantucket immigrant (Coffin, Bunker, Swallow, Pond, etc.) does not apply to the Persdotter surname group as delivered — that pattern belongs to a different surname line in this family tree and should not be conflated with this one.
3. **Duplicate-record artifact confirmed on Börta Persdotter.** Her event list contains what are clearly the *same two vital events* (a c.1620 birth and a 1675 death) recorded **six separate times**, each with a slightly different jurisdictional/place-name rendering of the same location:
   - Birth "Västra Röra, Långelanda, Myckleby, Orust Östra, Västra Götaland, Sverige"
   - Birth "...Orust, Västra Götaland, Sverige"
   - Birth "...Orust, Bohuslän, Sweden"
   - Birth "...Orusts Östra, Göteborgs- och Bohus län, Bohuslän, Kingdom of Denmark & Norway" (anachronistic/wrong political entity for 1620 — Bohuslän was part of Norway/Denmark-Norway until the 1658 Treaty of Roskilde, so a "Kingdom of Denmark & Norway" tag on an event coded elsewhere as "Sverige"/"Sweden" is itself an internal contradiction)
   - Death "Backa, Myckleby, Orust, Västra Götaland, Sverige"
   - Death "Backa, Myckleby, Orusts, Bohuslän, Sweden"
   This is a textbook unmerged-duplicate pattern: the same GEDCOM/import event was pulled in multiple times under different place-name normalizations (old vs. modern county names, and inconsistent political-jurisdiction tags). Recommend de-duplicating to one birth event and one death event per person, and picking a single consistent place-name convention (era-appropriate would be "Orust härad, Bohuslän" pre-1658 rather than "Västra Götaland," which is a modern county name).
4. No sources are cited in the tree for any of the four Persdotter individuals (`"sources": []` for all four, and every event is explicitly flagged `[unsourced]`). Given that, and given that web search could not confirm any of the four as specific, individually-identifiable historical persons (only same-name/same-village women with different, non-matching dates), all four should be treated as **legend/unverified tree lore** pending a primary-source check against Swedish parish registers (husförhörslängder / births-marriages-deaths, available via Riksarkivet/ArkivDigital or SVAR).
5. Since none of these four is confirmed as the same person as any web-search hit, no findagrave/FamilySearch/WikiTree profile can responsibly be cited as *this* tree's subject — the near-matches found are flagged above only as leads for the tree owner to check against the tree's cited (but absent) original sources, not as confirmations.
