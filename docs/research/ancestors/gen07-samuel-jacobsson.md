# Samuel Jacobsson & Sofia Nilsdotter — Generation 7

> **Duplicate-person case.** `tools/audit_tree.py` flagged two Samuel Jacobsson records sharing name and birth year 1806: `I182382755763` and `I182195861248`. Investigation shows the duplication is not limited to Samuel himself — it runs through his whole nuclear family (wife, and at least one child), matching the already-resolved "John Albertson & Ann Pine" tree-duplication pattern (gen 6; see `docs/research/direct-line-issues.md`). This dossier resolves the pair and traces how far the duplication extends.

## Overview

Samuel Jacobsson was born 2 July 1806 and died 15 April 1854, both at Hjälmvik Nolgården farm, Stala parish, on the island of Orust, Göteborg och Bohus county (Bohuslän), Sweden. He married Sofia Nilsdotter (b. 16 Jan 1805, Haga, Stala; d. 26 Jan 1887, Hjälmvik Ng, Stala) on 13 May 1836, at the same farm. Their daughter Gustava Samuelsdotter (b. 11 May 1842, Hjälmvik Nolgården; d. 28 Jul 1912, North Easton, Bristol Co., Massachusetts) married Sven Johan Samuelsson and emigrated with the family to the Swedish colony around Easton, Massachusetts (Ames Shovel Works / Holy Trinity Lutheran Church milieu), where her grandson Simon Swanson's line is independently documented in two sibling dossiers already in this repo (`gen05-simon-swanson-anna-josephina-svenson.md`, `gen04-ellen-josephine-swanson.md`).

The tree contains this entire family group **twice**, evidently from two merged tree imports: one branch is unsourced ("inferred" confidence, no citations) but carries a few extra relatives (Samuel's parents, one grandchild's death date); the other branch is sourced to Ancestry Family Trees and carries the fuller vital-record detail (full marriage date/place, both of Sofia's parents, an extra daughter). Swedish parish-level primary sources for Samuel and Sofia themselves are gated (ArkivDigital/Riksarkivet, subscription/captcha) and could not be reached on the open web; no independent web source corroborates Samuel Jacobsson by name. The resolution here is therefore internal (tree cross-referencing), not confirmed against a Swedish primary record.

## Corrections to the tree

- **Merge `I182382755763` into `I182195861248`** (Samuel Jacobsson). The duplicate is unsourced and lacks the 13 May 1836 marriage record that the canonical copy carries; it should not be treated as a separate person.
- **Merge `I182382755760` into `I182195861230`** (Sofia Nilsdotter, spouse). Same reasoning — the duplicate lacks sourcing and lacks Sofia's father (Nils Olafsson) entirely.
- **Merge `I182292318217` into `I182195860594`** (Gustava Samuelsdotter, daughter). The duplicate is unsourced and lists only one child; the canonical copy additionally carries daughter Selma Swanson (`I182195860595`, b. 1872 Gustorp farm, Stala / d. 1927, Easton, Bristol Co., MA), who has no counterpart anywhere in the other branch and should not be lost in a naive merge.
- **New, previously unflagged duplicate found one generation further down:** Gustava's son **Simon Swanson** also exists twice — `I182197442974` (parented under the *duplicate* Gustava/Sven Johan pair, but itself carrying the better individual sourcing: Massachusetts vital & marriage records, the 1900 US census, Swedish-American Lutheran church records, and Geneanet, plus three sourced children) vs. `I182195860381` "Simon A Swanson" (parented under the *canonical* Gustava/Sven Johan pair, but sourced only generically to "Ancestry Family Trees," with one child). This is the reverse of the pattern seen further up the tree: at Samuel/Sofia/Gustava the "documented" Swedish-marriage-record branch is better sourced, but by the time the family reaches Massachusetts, sourcing quality flips to favor the *other* branch's copy of the individual. This pair is not yet in `direct-line-issues.md` — recommend adding it (see below) and, on resolution, re-pointing Simon's parents to the canonical Gustava/Sven Johan Samuelsson records while keeping `I182197442974` as Simon's own canonical record, folding in the 17 Mar 1921 death date that only `I182195860381` carries.

## New findings (not in the tree)

- **The duplication is a whole-family chain, not just Samuel:** Samuel Jacobsson → Sofia Nilsdotter → Sofia's parents Nils Olafsson/Greta Nilsdotter → Gustava Samuelsdotter → Sven Johan Samuelsson → (at least) Simon Swanson all exist as parallel pairs, each pair traceable to the same "sourced Ancestry-Family-Trees copy" vs. "unsourced copy" split, exactly mirroring the John Albertson & Ann Pine gen-6 case cited as the house-style precedent.
- **Asymmetric extra relatives on each side:** the *unsourced* Samuel Jacobsson branch is the only one carrying his parents, Jakob Kristoffersson (`I182391485866`, b. 1770, Stala/Holm/Tegneby; d. Aug 1838, Hjälmvik Nedergård) and Annika Torbjörnsdotter (`I182391485891`, b. 1771) — neither sourced. The *sourced* Gustava branch is the only one carrying daughter Selma Swanson. A clean merge needs to preserve both sets of unique relatives rather than discarding whichever copy is marked "duplicate."
- **Independent corroboration from a completely separate research pass:** this repo's `gen05-simon-swanson-anna-josephina-svenson.md` and `gen04-ellen-josephine-swanson.md` dossiers, produced from a different work item and citing a secondary compiled genealogy (yourroots.com, itself aggregating US census/vital/church records), independently arrived at "Sven Johan Samuelsson (1839–1877)" and "Gustafva Samuelsdotter (1842–1912)" as Simon Swanson's parents — matching this tree's dates for both individuals exactly, with no coordination between the two research passes. This substantially increases confidence that the underlying family reconstruction (Swedish-immigrant nuclear family, Samuel Jacobsson through Simon Swanson) reflects a real family, even though Samuel Jacobsson's own generation could not be independently corroborated on the open web.
- **Geographic coherence:** Hjälmvik Nolgården farm sits within Stala parish on Orust, Bohuslän — general web sources (Bohus Arkivguide's Stala dödlistor 1792–1858 transcription project, Wikipedia's Hjälmvik entry) confirm Hjälmvik as a real, small settlement in Stala socken, consistent with the tree's place data, though no entry for this specific Samuel Jacobsson (1806–1854) was found in the (partial, non-comprehensive) online death-list transcriptions checked.

## Open questions

- **No independent (non-Ancestry-tree) source was found for Samuel Jacobsson or Sofia Nilsdotter themselves.** Swedish church books for Stala parish (födelse-/vigsel-/dödböcker) covering 1806, 1836, and 1854 would be the definitive source and are held at Riksarkivet/ArkivDigital — both gated (subscription and/or CAPTCHA) and not reachable in this pass. This is flagged, not fabricated: the "documented" confidence tag on the canonical records reflects only that they cite a source (Ancestry Family Trees, a compiled/secondary aggregator), not a primary Swedish record.
- **Are Jakob Kristoffersson and Annika Torbjörnsdotter (Samuel's parents, only on the unsourced branch) real, or artifacts of whichever contributor built that side of the tree?** No source is attached to either. If genuine, they should be re-attached as Samuel's parents on the canonical record once merged.
- **Selma Swanson (Gustava's daughter, only on the sourced branch)** — is she a real, previously-uncaptured child who simply wasn't in the other contributor's tree, or could she be a duplicate of some other child recorded elsewhere in the tree under a different name? A targeted name search found no second "Selma Swanson" record in this dataset, so for now she is treated as a genuine unique child needing preservation in any merge.
- **Should the newly-found Simon Swanson duplicate (`I182197442974`/`I182195860381`) be formally added to `direct-line-issues.md`?** Recommended — see interpretation line below. Its resolution should also fold in the disagreement over Simon's death place (this repo's own `gen05-simon-swanson-anna-josephina-svenson.md` flags a further compiled-source conflict between "Easton" and "Hanson, MA" for that same death).
- **Greta Nilsdotter (Sofia's mother) and Sven Johan Samuelsson (Gustava's husband)** are each already tracked as their own separate gen-7/gen-8 duplicate-person cases in `direct-line-issues.md`. This dossier does not resolve those independently but confirms their duplication is mechanically linked to the same two-tree-import merge that produced the Samuel Jacobsson duplication — resolving one should use the same "sourced-Ancestry-copy is primary" rule found consistently at every generation from Samuel Jacobsson down to Sven Johan Samuelsson.
