# Howard Edwin Albertson Jr (1930–1998) & Elizabeth Jane Holst (1931–2014)

Generation 2 direct ancestors. Deep-dive dossier, researched 3 Jul 2026.

## Overview

Howard Edwin Albertson Jr was born 8 Feb 1930 in Trenton, Mercer County, New Jersey, son of Howard Albertson (Sr) and Bertha E. Hohmann; Elizabeth Jane Holst was born 10 May 1931 in Boston, daughter of Douglas Leon Holst and Abigail Elizabeth Morrow. They married in Trenton in October 1952, raised five children in Boston (Dorchester) and later Brockton, Massachusetts, and are buried side by side in Knollwood Memorial Park, Canton, Norfolk County, Massachusetts (Meditation section, lot 25D, graves 1 and 2). This research confirmed every core tree date directly against the cemetery's own burial database and against the 2021 obituary of their eldest son David, and newly identified Howard's maternal grandparents (Frank and Mary Ann Hohmann of Trenton) and his mother's siblings.

## Verified facts

- **Howard's birth 8 Feb 1930**: confirmed — Knollwood Memorial Park burial database shows birthDate 02/08/1930 (webCemeteries record for Howard Albertson); MyHeritage aggregation of the SSDI likewise gives b. 8 Feb 1930, d. 5 Jun 1998, age 68.
- **Howard's death 5 Jun 1998**: confirmed — Knollwood record deathDate 06/05/1998; SSDI (via MyHeritage names index) agrees; last residence Massachusetts.
- **Howard's burial at Canton, Norfolk Co., MA**: confirmed and made precise — Knollwood Memorial Park (Canton MA) official burial-search database: Howard Albertson, Meditation section, lot 25D, grave 1, service date **10 Jun 1998** (grave GPS 42.148616, -71.16424).
- **Elizabeth's birth 10 May 1931, Boston**: confirmed — Knollwood record birthDate 05/10/1931; tree cites Massachusetts Birth Index 1860–1970 (consistent).
- **Elizabeth's death 8 Jan 2014 at Brockton**: confirmed — Knollwood record deathDate 01/08/2014; Legacy.com death notice published in The Enterprise (Brockton) 10 Jan 2014, funeral by Dahlborg-MacNevin Funeral Home.
- **Elizabeth's burial 11 Jan 2014, Canton MA**: confirmed — Knollwood record serviceDate 01/11/2014, Meditation section, lot 25D, grave 2 (beside Howard).
- **Marriage of Howard and Elizabeth (tree: Oct 1952, Trenton NJ)**: consistent — their son David Howard Albertson's 2021 obituary names him "son of the late Howard and Elizabeth (Holst) Albertson," born in Boston 26 Sep 1953, i.e. within a year of the Oct 1952 marriage. (Marriage index entry itself is behind Ancestry paywall; not independently re-verified, but nothing found contradicting it.)
- **Parents of Howard Jr — Howard Albertson (Sr) and Bertha E. Hohmann**: supported — Find A Grave memorials for Mary Ann Hohmann (d. 29 Mar 1946) and her son John A. Hohmann (1885–1948) both list a surviving daughter/sister "Mrs. Howard Albertson" of the Trenton NJ Hohmann family, corroborating that a Hohmann daughter married a Howard Albertson in Trenton. AncientFaces/SSDI shows Bertha E. Hohmann, b. 8 May 1901, d. Jan 1995, of Trenton, Mercer Co., NJ.
- **Children (5)**: confirmed — David's 2021 obituary (Russell & Pica Funeral Home, Brockton) lists his siblings: Douglas Albertson (Hudson NH), BettyJayne Lyons (Webster MA), Janis Pennini (Bridgewater MA), Scott Albertson (Brockton MA). Matches the tree's David, Douglas, Janis L, Bette (Lyons), Scott.
- **Boston/Dorchester then Brockton residences**: consistent — David was born in Boston (1953) and graduated Boston Technical High School (1971); the family later appears in Brockton (tree Public Records Index entries 1992–1996; Elizabeth died in Brockton 2014; David described as "formerly of Brockton").
- **Howard's military entry (Korean-era, New Jersey)**: the tree's source is the U.S. Korean War Era Draft Cards, 1948–1959 — note this documents draft *registration* in New Jersey (he turned 18 in Feb 1948), not necessarily active service. No independent service record found.

## Corrections to the tree

- **"Douglas Leon Holst BIL" (Elizabeth's father)** — the trailing "BIL" is almost certainly a data-entry artifact (looks like a "brother-in-law" relationship tag pasted into the name field), not part of his name. Recommend correcting to "Douglas Leon Holst".
- **Residence place "Dorcehster, Massachusetts" (Howard)** — typo for **Dorchester** (the Boston neighborhood; matches Elizabeth's Dorchester entry and their 1964 Boston residence).
- **Father's name "Howard I Albertson"** — the middle initial "I" is suspect. The subject is styled "Jr," so his father should be Howard (Edwin) Albertson Sr; period sources found here (Hohmann family obituaries, 1946 and 1948) call him simply "Howard Albertson." The "I" may be a misread "E" or a generational "I". Not disproven, but treat the initial as unverified.
