## Overview

Gustaf Rånge (1838–1902) and Maria Kristina Johansdotter (1841–1929) were Swedish immigrants from the Hjo/Dimbo area of Skaraborg, Västergötland, who emigrated to the Swedish immigrant colony of Easton/Brockton, Massachusetts, in 1885 — arriving in New York on 29 May 1885 after departing Skaraborg on 11 May 1885 via Liverpool/Queenstown. They anglicized their surname to "Range" in America. Gustaf died in Easton in 1902; Maria Kristina outlived him by 27 years, dying in Brockton in 1929 and being buried in North Easton. Their daughter Anna Josephina (Svenson) Rånge, who married Simon Anders Svensson (Swanson), is independently documented in two sibling dossiers already in this repository, which corroborates this couple's existence, general dates, and Sweden origin, while also flagging the surname and several details as unverified. This pass could not locate independent Swedish parish records, a Find A Grave memorial, an obituary, or a pre-1900 U.S. census entry for either spouse; nearly everything below rests on the tree's own cited record collections (which could not be re-opened directly — see Open questions) plus historical/community context and cross-references from related dossiers already in this tree.

## Corrections to the tree

- **Maria Kristina's "departure: 1870 @ Skaraborg" almost certainly does NOT mean she emigrated in 1870.** The very next tree event is "residence: 1880 @ Hjo, Skaraborg, Sverige," i.e., she is independently documented still living in Sweden a decade later. Swedish parish clerks recorded *utflyttning* (moving out of a parish, e.g., from her birth parish to Hjo) using the same "departure" terminology genealogy software applies to emigration; this 1870 event is far more plausibly an internal, parish-to-parish move within Sweden, not a trip to America. Her actual, single emigration almost certainly coincided with her husband Gustaf's documented 1885 voyage (departure 11 May 1885, arrival NY 29 May 1885) — there is no evidence of two separate Atlantic crossings for her. **Recommended reading: "departure 1870 @ Skaraborg" = an internal Sweden move, not emigration; true emigration = 1885, alongside Gustaf.**
- **The apparent "three spouses" listed for Gustaf are almost certainly one person, recorded three ways**, not evidence of multiple marriages: "Maria Kristina Johansdotter" appears twice (duplicate) and "Maria C. Range" a third time. Since Gustaf predeceased Maria Kristina by 27 years (d. 1902 vs. 1929), he could not have remarried after her death, and no other candidate wife appears anywhere in the source list. "Maria C. Range" is simply Maria Kristina under her Americanized married surname. **This is a tree data-merge artifact, not three marriages.**
- **Duplicate children entries**: "Maria Wilhelmina" and "Gustaf Oskar" each appear twice in Gustaf's own children list (but only once each in Maria Kristina's), while "Carl W. Range" appears only in Gustaf's list and not in Maria Kristina's. These asymmetries point to unmerged duplicate person-records in the underlying tree database rather than real facts (e.g., a different number of children per parent). **Recommend a de-duplication pass on this family's children in the tree software** rather than treating the counts as meaningful.
- **This same duplicate-parent-name artifact was already caught independently one generation down**: the sibling dossier `gen05-simon-swanson-anna-josephina-svenson.md` flags that Anna Josephina's own tree record lists her mother's name twice ("Gustaf Rånge, Maria Kristina Johansdotter, Maria Kristina Johansdotter"). This is the same bug pattern seen here, now confirmed from both directions (parent-side and child-side records), strengthening the case that it is a systemic tree-import issue rather than a one-off.
- **The "Massachusetts, Marriage Records, 1840–1915" source cited for Gustaf is likely misattributed to this couple's own marriage.** Given Maria Kristina's residence in Sweden as late as 1880 and their daughter's 1870 Swedish birth, Gustaf and Maria Kristina almost certainly married in Sweden, not Massachusetts. This citation more plausibly belongs to one of their U.S.-born-or-married children (e.g., Carl W. Range's or Gustaf Oskar's own Massachusetts marriage), and appears to have been attached to the parent record by an automated hint/merge rather than reflecting a real marriage-in-Massachusetts event for Gustaf and Maria Kristina themselves. Flagged as unverified rather than corrected outright, since the underlying record was not re-opened this pass.
