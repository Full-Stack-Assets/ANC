# Angelo Prospus (Prospero) (c. 1859/1862–1945/46) & Vincenza Minochelli (1878–after 1950)

> Note on assignment: the task header named "John P Donahue & Ellen J. Swanson," but the item data file (A12.json) contains Angelo Prospus (Prospero) and Vincenza Minochelli, and this dossier path matches the Prospero couple. This dossier covers the couple in the data file.

## Overview

Angelo Prospero (Americanized progressively as Prospus / Prospor / Prosper) was born in or near Naples, Campania, Italy about 1859–1862, son of Antonio Di Prospero and Antonia Riccinta, emigrated to Boston in the 1870s–1880s, married twice there (Delia Lawrence, 1889; Vincenza Minochelli, 1902), and by 1910 had settled in Brockton, Plymouth County, Massachusetts, where the large family remained for the rest of their lives. Vincenza Minochelli, daughter of Tobia Minochelli and Filomena Dreana, was born in Italy in 1878, arrived around 1900–1901, and married Angelo in Boston on 8 January 1902; contrary to the tree's 1906 death date, she demonstrably lived in Brockton through at least the April 1950 census.

## Corrections to the tree

1. **Vincenza's death "1906 @ Boston" is almost certainly wrong.** The tree itself carries *sourced* residences for her in Brockton in 1910, 1920, 1922, 1927, 1930, 1940, and **1950** (1950 US census attached), plus twelve children whose births clearly span well past 1906. A "Massachusetts Death Index 1901–1980" entry for a Vincenza Prospero dying in Boston in 1906 has evidently been mis-attached — it may belong to an infant child of the couple or to an unrelated woman. **Correct value: died after April 1950**, place presumably Brockton; exact date not yet found.
2. **Angelo's death year: 1945 (tree) vs 1946 (Find A Grave memorial #273960702).** The gravestone/memorial says 1946. One of the two is off by a year; the Massachusetts death index or the Brockton Enterprise obituary would settle it. Treat 1945 as unconfirmed pending a primary record.
3. **Angelo's birth year: "abt 1862" (tree) vs 1859 (Find A Grave).** Census-age drift is normal for this cohort; the tree's three different arrival years (1870, 1880, 1881) show the same census-derived scatter. Not a firm correction, but the tree should carry the range c. 1859–1862 rather than a fixed 1862.
4. **Vincenza's birth event "30 1878" is malformed** — a day number without a month. The underlying record (probably the 1902 marriage return or the sacramental record) should be re-read to recover the full date.

## New findings (not in the tree)

- **Burial place for Angelo: Calvary Cemetery, Brockton** (Find A Grave #273960702). The tree records death in Brockton but no burial. Vincenza has no memorial findable under Prosper/Prospero/Minochelli variants — she is plausibly in the same Calvary plot, unphotographed/unindexed; a Calvary Cemetery office lookup (City of Brockton cemetery dept.) would likely locate her and give exact death date.
- **1950 census household anchors**: sons Dominic (ED 16-28) and John G. (ED 16-32) headed or appeared in Brockton households in April 1950; the tree's sourced 1950 residence for Vincenza means she was enumerated in one of these (or an adjacent) Prosper households — worth pulling both schedules' images from the NARA 1950 site.
- **Contextual note on origins**: the tree gives Naples as Angelo's birthplace, but the patronymic form "Di Prospero" is heavily concentrated in Abruzzo/Lazio/Campania hinterland rather than Naples city; "Naples" in US records often means the province or the port of embarkation. The exact commune remains open.

## Open questions

- Exact death date and burial location of Vincenza (after April 1950). Check Massachusetts death index 1951+, Brockton Enterprise obituaries, and Calvary Cemetery interment books.
- Angelo's exact death date (1945 vs 1946) — Massachusetts death index or Brockton death return.
- Whose household Vincenza was in for the 1950 census (Dominic's ED 16-28 vs John's ED 16-32 schedule images).
- Passenger arrivals: no manifest found for either spouse; Angelo's census-reported arrival years scatter 1870–1881, Vincenza's 1882–1901. Her marriage in Jan 1902 shortly after a c. 1901 arrival suggests the 1900/1901 figures are right for her and 1882 is a census error (or she made two trips).
- Naturalization: no petition/declaration found in free indexes for Angelo Prospero/Prosper; NARA Boston pre-1906 New England index and Plymouth County Superior Court records are the next stops.
- The identity of the 1906 Boston death-index entry attached to Vincenza — possibly an infant child of the couple who bore a family name.
- Italian commune of origin for both families (Di Prospero; Minochelli/Minichelli, father Tobia — note son "Toby J Prosper" named for him; Dreana is likely a mangling of Adriana/Andreana or of a surname like D'Reana/Di Riana).
