# John Oscar Holst (1858/59–1938) & Mina Droulet Stone (c.1869–1925)

Generation 4 direct ancestors. Researched 3 Jul 2026 from web sources; the standout find is a
descendant-compiled family site (kristinhall.org, by Kristin C. Hall, a great-great-granddaughter
via Esther Gladys Holst) that transcribes Massachusetts vital records, John's naturalization file,
and 1942 family recollections — effectively primary-source citations for most of John's life.

## Overview

John Oscar Holst was a Swedish immigrant machinist and locomotive repairman, born 14 August
1858 or 1859 in Göteborg (Gothenburg), Sweden, who arrived in Boston 21 June 1879, settled in the
Swedish community of East Boston, and spent ~50 years repairing locomotives at the New York, New
Haven & Hartford Railroad's Readville car shops. Widowed in 1898 when his first wife Augusta
Matilda Svensson died of empyema, he married Mina Droulet Stone that same year and had six more
children with her. Mina (b. abt 1869, North Adams, Mass., per the tree; parents Napoleon A. Stone
and Addie Martin) died in 1925, after which John married a third time, to a woman named Emma. He
died of prostate cancer on 16 December 1938 in Norfolk, Mass., and is buried in Prospect Hill
Cemetery, Millis.

## Verified facts

**John Oscar Holst**

- Born 14 Aug in Göteborg, Sweden — year given as **1858** by kristinhall.org (citing marriage and
  family records) and WikiTree, as **1859** by Find a Grave (memorial #68223024, "1859–1938") and
  FamilySearch (LYYK-W32, "1859–1938"). The death-record age transcribed by kristinhall.org
  ("79y:4m:2d" at death 16 Dec 1938) computes to a birth date of 14 Aug **1859**, matching the tree.
  [kristinhall.org JohnHolst.html; WikiTree Holst-616; findagrave 68223024; FamilySearch LYYK-W32]
- Parents: Andres (Anders) Holst and Sabina Olsdotter, both of Sweden — matches the tree (spelling
  "Olsdotter" rather than "Oldsdotter"). [kristinhall.org, citing 1938 death record and 1882
  marriage record]
- Emigrated from Sweden to Boston, **arriving 21 June 1879**. [kristinhall.org, citing John Holst
  Naturalization Record, U.S. Circuit Court, Boston, file 221/11A, Federal Archives, Waltham MA]
- Declaration of intention filed 21 May 1885; **naturalized 15 May 1891** (paperwork recorded
  19 Sep 1891), U.S. Circuit Court, Boston. [same naturalization file]
- Marriage #1: **Augusta Matilda Svensson** (daughter of John Svensson & Christina ——), 15 Nov 1882,
  Boston, by Rev. D. H. Taylor. Matches the tree's "Augusta M Holst m. 15 Nov 1882 @ Boston".
  [kristinhall.org, citing MA marriage record 1882-83 vol 336 p 204 no 3665]
- Augusta died **23 May 1898, Boston**, of empyema. [kristinhall.org citing MA death record 1898 vol
  483 p 23 no 4190; Dorchester Historical Society WWI biography of son George]
- Marriage #2: **Mina Droulet Stone**, 1898 (tree says 1 Sep 1898, Boston; the Dorchester Historical
  Society bio confirms "later that year [1898] John remarried, wedding Mina Droulet Stone" — the
  exact 1 Sep date is confirmed only by the tree). [Dorchester Historical Society blog p=3583;
  Dorchester Atheneum]
- Occupation: **machinist & locomotive repairman at the Readville Car Shop of the NY, NH & Hartford
  Railroad**, roughly 50 years; forced to retire in 1936 when the company learned his age.
  [kristinhall.org, citing death record, marriage record, and 1942 Ella Hall recollections;
  Dorchester bios: "John was a machinist"]
- Died **16 December 1938** (not 17 Dec — see Corrections), in Norfolk, Norfolk Co., Mass.; cause:
  carcinoma of prostate with metastases to lungs and throat (onset Jan 1938). Residence at death:
  Ruggles [St.], Franklin, Norfolk Co.; died on Main Street, Norfolk. [kristinhall.org, citing MA
  death record 1938, Norfolk, 59]
- Buried **18 December 1938, Prospect Hill Cemetery, Millis, Norfolk Co., Mass.** ("the
  Millis–Norfolk Big Cemetery") — confirms the tree's Millis burial. [kristinhall.org; findagrave
  memorial 68223024 exists for him]
- Children with Augusta (all confirmed): Albert (b. 1883), Victoria (b. 1885), George Andrew
  (b. 1 Mar 1888, at 85 Condor St., East Boston), Esther Gladys (b. 10 Mar 1895, Boston), Florence
  (b. 1897). [Dorchester Historical Society bio; kristinhall.org with MA birth-record citations]
- Children with Mina (all six confirmed): **Grace (b. 1900), Olga (b. 1901), Alice (b. 1904),
  Lorimer (b. 1906), Douglas (b. 1908), John Oscar Jr. (b. 1910)** — matches the tree's list.
  Lorimer Addison Holst b. 29 Jan 1906, Boston (WikiTree Holst-615). [Dorchester Historical Society
  bio; WikiTree]
- Residences (household of John Oscar Holst): 8 Harmony Place, East Boston (1891, naturalization);
  1 Union Place, East Boston (1895); 98 Loudon St., East Boston (1898); Dedham (1900 census, per
  Dorchester bio); 19 Ericsson St., Port Norfolk section of Dorchester (1910 census); 12 Huntoon
  St., Dorchester (1918 — "home of her parents Mr. & Mrs. John Oscar Holst" at Esther's wedding,
  i.e. John and Mina's home); later Millis / Franklin, Norfolk Co. [kristinhall.org;
  Dorchester Historical Society bio]
- Surname variants encountered in records: **Holst / Holz / Holtz** (Soundex also under Holt).
  [kristinhall.org]

**Mina Droulet Stone**

- Confirmed as John's second wife, married 1898, mother of the six youngest children — the
  independent Dorchester Historical Society / Dorchester Atheneum biographies (drawn from vital
  records and censuses for the WWI service-member project) name her exactly as "Mina Droulet
  Stone." [Dorchester Historical Society blog p=3583; dorchesteratheneum.org]
- She appears as "Mrs. John Oscar Holst" at 12 Huntoon St., Dorchester, at stepdaughter Esther's
  31 Dec 1918 wedding. [kristinhall.org EstherHolst.html, citing 1918 marriage record]
- Notably, the descendant line that produced kristinhall.org knew her only as "Mina [surname not
  known]" — the tree's fuller identification (Stone, North Adams birth, parents Napoleon A. Stone
  & Addie Martin, death 1925 Boston) goes beyond what that site knew, but I could not verify any
  of it in open web sources (see Open questions).

## Corrections to the tree

- **Death date 17 Dec 1938 → 16 Dec 1938.** The Massachusetts death record (1938, Norfolk, vol/no
  59), as transcribed on kristinhall.org, and WikiTree both give 16 December 1938; burial followed
  on 18 December. The tree's 17 Dec is off by one day.
- **Mother "Sabina Oldsdotter" → Sabina Olsdotter** (patronymic from Ols/Olof; kristinhall.org,
  citing the death and marriage records, consistently spells Olsdotter; also rendered "Saben"/
  "Sibina" in some notes). Father appears as both Anders and Andres.
- **Birthplace spelling**: "Gottenburg" → Gothenburg / Göteborg, Sweden. (Also "Massachisetts" typo
  in Mina's birthplace.)
- **Birth year 1859 — flag, not a firm correction**: kristinhall.org and WikiTree give 14 Aug 1858;
  Find a Grave and FamilySearch give 1859; the death-record age arithmetic (79y 4m 2d) supports
  14 Aug 1859. The naturalization note ("naturalized 15 May 1891, æ 32") slightly favors 1858.
  Recommend recording "14 Aug 1858/1859" until the death or birth record image is checked.

## New findings (not in the tree)

- **Immigration and naturalization**: arrived Boston 21 June 1879; declaration of intention
  21 May 1885; naturalized 15 May 1891 (recorded 19 Sep 1891), U.S. Circuit Court, Boston.
  Naturalization file: 221/11A, Federal Archives, Waltham, MA.
- **Occupation**: machinist / locomotive repairman at the NYNH&H Railroad's Readville car shops for
  ~50 years. Family story: forced to retire in 1936 when the railroad discovered his age; he was
  "madder than hell" and did chin-ups on the door frames to prove his fitness. (Family
  recollection, 1942 — treat as anecdote/legend.)
- **Cause of death**: prostate carcinoma with metastases (onset January 1938); died on Main Street,
  Norfolk while residing in Franklin, Mass.; buried 18 Dec 1938, Prospect Hill Cemetery, Millis.
- **A third marriage**: after Mina's death, John married a third wife, **Emma [surname unknown]** —
  she appears on/via his 1938 death record per kristinhall.org. Not in the tree at all.
- **Brother**: **Axel Holst** (b. abt 1867, Sweden), arrived Boston 22 Sep 1887 aboard the
  **SS Scythia** as a laborer; became a fireman in East Boston; lived 5 Union Place (next door to
  John at 1 Union Place); married Bertha ——, children Olaf Henry, Henry, George, Mabel, Lillian.
  (Caution: an "Olaf Henry Holst" Boston birth record of 2 Jan 1895 is cited on kristinhall.org —
  same register page as Esther's — and belongs to Axel's family, not John's.)
- **Paternal legend**: family lore says Andres Holst migrated to Hull, England, where he was a
  "bonesetter or surgeon" — legend only, no record support.
- **First wife's family**: Augusta Matilda Svensson was the daughter of John Svensson and
  Christina (surname unknown); she died of empyema, 23 May 1898, Boston.
- **Family anecdotes** (1942 recollections; legend tier): after Augusta's death John "took to
  drink"; he was a big, tall man who sent his children to work straight after grammar school.
- **Fates of the children** (from kristinhall.org and the Dorchester bios; useful leads):
  George Andrew (1888–1977) — machinist, WWI Navy Mechanic 1st Class at the U.S. Experimental
  Station, New London CT; lived on Main St., Millis by 1930; d. Cambridge 14 Mar 1977; Charles
  River Masonic Lodge; buried with wife Marion Edith DeLappe (1893–1941) — findagrave memorials
  215892126 / 215892363. Esther Gladys (1895–1969) m. LaForrest Leroy Hall 1918, chocolate wrapper
  at Walter Baker Chocolate, buried Blue Hill Cemetery, Braintree. Victoria (d. 15 Feb 1971,
  Quincy) and Albert (murdered — shot by his wife Louise, per family notes) are buried in Cedar
  Grove Cemetery, Dorchester. Florence (d. 1 Nov 1964, Quincy). Of Mina's children: Grace was
  living in Florida around 2000 at ~age 100; Olga married William Pitt (children Marjorie, Edith,
  Billie); Alice married a Lacroix; Lorimer was a machinist (Mason Regulator Co.), married Annie,
  and named a daughter **Mina** — after his mother; Doug married Abbie; John Jr. was a social
  worker in Naples, Florida.

## Open questions

- **Birth year 1858 vs 1859** — order the Göteborg (Gothenburg) parish birth/baptism record
  (Swedish church books, 14 Aug 1858/59) or the Boston 1938 death record image to settle it.
  A Gothenburg emigration register entry (~June 1879 departure) would also give his parish.
- **Everything about Mina before 1898 is unverified.** Tree: b. abt 1869 North Adams, parents
  Napoleon A. Stone & Addie Martin, d. 1925 Boston. No trace found in open sources: no findagrave
  memorial, no FamilySearch tree page surfaced, and no "Napoleon Stone" appears in the 1872 Adams
  or 1879 North Adams directories on Archive.org. Given the North Adams French-Canadian community
  and "Droulet" (= Drolet), "Stone" may be an anglicization (e.g. Lapierre → Stone) — worth
  checking the 1870/1880 census for North Adams and Quebec parish registers. Her 1925 Boston death
  record (Massachusetts vital records are open through 1925) would give parents and exact birth.
- **Marriage record, John & Mina, 1 Sep 1898, Boston** — the year is confirmed; pull the Boston
  1898 marriage register to confirm the exact date and to get Mina's stated age, birthplace, and
  parents (this single record would resolve most of the Mina questions).
- **Where is Mina buried?** No memorial found; possibly Prospect Hill Cemetery, Millis, with John,
  or a Boston cemetery. John's findagrave memorial (68223024) may have family links — it was not
  readable (Cloudflare-blocked); check manually.
- **Third wife Emma** — surname, marriage date/place (likely 1925–1938, Norfolk Co. or Boston),
  and whether she survived John.
- **Census households**: 1880 (John, newly arrived, East Boston?), 1900 (Dedham), 1910
  (19 Ericsson St., Dorchester), 1920 (Dorchester — 12 Huntoon St.?), 1930 (Millis/Franklin, with
  Emma?) — all exist behind subscription walls; the Dorchester bio confirms the 1900/1910
  households indirectly. Pulling these would also verify Mina's reported birthplace and her
  parents' birthplaces (expect "Canada French" if the Drolet theory is right).
- Grace Lillian Holst (b. 1900) was reportedly alive ~2000 in Florida; if she has no death date in
  the tree, treat with care, though she would now be 126.
