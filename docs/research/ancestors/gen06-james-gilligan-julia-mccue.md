## Overview

James Gilligan (b. abt 1826, Ireland; d. 22 Sep 1881, Weymouth, Norfolk
County, Massachusetts) and Julia McCue (b. 6 Jun 1826, Ireland; d. 6 Jul
1892, Weymouth, Massachusetts) were an Irish immigrant couple who married in
Massachusetts on 6 Apr 1850 and raised at least six children in Weymouth, a
South Shore town whose iron industry gave way to boot-and-shoe manufacturing
in this period and which — like much of the "Weymouth to Plymouth" corridor —
drew heavy Irish immigrant settlement from the Famine era onward. Despite an
extensive search, no freely accessible primary record (census, vital record
image, findagrave memorial, obituary, or city directory listing) could be
independently located for this specific couple or their children this pass;
the underlying records almost certainly sit inside the login-gated
Ancestry/FamilySearch/Find a Grave collections the tree itself already cites.
This is a genuinely thin result beyond the tree's existing citations, offered
honestly rather than padded with unverified inference.
