# The Bunker Line — Lineage Survey (Nantucket, Massachusetts, c.1620–1759)

## Overview

This is a lineage survey of the "Bunker" surname segment of the tree, which in the supplied
data file spans only generations 10–12 (Judith Mary Bunker → Susannah Bunker → William
Bunker/Mary Bunker). The line is part of the classic, heavily-intermarried Nantucket founding
stock (Bunker–Coffin–Macy). The immigrant ancestor is **George Bunker (1620–1658)**, who
brought his family from Tingrith/Maulden, Bedfordshire, England to Massachusetts around
1650–53, settling first at Ipswich, then Topsfield, where he drowned; his widow and children
then removed to Nantucket. Web research using a period-referenced compiled genealogy (the
"Bunker Family" chapter of the Putman/Canaday family history, cross-checked against
WikiTree/FamilySearch/Ancestry summaries) turned up a documented, dated chain from George
Bunker down through Peleg Bunker and Judith Bunker that **corrects two identity errors** in
the tree: a mother/daughter surname swap at generation 11, and a spouse mislabeled by surname
at generation 12. The documented paper trail (dated marriages, wills, vital records as compiled
in secondary sources) runs cleanly from generation 12 down to generation 9 (Rebecca Coffin,
not in this file); it also extends, in outline only and via an uncited family compiler, several
generations further back into 15th–16th century Bedfordshire, England — but that deeper reach
should be treated as unverified lore until parish-register sources are found.

## Verified facts

- **William Bunker** (tree gen. 12) was born 17 June 1648 in Tingrith, Bedfordshire, England,
  and died in Nantucket, Massachusetts (year 1712; see conflict note below). He was the son of
  George Bunker and Jane Godfrey, came to Massachusetts as a child, and after his father's death
  moved with his mother and stepfather Richard Swain to Nantucket. — *Sources: "THE BUNKER
  FAMILY" (Putman/Canaday family history, billputman.com); WikiTree Bunker-94; FamilySearch
  ancestors profile LCRJ-C1H; Find a Grave memorial #10904515 (Founders Burial Ground,
  Nantucket).*
- William Bunker married **Mary Macy** on 11 April 1669 in Nantucket. Mary Macy was born 4
  December 1648 in Salisbury, Massachusetts, daughter of **Thomas Macy** (early Nantucket
  settler) and Sarah Hopcott; she died c.1729. — *Sources: Putman "Bunker Family" history;
  Find a Grave memorial #64024346 (Mary Macy Bunker, 1648–1729).* This marriage/spouse is
  **absent from the tree data** entirely.
- William Bunker and Mary Macy had seven children on Nantucket, including **Peleg Bunker**
  (b. 1 Dec 1676) — the tree's generation-11 ancestor, there mislabeled "Peleg Coffin" (see
  Corrections below). — *Source: Putman "Bunker Family" history.*
- **Mary Bunker** (tree gen. 12) was born 5 May 1652 in **Topsfield, Massachusetts** (not
  Newburyport as the tree states — see Corrections), daughter of George Bunker and Jane
  Godfrey, i.e. William Bunker's own sister. She married **Stephen Coffin** on 8 October 1668
  in Nantucket. Stephen Coffin (11 May 1652 – 14 Nov 1734) was the son of **Tristram Coffin**
  and Dionis Stevens. Mary Bunker died 18/19 May 1724 in Nantucket. — *Sources: Putman "Bunker
  Family" history; WikiTree Bunker-5 ("Mary (Bunker) Coffin, 1652-1724"); FamilySearch
  ancestors profile LZD5-RJG; Find a Grave #171369666 (Stephen Coffin Sr., 1652–1734).*
- Stephen Coffin and Mary Bunker's children included **Susanna (Coffin) Bunker**, born c.1680
  on Nantucket — the tree's generation-11 ancestor, there mislabeled "Susannah Bunker" as if
  Bunker were her own birth surname (see Corrections). — *Sources: Putman "Bunker Family"
  history; NHA Eliza Starbuck Barney Genealogical Record, Family Group F400 (Stephen Coffin /
  Mary Bunker).*
- **Peleg Bunker** (b. 1 Dec 1676, Nantucket) married **Susanna Coffin** on 9 January 1700 on
  Nantucket. He died 1 June 1730; she died 11 June 1740. — *Source: Putman "Bunker Family"
  history.*
- Peleg Bunker and Susanna Coffin's first child was **Judith Bunker**, born **21 September
  1701** — matching the tree's generation-10 person's birth date exactly (tree calls her
  "Judith Mary Bunker"). She married **Bartlett Coffin** on 1 January 1718/19, son of Peter
  Coffin and his first wife Christian Conde. — *Source: Putman "Bunker Family" history
  (matches the tree's own recorded birth date of 21 Sep 1701 and spouse name Bartlett
  Coffin).*
- Judith Bunker and Bartlett Coffin's seven children included **Rebecca Coffin** — confirming
  the tree's listed child for this couple. — *Source: Putman "Bunker Family" history.*
- The immigrant ancestor of this surname line, **George Bunker**, was born 1620 in England,
  married Jane Godfrey (daughter of Richard Godfrey and Jane Turner of Bedfordshire) 5 May 1644
  at Maulden, Bedfordshire, sailed to Massachusetts in the early 1650s, settled at Ipswich then
  Topsfield, and drowned there 16/26 May 1658 crossing a flooded stream with a wagon (his son
  William, a child at the time, was present and survived). His widow Jane remarried Richard
  Swain of Rowley later in 1658, and the combined family removed to Nantucket; Jane's death on
  31 October 1662 is recorded as the first death on Nantucket Island. — *Source: Putman
  "Bunker Family" history, corroborated in outline by WikiTree Bunker-44 (George Bunker,
  bef.1621–abt.1658) and Find a Grave #86386943.*
- Stephen Coffin's father, **Tristram Coffin** (1609/10–1681), baptized at Brixton parish near
  Plymouth, Devon, England, immigrated to Massachusetts in 1642 and later became one of the
  original proprietors/purchasers of Nantucket (1659) and its first chief magistrate (1671).
  He is the immigrant ancestor on the intermarrying Coffin side of this cluster (not the Bunker
  line itself, but directly relevant since two Bunker-line spouses in a row are Coffins). —
  *Sources: Wikipedia, "Tristram Coffin (settler)"; Find a Grave #10904410; digitized book
  "The Coffin Family..." by Louis Coffin, archive.org.*

## Corrections to the tree

- **"Susannah Bunker" (gen. 11, id I182382755270) → should be Susanna Coffin.** The tree
  lists her parents correctly as Stephen Coffin and Mary Bunker, but Stephen Coffin's own
  daughter takes his surname — she was born **Susanna Coffin**, not "Bunker." "Bunker" was her
  mother's maiden name, apparently misapplied to the daughter — most likely a spreadsheet/
  import artifact where the mother's surname bled into the child's name field. *Evidence:
  Putman "Bunker Family" history ("Susanna Coffin... a daughter of Stephen Coffin and Mary
  Bunker"); NHA Barney genealogy Family Group F400 lists a Coffin daughter "Susanna (Coffin)
  Bunker" among Stephen & Mary's children — i.e., "Bunker" is her *married* name (from husband
  Peleg Bunker), not her birth name.
- **Her spouse "Peleg Coffin" → should be Peleg Bunker.** The tree records Susanna's husband
  (and Judith's father) as "Peleg Coffin," but the documented husband is **Peleg Bunker**
  (b. 1 Dec 1676, son of William Bunker and Mary Macy — see next item). This appears to be the
  mirror image of the same swap: the couple is Susanna *Coffin* + Peleg *Bunker*, and the tree
  has effectively swapped their surnames with each other. *Evidence:* Putman "Bunker Family"
  history states plainly, "On January 9, 1700 he [Peleg Bunker] married Susanna Coffin on
  Nantucket. Susanna was a daughter of Stephen Coffin and Mary Bunker."
- **William Bunker's listed child "Peleg Coffin" (gen. 12, id I182528279217) → should be Peleg
  Bunker.** Consistent with the above, William Bunker's son was Peleg *Bunker*, not a person
  named "Peleg Coffin." No independently attested "Peleg Coffin" of the right generation
  (b. c.1676, Nantucket) was found in web sources; there was a later, unrelated Nantucket-born
  Peleg Coffin (18th-century Continental Congress-era figure), and it is plausible the tree's
  error stems from confusing that unrelated same-named Coffin, or from an automated
  spouse-name-as-child import bug, given the identical pattern of confusion seen with Susanna
  above.
- **Mary Bunker's birthplace: "Newburyport, Essex, Massachusetts" → should be Topsfield,
  Massachusetts.** Every secondary source found (Putman family history, WikiTree Bunker-5,
  FamilySearch LZD5-RJG) places her birth (5 May 1652, per the Putman source; some indexes
  give 11 May) at **Topsfield**, matching the documented settlement history of her parents
  George Bunker and Jane Godfrey (Ipswich, then Topsfield, in the early 1650s). No source found
  places any Bunker family member at Newburyport in this generation.
- **Mary Bunker's parents: listed as none in the tree → should be George Bunker and Jane
  Godfrey.** Multiple sources agree. This also makes Mary Bunker (gen. 12) the sister — not
  merely a same-surname contemporary — of William Bunker (gen. 12) in the same tree file, since
  both are children of George Bunker and Jane Godfrey.
- **William Bunker's parents: listed as none in the tree → should be George Bunker and Jane
  Godfrey**, per the same sources.

## New findings

- William Bunker's wife, **Mary Macy**, and her Macy ancestry (daughter of Thomas Macy, one of
  Nantucket's most storied early proprietors, subject of Whittier's poem "The Exiles") is
  entirely missing from the tree — a whole spousal line not currently captured.
- Dated marriages not present in the tree: Mary Bunker × Stephen Coffin, 8 Oct 1668; William
  Bunker × Mary Macy, 11 Apr 1669; Peleg Bunker × Susanna Coffin, 9 Jan 1700; Judith Bunker ×
  Bartlett Coffin, 1 Jan 1718/19.
- Susanna Coffin's death date, 11 June 1740 — the tree records no death year for her.
- Bartlett Coffin's parents: Peter Coffin and (his first wife) Christian Conde.
- The immigrant generation's fuller story: George Bunker's 1658 drowning death at Topsfield,
  his widow Jane Godfrey's remarriage to Richard Swain and removal to Nantucket, and her 1662
  death being the first recorded death on Nantucket Island.
- A documented (though loosely cited) English pedigree reaching back roughly five more
  generations beyond George Bunker, all sited at Tingrith, Bedfordshire: Timothy Bunker
  (1578–1657) → Oliver Bunker (1535–1616) → William Bunker (1497–1558) → Roger Bunker
  (b. c.1470, d.1515). This is **compiled/secondary** (drawn from a rootsweb "Bunker Family
  Association" page cited by the Putman compiler, without parish-register citations of its
  own) and is not independently verified here — flagged as unconfirmed rather than as a
  correction, since the tree file does not currently extend that far back at all.
- Context: Stephen Coffin's father Tristram Coffin (1609/10–1681) was one of the nine original
  European proprietors of Nantucket and its first chief magistrate — useful context for why
  this Bunker line is so thoroughly interwoven with the Coffin surname (two consecutive
  generations of Bunker-line ancestors married Coffins, in a pattern typical of small-island
  colonial communities).

## Open questions

- **William Bunker's death date is inconsistent across sources**: the tree says 26 Jul 1712;
  the Putman compiled genealogy says 6 June 1712; a WikiTree/search summary says 26 June 1712.
  None of these could be confirmed against a primary probate or town record in this session.
  Needs a direct look at Nantucket town/probate records or the Find a Grave memorial detail
  page (blocked by a 403 during this research) to resolve.
- Is "Mary" a documented middle name for Judith Bunker (tree's "Judith **Mary** Bunker")? Not
  found in any source consulted; may be a later embellishment, confusion with another Judith,
  or simply undocumented.
- The pre-1620 English generations (Roger → William → Oliver → Timothy Bunker, all of
  Tingrith, Bedfordshire) rest on a single uncited family compiler; parish-register
  confirmation was not attempted in this session and should be sought (Bedfordshire Archives /
  FamilySearch parish register images) before treating them as documented.
- The mechanism behind the Susanna/Peleg surname swap (spreadsheet import bug vs. confusion
  with the unrelated later "Peleg Coffin") could not be determined; flagged only as a probable
  artifact.

## Duplicate/data-quality artifacts flagged

- The tree's own record for Mary Bunker (I182382755326) contains **six event lines for what
  should be two events** (one birth, one death), each repeated with cosmetically different
  place-string formatting (e.g., "Nantucket, Nantucket, Massachusetts, USA" vs. "Nantucket,
  Nantucket County, Massachusetts, United States of America" vs. "Nantucket,Nantucket,
  Massachusetts,USA") and one birth date off by six days (11 May vs. 5 May 1652). This is a
  textbook unmerged-duplicate-record artifact from a GEDCOM/database merge, not evidence of
  two different events.
- The Susanna/Peleg surname-swap error (above) has the hallmarks of an automated or careless
  merge as well — a spouse's surname bleeding into a person's own name field twice in
  consecutive generations of the same small family cluster.
