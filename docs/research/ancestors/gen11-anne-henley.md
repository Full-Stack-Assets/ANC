# Anne Henley (1672–1750) — Generation 11

## Overview

The tree records Anne Henley as born 1672 in Sudbury, Suffolk, England, married to John William Whitlock (b. Dec 1666, Paulersbury/Paulerspury, Northamptonshire; d. 17 Feb 1739, Silverstone, Northamptonshire), and dead in 1750 in Silverstone, Northamptonshire — a wholly English life. The audit tool flagged her as *parent-too-young*: age 10 at the 1682 birth of her one listed child, Elizabeth Whitlock ("7th GGM," `I182541959080`). A 10-year-old mother is biologically impossible, so something in this record set is wrong.

The real explanation turns out **not** to be a birth-year typo on Anne's part. It is a mis-attached child: the Elizabeth Whitlock who continues this tree's direct line married William Paullin, a Quaker of Bucks County, Pennsylvania, and lived out her life in colonial Pennsylvania/New Jersey — she has no documented connection whatsoever to this English Whitlock/Henley couple. Every fact about Anne Henley and John William Whitlock in this tree is unsourced (no citations anywhere in the branch), and the child link appears to be a surname-only hint-match of the kind already documented elsewhere in this repo (Nancy Davis gen 7, Eliza Bolton Taber gen 10).

## Verified facts

- **Elizabeth Whitlock, wife of William Paullin, is a real, independently documented person — but an American one.** WikiTree profile Whitlock-104, "Elizabeth (Whitlock) Paullin" (https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Whitlock-104), records her as born **c. 1695 in Bucks County, Pennsylvania**, parents unknown, died **1750** (place not stated), wife of William Paullin. The citation given is Elmer Garfield Van Name, *The Paullin Family of Southern New Jersey* (Haddonfield, N.J., 1958; digitized in an Ancestry Family Trees database). [documented — secondary/compiled, book not independently viewed]
- **Her husband William Paullin matches this tree's record exactly.** WikiTree profile Paullin-17 (https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Paullin-17) gives William Paullin's birth as "about 1682 in Bucks County, Province of Pennsylvania" and death as "about 12 Nov 1757 ... Pilesgrove, Salem, Province of New Jersey" — matching this tree's `I182541959101` (William PAULLIN, b. 1682 Bucks County PA, d. 12 Nov 1757 Pilesgrove, Salem, NJ) on both dates and both places. WikiTree lists him as son of Henry Paullin and Sarah Sanford, "probably married twice: Eve Elwell and Elizabeth Whitlock," and names his children — including **Henry Paullin**, which matches this tree's `I182541958181` (Henry Paullin, b. 2 May 1715, Pilesgrove Twp., Salem, NJ). [documented]
- **The Paullin family's Bucks County/Quaker origin is independently attested.** General search results describe "Henry Paullin, a Quaker, [who] immigrated from England to Bucks County, Pennsylvania about 1681, and later moved to Salem County, New Jersey" — matching William Paullin's father Henry (WikiTree Pawlin-7/Paullin-17: Henry Pawlin Jr., b. abt 1650 Padbury, Buckinghamshire, England; arrived Pennsylvania 1682; d. 1723 Salem, NJ). [documented, secondary]
- **A "Whitlock" given name recurs among William and Elizabeth's descendants** — a son "Whitlock Paullin" (b. 6 Jun 1722, Downs Twp., Salem Co., NJ) and later generations continuing the name "Whitlock Paullin" into the 1750s/1825 — the ordinary practice of honoring a mother's maiden surname as a child's given name, which independently corroborates that the family itself believed "Whitlock" was Elizabeth's real maiden name, whatever her actual parentage. [inferred, from WikiTree Paullin genealogy listing]
- **The English Whitlock/Henley chain in this tree carries no source citations at any generation.** Anne Henley, her father Adam Henley (`I182541964369`), grandparents Alexander Henly/Margaret Edwards (`I182541966026`/`I182541966032`), husband John William Whitlock (`I182541959142`), his parents Anthony Whitlock/Bridget Boyson (`I182541964356`/`I182541964361`) — every single vital event across this whole branch has an empty `sources` array and `"confidence": "inferred"`. This is consistent with an unsourced compiled-tree submission rather than parish-register research. [structural observation, not itself a genealogical claim]

## Corrections to the tree

- **The parent-child link between Anne Henley/John William Whitlock and Elizabeth Whitlock (`I182541959080`) is the error**, not Anne's own 1672 birth year. Elizabeth Whitlock who married William Paullin was born in colonial Pennsylvania (c. 1695 per WikiTree, or this tree's own 1682 — either way, in America), to unknown parents. No emigration record, no marriage abroad, and no other documentary link connects her to the English Whitlock/Henley couple 3,000 miles away in Northamptonshire/Suffolk. `manual.notes` added to all three central records (Anne Henley, John William Whitlock, Elizabeth Whitlock) rather than repointing the machine-owned `relationships` arrays, per this repo's convention.
- **Elizabeth Whitlock's recorded birth year (1682) likely does not match the best external source (c. 1695, per WikiTree/Van Name 1958)**, though her recorded death year (1750) does match. Not corrected in the record itself — no primary source was viewed, only a secondary WikiTree citation of an unexamined 1958 book — but flagged in `manual.notes` as an open discrepancy.

## New findings (not in the tree)

- **Elizabeth Whitlock's real husband, children, and dates are far better attested on the American side than on the English side of this tree.** William Paullin (`I182541959101`), her husband, and their children William, Henry, Whitlock, Elizabeth, Joseph, Esther, David, Grace, Jacob, and Sarah Paullin are named on WikiTree Paullin-17 — a fuller list than this tree carries for that couple.
- **William Paullin's first wife may have been Eve Elwell**, per WikiTree, with Elizabeth Whitlock as a probable second marriage — worth noting if the Paullin-side records in this tree are ever revisited, since it could affect which children belong to which mother.
- **The task brief noted a possible duplicate-person case for "John William Whitlock" already tracked in this repo's backlog — this was checked and does not exist.** A full search of `docs/research/deep_dive_backlog.md` and `docs/research/direct-line-issues.md` turns up only four Whitlock-surnamed entries in the whole repo: Anne Henley's own flag, and three unflagged "needs baseline verification" stubs (Elizabeth Whitlock, Anthony Whitlock, Thomas Whitlock). None describe a duplicate-person finding for John William Whitlock (`I182541959142`), and no second Whitlock record sharing his name/birth year exists in `data/people/`. This lead did not pan out; reported honestly rather than fabricating a resolution for it.

## Open questions

- **Elizabeth Whitlock's true parents remain unknown.** WikiTree's own Whitlock-104 profile leaves her parents blank ("[father unknown]"/"[mother unknown]") and notes only "Mention of Elizabeth Whitlock/Witlock and a William Whitlock probably her relative but not stated how" — i.e., even the dedicated Paullin-family researchers who cite a 1958 published genealogy could not identify her parents. This tree's English Whitlock/Henley attachment should be treated as refuted rather than replaced with a new guess.
- **Anne Henley's own life (b. 1672 Sudbury, Suffolk; d. 1750 Silverstone, Northamptonshire) could not be independently confirmed or refuted.** No Suffolk or Northamptonshire parish-register hit, no WikiTree/FamilySearch/Geni profile, and no published Whitlock or Henley family genealogy naming her was found on the open web. Given the unsourced state of this whole branch, whether Anne Henley existed as described, married a John William Whitlock, or had any children at all, is unverified. If she is real, her real children (if any) are unknown from open sources.
- **Adam Henley (Anne's father, `I182541964369`) and the Alexander Henly/Margaret Edwards generation are likewise entirely unsourced** and could not be checked against Wiltshire parish records (Brinkworth, Warminster), which are gated (Ancestry, FindMyPast, Wiltshire and Swindon History Centre).
- **Van Name's 1958 book, "The Paullin Family of Southern New Jersey," was not viewed directly** — only WikiTree's citation of it. A library/archive copy (it is unlikely to be digitized on the open web) could resolve Elizabeth Whitlock's exact birth year and confirm or deny any stated parentage.
