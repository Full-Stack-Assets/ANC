# The Whitney Line — Surname-Line Survey (Watertown/Dedham, Massachusetts, 17th–18th c.)

## Overview

The tree's Whitney data for this line is thin — only two generations are actually
present in the working file (generation 11: Eleazer Whitney, b. 1662; generation 10:
his daughter Dorothy Whitney, b. 1700, who married into the Pond family of
Dedham/Roxbury). Both individuals, however, are readily identifiable in Frederick
Clifton Pierce's 1878 published genealogy *Whitney: The Descendants of John Whitney,
Who Came from London, England, to Watertown, Massachusetts, in 1635* (Archive.org),
which traces this family back to the immigrant ancestor, **John Whitney (bap. 1592,
London – d. 1673, Watertown)**. Cross-referencing that book against Edward Doubleday
Harris's 1873 *Genealogical Record of Daniel Pond and His Descendants* (Archive.org)
independently corroborates Dorothy Whitney's marriage into the Pond family and her
1786 Dedham death date almost to the day. The tree's stated parentage for Eleazer
Whitney (father "Richard Whetny") is contradicted by both a contemporary 1687 marriage
record and Pierce's compiled genealogy, which agree his father was **Thomas Whitney**,
Richard's brother — a sibling mix-up, not a sourced fact. The documented paper trail
for this line, as given in the tree, effectively ends at Dorothy Whitney's marriage;
nothing past her generation was in the working data to research further forward, and
nothing was found tracing this specific nuclear family further than Thomas Whitney's
own parentage (John Whitney the immigrant) going back.

## Verified facts

- **John Whitney**, the immigrant ancestor of this surname line, was baptized 20 July
  1592 at St. Margaret's Church, London, immigrated with wife Elinor and sons to
  Watertown, Massachusetts, arriving ca. June 1635, and was admitted a freeman there 3
  March 1636. Elinor Whitney died in Watertown 11 May 1659 aged 54; John Whitney died
  1673. — *Whitney: The Descendants of John Whitney* (F.C. Pierce, 1878), Archive.org
  full text, pp. surrounding the family narrative (lines ~3525–4062 of the OCR text).
- **Thomas Whitney** (son of John the immigrant), b. England 1629, admitted freeman
  18 Apr 1690, married in Watertown 11 Jan 1654 **Mary Kedall/Kettle** (spelled both
  ways in the source), died 20 Sept 1719, resided Watertown and Stow, Mass. — Pierce,
  entry "6. Thomas Whitney (John)."
- **Eleazer Whitney**, son of Thomas Whitney and Mary Kedall/Kettle, was born in
  Watertown — Pierce's compiled genealogical entry gives **7 April 1662**; a separate
  vital-records-style listing in the same book gives 2 Sept 1662 (see Corrections,
  below, for the conflict). He married in Watertown **11 August 1687** to **Dorothy
  Ross**, daughter of James Ross of Sudbury; the contemporary marriage-record
  transcription reads: "Upon ye 11th of August 1687 I maryed in my house at Watertown
  Eliezer Whitney (ye son of Thomas Whitney of Watertown) now living in Sudbury, &
  Dorothy Rosse ye daughter of James Rosse of Sudbury w'th ye consent of parents,
  being legally published." He was a wheelwright, resided in Sudbury in 1692, and died
  22 June 1731 (a testamentary deed of his was recorded 30 Sept 1728, three years
  before his death — see Corrections). — Pierce, entry "32. Eleazer Whitney (Thomas,
  John)"; marriage record as quoted on WeRelate/period vital-records compilations.
- Eleazer and Dorothy (Ross) Whitney's children, per Pierce (entries 129–136),
  included: Eleazer (b. 5 Mar 1690, d. young), James (b./d. 1697), Thomas (bap. 28 Jan
  1699), James (bap. 28 Jan 1699, d. young), Mary (b. 20 Nov 1697), **Dorothy (b. 24
  April 1700)**, Eleazer (bap. 15 Apr 1702), and Elnathan (b. 5 May 1705). The tree's
  birthdate for Dorothy Whitney (24 April 1700, Watertown) matches this entry exactly.
- **Dorothy Whitney**'s husband, per the tree, was Joshua Pond. A Joshua Pond, son of
  Caleb Pond, was born at Dedham 12 April 1700, styled "husbandman," lived in
  Dedham/West Roxbury; his (unnamed-by-surname) wife **Dorothy** was admitted to the
  Dedham church 28 Feb 1724/5; he died early in 1775; his widow **died shortly before
  6 July 1786** (Harris's book: "the widow's share... she having deceased before July
  6, 1786"), and their estate was divided among three surviving children including a
  son **Jonas**, born at Dedham 11 Oct 1730, baptized 10 Jan 1730/1 — matching the
  tree's listed child "Jonas Pond." — E.D. Harris, *A Genealogical Record of Daniel
  Pond, and His Descendants* (1873), Archive.org full text, entries "(11) 46. Joshua
  Pond" and "143. Jonas Pond."
  - The death-date match (6 July 1786, to the day) between the tree's Dorothy Whitney
    and Harris's unnamed "widow Dorothy Pond" is strong corroborating evidence that
    they are the same woman, though Harris's book never states his Dorothy's maiden
    surname — see Open Questions.

## Corrections to the tree

- **Eleazer Whitney's father: "Richard Whetny" → Thomas Whitney.** The tree's second
  Eleazer Whitney record (I182541966281) lists parents "Richard Whetny" and "Mary
  Flowers Kettle Kendall Kedell." Both the 1687 marriage-record transcription ("Eliezer
  Whitney, ye son of Thomas Whitney of Watertown...") and Pierce's numbered genealogy
  entry ("32. Eleazer Whitney (Thomas, John)") independently identify his father as
  **Thomas Whitney**, not Richard. Richard Whitney (John's other son, b. England 1626,
  m. Martha Coldam) was Thomas's brother and had his own son born the same year,
  **Elisha, b. 26 Aug 1662** — a name and date close enough to "Eleazer, b. [~]1662"
  that a sibling/cousin mix-up is the likely origin of this tree error.
- **Mother's surname is a garbled concatenation, not a real compound name.** "Mary
  Flowers Kettle Kendall Kedell" appears to be several source variant-spelling
  attempts ("Kettle," "Kendall," "Kedell" — all found as spellings of the same Watertown
  family in Pierce's book, e.g. "Mary Kedall or Kettle") mashed into one field, plus an
  unexplained "Flowers" with no supporting source found. This reads as a data-merge
  artifact rather than a documented middle/maiden name; the correct rendering per
  Pierce is simply **Mary Kedall (Kettle)**.
- **Eleazer Whitney's death date: 30 Sept 1728 → 22 June 1731.** The tree lists his
  death as "30 09 1728 @ Watertown." Pierce's entry states he died 22 June 1731, and
  separately notes "a testamentary deed is recorded Sept. 30, 1728" — i.e., 30 Sept
  1728 was the recording date of a legal deed made during his lifetime, not his date
  of death. The tree appears to have mis-mapped the deed-recording date into the death
  field.
- **Eleazer Whitney's birth date is internally inconsistent, and the tree preserves
  both versions unresolved.** One tree record gives "2 September 1662"; the duplicate
  record adds a second, conflicting birth event "7 Apr 1662... val=None." Pierce's own
  book contains the same contradiction: a vital-records-style passage lists "Eleazer,
  b. Sept. 2, 1662" (immediately followed by twin brother Elnathan, "b. Sept. 2, 1662"),
  while Pierce's numbered genealogical entry gives "b. Watertown Apr. 7, 1662." This
  looks like an error already present in the 1878 source (likely a transcription
  slip conflating Eleazer's birth line with his twin Elnathan's), which the tree has
  faithfully — if confusingly — carried forward as two separate birth events rather
  than resolving it.

## New findings (facts not in the tree)

- Immigrant ancestor identified: **John Whitney**, bap. 1592 London, emigrated to
  Watertown, Mass. ca. 1635, freeman 1636, wife Elinor (d. 1659), d. 1673. He is the
  documented head of this entire Massachusetts Whitney line (not present at all in
  the tree data supplied).
- Intermediate generation identified: **Thomas Whitney** (son of John), b. England
  1629, m. Watertown 11 Jan 1654 Mary Kedall/Kettle, freeman 1690, d. Watertown 20
  Sept 1719 — this is Eleazer Whitney's actual father and is entirely absent from the
  tree (which instead names the wrong brother, Richard).
- Eleazer Whitney's wife's identity and origin: **Dorothy Ross**, daughter of James
  Ross of Sudbury, Mass., married 11 Aug 1687 — not present in the tree at all (the
  tree gives no spouse for Eleazer).
- Eleazer and Dorothy (Ross) Whitney had at least seven other children besides Dorothy
  (b. 1700): Eleazer (d. young), James (twice, both apparently died young or are
  duplicate register entries), Thomas, Mary, a second Eleazer (bap. 1702, m. Mary
  Grow), and Elnathan (m. Sarah Perry) — none of these siblings are in the tree.
- Dorothy Whitney's husband **Joshua Pond** is independently documented: son of Caleb
  Pond of Dedham, b. 12 Apr 1700, husbandman, owned land near Cow Island and in
  Dedham/West Roxbury, died early 1775. His estate was administered by his widow and
  son Jonas starting 31 Mar 1775, inventoried at £531 19s. 2d., and settled in 1776.
- Additional children of Joshua and Dorothy Pond beyond "Jonas" (the only child in the
  tree): **Joshua Pond** (b. Dedham 16 Jan 1722/3) and **Miriam Pond** (b. Roxbury 17
  Sept 1725, m. a Brackett). The estate records confirm exactly three surviving
  children split the estate in 1776–1787, consistent with these three.

## Open questions

- **Was the tree's scope for this line correctly bounded at generations 10–11?** The
  task brief describes a survey of generations 7–12, but the tree-data file provided
  only contained these two generations (Eleazer Whitney and his daughter Dorothy). No
  earlier (gen 12+) or later (gen 9-) Whitney individuals were present in the supplied
  data to research, so this dossier could not extend the chain further using the tree
  itself — the generations 7–9 and 12 portions of the line, if they exist elsewhere in
  the tree, were outside this file's scope.
- **Is Dorothy Pond's maiden name definitively "Whitney"?** No single record found
  states this explicitly. The identification rests on convergence of two independent
  published genealogies: Pierce's Whitney book places a daughter "Dorothy, b. Apr. 24,
  1700" in exactly the right Watertown Whitney family, while Harris's Pond book places
  an unnamed-maiden-surname "Dorothy," wife of Joshua Pond of Dedham, who died just
  before 6 July 1786 — the same death date the tree assigns to Dorothy Whitney. This is
  a strong circumstantial match but not a document that names her "Dorothy Whitney"
  outright; a marriage record or an intentions-of-marriage filing for Watertown/Dedham
  ca. 1720s would close this gap.
- Neither source reviewed gives an exact marriage date for Joshua and Dorothy Pond
  ("marriage of Joshua Pond is not recorded" — Harris). Her church admission (28 Feb
  1724/5) is the earliest firm date placing her in the Pond household.
- The tree's duplicate Eleazer Whitney record's conflicting birth dates (2 Sept vs. 7
  Apr 1662) were not resolved by any source found beyond noting that Pierce's own book
  contains the same internal inconsistency — worth checking Watertown's original town
  vital records (published as *Watertown Records*) directly if greater precision is
  wanted.

## Duplicate-record artifacts flagged

- **Dorothy Whitney** appears twice in the tree data with fully identical fields
  (IDs `I182541959431` and `I182541966268`) — same birth/death events, same spouse,
  same child, same (single) parent. These are unmerged duplicates of one person.
- **Eleazer S Whitney** appears twice (IDs `I182541959480` and `I182541966281`). The
  two records disagree: one has no parents listed and a single birth event (2 Sept
  1662); the other adds a second birth event (7 Apr 1662) and lists parents ("Richard
  Whetny" + the garbled "Mary Flowers Kettle Kendall Kedell"). These should be merged
  into a single person record, and per the Corrections above, the parent field should
  be corrected to Thomas Whitney + Mary Kedall/Kettle rather than dropped or left as
  Richard Whitney.
