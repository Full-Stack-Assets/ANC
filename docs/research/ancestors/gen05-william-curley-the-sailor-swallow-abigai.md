# William "Curley the Sailor" Swallow (1846–1903) & Abigail Jenkins (1851–1912)

Generation 5 direct ancestors. Little Pond / Grand River, Lot 56, Kings County, Prince Edward Island, Canada.

## Overview

William "Curley" "The Sailor" Swallow was a farmer of Little Pond, Lot 56, Kings County, PEI, grandson of Robert Swallow, a Yorkshire seaman who settled at Grand River, Lot 56, and of the Blackett family who pioneered that shore. He married Abigail Jenkins, daughter of John "the Councillor" Jenkins of Mount Albion and Charlotte Sophia Murray of Tabusintac, New Brunswick. Both are recorded as buried in the Annandale United Baptist Cemetery, and the family was Baptist. A period newspaper announcement of their 1868 marriage was found (The Islander), which conflicts with the tree's marriage place.

## Verified facts

- **William's birth, 10 Jun 1846, Little Pond, PEI** — matches the Island Register compiled genealogy "The Descendants of Robert Swallow and Kate Blackett" (submitted by Fulton Underhay): "William 'Curley' 'The Sailor' Swallow #4604 ... born 10 Jun 1846, Little Pond Prince Edward Island, Occ./Title: Farmer." (A second Island Register file, the Banks genealogy, gives 10 Jun 1847 — see Open questions.)
- **Nicknames "Curley" and "The Sailor"** — confirmed exactly as in the tree by the Island Register Swallow genealogy.
- **William's death, 16 Jun 1903, Little Pond** (tree had this unsourced) — corroborated by the Island Register Swallow genealogy ("William died 16 Jun 1903, Little Pond ... Interred: Annandale United Baptist Cemetery") and by the Banks genealogy ("d. JUNE 1903, buried in Annandale United Baptist"). Compiled sources only, but two independent files agree.
- **Abigail's birth, 9 Feb 1851** — matches Island Register Swallow genealogy ("Abigail Jenkins #4603, born 9 Feb 1851"). One search-indexed variant gives 9 Feb 1852; the tree's date is the majority reading.
- **Abigail's death, 24 Jul 1912** (tree had this unsourced) — corroborated by the Island Register Swallow genealogy ("died 24 Jul 1912, Interred: Annandale United Baptist Cemetery, Religion: Baptist, Annandale"); Banks genealogy agrees on "d. 1912, buried in Annandale United Baptist."
- **Marriage in June 1868** — documented by a period newspaper: *The Islander* (Charlottetown), issue of 26 Jun 1868, marriage notices (R.J. Reid transcription on Island Register): "At Georgetown on the 26th inst., by D. Gordon, Esq., J.P., Mr. William Swallow, of Grand River, to Miss Abigal Jenkins of Birch Hill, Lot 49." This is the strongest (period) source found for the couple. Note it gives Georgetown and a Justice of the Peace, not Pownal Methodist Church (see Corrections).
- **Abigail's parents, John "The Councillor" Jenkins and Charlotte (Sophia) Murray** — confirmed by the Island Register Swallow genealogy ("daughter of John 'Councellor' Jenkins #4610 and Charlotte Murray #4609") and by WikiTree profile Jenkins-6778 (John Jenkins, b. 1812 Mount Albion, PEI, nickname "Councillor", d. 12 Feb 1894 Greek River, Lot 63, Kings, PEI; wife Charlotte Sophia Murray, b. 1819 Tabusintac, Alnwick, Northumberland, New Brunswick, m. 21 Mar 1838 Charlottetown, d. 1894 Greek River).
- **William's father Robert Swallow Jr.** — confirmed: Island Register Swallow genealogy makes William son of Robert Swallow #4612 (b. abt 1820, mariner), son of Robert Swallow #4620 (b. abt 1790, Yorkshire, England, seaman/farmer, d. 21 Apr 1865, Grand River, Lot 56) and Catherine "Kate" Blackett #4619 (b. 1786, d. 28 Dec 1867, Grand River, Lot 56).
- **William's mother Margaret (Banks)** — the tree's "Margaret Swallow (Banks)" is supported by the Island Register Banks genealogy ("The Descendants of Robert Banks and Mary McKie"): "Margaret Banks, b. 28 Apr 1816, d. 27 Aug 1883 in Boston. She married Robert Swallow, married 17 Dec 1839, b. 1 Oct 1820, (son of Robert William Swallow and Catherine (Kate) Blackett) d. 9 May 1904 in Boston," with son "William Swallow, b. 10 Jun 1847 ... married Abigail Jenkins." (The Swallow genealogy file instead calls Robert Jr.'s wife "Sarah Banks, daughter of George Banks and Mary MacKenzie," with the same 17 Dec 1839 marriage date — a first-name conflict between two compiled files; see Open questions.)
- **1891 residence at Lot 56, Kings** (sourced in tree from 1891 Census of Canada) — consistent with everything found: Little Pond and Grand River are both in Lot 56, and the 1868 marriage notice calls William "of Grand River." Census image itself not independently viewable from here (LAC and FamilySearch index access blocked/login-walled).
- **Children Frederick Kitson, Sarah Jane (Morrow), Priscilla, Ulysses, Edna, Gertrude "Gertie", Mabel, Lilla Belle, Frances "Fanny", Ella** — all ten appear as children of this couple in the Island Register Swallow genealogy, with dates (see New findings).
- **Daughter Edna died in childhood** — Find a Grave memorial #104684005, "Edna Swallow (1881–1892)," United Baptist Church Cemetery, Annandale, Kings County, PEI (surfaced via search; page itself is bot-blocked).

## Corrections to the tree

- **Marriage place "Methodist Church, Pownal, Lot 49" → Georgetown, by D. Gordon, Esq., J.P. (civil ceremony)** — *The Islander* of 26 Jun 1868 announces the marriage "At Georgetown ... by D. Gordon, Esq., J.P., Mr. William Swallow, of Grand River, to Miss Abigal Jenkins of Birch Hill, Lot 49." A period newspaper outranks tree lore. Georgetown is the shire town nearest Grand River/Little Pond, which fits; a Pownal Methodist ceremony does not fit a J.P. officiant. (The exact day is less certain: the tree and Island Register say 20 Jun 1868; the notice says "the 26th inst." in the 26 Jun issue, which may be a typesetting slip. Recommend keeping 20 Jun 1868 but changing place/officiant, or flagging both pending the marriage register.)
- **Abigail's residence at marriage: "Birch Hill, Lot 49"** — per the same notice. The tree gives her birthplace as Mt. Albion; both are consistent with the Jenkins family being in the Lot 49 area of Queens County, but "Birch Hill, Lot 49" is what the period record says for 1868.
- **Child "Harvey Swallow" is almost certainly spurious** — no son Harvey appears in either Island Register file. Daughter Priscilla Swallow (b. 27 Feb 1875) married **Joseph "Harvey" Dingwell** (b. 16 Aug 1875, Sailors Hope); the tree's "Harvey Swallow" looks like a mangled entry for this son-in-law. Recommend removing or re-verifying.
- **Duplicate daughters: the tree's 12 children are really 10** — "Priscilla Swallow" (b. 1875, m. Joseph Harvey Dingwell) and "Priscilla Lilla Belle Swallow" / "Lilla Swallow" (= one person, Lilla Belle Swallow, b. 29 Oct 1884, m. Charles Ernest Dingwell 1911) have been split into three or four entries. Island Register lists exactly ten children: Frederick Kitson, Sarah Jane, Priscilla, Ulysses, Edna, Gertrude, Mabel, Lilla Belle, Frances, Ella.

## New findings

**The couple**
- Occupation: William was a farmer (earlier "of Grand River," so likely also mariner stock — his father and grandfather were mariners/seamen, hence "The Sailor").
- Religion: Baptist. Both buried **Annandale United Baptist Cemetery, Annandale, Kings County, PEI** (Island Register; cemetery exists on Find a Grave as "United Baptist Church Cemetery"/"Annandale United Baptist Cemetery," Annandale).

**William's ancestry (new generations)**
- Paternal grandparents: **Robert Swallow** (b. abt 1790, Yorkshire, England; seaman, farmer; d. 21 Apr 1865, Grand River, Lot 56) and **Catherine "Kate" Blackett** (b. 1786, d. 28 Dec 1867, Grand River, Lot 56), daughter of John William Blackett and Diana Applecross. The Blacketts were the pioneer family of Grand River, Lot 56; George Saville's 1902 "Annandale History" notes "after the Blackett's left in 1812 the first Swallow's settled there for a time."
- Parents' later life: Robert Swallow Jr. (b. 1 Oct 1820) and Margaret (Banks) Swallow **removed to Boston, Massachusetts** — Margaret d. 27 Aug 1883 in Boston; Robert d. 9 May 1904 in Boston (Banks genealogy). Worth pursuing in Massachusetts death registrations.
- Margaret Banks's parents (per Banks genealogy): Robert Banks and Mary McKie line of the Annandale area.
- William's siblings (Banks genealogy): John "Black John" Swallow (b. 1836, m.1 Mary Ann Howlett, m.2 Anne W. M. Tassell), Margaret Ann (b. abt 1849, m. William Tassell), Sarah (b. abt 1851), Ann. (The Swallow file assigns some of these differently — see Open questions.)

**Abigail's ancestry (new generations)**
- Father John "The Councillor" Jenkins: b. abt 1812, Mount Albion, PEI, son of **George Jenkins and Margaret Acorn** of Mount Albion (compiled Island Register data via search; WikiTree Jenkins-6778 agrees on dates/places); d. **12 Feb 1894, Greek River, Lot 63, Kings, PEI**.
- Mother Charlotte Sophia Murray: b. 1819, Tabusintac, Northumberland Co., New Brunswick, daughter of **John Murray and Honoria Hierlihy** of Tabusintac; m. John Jenkins 21 Mar 1838 in Charlottetown; d. 1894, Greek River. Compiled sources (Stymiest trees; charlottetaylor.ca) make Honoria Hierlihy a daughter of Philip Hierlihy and Charlotte Taylor, the well-known Miramichi pioneer, and say John Murray (b. 23 Aug 1791, PEI) drowned at Tabusintac abt 1821–22 — secondary, worth verifying.
- Abigail's known siblings (compiled): Ephraim (b. 4 May 1839, m. Sarah Ann Roberts 1863, d. 11 Jan 1911 Murray River, Lot 64), George (b. abt 1843, m. Susannah Roberts 1866, d. 21 Mar 1892), Rebecca Elizabeth (b. 1844), John Murray Jenkins (b. 21 Dec 1844), Ann (b. 1847, m. Dingwell), and (Sarah) **Priscilla Jenkins** (b. 1857/58, d. 21 Feb 1938), who married **John "Red John" Swallow** (1851–1918) — a second Swallow–Jenkins marriage tying the families twice.

**The ten children (Island Register Swallow genealogy)**
1. Frederick Kitson Swallow, b. 2 Feb 1871, Little Pond; farmer; m. Agnes "Aggie" Marion Dingwell (1892–1982); d. 2 Aug 1959, Little Pond; buried Annandale United Church Cemetery.
2. Sarah Jane Swallow, b. abt 1873; m.(1) 17 Sep 1892 at Annandale, Charles Palmer (d. abt 1900); m.(2) abt 1909, Russell William McDonald Morrow (abt 1871–abt 1940) — hence the tree's "Sarah Jane Morrow."
3. Priscilla Swallow, b. 27 Feb 1875; m. Joseph "Harvey" Dingwell of Sailors Hope; d. bef. 1928.
4. Ulysses Swallow, b. 10 May 1879, Little Pond; school trustee; m. 23 Mar 1899 in the Presbyterian Manse, Georgetown, Rebecca Burhoe of Nova Scotia; d. 1 Apr 1950, Dundas, PEI.
5. Edna Swallow, 1881–1892 (died age ~11); buried United Baptist Church Cemetery, Annandale (Find a Grave #104684005).
6. Gertrude "Gertie" Swallow, b. abt 1882; buried Annandale United Baptist Cemetery (no marriage recorded — likely died young).
7. Mabel Swallow, b. abt 1884, Little Pond; m. Pius E. "Sailor" MacDonald of Cape Spry.
8. Lilla Belle Swallow, b. 29 Oct 1884, Little Pond; m. 10 Jan 1911 in Dundas Presbyterian Manse, Charles Ernest Dingwell (1876–1923) of Little River; d. 29 Apr 1968, Little Pond; buried Annandale United Baptist Cemetery.
9. Frances "Fanny" Swallow, b. 29 Aug 1888, Little Pond; m. Warren Smith; **d. 2 Mar 1954 in Boston, Suffolk, Massachusetts** — another Boston migrant.
10. Ella Swallow, b. 29 Apr 1893; m. abt 1913 William "Warren" Dingwell (1868–1917) of Sailors Hope; d. 12 Apr 1919 (age 25); buried Annandale United Baptist Cemetery.

Three of William & Abigail's daughters married Dingwells, and a granddaughter-in-law was a Dingwell — the Swallow, Dingwell, Jenkins, Banks, Blackett and Tassell families of the Annandale–Little Pond shore intermarried repeatedly.

## Open questions

- **William's birth year 1846 vs 1847**: the Swallow file says 10 Jun 1846, the Banks file 10 Jun 1847. The PEI Baptismal Index (PARO, gov.pe.ca/parosearch — POST-only form, not reachable from this session) should settle it.
- **Robert Jr.'s wife: Margaret Banks or Sarah Banks?** Two Island Register files disagree on the given name (and on her parents: Robert Banks & Mary McKie vs George Banks & Mary MacKenzie) while agreeing on the 17 Dec 1839 marriage date. The tree says Margaret. The original marriage record (PEI marriage registers 1832–1888, FamilySearch collection 1827167 — login required) would resolve this. Note also both files scramble which cousin ("Black John" b. 1836, "Red John" b. 1851, Margaret Ann b. 1849) belongs to which Swallow brother.
- **Exact marriage date/place**: tree 20 Jun 1868 Pownal Methodist vs Islander notice "26th inst." at Georgetown by J.P. D. Gordon. Check the original register entry (PEI Marriage Registers, RG19).
- **Census households 1881, 1891, 1901**: index access blocked from this session (LAC collection search 403, FamilySearch login-walled, AutomatedGenealogy timed out). The family should appear at Lot 56, Kings, in 1881/1891/1901 (William) and 1911 (Abigail, widow). Worth pulling household composition, which could also confirm/deny "Harvey."
- **Find a Grave memorials for William and Abigail**: none surfaced via search (only daughter Edna, #104684005). The Annandale United Baptist Cemetery (Find a Grave cemetery #2306973 / #2371175, ~111 memorials) may simply not have their stones photographed/indexed yet — or the stones may not survive.
- **Abigail's 1912 death registration**: PEI civil registration began 1906; her 24 Jul 1912 death should be in the PARO death index. Charlottetown Guardian (digitized at islandnewspapers.ca, Cloudflare-blocked here) likely carried an obituary in late July 1912; likewise for William in June 1903.
- **Why "Curley the Sailor"?** His father and grandfather were mariners; no direct record of William's own sea service found. Crew lists / shipping registers for Georgetown, PEI would be the place to look.
