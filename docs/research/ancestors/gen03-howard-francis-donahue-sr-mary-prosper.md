# Howard Francis Donahue Sr. (1919-2008) & Mary Prosper (c.1918-1993) — Generation 3

*Deep-dive dossier, researched 3 Jul 2026. (Note: the work order's subject line read "Douglas Leon Holst BIL & Abigail Elizabeth Morrow," but the assigned tree-data file A04.json contains this Donahue/Prosper couple; the JSON data was treated as authoritative.)*

## Overview

Howard Francis "Jack" Donahue Sr. spent nearly his whole life in Brockton, Plymouth County, Massachusetts — the son of an Irish-surnamed father (John P. Donahue) and a Swedish-descended mother (Ellen Swanson). He drove trucks for the F.B. Washburn/Waleeco Candy Company and buses for Brockton Area Transit, and served in the Civilian Conservation Corps building roads in New Hampshire. His first wife Mary Prosper was the Brockton-born daughter of Italian immigrants Angelo Prospero/Prospus and Vincenza Minochelli; after her death in March 1993 Howard remarried (Lillian E. Burr Peluso) and moved to Raynham, dying at Morton Hospital in Taunton on 8 October 2008, aged 89.

## Verified facts

- **Howard's full identity and nickname**: Howard F. "Jack" Donahue, Sr., died aged 89 — *Conley/Russell & Pica Funeral Home obituary, Brockton* (conleyrussellpica.com, obId 2838681); also indexed at Legacy.com (The Enterprise, Brockton, id 20042134). Confirms tree birth 5 Jul 1919.
- **Death 8 Oct 2008**: "died Wednesday evening, October 8, 2008 at the Morton Hospital in Taunton"; last residence Raynham, "formerly of Brockton" — *funeral home obituary*. Confirms tree death date and supplies the missing place.
- **Burial**: Village Cemetery, North Easton (Bristol County), Massachusetts — *funeral home obituary*. Consistent with the tree's burial place "Easton, Bristol County"; the specific cemetery (est. 1877, behind Unity Church, 9 Hoe Shop Lane, run by the Village Cemetery Corporation) is confirmed by the Find a Grave cemetery page (cemetery #1989807) and CemeteryFind.
- **Parents**: son of John P. Donahue and Ellen (Swanson) — *obituary*, matching the tree exactly. The obituary further shows his mother's later surnames: Ellen (Swanson) (Donohue) (Lindquist) Card, and names a stepfather, George Lindquist.
- **Raised/schooled in Brockton**: "attended Brockton Schools" — *obituary*; consistent with the tree's 1920/1930/1935/1940/1950 Brockton census residences.
- **CCC service**: "during the War served in the Civilian Conservation Corps building roads in New Hampshire" — *obituary*. This is the substance behind the tree's bare "military [sourced]" event (whose cited source, the WWII Draft Cards Young Men 1940-1947 collection, is a draft *registration*, not proof of armed service).
- **Occupation**: career truck driver for F.B. Washburn/Waleeco Candy Company, then bus driver for Brockton Area Transit (BAT), then activity driver for Unda's Transportation after retirement — *obituary*.
- **Memberships**: the Elks; Amalgamated Transit Union Local #1547; life member, Teamsters Local #653 — *obituary*.
- **Children (as listed in Howard's obituary)**: Howard F. Donahue Jr. (wife Terry; Sharon, MA), Dennis A. Donahue (wife Pat; Arizona), Virginia E. Donahue (Arizona), and Jacqueline D. Medas (husband Joseph; Brockton). Seven grandchildren named (Brian Donahue, Christine Albertson, Jill Clifford, Dennis Donahue Jr., Debra DesJardins, Daniel DeGregorio, Alicia Foster), 14 great-grandchildren, 1 great-great-grandchild. (All potentially living — named here only, not researched.)
- **Second marriage**: at death Howard's wife was Lillian E. (Burr) (Peluso) Donahue — *obituary*. Her own obituary (same funeral home, obId 2838603) gives her dates 8 Jun 1926 - 18 Oct 2012, of Raynham; first husband Joseph Peluso Jr.; her only children were two Peluso sons; interment Coweeset Cemetery, Brockton. This confirms Mary Prosper was Howard's earlier wife and mother-generation of the tree line, and that Jacqueline Medas is NOT Lillian's daughter.
- **Half-siblings of Howard** (via mother's Lindquist remarriage): George "Link" Lindquist and Pauline Enos (both predeceased him); sister Phyllis Richards surviving in 2008 — *obituary*.

## Corrections to the tree

- **Death place (Howard): "None" → Morton Hospital, Taunton, Bristol Co., MA** (residence at death: Raynham, MA). Evidence: funeral home obituary, October 2008.
- **Children list (both spouses): add a fourth child, Jacqueline D. (Donahue) Medas** (wife of Joseph Medas, of Brockton in 2008). She is listed among Howard's children in his obituary and is not a child of second wife Lillian (whose obituary lists only her two Peluso sons). Whether her mother was Mary Prosper is highly probable but not yet documented.
- **Clarification, not error — "military" event (Howard)**: the sourced record is a WWII draft registration card; his obituary attributes his wartime service to the Civilian Conservation Corps (road-building in New Hampshire), not the armed forces. Recommend relabeling the event to avoid implying military enlistment.
- **Refinement — burial (Howard)**: "Easton, Bristol County" → specifically Village Cemetery, North Easton (obituary).

No tree fact was found to be outright wrong.

## New findings (not in the tree)

1. **Nickname "Jack"** — used in the obituary headline and throughout.
2. **Occupations**: candy-company truck driver (F.B. Washburn / Waleeco), BAT bus driver, Unda's Transportation activity driver; union man (Teamsters #653 life member, ATU #1547); Elk. The obituary calls him a "Jack of all Trades" with a green thumb.
3. **CCC service in New Hampshire.**
4. **Mother's remarriages**: Ellen (Swanson) Donohue later married George Lindquist (Howard's stepfather) and was later surnamed Card. This neatly explains the tree's otherwise puzzling record of Howard's **baptism at age 10 (20 Apr 1930) at First Lutheran "Bethesda" Church, Campello, Brockton** — a Swedish-American Lutheran congregation, i.e. his Swedish mother's (and Swedish stepfather's) church, recorded in the Swedish American Church Records collection.
5. **Half-siblings**: George "Link" Lindquist, Pauline Enos, Phyllis Richards.
6. **Second wife**: Lillian E. (Burr) (Peluso) Donahue, 1926-2012, of Raynham; married Howard sometime after Mary's death in 1993; buried Coweeset Cemetery, Brockton (Howard, by contrast, was buried with — presumably — Mary at Village Cemetery, North Easton).
7. **Surname-variant intelligence for Mary's family**: Geneanet's surname index shows **"Di Prospero" entries recorded at Brockton, Massachusetts** — a strong hint that Angelo's family may appear in records as Di Prospero as well as Prospero/Prospus/Prosper. (Compiled/secondary lead only.)
8. **Village Cemetery, North Easton has a searchable burial database** (CemeteryFind, cemetery ID 2e98535e-93bc-4e0a-8f45-33bc9f01ee96) — a POST-form search that could not be queried programmatically here but should list Howard's (and possibly Mary's) lot.

## Mary Prosper — research status

Open-web results for Mary were thin, as expected for a woman who died in March 1993 (before online obituary archives, which for the Brockton Enterprise begin ~2000 at ObitsArchive/Legacy). Nothing contradicting the tree was found. Her identity rests on the tree's census (1920-1950), SS Applications & Claims Index (which is what names her parents Angelo Prospus/Prospero and Vincenza Minochelli), and Ancestry trees. No Find a Grave memorial, obituary, or independent record was located for her, for Angelo, or for Vincenza in open indexes; the 1950 census OCR search at 1950census.archives.gov (Plymouth Co., MA "Donahue" hits) did not surface the household, though OCR quality makes this inconclusive.

## Open questions

2. **Mary's exact birth date and death date** — "abt 1918" / "Mar 1993" look SSDI-derived. Her SS Applications & Claims entry (already a tree source) should carry her full DOB; a March 1993 Enterprise obituary should exist on microfilm/GenealogyBank.
3. **Is Mary buried at Village Cemetery, North Easton with Howard?** Check the CemeteryFind burial search or Find a Grave (which blocked automated access during this research; the tree already cites the Find a Grave index for Howard, so a memorial exists — its ID should be captured).
4. **Jacqueline D. Medas's mother** — obituary order (listed after Virginia) suggests she is Howard's daughter, presumably by Mary; confirm via her own records when appropriate (she may be living — not researched).
5. **Prospero/Prospus/Di Prospero family** — Angelo's immigration, naturalization, WWI draft card, and Brockton directory entries were not findable in open indexes; the Geneanet "Di Prospero @ Brockton" lead and Brockton's Italian parish records (St. Patrick's / Our Lady of Lourdes) are the next veins for the generation-4 dive.
