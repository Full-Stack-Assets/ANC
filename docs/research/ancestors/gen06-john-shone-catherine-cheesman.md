# John Shone (1791–1839) & Catherine Cheesman (1790–1890)

Generation 6 direct ancestors (Gloucester County, New Jersey — the area around Glassboro/Gloucester Township that later split into Camden County). Researched 2026-07-03 by web search; several key secondary sources (WikiTree, Geni) could only be accessed as search-engine summaries because their pages did not return fetchable content — see the caveat under Open questions.

## Overview

John Shone and Catherine Cheesman married about 1813 in Glassboro, Gloucester County, New Jersey, and raised eleven children there, including Mary Ann Shone, who later married Isaac Albertson (see the Generation 5 dossier for that couple). John died in 1839 in Gloucester Township at about age 48; Catherine outlived him by 51 years, dying in 1890 at a reported age of about 99–100 (though one index suggests she may have been younger). Catherine's parents, Peter Thomas ("Major") Cheesman and his first wife Elizabeth Jackson, were established Gloucester County gentry; John Shone's own origins — the tree claims Cheshire, England — could not be corroborated by any source found in this search.

## Verified facts

- **John Shone died in Gloucester Township, NJ, in 1839** — corroborated independently of the tree by a genealogy.com forum post (compiled by researcher Richard Bart, 2010) discussing the descendants of Cornelius Pease, which states plainly that son Richard Shone "was the son of John Shone; d. 1839 in Gloucester Co, NJ." This is a second, independent secondary source agreeing with the tree's death year and county (the tree's precise day, 10 Sep 1839, is not corroborated by this source but is not contradicted either).
- **Catherine Cheesman's birth: 19 Sep 1790** — matches the tree exactly per a WikiTree profile (Cheesman-114, "Catherine (Cheesman) Shone," managed by Randy Hammock). A second secondary source (the genealogy.com/Pease forum post) gives a very close but not identical date, "Catherine Cheesman; b. Sep 16, 1790" — a three-day discrepancy between two independent secondary compilers, not resolved here.
- **Catherine's parents: Peter T. Cheesman and Elizabeth Jackson** — confirmed independently by the same genealogy.com forum post: "Catherine was the daughter of Peter T. Cheesman and his first wife, Elizabeth Jackson; b. Mar 30, 1768." This matches the tree's parent listing exactly, including Elizabeth Jackson's identity, and specifies Elizabeth was Peter's *first* wife (new detail, not in the tree).
- **Catherine died 17 Jul 1890** — the day and month match the tree exactly per the WikiTree profile summary, which also gives the same place-name pattern as her birth (Gloucester Township, Gloucester Co., NJ) rather than the tree's "Camden, New Jersey" — see Corrections.
- **Son Richard Shone (b. 1814, d. 1889)** — appears in the tree's children list and is independently documented in the genealogy.com/Pease forum post, which states he married Abigail Pease on 16 Apr 1835 in Gloucester Township (by Justice of the Peace James H. Chester) and that the couple appear in the 1850 and 1880 censuses (1880: Washington Township, Gloucester Co., with their three youngest children). This is a real, sourced marriage/census trail for one of John & Catherine's children, not previously in the tree.
- **Son Thomas [John] Shone married Sarah Jane Pine, 6 Jan 1853, Camden County** — confirmed via the New Horizons Genealogical Services transcription of Camden County Marriage Book A (grooms K–Z). Matches the tree's child "Thomas John Shone."
- **A "Cornelius P. Shone" married Catharine H. Kindel, 15 Dec 1863, Camden County** — same Camden Co. marriage-book transcription. This Cornelius is *not* in the tree's list of John & Catherine's children; per the genealogy.com forum, Richard Shone (son of John & Catherine) had a son "Cornelius b. ca. 1840," whose age fits a 1863 marriage — i.e., this Cornelius P. Shone is very likely a **grandson**, not a child, of John Shone and Catherine Cheesman. See Corrections/Open questions.

## Corrections to the tree

- **Catherine's birthplace "Pennsylvania, USA" (tree, unsourced)** → the only external source found, a WikiTree profile, instead gives **Gloucester Township, Gloucester County, New Jersey**. This is also more consistent with her being the daughter of Peter T. Cheesman, an established Gloucester County figure. Flagged as a likely correction, not fully proven (WikiTree is itself a compiled/secondary source).
- **Catherine's death place "Camden, New Jersey, United States" (tree, unsourced)** → the WikiTree profile instead gives **Gloucester Township, Gloucester County, New Jersey** (matching her birthplace, not the city/county of Camden). Camden County was carved out of Gloucester County only in 1844, after Catherine's mid-life, so "Camden, NJ" for an 1890 death is plausible on paper, but WikiTree's specific townhip claim conflicts with it; not fully resolved.
- **Catherine's age at death — possible conflict, unresolved**: the tree's own birth (1790) and death (1890) years imply she died at about age 99–100. A search-engine summary of a MyHeritage name-index for "Catherine Shone" turned up an entry for a Catherine who "passed away ... at age 93 ... in 1890, New Jersey" — which would imply a birth year closer to 1796/97, not 1790. This may or may not be the same woman (MyHeritage indexes contain many unrelated Catherine Shones), but it is flagged here as a discrepancy worth checking against a primary death record or the 1880/1900 census age columns, rather than silently accepted.

## New findings (not in the tree)

- **Catherine's father, Peter Thomas ("Major") Cheesman**, per Geni.com (accessed via search-engine summary only — the raw page would not fetch): born **9 Mar 1771**, died **24 Mar 1856** in **Blackwood, Camden County, NJ**; his own parents were **Richard D. Cheesman and Rachel (Williams) Cheesman**. This extends the maternal line one further generation back from what is in the tree.
- **Peter T. Cheesman remarried** after Elizabeth Jackson's death (or during her life, if the "first wife" wording implies divorce/death) to a **Sarah (Smallwood) Cheesman** as his second wife, per the same Geni summary and consistent with the genealogy.com/Pease forum's note that "Peter and his second wife were the in-laws of Abigail's sisters Moriah, Sophia, and Elizabeth" (the sisters of Abigail Pease, who married Richard Shone). This is compiled/secondary and not independently verified here.
- **Grandchildren via Richard Shone & Abigail Pease** (per the genealogy.com/Pease forum, itself compiling census and county-marriage data): **Cornelius (b. c.1840), Margaret (b. c.1856), Jacob (b. c.1858), Adam (b. c.1860)**, all born in New Jersey. None of these grandchildren appear in the tree's data file.
- **Local militia/War-of-1812 context**: the same forum post notes that Abigail Pease's father, Cornelius A. Pease, served in the War of 1812 "in Lieutenant Colonel Joshua Howell's Regiment, Captain Richard Cheesman's Company" — a different Richard Cheesman (presumably a relative of Peter T. Cheesman) who commanded a NJ militia company, situating the extended Cheesman family in local military leadership circles around 1812–1814.
- **No Find A Grave memorial could be located for either John Shone or Catherine Cheesman Shone**, despite multiple targeted searches — in contrast to their daughter Mary Ann (Shone) Albertson and son-in-law Isaac Albertson, both of whom have detailed, photographed Find A Grave memorials at Cedar Grove Cemetery, Gloucester City (see the Generation 5 dossier). Their burial place remains unknown from this search.
- **No probate/estate record for John Shone's 1839 death could be located.** The published "Calendar of New Jersey Wills, Administrations, etc." series (New Jersey Historical Society) — searched in full text for this project — only extends to about 1817 in its published volumes; it does not reach 1839, so the absence of a hit there is expected, not informative. Any 1839 Gloucester County probate would need to be sought directly from the NJ State Archives (Trenton) or the Gloucester County Surrogate's Court, neither of which was searched here.
- Searched the full text of the 1883 *History of the Counties of Gloucester, Salem, and Cumberland, New Jersey* (a standard county history with biographical sketches) for "Shone" and "Cheesman/Cheeseman." No mention of John Shone, Catherine Cheesman, or a biographical sketch of this specific family was found. The book does document an unrelated (or distantly related) Cheesman family at Turnersville and Squankum/Williamstown — including a Richard Cheesman who held land as early as 1768 and a Peter Cheeseman who built a grist-mill there before 1800 — confirming the Cheesman name was well-established across several Gloucester County townships in this era, but no direct link to Peter T. Cheesman or Catherine was established.

## Open questions

1. **Was John Shone really born in Cheshire, England (1791), as the tree states?** No source found here corroborates this — no immigration/naturalization record, no English parish record, no compiled genealogy linking a Cheshire Shone family to this John. WikiTree's Shone-surname index does include several unrelated Cheshire/Wales Shone families, but none matching these vital dates. This claim should be treated as **legend** pending a primary record.
2. **Who were John Shone's actual parents?** The tree lists "Jacob Shone*" and "Mary Skinner" — both names carry the tree's own uncertainty-flag asterisk on Jacob — and no independent source in this search corroborates a Jacob Shone/Mary Skinner couple in Gloucester County, NJ (or in Cheshire, England).
3. **Catherine Cheesman's exact birth date and age at death** — tree/WikiTree agree on 19 Sep 1790, but the genealogy.com/Pease forum gives 16 Sep 1790, and a MyHeritage index summary implies an age-93 death (i.e., a 1796/97 birth). A primary death record, gravestone, or dated census entry for Catherine would resolve this.
4. **Where are John and Catherine buried?** No Find A Grave or cemetery record was found for either. Given the family's presence in Glassboro/Gloucester Township, a Methodist or Quaker meeting burial ground in that area is a plausible next place to check.
5. **Full accounting of the other nine children** — this search traced Richard Shone (and his children) and Thomas J. Shone in some depth via marriage records, but Emaline, Elizabeth Ann, Jacob, Peter, John (Jr.), Henry M., William, and Sarah Jane Shone were not individually researched here.
6. **Is "Cornelius P. Shone" (married 1863) a grandson via Richard Shone, as this dossier infers, or could the tree's absence of a "Cornelius" among John & Catherine's own children be an omission instead?** Worth checking a primary source (his marriage record or a census) for his parentage.
7. **Reliability of the Peter T. Cheesman details** (birth/death dates, second wife Sarah Smallwood, parents Richard D. Cheesman & Rachel Williams) — these came through only as WebSearch-engine summaries of Geni.com and MyHeritage, because the raw pages would not fetch (likely JavaScript-gated). They should be treated as unverified compiled/secondary claims until the actual pages (or their underlying sources) can be reviewed directly.
