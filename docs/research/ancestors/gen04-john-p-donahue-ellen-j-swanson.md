# John P. Donahue (b. 1896) & Ellen J. Swanson (1900-1990) — Generation 4

*Deep-dive dossier, researched 3 Jul 2026. (Note: the work order's subject line read "Russell Morrow & Sarah Jane Morrow," but the assigned tree-data file A11.json contains this Donahue/Swanson couple; the JSON data was treated as authoritative, and the dossier path assigned matches this couple.)*

## Overview

John P. Donahue, a Brockton-born (23 Oct 1896), Boston-raised son of Irish-surnamed parents Patrick and Ellen F. Donahue, married Swedish-descended Ellen J. Swanson of North Easton in Brockton on 9 May 1919; their son Howard Francis Donahue Sr. (1919-2008) carries the tree owner's direct line. The marriage did not last: both remarried — John to Mary Ellen "Nell" Giessen, Ellen to George J. Lindquist, with whom she had at least three more children, and later (a fact new to this research) to a third husband surnamed **Card**. Ellen died 1 Aug 1990 at Bridgewater, Plymouth Co., MA; John's death date remains undiscovered but he predeceased his son's 2008 obituary ("the late John P. Donahue"). Open-web sources for this couple proved thin — the richest find was their son's 2008 funeral-home obituary, which documents the whole blended-family structure.

## Verified facts

- **The couple as parents of Howard Francis Donahue Sr.**: Howard's obituary (Conley/Russell & Pica Funeral Home, Brockton, 2008) names him "the son of the late John P. Donahue, stepson of the late George Lindquist, and Ellen (Swanson) (Donohue) (Lindquist) Card." This independently confirms both tree parents, Ellen's remarriage to George Lindquist, and adds a final Card surname. [Obituary, re-verified directly this session]
- **John P. Donahue predeceased October 2008** — "the late John P. Donahue" in the same obituary. First open-source bound on his unknown death date.
- **George Lindquist predeceased October 2008** — "the late George Lindquist," same obituary.
- **Ellen's Lindquist children**: the obituary names Howard's siblings "the late George 'Link' Lindquist and Pauline Enos" (i.e., Pauline Lindquist m. Enos) and a surviving sister **Phyllis Richards** — matching and extending the tree's children list for Ellen (George Lindquist Jr, Pauline Lindquist), and showing both George Jr. and Pauline died before Oct 2008.
- **Ellen's baptismal church is real and Swedish**: Holy Trinity Lutheran Church, North Easton, MA (125 Lincoln St) is an existing ELCA congregation of Swedish origin; its historical records appear in the "U.S., Evangelical Lutheran Church in America, Swedish American Church Records, 1800-1947" collection under the congregation name "Libanon," exactly as the tree's baptism event (17 May 1900) states. [ldsgenealogy.com North Easton parish list; church's current web presence] — corroborates venue, not the individual entry.
- **Geography check**: North Easton is a village of Easton, Bristol County, MA — the tree's two birth entries ("N Easton, Massachusetts" and "Easton, Bristol, Massachusetts") describe the same place and are mutually consistent.
- **No Boston Globe/Herald obituary for Ellen**: the Boston Public Library obituary index (Globe/Herald/Transcript, 1953-2010) returns no Lindquist, Card, or matching Donahue entry for her — consistent with a death-notice-only or Brockton Enterprise-only obituary in Aug 1990. (Negative result, checked this session via obits.bpl.org.)

Facts carried by the tree that are internally consistent and well-sourced but could not be independently re-verified on the open web (records sit behind Ancestry/FamilySearch logins): John's birth 23 Oct 1896 and Brockton RC baptism 9 Nov 1896 (Boston Archdiocese sacramental records); his 1900/1910 Boston, 1917-18 WWI draft (Brockton), 1920 Brockton Ward 3, 1930 Boston, and 1942 WWII draft events; Ellen's birth 4 May 1900 at North Easton (MA Birth Records 1840-1915), 1900 Easton and 1920 Brockton Ward 3 censuses, 1950 Brockton census, and death 1 Aug 1990 at Bridgewater; the 9 May 1919 Brockton marriage (MA Marriage Index 1901-1955).

## Corrections to the tree

- **Ellen's "birth: abt 1907 [sourced]" event → discard/detach.** Her birth is triply documented as 4 May 1900 (MA birth record, Libanon/Holy Trinity baptism 17 May 1900, and residence in her parents' household in the 1900 census). An "abt 1907" birth is irreconcilable with a 1900 census appearance and a 1919 marriage; it is almost certainly a record mis-attached from another person (or an age understated on a later record).
- **Ellen's spouse list is incomplete: add a third husband, surname Card**, married after George Lindquist and before her death in 1990. Evidence: her son's 2008 obituary styles her "Ellen (Swanson) (Donohue) (Lindquist) Card." Given name of Mr. Card not yet found.
- **Ellen's children list is incomplete: add a daughter, Phyllis (presumably Lindquist) Richards**, surviving in Oct 2008 (possibly still living — named here only, not researched, per privacy rule).

No tree fact other than the stray "abt 1907" birth was found to be wrong.

## New findings (not in the tree)

1. **Ellen's third marriage (Card)** — see above; explains why she cannot be found in death/burial indexes as "Ellen Lindquist": her 1990 death record, SSDI entry, and any grave marker are probably under **Ellen Card**.
2. **A third Lindquist child, Phyllis (Richards)**, in addition to George "Link" Lindquist Jr. and Pauline (Lindquist) Enos.
3. **Death bounds**: John P. Donahue, George J. Lindquist, George "Link" Lindquist Jr., and Pauline Enos all died before 8 Oct 2008; Phyllis Richards was alive then.
4. **Nickname**: Ellen's son by Lindquist was known as "Link"; note also that Howard Sr. himself was called "Jack" — useful when reading Brockton newspaper mentions.
5. **Spelling intelligence**: the family's Donahue/Donohue spelling is unstable — the 2008 obituary spells Ellen's married surname "Donohue" while the tree (and John's own records) use "Donahue"; John's second wife is carried in the tree as "Giessen **Donohue**." Search both spellings (and Donohoe) in any index.
6. **Family-structure inference (inferred, not documented)**: John and Ellen's marriage ended in divorce rather than death — both were alive and remarried (John registered for the WWII draft in 1942; Ellen was raising her children in the Lindquist household and had Howard baptized at age 10, in 1930, at the Swedish First Lutheran "Bethesda" Church, Campello, Brockton). The 1930 census places John in Boston, away from Brockton, consistent with a 1920s separation.
7. **1950 census OCR search (NARA)**: no Lindquist household surfaced in Brockton proper; the only Plymouth County OCR hits for the surname were two West Bridgewater households (EDs 12-142 and 12-144, one indexed "Lindauist Geo."), neither demonstrably Ellen's. OCR quality makes this inconclusive rather than negative; the tree's sourced 1950 Brockton residence for Ellen presumably rests on the Ancestry index, which is more reliable than NARA's raw OCR.

## Open questions

1. **John P. Donahue's death** — date, place, burial. He was alive for the 1942 WWII draft registration and dead by 2008. His second marriage (Mary Ellen "Nell" Giessen) and daughter Kathleen Cecelia "Kathy" Donahue (dates unknown; possibly living — not researched) suggest a post-divorce life possibly in Boston. Massachusetts death index 1942-2008 (Ancestry/AmericanAncestors) is the next step; Calvary Cemetery, Brockton and Boston Catholic cemeteries are candidate burials.
2. **Ellen's burial** — likely under "Ellen Card"; check Find a Grave (blocked to automated access this session) and Bridgewater/Brockton cemetery offices (Melrose Cemetery, Brockton, is where Brockton Swedes cluster).
3. **Mr. Card's identity** and the Lindquist and Card marriage dates — MA Marriage Index 1901-1955/1966-1970 (the latter already among the tree's cited collections, hinting the Card or Lindquist marriage may be the 1966-70 index entry).
4. **The divorce** — Plymouth County (Brockton) divorce records, 1920s, would document the end of the 1919 marriage.
5. **What the North Adams Transcript citation covers** — an odd western-Massachusetts newspaper source attached to Ellen; possibly a syndicated vital-records column or a mis-attached clipping. Worth opening in the Ancestry citation.
6. **John's parents' household** — Patrick and Ellen F. Donahue in Boston Wards 18/19 (1900/1910); extracting the full census households would yield John's siblings. Not reachable in open indexes this session.
7. **A search-snippet lead, unverified and conflicting**: one search-result snippet described an "Ellen J Lindquist, b. 31 Jul 1900, d. 4 Aug 1991, East Bridgewater, Plymouth, MA," with parents given as "Simon Anders Swanson Svensson and Anna Josephina Svenson" (matching this Ellen's parents). The parent names suggest it derives from an Ancestry member tree describing our Ellen, but its dates conflict with the tree's sourced 4 May 1900 / 1 Aug 1990 (Bridgewater). Could not be re-located or verified (source site Cloudflare-blocked). Treat as a compiled-tree variant to reconcile against the SSDI when accessible — noting again that the SSDI entry may sit under Card, not Lindquist.
8. **Ellen's siblings** — the Swanson/Svenson household in Easton (1900 census) and the Libanon (Holy Trinity) church books would name them; both are behind logins. The gen-5 dive on Simon Swanson & Anna Josephina Svenson should start there.
