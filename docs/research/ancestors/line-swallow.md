# The Swallow Line (Line 4) — Lineage Survey, Generations 7–12

## Overview

This is a surname-line survey of the Swallow ancestors, generations 7–12 (roughly 1676–1865), running from Robert William Swallow — a Yorkshire-born farmer/seaman who died at Grand River (Annandale), Prince Edward Island, Canada in 1865 — back through three claimed generations of West Riding Yorkshire Swallows to a William Swallow born about 1676 at Birstall. The tree data supplied for this line is thin: only four full person records exist across six generations, with generations 9 and 10 represented only as unlinked name stubs. The Prince Edward Island end of the line (gen. 7, Robert William Swallow and wife Catherine "Kate" Blackett) is corroborated by an independent compiled PEI genealogy; everything above that (Abraham Swallow, gen. 8, and all of gens. 9–12) has no independent corroboration found in this research pass and should be treated as unverified tree lore pending parish-register work.

**This is not a colonial New England/Nantucket line.** Unlike other surname lines in this family tree (Coffin, Bunker, Pond, etc.), the Swallow line is a 19th-century Yorkshire-to-Canada emigration, not a 17th-century emigration to Massachusetts. The "immigrant ancestor" for this line is Robert William Swallow himself, who appears to have emigrated from England to Prince Edward Island sometime before his eldest child's birth (c. 1820).

## Verified facts

- Robert Swallow (called "Robert William Swallow" in the tree), a seaman and farmer, was born c. 1790 in Yorkshire, England and died 21 April 1865 at Grand River, Lot 56 / Annandale, Prince Edward Island, Canada. — Source: *The Descendants of Robert Swallow and Kate Blackett*, The Island Register (islandregister.com), compiled PEI genealogy, https://www.islandregister.com/swallow.html
- His wife, Catherine "Kate" Blackett, was born c. 1786 and died 28 December 1867 at Grand River, Lot 56, Prince Edward Island. She was the daughter of John William Blackett and Diana Applecross/Applequest of Grand River, PEI. — Source: Island Register, swallow.html and blackett.html (https://www.islandregister.com/blackett.html)
- John Blackett (Kate's father), of Grand River, PEI, died by 1835; his will names children William (eldest son), George, Katherine, and Diana — it does not name a son-in-law Swallow, so it does not corroborate the marriage directly. — Source: "Will of John Blackett of Prince Edward Island (Grand River, -1835)," Island Register, https://www.islandregister.com/wills/johnblackett.html
- Robert and Kate (Blackett) Swallow had at least six children per the Island Register compilation: Robert Swallow (b. c.1820), William Swallow (b. c.1822), Mary Anne Swallow (b. Sept. 1829), Marie Swallow (b. c.1830), John "Black John" Swallow (b. c.1836), and one further unnamed child. The tree data in hand lists only two of these (Robert Jr. and William), so the tree's children list for this couple is incomplete, not necessarily wrong.
- The Blackett family of Grand River/Annandale were boatbuilders; William and John Blackett registered a boat named "Swallow" in 1849, built at the Grand River shipyard — an interesting, if circumstantial, namesake connection between the two families and the local maritime economy the Swallow-Blackett household lived in. — Source: "Blacketts of Prince Edward Island, Nova Scotia and Massachusetts," https://www.theblacketts.com/node/34
- Almondbury, Huddersfield, and Birstall — the three West Riding of Yorkshire locations named across generations 8, 11, and 12 of this line — are all genuinely neighboring parishes/townships in the historic wapentake of Agbrigg, within about 10 miles of one another, so a single interrelated Swallow family network across these places is geographically plausible. — Source: FamilySearch Wiki, "Almondbury, Yorkshire Genealogy," https://www.familysearch.org/en/wiki/Almondbury,_Yorkshire_Genealogy

## Corrections to the tree

- Tree gives Robert William Swallow's death place as "Annandale, Prince Edward Island, Canada." The independent Island Register compilation instead specifies "Grand River, Lot 56, Prince Edward Island." These are not necessarily in conflict — Annandale sits within/adjacent to the Grand River, Lot 56 area of Kings County, PEI, per the Island Register's own Annandale history page — but the tree's place name is less precise than the source record, and should ideally read "Grand River (Annandale), Lot 56, PEI." Evidence: https://www.islandregister.com/swallow.html and https://www.islandregister.com/1902annandale.html
- Tree omits Robert William Swallow's wife's death (28 Dec 1867, Grand River, PEI) and her parentage (daughter of John William Blackett and Diana Applecross/Applequest) entirely — not a "wrong" value, but a gap the tree should fill in. See New findings below.
- Tree's children list for Robert William Swallow and Catherine Blackett ("Robert (Jr.) Swallow Jr" and "William Swallow" only) is incomplete against the Island Register's fuller list of at least six children (Robert, William, Mary Anne, Marie, John "Black John," and one more unidentified). This is a completeness gap, not a factual error in what's listed.
- No independent source was found confirming "Pontefract, Yorkshire" as Robert William Swallow's specific birthplace; the only independent record found (Island Register) says simply "Yorkshire, England" with no town. This does not contradict the tree, but the town-level specificity in the tree is currently unsourced and unverified.

## New findings

- Catherine "Kate" Blackett's death date and place: 28 December 1867, Grand River, Lot 56, PEI (not in the supplied tree data at all).
- Catherine's parents: John William Blackett and Diana Applecross (also rendered Applequest), of Grand River, PEI; John Blackett's will (pre-1835) names Kate's siblings as William, George, and Diana.
- At least four additional children of Robert William Swallow and Catherine Blackett beyond the two in the tree: Mary Anne (b. Sept. 1829), Marie (b. c.1830), John "Black John" (b. c.1836), and one further unidentified child.
- The Blackett family (Kate's family) were PEI boatbuilders based at Grand River, connecting the Swallow household to the local shipbuilding economy of Kings County, PEI.

## Open questions

- **Where does the documented line actually end?** Independent corroboration was found only for generation 7 (Robert William Swallow) and, derivatively, his wife's Blackett family. No independent source (FamilySearch tree, WikiTree, findagrave, parish register transcription, or published local history) was found for Abraham Swallow (gen. 8), the unnamed "William Swallow" given as Abraham's father (gen. 9), Edmund Swallow (the implied gen. 10 link), James Swallow (gen. 11), or William Swallow of Birstall (gen. 12). All of generations 8–12 should be treated as **legend/unverified tree lore** pending direct parish-register research (Almondbury All Hallows, Huddersfield, and Birstall St Peter registers, held by West Yorkshire Archive Service) — none of these registers were accessible for direct lookup in this pass.
- **Missing generations 9 and 10 are not populated as full records in the supplied data.** The gen-9 "William SWALLOW" (Abraham's stated father) and the gen-10 "Edmund SWALLOW" (James Swallow's stated son) appear only as bare names attached to other people's parent/child fields — they have no birth/death dates, no sources, and no independent record connecting them to each other. As given, the chain from Abraham Swallow (gen. 8) up to James Swallow (gen. 11) and William Swallow of Birstall (gen. 12) is **not actually linked by any stated relationship** in this dataset; it is at best an assumed continuity based on shared surname and shared West Riding geography, not a sourced chain of custody from parent to child.
- **Possible duplicate/naming-collision artifact:** the name "William Swallow" appears twice in this line — once as an undated stub (gen. 9, father of Abraham) and once as a full record (gen. 12, b. abt. 1676, Birstall). Chronologically these cannot be the same person (a man born 1676 cannot be the father of a man born 1768; that gap requires the two intervening generations, James b.1696 and the unlisted gen-9/10 men, to be distinct individuals), so this is probably not a true duplicate-merge error, but the repeated name combined with the missing gen-9/10 records is exactly the kind of pattern that produces false-merge duplicates in unattended tree software. Whoever maintains this tree should verify that gen-9 "William Swallow" and gen-12 "William Swallow" are not, in fact, the same tree node erroneously split across two positions, and should add an actual sourced link (or an explicit "assumed" flag) connecting Abraham Swallow's ancestry to the Huddersfield/Birstall Swallows.
- No marriage record (date or place) was found for Robert William Swallow and Catherine Blackett; likely occurred in PEI given the wife's Blackett family was already established there.
- No ship, port, or date of emigration was found for Robert William Swallow's crossing from Yorkshire to Prince Edward Island. Given his first child's birth c. 1820, emigration likely occurred in the 1810s.
- "Hannah Hewood," Abraham Swallow's stated wife, is very likely a phonetic/transcription variant of the common West Riding surname Heywood or Haywood, but no baptism, marriage, or burial record under any spelling was found to confirm this identification or link her to Abraham Swallow.
