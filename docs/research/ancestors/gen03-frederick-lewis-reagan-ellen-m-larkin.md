# Frederick Lewis Reagan (1910–1978) & Ellen M. Larkin (1905–1995) — Generation 3

> Note on assignment: the orchestrator's subject line named "Howard Francis Donahue Sr & Mary Prosper," but the item file (A05.json) and its dossier path unambiguously contain this Reagan/Larkin couple. This dossier covers the couple in the data file.

## Overview

Frederick Lewis Reagan (also recorded as Regan/Reagan — his father appears in the tree as Dennis Francis **Regan** Sr) was a lifelong Brockton, Plymouth County, Massachusetts man, born there 4 Jan 1910 and dying there 11 Jan 1978. His wife Ellen M. Larkin was an immigrant with an unusually well-traveled early life: born 8 Nov 1905 reportedly in "Hamilton" (almost certainly Hamilton, Lanarkshire, Scotland — see Corrections), raised at Whitehaven, Cumberland, England (1911 census), emigrated via Quebec in 1921, crossed from Canada into the US at Bridgewater, Maine in 1929, worked in Belmont MA (1930) and Presque Isle ME (1935) before settling in Brockton, where she died 30 Mar 1995. Both are buried at Saint Michael's Cemetery, Avon, Norfolk County, Massachusetts (Ellen's burial there is documented; Frederick's is inferred).

## Corrections to the tree

- **"Hamilton, England" as Ellen's birthplace → almost certainly Hamilton, Lanarkshire, SCOTLAND.** There is no town of genealogical significance called Hamilton in England. The family's 1911 residence at Whitehaven, Cumberland (a west-coast coal port a short distance from Scotland) fits the very common two-way labor migration between the Lanarkshire coalfield (Hamilton/Motherwell) and the West Cumberland pits. Status: inferred correction — her Scottish statutory birth record (ScotlandsPeople, paywalled) would settle it; recommend searching Larkin births, Hamilton district, 1905-06.
- **Conflicting birth dates for Ellen (8 Nov 1905 / 13 Nov 1906 / 11 Jun 1910).** The 1905 date is the one carried by SSDI/death index and Find A Grave (1905–1995) and should be treated as primary; the 1906 and 1910 dates are almost certainly ages-off from the 1921 Form 30A and the 1929/1961 border-crossing or passenger documents. Recommend keeping 8 Nov 1905 as the preferred birth date and demoting the others to alternate-record notes.
- **Surname spelling**: the tree mixes Reagan/Regan (father is "Dennis Francis Regan Sr", subject is "Reagan"). Both spellings are genuine record variants for this family; the daughter's 2017 obituary and Ellen's gravestone entry use **Reagan**. No change needed, but researchers should search both forms (plus Ragan) in every index.

This Canadian interlude is a concrete research target (1921 Census of Canada, New Brunswick).

## Open questions

1. **Frederick Sr.'s grave**: no Find A Grave memorial found under Frederick Reagan/Regan (1910–1978) anywhere in Massachusetts. Check Saint Michael's Cemetery (Avon) office records and Brockton's Calvary Cemetery; a memorial may simply never have been created, or exists without dates.
2. **Frederick Sr.'s death (11 Jan 1978)** is unsourced in the tree and was not independently verifiable on the open web. The Massachusetts Death Index 1970-2003 (Ancestry) and a Brockton Enterprise obituary (GenealogyBank/Newspapers.com, Jan 1978) should both cover it.
3. **The 1939 England & Wales Register entry (Cumberland)**: if genuinely Ellen, she was back in England in Sept 1939 and re-crossed the Atlantic in wartime to appear in the April 1940 census at Weymouth, MA — possible but tight. Verify the 1939 register entry really matches (right birth date 8 Nov 1905) rather than another Ellen Larkin; likewise the unsourced 4 Nov 1961 Boston arrival (a return from a family visit?).
4. **Marriage date/place**: not in the tree. Kathleen's 1943 birth suggests a ca. 1941–42 Brockton marriage; the tree's cited Massachusetts Marriage Index 1901-1955 and Newspapers.com Marriage Index entries should yield the exact date.
5. **Ellen's parents Thomas Larkin & Mary Blackmore**: the 1911 Whitehaven census household (cited in tree) would give Thomas's occupation (likely coal miner), birthplaces (Ireland? Scotland?), and Ellen's siblings — worth extracting in full. Blackmore is an unusual pairing (typically a Devon/English surname) and worth verifying against the original image.
6. **What Ellen was doing in Belmont, MA in 1930** (1930 census, sourced in tree) — very likely a live-in domestic servant; the census image would confirm occupation and employer household.
7. **Frederick Sr.'s occupation and WWII draft card details** (card sourced in tree, 1942 Brockton) — the card image (Ancestry/Fold3) would give employer, physical description, and exact address.
8. **Dennis Francis Regan Sr & Emeline Jane Murphy (Frederick's parents)**: no open-web traces found in this pass; Brockton city directories and the 1910–1930 censuses under Regan/Reagan are the next step (separate gen-4 dossier).
