# Joseph Elwell Pedrick (1834–1913) & Mary E. Rankins (1847–1881)

Generation 5 direct ancestors. Wheelwright of Pedricktown, Oldmans Township, Salem County, New Jersey, and his wife, an orphan of Pedricktown.

> Note on assignment: the research tasking labeled this item "Josiah B Albertson & Susan Vanneman Turner," but the tree data file (A17.json) and this dossier path both identify the couple as Joseph Elwell Pedrick & Mary E. Rankins. This dossier covers the Pedrick/Rankins couple per the data file. (The Albertson connection is real but one generation down: their daughter Maria Rankin Pedrick married Edward Shone Albertson — see New findings.)

## Overview

Joseph Elwell Pedrick (24 Apr 1834 – 5 Jul 1913) was a seventh-generation descendant of Roger Pedrick, the 1682 founder of Pedricktown. He succeeded his father Francis Augustus Pedrick in the family wheelwright and wagon-building shop on Railroad Avenue, Pedricktown. He married Mary E. Rankins of Pedricktown (b. 1847), an orphan raised by Joseph Weatherby after her parents died young. Mary died in 1881 aged 34, leaving a large family; both are buried in Pedricktown Friends Cemetery. The couple is documented in remarkable detail in Hubert B. Shoemaker's 1938 published genealogy of the Pedrick family, which states its data was checked against "Trenton vital statistics and cem. stones."

## Verified facts

- **Joseph's birth 24 Apr 1834** — confirmed exactly: "7 JOSEPH ELWELL PEDRICK born 4-24-1834" (Shoemaker, *Pedrick Family*, 1938, p. 95). Tree date matches. (Birth *place* is questioned below.)
- **Joseph's death 5 Jul 1913** — confirmed exactly: "died 7-5-1913 age 79" (Shoemaker, p. 95). Cemetery transcription agrees: "Pedrick, Joseph E., b. 1834, d. 1913," Pedricktown Friends Cemetery (interment.net transcription).
- **Marriage to Mary E. Rankins** — confirmed: "married MARY E. RANKINS of Pedricktown" (Shoemaker, p. 95). Marriage date/place not given in any source found.
- **Mary's birth 1847** — confirmed: "She was born 1847 died 1881 age 34" (Shoemaker, p. 95); gravestone transcription "Pedrick, Mary E., b. 1847, d. 1881" (interment.net, Pedricktown Friends Cemetery). Tree's birthplace "Pedricktown" is consistent with the book's "of Pedricktown," though that phrase strictly describes residence.
- **Mary's death 1881** — confirmed by both the Shoemaker genealogy and the gravestone transcription (above). Death place not stated in sources; burial at Pedricktown fills the tree's blank.
- **Joseph's parents Francis Augustus Pedrick and Jane Ann Pierson** — confirmed: "6 FRANCIS AUGUSTUS PEDRICK ... married JANE ANN PIERSON" with Joseph Elwell Pedrick listed first among their issue (Shoemaker, pp. 94–95). Francis: b. 1 May 1810, d. 4 Nov 1895, age 85; Jane Ann Pierson: b. 1814, d. 12 Nov 1905, age 91.
- **Daughter Maria Rankin Pedrick** — confirmed: first listed child, "Maria Rankin Pedrick, born 11-23-1861 [year uncertain, see Open questions], died 1931, age 69, buried Trenton" (Shoemaker, p. 95).
- **Occupation: wheelwright** — "He succeeded his father Francis to the wheelwright business in Pedricktown" (Shoemaker, p. 95).
- **Burial: both in Pedricktown (Friends) Cemetery, Railroad Avenue, Pedricktown** — Shoemaker ("both buried at Pedricktown", buried column "Pedtn.") and interment.net transcription of Pedricktown Friends Cemetery.

## Corrections to the tree

- **Joseph's birthplace "Upper Penns Neck, Salem, NJ" → probably Woodbury, Gloucester County, NJ (or possibly Lower Penns Neck).** Evidence: Shoemaker states Joseph's father Francis "early went to Woodbury and learned the business of wheelwright with his uncle Joseph D. Pedrick, got married and opened a shop near the canal. He then came to Pedricktown about 1840" (p. 94). Joseph (b. 1834) and his brother John (b. 1835) were therefore born during the Woodbury years. The ancestral Pedrick farm of grandfather John Pedrick was in *Lower* Penns Neck (p. 69), not Upper Penns Neck. Secondary evidence only — flag, don't silently overwrite.
- **Mary's parents "William Rankin" and "Maria Rankin" → unsupported; treat as unverified.** Shoemaker says only that Mary was "of Pedricktown whose parents died young and she was raised by Joseph Weatherby" (p. 95) and names no parents. A mother whose maiden name equals her married name ("Maria Rankin") looks like a placeholder. The daughter's name "Maria Rankin Pedrick" suggests Mary's mother may indeed have been named Maria, but no record naming the parents was found.
- **Children: tree lists only Maria Rankin Pedrick → the couple had ten children** (see New findings).
- **Joseph's death place "Jefferson Hospital, Philadelphia" — neither confirmed nor contradicted.** Shoemaker gives only the date; no obituary located. Leave as unverified tree data.

## New findings

**The family's Pedrick line (per Shoemaker):** Roger Pedrick (c.1615 England – 1692, whose 1,000-acre 1682 survey became Pedricktown) → John → Jacob → Jacob Jr. → John Pedrick (m. 1801 Elizabeth Elwell, 1781–1862, of Lower Penns Neck — the source of the "Elwell" middle name) → Francis Augustus Pedrick → Joseph Elwell Pedrick.

**Joseph's father's business, which Joseph inherited:** Francis A. Pedrick trained as a wheelwright in Woodbury under his uncle Joseph D. Pedrick, moved to Pedricktown about 1840 where his father bought him a shop; the shop standing in 1938 was built about 1877 on Railroad (R.R.) Avenue next to the Methodist Episcopal Church. Joseph ran it after Francis; Joseph's son Henry Lawrence Pedrick ran it after him (still operating it in 1938). Three generations of Pedricktown wheelwrights.

**The ten children of Joseph and Mary (Shoemaker, p. 95, statuses as of 1938; cemetery dates from interment.net Pedricktown Friends transcription):**

1. **Maria Rankin Pedrick** (b. 23 Nov 1861 per book [year questionable], d. 1931, buried Greenwood Cemetery, Trenton) — married **Edward Shone Albertson of Haddonfield**, who worked at the Roebling wire mills in Trenton. Daughter Emma Frances Albertson m. Oliver T. Cadwalader of Trenton (d. 1918, buried Riverview, Penns Grove); granddaughter Helen Cadwalader (b. 1908) m. 1933 Harry Datz of Mullica Hill. **This is the tree owner's Albertson connection.**
2. **Jane Ann Pedrick** (1863 – 6 Apr 1916), single; buried Pedricktown Friends ("Jane A. Pedrick, 1863–1916"). Named for her grandmother Jane Ann Pierson.
3. **Francis Augustus Pedrick** (1865 – 3 Mar 1927) — wheelwright in Philadelphia, 30 years with one firm; m. Louise Martin; both buried North Cedar Hill Cemetery, Philadelphia (Sec. E, Lot 162); no children.
4. **Charles Vanderbilt Pedrick** (b. 1 Oct 1867, living 1938) — carpenter with the Pennsylvania Railroad 36 years; m. 1894 Sallie Frances Lloyd of Greenwood, Delaware (b. 10 Aug 1874); lived 508 Lakeview Ave., Pitman, NJ. Daughter Addie Leap Pedrick (b. 1899) m. Raymond E. Woodward, baker of Pitman.
5. **Henry Lawrence Pedrick** (b. 1869, living 1938; cemetery: 1869–1956) — carried on the family wheelwright shop in Pedricktown; unmarried.
6. **Ida Pedrick** (b. 1870, died in infancy, buried Pedricktown).
7. **Margaret Rankins Pedrick** (b. 1872, living 1938; cemetery: "Margaret R. Pedrick, 1872–1957") — never married; kept house in Pedricktown for her brother Henry.
8. **Walter Springer Pedrick** (1876 – 24 Dec 1902, single; cemetery: "Walter S. Pedrick, 1876–1902").
9. **Harvey Lewis Pedrick** (1878 – 6 May 1901, single; cemetery: "Harvey L. Pedrick, 1878–1901").
10. **Randolph Pedrick** (died in infancy).

Note that Mary died in 1881 with at least seven children under 18; the youngest survivors were raised in Pedricktown (Henry, Margaret, Walter, Harvey all appear on Pedricktown Friends stones).

**Joseph's siblings (children of Francis A. Pedrick & Jane Ann Pierson, Shoemaker pp. 94, 96–97):**
- **John Pedrick** (1835–1910, buried Hillside Cemetery, Philadelphia) — wagon builder, then a Philadelphia policeman under Mayors Stokley, King and Smith; m. Mary Elwell of Salem County.
- **Charles Pedrick** (24 Feb 1840 – 22 Jan 1889, buried Penns Grove) — vessel pilot who ran the produce boats "Richmond" and "Anna & Mary" between Pedricktown and Philadelphia carrying melons and sweet potatoes; m. Danelia Gandy of Tuckahoe (1849–1888). He died intestate and **his brother Joseph administered the estate** (a probate record trail for Joseph E. Pedrick, Salem County, 1889).
- **Emma L. Pedrick** (d. 1912, buried West Laurel Hill) — m. James Luke, stationary engineer and later restaurant keeper in Trenton.
- **Francis Augustus Pedrick Jr.** (9 May 1851 – 3 May 1853), buried Pedricktown.

**Cemetery context:** Pedricktown Friends Cemetery, east side of Railroad Avenue south of town, is divided into "Friends" and "others" sections; the sign says 1862 but older graves exist (interment.net; Find a Grave cemetery has 559 memorials). The Quaker cemetery burial is consistent with the Pedricks' Quaker origins (Roger Pedrick's line), though the family by this era was associated with the M.E. Church next to their shop — which section the couple lies in is not recorded in the transcription.

## Open questions

- **Maria Rankin Pedrick's birth year vs. her mother's.** The book prints Maria b. 11-23-1861 and d. 1931 "age 69" (internally consistent with ~1862), yet Mary E. Rankins b. 1847 would then have borne her at 14. Either Mary was born earlier than her gravestone states, or Maria was born later (e.g., 1866) and both book figures are off. The 1870 and 1880 censuses of Oldmans Township (not accessible without a subscription in this session) would settle both women's ages, as would Maria's Trenton death record (1931).
- **Marriage date and place of Joseph and Mary** — not found; likely Salem County, c. 1860–1866; NJ state marriage indexes (Trenton) would resolve.
- **Who were Mary's parents?** "Parents died young; raised by Joseph Weatherby" (Shoemaker). The tree's William/Maria Rankin needs a primary source — 1850/1860 census of Oldmans Twp. should show a Rankin(s) household and later a Weatherby household containing Mary.
- **Did Joseph die at Jefferson Hospital, Philadelphia?** Plausible (his brother and son lived in Philadelphia; Jefferson took regional patients) but unverified. A Philadelphia or Salem County death certificate (5 Jul 1913) or a *Penns Grove Record* obituary (the paper existed in 1913) would confirm.
- **An Ancestry user-tree hit claims a daughter "Margaret Ireland Pedrick" (b. 29 Apr 1876 Camden, d. 8 Jul 1881 Penns Grove) of Joseph Elwell Pedrick and Mary E. Rankins.** This conflicts with Shoemaker (Margaret Rankins Pedrick b. 1872; Walter b. 1876; no Camden residence). Treat as legend/probable conflation unless a vital record surfaces — though the 1881 child-death date coinciding with Mary's own 1881 death is curious enough to check in NJ death registrations.
- **Census households 1840–1910** were not directly examined (index sites requiring login). Expected: 1840 Woodbury or Pedricktown (in Francis's household); 1850–1860 Oldmans Twp. with Francis; 1870–1910 Oldmans Twp., own household, occupation wheelwright; 1900–1910 widower, likely with children Henry and Margaret.
