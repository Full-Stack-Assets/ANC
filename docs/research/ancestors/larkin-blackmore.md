# Ellen Larkin's mother: the Blackmore chimera refuted

Deep-dive verification, 2026-07-03. Method: live FreeBMD index queries (births,
marriages, deaths — coverage essentially complete 1904–1912), Ramsgate parish
transcripts, Maine marriage index, Whitehaven street gazetteers.

## What fell

- **No Larkin × Blackmore marriage exists in England & Wales, 1885–1911.** The
  recorded wife "Mary Blackmore" is a five-way merge of different women (baptisms
  1852–1886 in Devon, Wigan, and elsewhere; unrelated London/Manchester censuses).
- **The 25 Dec 1905 "St Lukes Ramsgate" wedding is another bride's**: Mary Ellen
  Blackmore of Elham district (Folkestone) married Elliott or Sneddon (GRO 2a/2421).
  No Thomas Larkin married in Thanet that quarter.
- **Ellen's birth is corrected to ~13 Nov 1906 at Whitehaven** — GRO registration
  Mar Q 1907, Whitehaven 10b/689, the only Ellen Larkin registered in Cumberland
  1904–1912 (and none in all of E&W Dec 1905–Dec 1906). "Hamilton, England" is a
  Whitehaven street (Hamilton Terrace/Lane, Corkickle), not a town.

## What stands

- **Probable brother**: Samuel Larkin, b. Jun Q 1903, Whitehaven 10b/733 — the only
  other Larkin birth in the district 1902–1916.
- **The relevant death hiding in the chimera**: Mary Ellen **Larkin**, aged 42, Mar Q
  1906, Whitehaven 10b/429 (not Lancashire, not Blackmore) — possibly Thomas's wife;
  if so, Ellen (b. Nov 1906) was a later partner's child. No Thomas Larkin remarriage
  registered in Cumberland 1906–1911, and no Thomas Larkin marriage in Cumberland at
  all 1880–1911 — the couple married elsewhere (Ireland, Scotland, or pre-1892 Maine).
- Thomas's reconciled arc: Liverpool/Ireland ~1860-64 → Benedicta, Aroostook Co.
  Maine (Irish Catholic colony) 1877 → England → Whitehaven by 1903 → military 1914 →
  Howe Brook, Maine 1918 → d. 1920 Maine. Ellen, ~14, followed via Quebec in 1921.

## The one lookup that settles it

A **free** GRO account (gro.gov.uk → index search) on *Births, Mar Q 1907, Whitehaven
10b/689, LARKIN Ellen* shows the **mother's maiden name**. Prediction: not Blackmore.
Run the same lookup on Samuel (Jun Q 1903, 10b/733): matching maiden names = same
mother; "LARKIN" in the column = registered illegitimate (later-partner scenario).
Backups: Ellen's SS application (US, mother's maiden name), the 1911 Whitehaven census
image, Mary Ellen Larkin's 1906 death certificate, St Begh's RC Whitehaven baptisms.
