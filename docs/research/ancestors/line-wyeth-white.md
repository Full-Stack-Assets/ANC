# The (Wyeth) White Line — Lineage Survey (Generations 11–12)

## Overview

The tree file for this "surname line" contains only **two distinct individuals**, each duplicated once: Phillip (Wyeth) White (b. 1662, d. 1743) and his purported father John (Wyeth) White (b. 1642, d. 1684). No generation-7-through-10 (or earlier) ancestors are present in the supplied data despite the task description implying a longer 1600s–1700s chain — the documented/undocumented chain in this file **stops at generation 12** (John White) with no further upstream links recorded. Web research shows the tree's "John (Wyeth) White" record is very likely a **conflation of two unrelated colonial John Whites** (Hartford's "Elder John White" and "John White of Maine," a Sheepscot/Kennebec settler), and that Phillip White's real parentage more plausibly runs through the latter, unsourced, man. The "(Wyeth)" tag on this surname does not correspond to any known genealogical Wyeth-White marriage found in research — it appears to be an internal tree/software label rather than a documented fact.

## Verified facts

- A real, well-documented "Elder John White" of Hartford, Connecticut existed: born c. 1597 in Messing, Essex, England; emigrated to Massachusetts on the ship *Lyon*, arriving Boston 16 Sept 1632; settled first in Cambridge, then moved to Hartford, CT with Rev. Thomas Hooker's company c. 1636; married Mary Levett (Levitt) 26 Dec 1622 in Messing, Essex; served as Hartford selectman in 1642, 1646, 1651, 1656; ordained Ruling Elder of Hartford's Second Church, March 1677; died before/on 23 Jan 1683/84 in Hartford. — Source: Founders of Hartford biography (foundersofhartford.org) and WikiTree White-548.
- A separate, real "John White of Maine" is documented in the *Genealogical Dictionary of Maine and New Hampshire* (Noyes, Libby & Davis, 1928–39; Archive.org id `genealogicaldict0000noye`): he and James Phipps jointly bought Jeremisquam Neck (Kennebec, Maine) in 1646; after Phipps' death, White married Phipps' widow Mary (deed dated 4 Oct 1679); he died at Sheepscot after the First Indian War (King Philip's War, 1675–78); his eight children included Peter (b. 1653), John (b. 1655), Benjamin, and **Phillip (b. 1662)**. — Source: Genealogical Dictionary of Maine and New Hampshire, as excerpted via rootsweb (sites.rootsweb.com/~walkersj/JohnWhite.htm) and Geni "John White, of Maine."
- A Phillip White born c. 1662 at Jeremisquam Neck, Kennebec (now Woolwich/Newcastle area), Maine, and living/dying in the Newton/Dedham, Massachusetts area matching the tree's dates (d. 1743/1744) is independently attested on FamilySearch ("Philip White 1662–1744," ancestors.familysearch.org/en/L65Y-NW7) and referenced via Geni.
- A compiled tree (Geneanet, "WHITE (lines)," user tdowling) records a Philip White (1661/1662–1743) married to Deborah Mansfield (1663/1665–1722) in 1685, consistent with the tree's stated spouse "Deborah Mansfield."
- Geni's profile for "Andrew White (1694–1770)," born 9 Sept 1694 in Beverly, Essex, MA, died 30 Mar 1770 in Dudley, Worcester, MA, lists his father as "Philip White, of Maine" and his wife as Mary Woodbury — consistent with the tree's child "Andrew WHITE" of Phillip (Wyeth) White.

## Corrections to the tree

- **John (Wyeth) White's birth year "1642" and birthplace "Shalford, Colchester, Essex, England" → almost certainly wrong or misattributed.** If this person is meant to be Hartford's Elder John White (whose death date, 23 Jan 1684, and death place, Hartford, CT, exactly match the tree entry), his actual birth year was c. 1597–98 in Messing, Essex (not 1642), and his wife was Mary Levett, not Mary Phillips. Evidence: Founders of Hartford biography; WikiTree White-548. It is plausible "1642" is a transcription error for the year he was first chosen Hartford selectman (1642), mistakenly entered as a birth date — this is an inference, not confirmed.
- **Spouse "Mary Phillips" → likely incorrect for either candidate father.** Elder John White of Hartford's wife is documented as Mary Levett/Levitt. "John White of Maine" married Mary, widow of James Phipps — her maiden name is not given in the sources found. No source found supports a wife surnamed "Phillips" for either man.
- **Implied parentage (Phillip White's father = the Hartford-death-dated "John White") → more plausibly wrong.** Chronologically, Elder John White's family emigrated from England in 1632; no child of that family could have been born in Shalford, Essex in 1642, ten years after emigration. By contrast, "John White of Maine" is documented to have had a son "Phillip, born 1662" — a far better fit for the tree's Phillip White (b. 1662). The tree's link from Phillip White up to a Hartford-based father is likely a mismerge of two unrelated John Whites who share only a first and last name.
- **Duplicate records in the tree.** The supplied data contains Phillip (Wyeth) White twice (IDs I182541959549 and I182541966184) and John (Wyeth) White twice (IDs I182541959569 and I182541966203), with identical names, dates, places, spouses, and children in each pair. These are unmerged duplicate-record artifacts, not two different people, and should be merged in the tree.
- **The "(Wyeth)" designation is not a documented surname link.** No source found ties either White individual to the (unrelated, well-documented) Wyeth family of Cambridge, MA (immigrant ancestor Nicholas Wyeth, c. 1600–1680, of Saxtead, Suffolk). "(Wyeth) White" is most likely an internal tree-software label (e.g., marking a branch or distinguishing this White line from another White line elsewhere in the same tree) rather than a genealogical fact. Treat it as a label, not a lineage claim, pending clarification from the tree's own documentation.

## New findings (not in the tree)

- Phillip White's likely real father, per published Maine colonial genealogy, is "John White of Maine" — a Kennebec-region settler and land partner of James Phipps (whose family produced Sir William Phips, first royal governor of Massachusetts) — not the Hartford, CT "Elder John White." This is a plausible/inferred correction, not the tree's current claim, and needs primary-source confirmation.
- Phillip White was very likely born at Jeremisquam Neck, Kennebec (coastal Maine, in the present Woolwich/Wiscasset/Newcastle area), not at Newton, Middlesex, Massachusetts as the tree states. He appears to have relocated south to the Newton/Dedham, Massachusetts area later in life — consistent with the family's forced abandonment of the Maine frontier after the Indian wars of the 1670s–90s, though this migration reasoning is inference, not sourced.
- A likely son of Phillip White, Andrew White (b. 9 Sept 1694, Beverly, Essex Co., MA; d. 30 Mar 1770, Dudley, Worcester Co., MA), married Mary Woodbury — matching the tree's listed child "Andrew WHITE" but adding names, dates, and places absent from the tree. (Compiled/secondary source — Geni.)
- The Genealogical Dictionary of Maine and New Hampshire (Noyes, Libby, Davis) is available for research at Archive.org (`archive.org/details/genealogicaldict0000noye`) and is the standard published reference for verifying "John White of Maine" and his descendants.

## Open questions

- Is the tree's "John (Wyeth) White" meant to represent Elder John White of Hartford, "John White of Maine," or a third, still-unidentified man? The current record's data (birth year/place vs. death date/place) appears to blend two different people and cannot be resolved without seeing what source(s) the compiler originally used.
- What is the actual documentary basis (if any) for "Mary Phillips" as a wife's name? No period source located names either candidate father's wife as a Phillips.
- Is there a primary source (Dedham or Newton vital records, probate, deed) directly connecting Phillip White (1662–1743) of Newton/Dedham to Deborah Mansfield and to a son Andrew White? Only secondary/compiled trees (Geneanet, Geni, FamilySearch user tree) were found; no primary vital record, deed, or probate record was directly examined.
- Where does the "(Wyeth)" surname tag originate in the source tree software — is it a cross-reference to another line, a maiden-name note, or a merge artifact? This should be checked against the original tree/database rather than assumed.
- Why does the supplied tree data for this "line" stop at generation 12 with no ancestors of John White listed? Either the upstream generations were never entered, or they exist elsewhere in the tree under a different line file — worth checking the tree's other line exports for a continuation.
