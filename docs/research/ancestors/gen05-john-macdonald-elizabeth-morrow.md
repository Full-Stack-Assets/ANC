# John Macdonald (1842?–1876) & Elizabeth Morrow (c. 1831–1901)

Generation 5 direct ancestors. John Macdonald, a Main-à-Dieu (Cape Breton, Nova Scotia) man from a mariner's family, married Elizabeth Morrow, a Prince Edward Island-born housemaid roughly eleven years his senior. John died young at Main-à-Dieu in November 1876; the widowed Elizabeth ended her days about 1901 in Mattapan (Boston), Massachusetts, where their son Russell — who carried the Morrow surname — had settled. His civil death registration survives in the Nova Scotia registers and was located and read in the original during this research.

## Overview

- **John Macdonald** — b. Main-à-Dieu, Cape Breton Co., NS (tree: 30 Nov 1842); d. 24 Nov 1876, Main-à-Dieu, of heart disease, aged 34, married. Son of Laughlan Macdonald (a mariner) and Susan(nah) Macdonald (tree: Susannah Joanna LeVatte).
- **Elizabeth Morrow** — b. about 1831, Prince Edward Island (tree says Vernon River; the compiled Morrow genealogy places her family at East Point / North Lake, Kings Co.); occupation housemaid; religion Christian Presbyterian; d. about 1901, Mattapan, Suffolk Co., Massachusetts. Daughter of John (III) Morrow and Margaret Coffin (tree's "Harriet Coffin" appears to be an error — see Corrections).
- **Child:** Russell William McDonald Morrow, b. abt 1871 (used the surname Morrow).

## Verified facts

- **John McDonald died 24 Nov 1876 at Main-à-Dieu** — Nova Scotia death registration, Cape Breton County 1876, Book 1803, page 142, entry no. 219 (register image read directly). Confirms the tree's death date and place exactly. [NS Archives record ID 7217]
- **Register details (same entry):** male, age 34, married, born Main-a-Dieu; parents "Laughlan [and] Susan McDonald," father's occupation **Mariner**; certified cause of death **Heart Disease**; informant Hector McDonald; registered at Main-a-Dieu by deputy registrar Geo. Rigby.
  - Confirms: father Laughlan (tree ✓), mother Susan/Susannah (tree ✓, maiden name not stated in register), birthplace Main-à-Dieu (tree ✓), and that he was married at death (consistent with wife Elizabeth surviving him).
- **A Laughlan McDonald household (5 males, 5 females) appears in the 1860–61 census of Main-à-Dieu** — transcription of the 1860-61 Nova Scotia census (heads of household only) by Tony McNeil, capebretongenweb.com (retrieved via Wayback Machine). John, then ~18, would be among the 5 males. A **Catherine Levatte** household (3m/3f) and a Hector McDonald household are enumerated a few lines away — the LeVatte family and the eventual death-record informant were near neighbours.
- **Elizabeth Morrow #1421, b. about 1831, dau. of John (III) Morrow; m. John MacDonald; occupation Housemaid; religion "C. Presbyterian"; died about 1901, Mattapan, Massachusetts** — compiled genealogy "The Descendants of John Morrow" (Island Register, submitted by Fulton Underhay). Compiled/secondary, but matches the tree's dates and Mattapan death independently.
- **Son: "Russell William McDonald Morrow #3151, b. Abt. 1871"** — same compiled Morrow genealogy. Confirms the tree's child "Russell Morrow" and explains the Morrow surname: the son of John MacDonald and Elizabeth Morrow was recorded under his mother's family name.
- **The LeVatte family belonged to Main-à-Dieu/Louisbourg** — Fortress of Louisbourg research pages (krausehouse.ca): "The Levatte family came from Main-a-Dieu"; H.C.V. LeVatte of Louisbourg was Warden of Cape Breton County c. 1906; numerous LeVatte vital registrations at Louisbourg and Main-à-Dieu 1874–1917 in the NS registers. Supports (though does not prove) the tree's LeVatte maiden name for John's mother.

## Corrections to the tree

- **Elizabeth's mother "Harriet Coffin" → almost certainly "Margaret Coffin."** The compiled Morrow genealogy states John (III) Morrow (b. 17 Jul 1792, East Point PEI; d. 20 Aug 1872, North Lake PEI) "m. 4 Jan 1815, Margaret Coffin," and lists Elizabeth b. abt 1831 among their children. A **Harriet Coffin** (b. c. 1795, Lot 38, dau. of Elisha Coffin and Jane Robbins) existed but married **James Morrow** — a different couple in the same extended PEI Morrow/Coffin network — which is the likely source of the tree's conflation. (Secondary evidence; both from Island Register compilations.)
- **Elizabeth's birthplace "Vernon River" is doubtful.** Her father's family was consistently of **East Point / North Lake, Kings County, PEI** (father born East Point 1792, died North Lake 1872; brother William born East Point). Vernon River is in Queens County, ~60 km away, and nothing found supports it. Suggest changing to "East Point area, Kings Co., PEI" or "Prince Edward Island" until a baptism is found.
- **Minor — John's birth date:** the register gives his age as 34 at death on 24 Nov 1876. If born 30 Nov 1842 (tree), he was still 33, six days short of 34. The age is probably just rounded, but a birth in ~Nov 1841–Nov 1842 is what the record supports; the exact 30 Nov 1842 date remains unsourced.

## New findings (not in the tree)

- **Cause of death: heart disease, at Main-à-Dieu** — dying at home aged 34. Notably, the compiled Morrow genealogy instead says of John MacDonald "d. Burnt at Sea." The official register contradicts this (or the "burnt at sea" story attaches to some other event/person). Treat "burnt at sea" as **family legend**; the civil registration is authoritative for the 24 Nov 1876 death.
- **Laughlan Macdonald's occupation: Mariner** (from his son's death registration).
- **Deeper Morrow ancestry (compiled, Island Register):**
  - Father: John (III) Morrow, b. 17 Jul 1792 East Point PEI, d. 20 Aug 1872 North Lake PEI; m. 4 Jan 1815 Margaret Coffin.
  - Grandparents: John (Jr.) Morrow and first wife **Eunice Coffin, b. 7 Jul 1766, Nantucket, Massachusetts** — a link into the Nantucket Coffin whaling families who resettled at East Point, PEI. (John Jr.'s second wife was Hepsibah Coffin, dau. of Uriah Coffin & Hepsabeth Bunker.)
  - Great-grandfather: John Morrow, b. Ireland; his wife d. 25 Jan 1815.
  - Elizabeth's siblings: John (IV) (b. abt 1819), James, David George (b. abt 1827), Artemas (b. 27 Feb 1835), Joseph (d. after 1872), and William Morrow (b. East Point).
- **Possible elder kin at Main-à-Dieu:** deaths registered at "Mainadieu" in 1867 — John McDonald, 60, widower, b. Scotland (d. 5 Mar 1867, consumption) and Ellen McDonald, 79, married, b. Scotland (d. 7 Feb 1867, old age) — NS deaths Book 1803, p. 17, nos. 119–120. Right age bracket to be Laughlan's parents' generation; unproven, worth pursuing.
- **Negative results worth recording:**
  - No NS marriage registration found for John McDonald × Elizabeth Morrow (bride-surname Morrow search across Cape Breton Co., 1864–1946, returns nothing matching). They may have married in PEI or the marriage went unregistered.
  - No NS birth registration for Russell (~1871): no McDonald births at all registered at Main-à-Dieu 1864–1877 (compliance there was poor).
  - No deaths for Laughlan or Susan(nah) McDonald at Main-à-Dieu in the registers — NS civil death registration lapsed **1877–1908** outside Halifax, so they most likely died in that gap.
  - Other Main-à-Dieu McDonald records checked and excluded: Mary Jane McDonald d. 1875 (parents Malcolm & Catharine — different family); Euphemia McDonald m. John Martell 1877 (parents Angus & Sarah); Margaret McDonald m. George Shaw 1877 (parents Norman & Mary) — none are children of Laughlan & Susan.

## Open questions

- Marriage of John and Elizabeth (c. 1865–1871): PEI church registers (East Point area was Presbyterian — consistent with Elizabeth's "C. Presbyterian") or Cape Breton Anglican/Presbyterian registers at Main-à-Dieu.
- Elizabeth's exact 1901 death: Massachusetts Vital Records (Deaths 1841–1915, on FamilySearch/Ancestry — login required, not searchable in this session) should give an exact date, address in Mattapan, parents' names, and burial place. Her death record naming her father would settle the Margaret-vs-Harriet Coffin question.
- Russell William McDonald Morrow (b. ~1871): Boston-area census (1900+), marriage, and death; he is the bridge generation and the reason the family surname continued as Morrow.
- John's baptism (~1842): Main-à-Dieu-area church records (St. Mary's Anglican or Catholic mission registers), which predate civil registration.
- Proof of Susannah's LeVatte maiden name: her own death/burial record, or a marriage record for Laughlan McDonald × Susannah LeVatte (pre-1864, so church registers only).
- 1871 and 1881 Canadian censuses at Main-à-Dieu (Library and Archives Canada; index searchable via FamilySearch/Ancestry with login) — should show John & Elizabeth with young Russell (1871) and the widowed Elizabeth (1881) before her move to Boston.
