## Overview

Howard F. "Jack" Donahue Sr. was born 5 July 1919 in Brockton, Massachusetts, son of John P. Donahue and Ellen (Swanson) Donahue (who later remarried, becoming Ellen Lindquist and finally Ellen Card). A lifelong transportation man — candy-company truck driver, then Brockton Area Transit bus driver — he spent the WWII years in the Civilian Conservation Corps building roads in New Hampshire, and died 8 October 2008 at Morton Hospital, Taunton, MA, aged 89, a resident of Raynham. The tree's "Mary Donahue" (b. abt 1918, his wife in the 1950 Brockton census) was his earlier wife; at his death he was married to Lillian E. (Burr)(Peluso) Donahue. Mary's maiden name and fate remain the chief open question.

## Corrections to the tree

- **Spouse "Mary Donahue" was not his only (or final) wife.** At death in 2008 his wife was **Lillian E. (Burr)(Peluso) Donahue** (b. 8 Jun 1926, Holbrook, MA; d. 18 Oct 2012, Providence, RI; buried Coweeset Cemetery, Brockton; daughter of Roy and Ethel (Phillips) Burr; widow of Joseph Peluso Jr.). Mary (b. abt 1918), the 1950-census wife, is an earlier marriage. The obituary does not mention Mary at all — not even as "the late" — which hints at divorce rather than widowhood, though this is inference only. Evidence: Howard's 2008 obituary; Lillian's 2012 obituary.
- **Child "Denis Donahue" → "Dennis A. Donahue"** per the 2008 obituary (which places him in Arizona with wife Pat).
- **Birthplace can be refined**: "Massachusetts" → **Brockton, Plymouth Co., Massachusetts** (obituary).
- **Death place (blank in tree)**: **Taunton, Bristol Co., Massachusetts** (Morton Hospital), while residing in Raynham (obituary).
- **Missing father**: the tree lists only Ellen Josephine Swanson as parent; his father was **John P. Donahue** (obituary; predeceased him).

## New findings

- **Nickname "Jack"** — used in the obituary headline ("Howard F 'Jack' Donahue, Sr.").
- **Father: John P. Donahue; stepfather: George Lindquist.** Mother Ellen's chain of surnames — (Swanson) (Donohue) (Lindquist) Card — implies at least three marriages: Donahue/Donohue, Lindquist, and a final Card marriage. John P. Donahue evidently died or left the family early enough for Ellen to remarry Lindquist and raise Howard partly as a Lindquist stepson.
- **Siblings** (from obituary): sister **Phyllis Richards** (surviving in 2008); sister **Pauline Enos** (predeceased); half-brother **George "Link" Lindquist** (predeceased).
- **Two additional children** beyond the tree's list: **Virginia E. Donahue** (of Arizona in 2008) and **Jacqueline D. Medas** (wife of Joseph Medas, of Brockton in 2008). Whether all four children are Mary's has not been proven, but the eldest two (Howard Jr., Dennis) match the tree's children of Howard & Mary.
- **Grandchildren named in 2008**: Brian Donahue, **Christine Albertson**, Jill Clifford, Dennis Donahue Jr., Debra DesJardins, Daniel DeGregorio, Alicia Foster — plus 14 great-grandchildren and 1 great-great-grandchild. (The grandchild surname Albertson likely marks the tree owner's connecting line; not researched further per privacy rule.)
- **WWII-era service**: during the war he served in the **Civilian Conservation Corps building roads in New Hampshire** — CCC, not military service. (No draft-card image located in free indexes; a 1940–42 WWII draft registration card almost certainly exists on Ancestry/Fold3.)
- **Occupations**: truck driver for **F.B. Washburn / Waleeco Candy Company** (the Brockton maker of the Waleeco coconut bar); bus driver for **Brockton Area Transit (BAT)**; in retirement, activity driver for **Unda's Transportation Company**.
- **Memberships**: the Elks; **Amalgamated Transit Union Local #1547**; life member, **Teamsters Local #653** (Brockton).
- **Burial apart from second wife**: Howard lies in Village Cemetery, North Easton; Lillian was interred at Coweeset Cemetery, Brockton — mildly supporting the possibility that Howard's plot connects to his own (or Mary's) family rather than Lillian's.
