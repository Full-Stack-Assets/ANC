# John (III) Morrow (1792–1872) & Margaret Coffin — Generation 6 Deep Dive

Research date: 2026-07-03. Subject: **John Morrow**, b. 17 Jul 1792, East Point, Kings Co., Prince
Edward Island; d. 20 Aug 1872, North Lake, Kings Co., PEI — a direct ancestor of the tree owner
through daughter Elizabeth Morrow (who married John MacDonald; their son Russell Morrow carried the
Morrow surname forward to Boston, Massachusetts). The tree names his spouse as "Harriet Coffin";
this research finds that is an error for **Margaret Coffin**.

*Note on scope:* the task brief accompanying this file named "Cornelius Regan & Julia Cronin" as the
research subject, but the tree-data JSON supplied (`A41.json`) and its `dossier_path`
(`gen06-john-morrow.md`) are for John Morrow, not Regan/Cronin. This dossier follows the actual data
file and path as instructed. The Regan/Cronin couple is not addressed here.

## Overview

John Morrow was the third-generation head of a Kings County, PEI farming family descended from an
Irish immigrant (his grandfather, born County Armagh) and linked by marriage to the Nantucket Coffin
whaling family that resettled at East Point. He was born and died within a few miles of each other —
East Point and North Lake, adjoining eastern-Kings-County communities — and the compiled Island
Register genealogy (Fulton Underhay's "Descendants of John Morrow") independently corroborates both
his birth and death dates exactly as given in the tree. His wife, however, is misidentified in the
tree: the documented spouse is Margaret Coffin (m. 4 Jan 1815), not Harriet Coffin, who was actually
the second wife of John's younger brother James Morrow.

## Verified facts

- **Birth 17 Jul 1792, East Point, Prince Edward Island** — "The Descendants of John Morrow," Island
  Register (compiled by Fulton Underhay): "John (III) Morrow #396 ... b. 17 Jul 1792, East Point
  Prince Edward Island." Matches the tree's sourced-as-unsourced date exactly. (Compiled/secondary,
  but internally consistent with the wider family reconstruction below.)
- **Death 20 Aug 1872, North Lake, Prince Edward Island** — same source: "John died 20 Aug 1872,
  North Lake Prince Edward Island." Matches the tree exactly.
- **Mother Eunice Coffin confirmed** — same source: John (Jr.) Morrow #332 "m. (1) about 1785, Eunice
  Coffin #331, b. 7 Jul 1766, Nantucket Massachusetts, (daughter of Elisha Coffin #296 and Eunice
  Myrick #297) d. 20 Dec 1805, East Point Prince Edward Island." John (III) is listed as the third
  child of this marriage. Confirms the tree's mother's name.
- **Daughter Elizabeth Morrow confirmed, with added detail** — same source: "Elizabeth Morrow #1421
  b. about 1831 ... occ. Housemaid ... m. John MacDonald ... d. about 1901, Mattapan Massachusetts,"
  religion "C. Presbyterian." This is the tree's listed child, and matches the sibling dossier

  Elizabeth and her husband John MacDonald of Main-à-Dieu, Cape Breton, NS.
- **Marriage to Margaret Coffin, 4 Jan 1815** — same source, primary entry for John (III): "m. 4 Jan
  1815, Margaret Coffin #397, Occ.: Homemaker." No birth/death date or parents are given for this
  Margaret Coffin in the compiled genealogy (see Open Questions — a same-named, similarly-dated
  Margaret Coffin elsewhere on the same page is a different woman; see below).
- **Full sibling group (by Eunice Coffin) confirmed**: Abigail (b. 6 Apr 1788, d. Apr 1798, both East
  Point), Elisha (b. 14 Mar 1790), John (III) himself, James (b. 20 May 1795), Andrew (b. 19 Aug
  1798), Thomas (b. abt 1799), Benjamin (b. 25 Dec 1802), William (b. abt 1805, East Point postmaster,
  Baptist). All from the same Island Register page.
- **Father's remarriages and half-siblings confirmed**: John (Jr.) Morrow remarried (2) Hepsibah
  Coffin (daughter of Uriah Coffin and Hepsabeth Bunker, both Nantucket Quakers/"Society of Friends"),
  producing Eunice, Phoebe "Feebie," Mary, and Artemas Morrow; and (3) Catherine Campbell, producing
  Isabella, Lemuel A., Catherine, and Elizabeth Morrow — all half-siblings of John (III). Cross-checked
  against the separate Island Register page "The Descendants of Uriah Coffin and Hepsabeth Bunker"
  (islandregister.com/ucoffin.html), which independently lists the same Hepsibah-Coffin children.
- **Grandfather confirmed**: John (Jr.) Morrow #332, b. about 1762, **County Armagh, Ireland**
  (occupation Mariner), married Eunice Coffin, Hepsibah Coffin, and (3rd) Catherine Campbell in turn;
  died 27 Jul 1841 at Surveyors Inlet, PEI. (The specific birthplace "County Armagh" is new detail
  beyond what the sibling dossier `gen04-russell-morrow-sarah-jane-morrow.md` had recorded as simply
  "b. Ireland.")
- **Great-grandfather confirmed**: John Morrow #2170, "b. Ireland," married a wife (name not given in
  the source, recorded only as "female (Morrow) #2171"), b. abt 1735, d. 25 Jan 1815 "in Ireland" per
  the source structure (matches prior sibling-dossier note that "his wife d. 25 Jan 1815").
- **Burial-ground context**: the Red Bank Pioneer Cemetery at North Lake, PEI (recognized as a
  Provincial Heritage Place, active for interments 1794–1840) is recorded as containing "John Morrow
  and his wives Eunice (Coffin) Morrow and Hepsabeth Coffin" and "Abigail Morrow" (John's daughter).
  Given the cemetery's active dates (burials ending ~1840) and that Abigail Morrow (d. 1798, dsp) and
  Eunice Coffin (d. 1805) and Hepsabeth Coffin are all associated with **John (Jr.) Morrow** (#332, d.
  1841) rather than John (III) (d. 1872), this cemetery almost certainly holds the **father's**
  generation, not John (III) himself — see Open Questions.

## Corrections to the tree

1. **Spouse "Harriet Coffin" is wrong — should be Margaret Coffin.** The Island Register genealogy
   gives John (III) Morrow's wife, married 4 Jan 1815, as Margaret Coffin #397. A Harriet Coffin does
   exist in the same extended family (b. abt 1795, Lot #38 PEI, daughter of a different Elisha Coffin
   and Jane Robbins) — but she married John's **brother James Morrow** (#401, b. 1795), not John
   himself, per the same source: "James Morrow #401 ... m. (2) Harriet Coffin #402 ... dsp[died
   without issue] ... d. North Lake Prince Edward Island." James died 10 Dec 1879 at North Lake and was
   interred at South Lake Disciples of Christ Cemetery. This is a plausible source of the tree's
   conflation — two brothers, both eventually of North Lake, both married a "Coffin" woman, and the
   Coffin surname recurs heavily in this intermarried Kings County settler network. This same
   correction was independently reached in the related sibling dossier
   `gen05-john-macdonald-elizabeth-morrow.md` while researching John's daughter Elizabeth; this
   research confirms it directly from the primary compiled source and adds the corroborating detail
   that Harriet's marriage was "dsp" (no children) and that she and James both died at North Lake.
2. **Parents' names: tree's "William John Morrow (Morrogh)" is not supported — the father's actual
   recorded name is John (Jr.) Morrow.** No genealogical source found in this research names a
   "William John Morrow" as John (III)'s father, and no "Morrow (Morrogh)" spelling was found attached
   to this specific family. ("Morrogh" is a genuine, independently attested historical Irish anglicized
   variant of the Morrow surname — see surname-origin sources — so the parenthetical may reflect
   general family lore about Irish origins rather than a specific record, but it is not evidence for
   the given name "William.") The Island Register genealogy consistently and repeatedly names the
   father as **John (Jr.) Morrow #332** (b. c. 1762, County Armagh, Ireland; mariner; d. 27 Jul 1841,
   Surveyors Inlet, PEI). Recommend correcting the tree's father-of-John(III) entry from "William John
   Morrow (Morrogh)" to "John Morrow (Jr.)," while keeping "Morrogh" noted as a surname-origin footnote
   if desired.
3. **Caution, not a tree error but a research pitfall found this session:** a second, unrelated
   "Margaret Coffin #604" (b. 4 Jan 1815, Lot #35 PEI, daughter of William "Kemble" Coffin and
   Margaret "Peggy" Dingwell, d. 9 Jun 1896, interred Union Cemetery, Souris West) appears elsewhere on
   the same Island Register page as the wife of **John Frederick Morrow** (John (III)'s nephew, son of
   brother Elisha Morrow). Because her name is identical to John (III)'s wife and her birth date (4 Jan
   1815) happens to coincide with John (III) and Margaret's marriage date (4 Jan 1815), an earlier
   automated read of this same page in this research session briefly and wrongly attributed her birth
   (1815)/death (1896)/parents (Kemble Coffin/Dingwell) to John (III)'s wife. That attribution is
   **rejected** after checking the raw source text directly: John (III)'s Margaret Coffin (#397) has no
   birth/death date or parents recorded in this genealogy at all. Flagging this here so it is not
   reintroduced into the tree by a future pass.

## New findings (not in the tree)

- **Grandfather's specific Irish origin: County Armagh** (John (Jr.) Morrow, b. c. 1762), refining the
  prior known "b. Ireland."
- **Grandfather's occupation: Mariner**, and he married three times (Eunice Coffin, then Hepsibah
  Coffin, then Catherine Campbell), dying 27 Jul 1841 at Surveyors Inlet, PEI.
- **Full sibling and half-sibling roster for John (III)** — eight full siblings by Eunice Coffin
  (Abigail, Elisha, John III himself, James, Andrew, Thomas, Benjamin, William) and eight known
  half-siblings by the father's later two marriages (Eunice, Phoebe, Mary, Artemas by Hepsibah Coffin;
  Isabella, Lemuel A., Catherine, Elizabeth by Catherine Campbell) — sixteen children of the father in
  total across three marriages.
- **Seven children of John (III) and Margaret Coffin listed** (beyond the tree's single recorded child
  Elizabeth): John (IV) (b. abt 1819, m. Mary Townshend, d. 30 Mar 1879), James (m. Melvina Cooper),
  David George (b. abt 1827, d. 30 Sep 1896 at Mystic, Connecticut — another emigrant to New England,
  like his sister Elizabeth), Elizabeth (the tree's known child), Artemas (b. 27 Feb 1835, d. 1921 at
  Eglington, PEI — this is the same Artemas already identified in the sibling dossier
  `gen04-russell-morrow-sarah-jane-morrow.md` as the likely household where grandson Russell Morrow
  was born/raised), Joseph (living as of Sep 1872, shortly after his father's death — consistent with
  settling the estate), and William (East Point).
- **Mother-line Nantucket Coffin whaling family**: Eunice Coffin's parents were Elisha Coffin (#296)
  and Eunice Myrick (#297) of Nantucket, Massachusetts — Eunice Myrick herself daughter of Andrew and
  Jedidah (Pinkham) Myrick of Nantucket, married 22 Jan 1761. This ties John (III)'s maternal line
  directly into the well-documented Nantucket Quaker Coffin/Myrick whaling community that resettled at
  East Point, PEI in the later 18th century.
- **Great-grandfather's wife's death date confirmed independently**: "female (Morrow) #2171... d. 25
  Jan 1815" (b. abt 1735) — matching the prior sibling dossier's note, now traced to its exact source
  line.
- **Burial-ground candidate for the wider family**: Red Bank Pioneer Cemetery, North Lake, PEI
  (recognized Provincial Heritage Place, 195 North Lake Harbour Road; active for interments
  1794–1840), associated by local history sources with "John Morrow and his wives Eunice (Coffin)
  Morrow and Hepsabeth Coffin" and daughter Abigail Morrow — i.e., almost certainly John (Jr.)'s family
  plot, one generation up from our John (III).
- **Colony name context**: John (III)'s birth (1792) and his parents' marriage (c. 1785) took place
  while the colony was still legally named "St. John's Island" (renamed Prince Edward Island in 1799);
  several of his siblings' and cousins' marriage records in the same source explicitly use "St. John's
  Island" for pre-1799 events, consistent with the family's residence there from at least the 1780s.

## Open questions

- **Margaret Coffin's own birth/death dates and parents are not established.** The compiled genealogy
  gives none. She may belong to yet another Coffin sub-line active in Kings County PEI at the time
  (the William "Kemble" Coffin/Margaret "Peggy" Dingwell family of Lot 35 is a tempting but *unproven*
  candidate, given the coincidental shared name and date — see Correction #3 above cautioning against
  this exact conflation). A pre-1832 PEI marriage record (church register, since PEI civil marriage
  registration/bonds most fully cover 1787–1832 and later) for "4 Jan 1815, John Morrow x Margaret
  Coffin" would settle this; try Anglican (St. Paul's, Charlottetown) or local Kings County church
  registers, and the PARO Collections Database (gov.pe.ca/parosearch) marriage-bonds series, which
  could not be queried directly by URL in this session (form-based search, not reachable via WebFetch).
- **No findagrave memorial located for John Morrow (1792–1872) or Margaret Coffin.** Not found under
  likely name variants; PEI-wide findagrave coverage for rural 1870s burials is patchy. Worth a manual
  findagrave.com search/cemetery browse for Kings County, PEI cemeteries near North Lake/East Point
  (Red Bank Pioneer Cemetery memorial page and any Baptist/Presbyterian cemetery at North Lake).
- **No PEI census record (1841, 1848, 1861, 1871) located for John Morrow's household.** These
  pre-Confederation PEI censuses are indexed on FamilySearch/Ancestry, both of which required a login
  not available in this session (same limitation noted in the related sibling dossiers). A located
  1861 or 1871 census entry would give household composition, religion, and landholding detail (the
  1841 PEI census in particular records "years and manner of holding land" and agricultural detail).
- **No will or probate record found** for John Morrow in the Island Register's PEI Wills Database
  search (checked; only unrelated Morrow entries — Elisha Morrow d. 1858, Angus Murray/Morrow d. 1858
  — appear). A probate file, if one exists at PARO for 1872, could name all surviving children and any
  landholding.
- **No 1872 death notice located** in the transcribed "Islander" newspaper deaths (coverage checked
  only extended to the early 1860s in the fetched excerpt); Charlottetown newspapers of Aug/Sep 1872
  (available on microfilm at PARO, and via Peel's Prairie Provinces / Canadiana for some Maritime
  papers) were not searched directly.
- **Exact relationship of "Surveyors Inlet"** (father's death place, 1841) to North Lake/East Point
  is unclear — likely a nearby cove/inlet place-name in far-eastern Kings County; not independently
  verified in this session.
