# The Hohmann line: Bertha ← Frank ← John Heinrich, and the Braddock splice

Deep-dive verification, 2026-07-03. Sources: Find A Grave obituary quotations
(Morrisville Cemetery, Bucks Co. PA), Trenton Historical Society directory
transcriptions (checked verbatim), All Saints Braddock cemetery transcription,
NJ State Archives death index (pointer), WikiTree/FamilySearch negative checks.

## Corrected chain

| Link | Verdict |
|---|---|
| Bertha E. Hohmann (b. ~1898) ← Frank & Mary Ann (Crist) Hohmann | **documented** — Mary Ann's 1946 obituary lists "Mrs. Howard Albertson" among surviving daughters |
| Frank ("Franklin P. S.") ← John Heinrich Hohmann (b. 11 Aug 1832) & Anna Elizabetha Kohlhepp | **plausible** — the export's link, independently supported by naming (son remembered as "John F."); decisive proof = Franklin's 1898 NJ death certificate |
| John Heinrich (1832) ← John Henry Hohman of Braddock (b. 1880) | **refuted — splice severed** (father born 48 years after son; unrelated Catholic family, All Saints Braddock cemetery) |
| Above John Heinrich | **unknown** — no parish of origin documented |

## Key findings

- **His name wasn't "Franklin P. S."** — his wife's 1946 obit says "the late Frank
  Hohmann," his son's 1948 obit says "son of the late **John F. Hohmann**." Most likely
  John Franklin Hohmann, called Frank. The named-for-President-Pierce story is lore —
  probably back-formed from his son *Franklin Pierce* Hohmann (b. 1895).
- **Morrisville, PA — not Trenton.** No Hohmann in Trenton directories 1881/1882/1900
  (checked verbatim; two apparent hits proved to be Hartmann/Hoffman misreads). The
  family plot is Morrisville Cemetery, Bucks Co.; St. Francis Hospital, Trenton (d. 30
  Jun 1898) was just the nearest hospital across the river.
- **Five children confirmed** by the 1946 obit: John A. (1885–1948), Frank (b. 1895),
  Anna, Bertha (Mrs. **Howard** Albertson — the direct line), and a second daughter,
  Mrs. **Charles L.** Albertson: two Hohmann sisters married Albertson men.
- **The Braddock splice, anatomized**: the export made John Henry Hohman (1880–1935,
  Braddock) the *father* of John Heinrich Hohmann (1832) — chronologically impossible,
  the classic accepted-hint chain. The Braddock family (wife Elizabeth M., eight
  children, plus Maurice "Morris J." Hohman & Elizabeth Huffman above them) drops out
  of the pedigree entirely.
- The stray "Virginia, U.S., Marriage Records, 1936-2014" source on Franklin: confirmed
  irrelevant.

Note: an earlier work-list of ours referred to German couples "Johan Ludwig Hohmann ∞
Bornscheuer" and "Johann Adam Hohmann ∞ Doepp" — **those names do not exist in this
export** (labeling error on our side); no German generations above John Heinrich are
present in the tree at all.

## The one cheap decisive document

**Franklin's 30 Jun 1898 death certificate** — NJ State Archives death index (June
1878–Dec 1900) is free to search in a browser
(https://wwwdnet-dos.nj.gov/DOS_ArchivesDBPortal/DeathIndex.aspx, bot-blocked); the
certificate (~$10) should name his parents and settle the John Heinrich link.

Other login-gated targets: Morrisville 1900 census (widow Mary Ann's household —
Bertha's exact birth date and whether she was born after her father died); Philadelphia
censuses 1860–1880 for a Hohmann household with Franklin b. ~1855 + father b. ~1832;
Trenton newspapers 1 Jul 1898 for the death notice.
