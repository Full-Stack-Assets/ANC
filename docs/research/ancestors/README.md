# Ancestor Research Dossiers

Index of the deep-research dossiers produced by the direct-line ancestor research effort: an initial pair of targeted deep-dives (William and Josiah Albertson, folded directly into PRs #1-#4 and the earlier verification report), three further pre-workflow targeted deep-dives below, and then a single 89-task parallel research workflow (`wf_b4761733-628`) that swept the entire reachable pedigree — every direct-line couple back to generation 6, then progressively coarser surveys (surname-line, fragment-triage, deep-chain credibility audit) for the thinner, deeper branches out to generation 25.

**Status key** — **Rich**: independently corroborated against outside sources, often with new facts recovered. **Conflicts**: the dossier found at least one specific correction to the tree (wrong name/date/parentage/duplicate record), which has been folded into the relevant `data/people/*.json` record's `manual.notes`. **Thin**: little to no independent corroboration available; tree data taken largely at face value.

89/89 workflow tasks completed (49 rich, 27 conflicts, 16 thin), plus 3 pre-workflow targeted dossiers (92 files total). One task (the English/British deep-chain audit) completed its research and wrote its dossier but failed to report a structured summary back to the orchestrator after repeated retries — its file is complete and is included here with a manually-written summary.

## How corrections get used

Every specific, well-supported correction named in these dossiers has been folded into the corresponding person's `data/people/{id}.json` `manual.notes` array — never into the machine-owned `relationships`/`vitals` fields, which stay a faithful (if sometimes wrong) record of the original GEDCOM export. `manual.notes` is where the corrected story lives; regenerating machine data from a fresh export is always safe and won't lose this research. Deep-generation individuals (roughly gen 13+) frequently have no `data/people/*.json` record at all — the GEDCOM export doesn't reach that far for most branches — in which case the correction lives only in the dossier file itself.
