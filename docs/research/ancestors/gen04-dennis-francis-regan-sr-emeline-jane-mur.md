# Dennis Francis Regan Sr (1872–1939) & Emeline Jane Murphy (1882–1979)

Generation 4 direct ancestors (parents of Frederick Lewis Reagan, gen 3; see
`gen03-frederick-lewis-reagan-ellen-m-larkin.md`).

## Overview

Dennis Francis Regan Sr was born 17 Dec 1872 in Peterborough, Hillsborough
County, New Hampshire, to Irish parents Jeremiah Regan and Mary Donovan, and
moved to Brockton, Plymouth County, Massachusetts — the booming "Shoe City" —
where on 14 Feb 1899 he married Emeline Jane Murphy (b. 29 Jan 1882, Rockland,
Plymouth County, MA, daughter of William John Murphy and Bridget Mary
Gilligan). The couple raised at least six children in Brockton; Dennis died
there 23 Oct 1939, and Emeline survived him by nearly forty years, dying in
Brockton 28 Mar 1979 at age 97. This deep dive found the free open web thin
for this working-class Brockton family — the key record indexes (Ancestry,
FamilySearch, Find a Grave) are login-gated — but produced corroboration of
the family structure from indexed Ancestry data, plus new Peterborough NH
Reagan-family context from two digitized town histories.

## Corrections to the tree

No tree fact was found to be wrong. Two clarifications:

- **"residence: 1973 @ Massachusetts val=None [sourced]" (Emeline)** → this is
  not a distinct residence event; it is the SSDI last-residence/last-benefit
  field bleeding into the tree as a dated event. Suggest annotating or
  deleting it. Evidence: the cited source is the SSDI itself; SSDI carries no
  true 1973 residence event.
- **Surname spelling** → keep both *Regan* (parents' records) and *Reagan*
  (children's records); add *Ragan* as a search variant — the Ancestry index
  snippet renders the grandfather as "Jeremiah **Ragan**" and the 1938
  Peterborough town history uses "**Ragan**" (below).

## New findings (not in the tree)

- **Son Dennis F Reagan's full dates: b. 18 Dec 1901 Brockton, d. 4 Aug 1980
  Brockton** (Ancestry public genealogy index; compiled). The tree lists him
  without dates.
- **Son Frederick Lewis Reagan: b. 4 Jan 1910 Brockton, d. 11 Jan 1978
  Brockton**, buried (inferred) St. Michael's Cemetery, Avon, Norfolk Co., MA
  — from the existing gen-3 dossier in this repo; relevant here as the
  family's likely burial orbit (see Open questions).
- **Reagan/Ragan family presence in Peterborough NH, 1861–1938 (context for
  Jeremiah & Mary's household):**
  - *History of the Town of Peterborough* (Albert Smith, 1876, Archive.org
    item `historyoftownofp00smit`), Civil War roster: "May 15 [recte ~May
    1861], **John Reagan, age 23, 2d Reg., Co. G, 3 yrs. Wounded at first Bull
    Run battle, and subsequently taken prisoner at Glendale, Va.**" — a John
    Reagan (b. ~1838) of Peterborough served in the 2nd New Hampshire Volunteer
    Infantry, Co. G. Plausibly a brother or close kinsman of Jeremiah Regan
    (same town, right generation). [documented, period publication; kinship
    unproven]
  - *Historical Sketches of Peterborough* (1938, Archive.org item
    `historicalsketch00unse_13`): lot **No. 28 on the mountain road** "has
    since been owned or occupied by Samuel Morrison, Samuel Miller Jr., Joseph
    Mace, Betsy Farwell and **Mary Ragan**. A cellar hole marks the spot where
    the house formerly stood... 125 rods south of the corner of the road
    leading past the Asa Carley place." — a Mary Ragan owned/occupied a small
    farm lot in Peterborough; possibly Dennis's mother Mary (Donovan) Regan.
    [documented mention; identification inferred, unproven]
- **WWI draft-card lead:** Dennis Sr, born 17 Dec 1872, falls *inside* the
  third (12 Sept 1918) WWI registration, which covered men born on or after
  11 Sept 1872 — by six days. A 1918 draft card for Dennis F. Regan of
  Brockton very likely exists in *U.S. WWI Draft Registration Cards
  1917-1918* (login-gated; not yet retrieved). It would give his exact
  address, employer and physical description.
- **Emeline's 79+ years of widowhood context:** widowed at 57 (1939), she
  lived to 97 (1979) — she should appear as a widow in the 1940 and 1950
  Brockton censuses. A scan of the NARA 1950-census AI name index (Plymouth
  Co., MA) found ~31 Regan/Reagan-variant households in Brockton but no
  legible "Emeline Regan"; the handwriting index is too noisy to rule her
  in/out (she may be enumerated in a child's household or under a mangled
  spelling).

## Negative results (searched, not found on the open web)

- **Find a Grave:** no locatable memorial for Dennis (d. 1939) or Emeline
  (d. 1979) via search-engine queries against Brockton cemeteries (Calvary,
  St. Patrick's, Melrose); Find a Grave's own search is bot-blocked. Absence
  of an indexed memorial is plausible — many Calvary Cemetery graves are
  unphotographed/unindexed.
- **Obituaries:** the Brockton *Enterprise* (the obvious paper for both 1939
  and 1979 obits) is not freely digitized; Chronicling America has no
  Brockton titles.
- **City directories:** no Brockton directories on Archive.org; HathiTrust and
  Google Books full-text search were inaccessible from this environment.
- **Census records 1880–1940:** indexes exist behind Ancestry/FamilySearch
  logins; no snippet-level hits retrievable for the Regan household.

## Open questions

1. **Burial:** where are Dennis (1939) and Emeline (1979)? Best candidates:
   Calvary Cemetery or St. Patrick's Cemetery, Brockton (Catholic), or
   St. Michael's Cemetery, Avon (where son Frederick's family is buried).
   Check Find a Grave and the Brockton diocesan cemetery office directly.
2. **Dennis's occupation and addresses:** 1900–1930 censuses and Brockton
   directories (Brockton Public Library holds the run) — was he a shoe
   worker like most of Catholic Brockton?
3. **1880 census, Peterborough NH:** find Jeremiah Regan / Mary Donovan
   household with 7-year-old Dennis; establishes siblings of Dennis and the
   parents' Irish birthplaces.
4. **Is the 1938 "Mary Ragan" of mountain-road lot 28 Dennis's mother?**
   Peterborough deed/probate records would settle it.
5. **Is Civil War soldier John Reagan (2nd NH Inf., Co. G) a brother of
   Jeremiah?** NH adjutant-general rosters and pension files would say.
6. **1918 draft card** for Dennis F. Regan, Brockton (third registration).
7. **Emeline in 1940/1950 censuses:** locate the widowed Emeline's household
   (likely with an adult child) — the AI index needs a manual ED-by-ED look.
8. **Fates of the other children** (Emeline Josephine, William H, Alice
   Camilla, Arthur Joseph): birth years, marriages, deaths. All were born
   before ~1920 and are almost certainly deceased, but no dates were found
   this pass.
9. **Emeline's 1979 death record** in *Massachusetts Death Index 1970-2003*
   and her Brockton Enterprise obituary (microfilm at Brockton Public
   Library) — the obituary would enumerate surviving children and
   grandchildren.
