## Overview

Sven Johan Samuelsson (1839–1877) and Gustava (Gustafva) Samuelsdotter (1842–1912) were a farming couple from the Bohuslän coast of western Sweden — he from Långelanda och Torp parish, she from Stala parish, both on the island of Orust in Göteborg och Bohus County. Sven died relatively young in Sweden in 1877; Gustava outlived him by 35 years, eventually emigrating (evidently following her son) to the Swedish immigrant colony of North Easton, Bristol County, Massachusetts, tied to the Ames Shovel Works, where she died in 1912. Their son Simon Swanson (b. Simon Anders Svensson, 1868) is the family's emigrant link and the direct ancestor of the tree owner through the generation-5/generation-4 chain already researched in sibling dossiers.
