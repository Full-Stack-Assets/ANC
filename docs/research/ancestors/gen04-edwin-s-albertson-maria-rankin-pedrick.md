# Edwin S. Albertson (1860-1932) & Maria Rankin Pedrick (1861-1929)

Generation 4 direct ancestors (parents of Howard I. Albertson). Research date: 2026-07-03.

## Overview

Edwin S. Albertson, a Haddonfield (Camden County) native of the old South Jersey Albertson family, married Maria Rankin Pedrick of Pedricktown, Salem County, in Trenton on 3 Sep 1882 (per the tree) and raised a large family in Trenton, where he worked as a boilermaker and where at least two of his sons worked in the Roebling wire mills. This dive verified both death dates against primary state death indexes — Maria in Mercer County, NJ, Nov 1929, and Edwin in Philadelphia, 5 Nov 1932 (PA certificate no. 96140) — and identified the family's long-time home at 1041 Liberty Street, Trenton. A 1938 published Pedrick genealogy devotes a section to Maria's family, giving her exact birth date, her parents' dates, nine siblings, and the statement that her husband (called "Edward Shone Albertson" there) is buried in Greenwood Cemetery, Trenton — revealing that Edwin's middle initial "S" almost certainly stands for **Shone**, his mother Mary Ann Shone's maiden name.

## Verified facts

- **Maria's birth, 23 Nov 1861, Pedricktown, Salem Co., NJ** — confirmed exactly ("Maria Rankin Pedrick born 11-23-1861") by Hubert B. Shoemaker, *A Genealogical and Biographical Record of the Pedrick Family of New Jersey, 1675-1938* (Phila., 1938), p. 95 (archive.org, genealogicalbiog00shoe).
- **Maria's parents, Joseph Elwell Pedrick and Mary E. Rankins** — confirmed by the same book: "Joseph Elwell Pedrick born 4-24-1834 died 7-5-1913 age 79 married Mary E. Rankins of Pedricktown... She was born 1847 died 1881 age 34 both buried at Pedricktown." (Independently echoed in Ancestry-derived compiled data naming Joseph Elwell Pedrick and wife Mary E. Rankins as parents of Margaret Ireland Pedrick, 1876-1881.)
- **Maria's death, Nov 1929, Mercer County, NJ** — CONFIRMED by the New Jersey State Index of Deaths, 1925-1929, Mercer County: "Albertson, Maria — [192]9 — Nov" (Reclaim The Records reel 12, leaf 27, archive.org). The tree's unsourced "Nov 1929 @ Mercer, NJ" is exactly right.
- **Edwin's death, 5 Nov 1932, Philadelphia, PA** — CONFIRMED by the Pennsylvania Death Index 1932 (soundex A416): "EDWIN ALBERTSON, PHILA [county 51], 11-5-32, certificate no. 96140" (PA State Archives, RG-11 death indices, file "D-32 A-A.pdf", printed p. 27). The tree's "Jefferson, Philadelphia" is consistent with a death at Jefferson Hospital (inference — see Open questions).
- **Residence and occupation, Trenton** — *Fitzgerald's Trenton and Mercer County Directory*, 1919: "Albertson Edwin S, boilermaker, h 1041 Liberty," with Clara E (operator), Howard (wireworker), and Walter M (U.S. Army) at the same address, and Wilbert (machinist) at 121 Ward Ave. Corroborates the tree's Trenton residence and children Clara E., Howard, Walter M., and Wilbert.
- **Marriage Albertson-Pedrick** — the Pedrick book confirms "Maria married Edward [sic] Shone Albertson of Haddonfield" (supporting the tree's Haddonfield origin and the union itself). The tree's specific date/place (3 Sep 1882, Trenton) was not independently confirmed (see Open questions).
- **Children corroborated by records found**: Emma Frances (m. Oliver T. Cadwalader — Pedrick book), Clara E., Howard, Walter M., Wilbert (1919 directory; Roebling employment cards).

## Corrections to the tree

- No factual corrections required — the two unsourced death entries both checked out against state death indexes (see above). Two data-hygiene flags:
  - **"Bertha M Albertson" and "Bertha Albertson" both appear in the children list** — almost certainly the same child duplicated; recommend merging.
  - The published Pedrick genealogy calls him **"Edward Shone Albertson"** and says Maria "died 1931 age 69." Both book details are wrong: city directory + PA death index say **Edwin**, and the NJ death index proves Maria died **Nov 1929** (she was 67, one to two weeks shy of 68). Do not "correct" the tree from the book.

## New findings

- **Middle name**: Edwin's "S" = **Shone**, his mother Mary Ann Shone's maiden name (Pedrick book: "Edward Shone Albertson of Haddonfield"). A Quaker-style use of the maternal surname as middle name.
- **Burial**: Edwin (and presumably Maria) buried **Greenwood Cemetery, Trenton** (Hamilton Twp.), per the Pedrick book ("buried Greenwood, Trenton"; the author states "Trenton vital statistics and cem. stones tally"). Greenwood's 65,000 interments are on index cards only — not searchable online; findagrave cemetery #100006.
- **Occupation**: boilermaker (1919 directory). The Pedrick book says "He worked at Roeblings wire-mills" — possibly as a boilermaker at the John A. Roebling's Sons plant; his sons certainly worked there (below).
- **Family home**: **1041 Liberty Street, Trenton** (1919 directory; Roebling employee cards 1919-1920). The tree's 1910 census has them in Trenton Ward 10.
- **Sons at Roebling's**: Trenton Public Library's transcription of John A. Roebling's Sons Co. Dept. 50 employee records shows:
  - Howard Albertson, age 24, American, 1041 Liberty St., employed 2/25/1919-9/17/1919 ("laid off, work slack"), next of kin "Albertson, Edwin — Father"; re-hired age 26, 21 Logan Ave., 8/18/1920-11/8/1920, next of kin "Albertson, Edwin (Mrs.) — Mother."
  - Walter M. Albertson, age 24, 1041 Liberty St., employed 6/2/1919-2/26/1920 ("discharged, unsteady"), next of kin "Albertson, Edwin — Father."
- **Walter M. served in the U.S. Army** — listed as "U S A" in the 1919 directory, i.e., in service at the time of canvassing (WWI era).
- **Maria's Pedrick line (per the 1938 Pedrick genealogy)**: Roger Pedrick (English immigrant, land at what became Pedricktown) > John > Jacob > Jacob > John > **Francis Augustus Pedrick** m. **Jane Ann Pierson** > **Joseph Elwell Pedrick**. Joseph succeeded his father Francis in the wheelwright business at Pedricktown.
- **Maria's mother's story**: Mary E. Rankins's parents "died young and she was raised by Joseph Weatherby"; she died 1881, age 34, when Maria was 19.
- **Maria's nine siblings** (Pedrick book, p. 95): Jane Ann (1863-6 Apr 1916, single); Francis Augustus (1865-3 Mar 1927, wheelwright in Philadelphia, m. Louise Martin, no children, buried North Cedar Hill, Phila.); Charles Vanderbilt (b. 1 Oct 1867, m. 1894 Sallie Frances Lloyd of Greenwood, Del., carpenter with the Pennsylvania R.R. 36 years, living at Pitman, NJ in 1938); Henry Lawrence (b. 1869, unmarried, carried on the family wheelwright shop in Pedricktown); Ida (b. 1870, died in infancy); Margaret Rankins (b. 1872, unmarried, kept house for brother Henry in Pedricktown); Walter Springer (1876-24 Dec 1902, single); Harvey Lewis (1878-6 May 1901, single); Randolph (died in infancy).
- **Daughter Emma Frances Albertson** married Oliver T. Cadwalader of Trenton; he died 1918, buried Riverview Cemetery, Penns Grove; their daughter Helen Cadwalader (b. 1908) married 1933 Harry Datz, a plumber of Mullica Hill (Pedrick book).
- **Other Trenton Albertsons in 1919** (probably kin of varying distance, useful for disambiguation): Asenath S. Albertson, milliner, 133 Mercer St. (died 9 May 1930 per Trenton Evening Times obit index); Charles, Charles F., Francis, John, Lewis A. (National Cash Register sales manager).
- **A different "Maria Albertson" died in Trenton 6 Feb 1932** (Trenton State Gazette death-column index) — this is NOT our Maria (who died 1929); noted here so nobody re-imports the wrong date.

## Open questions

- Marriage record, 3 Sep 1882, Trenton: the Trenton Historical Society's 1770s-1899 marriage index (letter A) does not include it; check the NJ State Archives marriage returns for Mercer County, 1882 (Albertson-Pedrick).
- Exact death date and cause for Maria: the NJ index gives only "Nov 1929" (certificate at NJ State Archives, Mercer Co. 1929); her obituary was not in the one Trenton paper indexed for 1929 (Sunday Times Advertiser). Trenton Evening Times for late Nov 1929 (GenealogyBank/paywalled) should carry it.
- Edwin's death certificate: Philadelphia, 1932, file no. 96140 — obtainable from PA State Archives (RG-11); would give cause, informant, exact burial. Would also settle whether "Jefferson" in the tree = Jefferson Hospital.
- Greenwood Cemetery (Hamilton Twp./Trenton) plot: records are index cards; a written request to the cemetery would confirm the Albertson lot and who else is in it. No findagrave memorials located (findagrave blocked to automated search; worth a manual look).
- Census trail: only the 1910 census is attached in the tree. The household should appear in 1900 (Trenton, with birth month "Aug 1860" for Edwin), 1880 (Edwin, 19, with Isaac J. and Mary Ann in Haddonfield/Camden Co.), and 1920 (1041 Liberty St.); Edwin as widower in 1930 (Trenton or Philadelphia with a child?). All need Ancestry/FamilySearch login to pull.
- Edwin's parents Isaac J. Albertson and Mary Ann Shone (Haddonfield): not researched in depth here — the Albertsons are an old Camden County Quaker family (Albertson/Alberson variants); a targeted dive on Isaac J. is the natural next step.
- Why did the 1938 Pedrick book get "Edward" and "died 1931" wrong? Possibly compiled from a family informant years after the fact; treat its Albertson details as secondary.
