# Hannah Stowell (1772–1829) & Joseph Hibbard (1751–?) — Generation 8

> **Note on scope:** `direct-line-issues.md` and `deep_dive_backlog.md` list Hannah Stowell (`I182541959332`/`I182541966157`) and Joseph Hibbard (`I182541959347`/`I182541965892`) as two separate gen-8 duplicate-person findings, immediately following Magnus Månsson Rånge in the Tier 1 backlog. Both are the parents of the gen-7 couple **Joseph Hibbard III & Hannah Pond**, already covered in a parallel research pass (`docs/research/ancestors/gen08-joseph-hibbard-iii-hannah-pond.md`, filed at a `gen08` path despite covering the gen-7 pair, "since the duplication runs through the whole branch"). That dossier independently reached the same primary/duplicate conclusions documented here for both Hannah Stowell and Joseph Hibbard, using the same key external source (A.G. Hibbard's 1901 published genealogy). This dossier is the dedicated write-up for the two gen-8 people themselves, cross-references the companion dossier rather than repeating it in full, and adds two findings the companion dossier does not carry: an external FamilySearch match for Hannah Stowell, and a place-name defect on Joseph Hibbard's birth record.

## Overview

The tree contains two Hannah Stowells (b. 26 Sep 1772, d. 21 Aug 1829, both Dedham, Norfolk Co., MA) and two Joseph Hibbards (b. 23 Apr 1751, Manchester, Essex Co., MA) — each pair identical in name and vital dates, one sourced and one not. This is not a coincidence limited to these two people: it is one small branch of the tree (Isaac Stowell Jr. → Hannah Stowell → m. Samuel Pond → dau. Hannah Pond → m. Joseph Hibbard III; and separately Joseph Hibbard Sr./Lois Ingersoll → Joseph Hibbard → m. Hannah Pond) that was imported into the tree twice — once with real archival citations attached, once as a bare, unsourced skeleton — and never merged. Both copies of Joseph Hibbard III and Hannah Pond converge on the identical shared child record (`I182541959707`), which is only possible if the "two couples" are actually one couple, proving the duplication mechanically rather than merely by resemblance.

Joseph Hibbard (the elder, this record) is independently corroborated by a 1901 published genealogy of the Hibbard family, which confirms his birth, both his marriages, and his son's birth almost to the letter. Hannah Stowell has a matching FamilySearch Family Tree profile, though its underlying sources were not extractable via the open web this session.

## Verified facts

- **Joseph Hibbard, b. 23 Apr 1751, Manchester, Essex Co., Massachusetts; m. (1) 1775 Mary Lyons; m. (2) 9 Jul 1780, Marblehead, Anna Dugan/Dugard.** Canonical record `I182541965892`, sourced to *North America, Family Histories, 1500-2000* (S447151841), *Geneanet Community Trees Index* (S508118847), and *Massachusetts, Town and Vital Records, 1620-1988* (S446915296, second marriage). [documented]
- **Externally corroborated by a published genealogy.** A.G. Hibbard, *Genealogy of the Hibbard Family Who Are Descendants of Robert Hibbard of Salem, Massachusetts* (Hartford: Case, 1901), entry 163: "Joseph⁶ (s. Joseph⁴, Jeremiah³, Joseph², Robert¹), b. Manchester, Mass., April 23, 1751; m. (1) 1775, Mary Lyons, (2) July 9, 1780, Mrs. Anna Dugan of Marblehead (b. 1760; d. Dec. 19, 1804)." This matches the tree's birth date/place and both wives exactly; the tree's "Dugard" vs. the book's "Dugan" is an ordinary period spelling/transcription variant, not a contradiction. The same entry (69) gives his parents, Joseph⁴ Hibbard (b. Manchester 22 Dec 1723, tree has 27 Dec 1723 — a minor 5-day discrepancy) and Lois Ingersoll (b. 27 Jun 1725, exact match to tree record `I182541965902`), m. 31 Oct 1744. The book's child list (entries 425–432) includes "427. Joseph, b. Nov. 19, 1787" — an exact match to this Joseph's son, Joseph Hibbard III. [documented — independently located 1901 published genealogy, archive.org]
- **Hannah Stowell, b. 26 Sep 1772, d. 21 Aug 1829, both Dedham, Norfolk Co., Massachusetts.** Canonical record `I182541966157`, daughter of Isaac Stowell Jr. (1733–1820, `I182620334338`) and Mary Oliver (1735–1812, `I182650549259`), whose 24 May 1753 Boston marriage is sourced to *Massachusetts, Town and Vital Records, 1620-1988* (S446915296). She married Samuel Pond (1764–1858, `I182541966151`) and their daughter Hannah Pond (1805–1865) married Joseph Hibbard III (son of the Joseph Hibbard covered above). [documented, via parents' sourced marriage; Hannah's own vitals carry no direct source]
- **A matching FamilySearch Family Tree profile exists for Hannah Stowell.** "Hannah Stowell (1771–1829)," ancestors.familysearch.org/en/2Z3C-KL1/hannah-stowell-1771-1829 — birth year is one year off (1771 vs. this tree's 1772) but the death year (1829) matches exactly. This is consistent with, though not conclusive proof of, the same person; the profile's underlying source citations could not be extracted through an automated fetch of the (JavaScript-rendered) page and would need a manual FamilySearch lookup to confirm fully. [documented — external tree match, sources not yet verified]
- **Duplicate-person, mechanically proven for the couple one generation down.** The unsourced pairing of Joseph Hibbard III (`I182541959308`) + Hannah Pond (`I182541959296`) and the sourced pairing (`I182541965880` + `I182541965883`) both list the same child, `I182541959707` ("Hannah Pond Hibbard"), as a parent — see the companion dossier for the full argument. Since Hannah Pond's mother is this record's Hannah Stowell, and Joseph Hibbard III's father is this record's Joseph Hibbard, the duplication of the child couple necessarily implies duplication of the parent couple as well, and indeed both parents have their own separately-flagged unsourced twins (`I182541959332` for Hannah Stowell; `I182541959347` for Joseph Hibbard). [documented — internal cross-reference]
- **The duplication continues at least one generation further up**, into the already-flagged, not-yet-resolved gen-9 pairs: Isaac Stowell Jr. (`I182620334338`/`I182541959366`), Samuel Pond's parents Jonas Pond (`I182541966171`/`I182541959422`) and Sarah White (`I182541966163`/`I182541959371`). Every one of these pairs follows the identical signature: one copy carries sources and a fuller relationship graph, the other is a bare, source-free stub. [documented — internal tree structure]

## Corrections to the tree

- **Duplicate-person: merge `I182541959332` into `I182541966157` (Hannah Stowell)**, and **`I182541959347` into `I182541965892` (Joseph Hibbard)**. The sourced/documented copies are primary; the unsourced/inferred copies are the duplicates, following the same pattern already resolved for John Albertson & Ann Pine (gen 6) and Joseph Hibbard III & Hannah Pond (gen 7).
- **Place-name defect on Joseph Hibbard's birthplace (both copies).** `I182541965892` and `I182541959347` both record his birthplace as parts `["Manchester","Mass","Massachusetts","USA"]` — the county "Essex" has been dropped, and "Mass." (a period abbreviation for Massachusetts) has been duplicated as a false county token. The correct form, matching his father's own record (`I182541965901`: "Manchester, Essex, Massachusetts, USA") and the published genealogy, is **Manchester, Essex Co., Massachusetts**.
- **No JSON relationship arrays were altered**, per house style (see the John Albertson/Ann Pine precedent — duplicates are annotated via `manual.notes` rather than deleted or re-pointed). All four records discussed here (`I182541966157`, `I182541959332`, `I182541965892`, `I182541959347`) now carry `manual.notes` documenting the primary/duplicate call, the FamilySearch cross-reference, and the place-name defect.

## New findings (not in the tree)

- **Independent confirmation, via a source the tree's own compiler evidently never consulted:** A.G. Hibbard's 1901 genealogy corroborates Joseph Hibbard's birth, both marriages, and his son's birth without having been cited anywhere in the tree's own source list — a strong, independent cross-check rather than a repetition of the tree's own claim.
- **A matching but not-yet-fully-verified FamilySearch profile for Hannah Stowell** (2Z3C-KL1) — a lead for anyone with FamilySearch login access to pursue further; it was not reachable via automated web fetch in this session.
- **The double-import is structural, not a one-off couple.** Counting this dossier and its companion, the same "sourced spine vs. unsourced stub" pattern now spans at least three generations of this family (Joseph Hibbard III/Hannah Pond, gen 7; Joseph Hibbard/Hannah Stowell and their spouses Samuel Pond, gen 8; Isaac Stowell Jr./Jonas Pond/Sarah White, gen 9) — almost certainly the result of a single GEDCOM or Ancestry-hint import that was never de-duplicated against an earlier, better-sourced entry of the same family.

## Open questions

1. **Samuel Pond (`I182541966151`/`I182541959337`, gen 8) and the gen-9 trio (Isaac Stowell Jr., Jonas Pond, Sarah White) are flagged in `direct-line-issues.md` but were not in scope for this research pass** — they are part of the identical double-import and should be resolved the same way (documented copy primary, unsourced copy annotated as duplicate) by whoever picks them up next.
2. **Hannah Stowell's own birth/death records** were not located directly in Dedham vital records via open web search this session (Dedham's published vital-records volume and the Massachusetts Town and Vital Records collection are effectively subscription/access-gated on the open web); the FamilySearch profile above is the best lead.
3. **A more consequential, prior question, per the companion dossier:** the parent link from this couple's granddaughter Ellen F. Donahue (gen 5) up to "Hannah Pond Hibbard" is independently doubted there as a probable Ancestry-hint mis-merge. If that is correct, this entire branch — Hannah Stowell and Joseph Hibbard included — may not belong in the true direct line at all, regardless of the duplication resolved here.
