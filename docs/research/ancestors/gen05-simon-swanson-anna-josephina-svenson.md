## Overview

Simon Swanson (born Simon Anders Svensson) and Anna Josephina Svenson were Swedish immigrants who married in Easton, Bristol County, Massachusetts on 14 May 1898 and settled in the town's Swedish immigrant colony tied to the Ames Shovel Works / Holy Trinity Lutheran Church community. Anna died young, on 23 August 1902, leaving Simon a widower with at least three small children (Clara, Ellen, and Emma); Simon appears to have remarried at least once (and possibly twice) afterward and lived until 1921. Their daughter Ellen Josephine Swanson is the tree owner's direct ancestor (via her son Howard F. Donahue), making this couple generation-5 great-great-great-grandparents.
