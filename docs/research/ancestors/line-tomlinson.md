# The Tomlinson Line (Surname Line 4) — Colonial Gloucester County, New Jersey

## Overview

The tree supplies only four Tomlinson records for this surname line — Eleanor Tomlinson (gen. 7, b. 1741, d. 1777), her father John Tomlinson (gen. 8), and John's parents "Elijah Tomlinson" and "Grace Tomlinson" (gen. 9, no dates, no sources) — not the full generations 7–12 the survey scope anticipated. The documented paper trail runs out at generation 9 in the tree, but web research turned up an independently documented Quaker Tomlinson family of Gloucester Township, Gloucester Co., NJ, whose details (a John Tomlinson who married a Mary Fairlamb of Chester Co., PA, in 1736, resident in Gloucester Twp.) match the tree's generation‑8 John Tomlinson closely enough to be a strong candidate identification — but that family's documented father is **Joseph Tomlinson** (d. 1719/20), not "Elijah." This is flagged below as the line's central open question/correction. If the Joseph Tomlinson identification is correct, he is also the immigrant ancestor (from London, per compiled sources), meaning the documented line already reaches its American origin point at generation 9/10 — one generation short of what the tree currently shows.

## Verified facts

- **Eleanor Tomlinson**, b. 1741 "Newton, Gloucester, New Jersey" (per tree, unsourced), d. 2 Oct 1777, New Jersey — death date and NJ location carry a "sourced" flag in the tree citing *U.S., Find A Grave Index, 1600s–Current*. I could not independently pull the specific Find A Grave memorial (findagrave.com returned HTTP 403 to automated fetch), so I cannot verify the memorial ID or its cited underlying record; treat the death date as tree-documented-but-not-independently-confirmed by this pass.
- Eleanor's burial location, **Haddonfield, Camden County, NJ**, is also flagged "sourced" in the tree. Haddonfield in this period was a Quaker (Religious Society of Friends) community with several period burying grounds, including Haddonfield Friends Cemetery — consistent with a Quaker Tomlinson family (see below). [Find a Grave: Haddonfield Friends Cemetery](https://www.findagrave.com/cemetery/2254754/haddonfield-friends-cemetery)
- **John Tomlinson** (gen. 8, Eleanor's father) has a "sourced" residence event: "2 Jan 1755 @ Gloucester, Gloucester, New Jersey." The tree also cites *New Jersey, Abstract of Wills, 1670–1817* as a source for this person, implying a will was probated for a John Tomlinson of Gloucester Co. in that window — I could not pull the specific abstract text in this pass (see Open Questions).
- A documented, independently-sourced Tomlinson family of Gloucester Township, Gloucester Co., NJ exists in the historical record:
  - **Joseph Tomlinson** (d. 1719, Gloucester Township) left a will dated 20 Aug 1719, proved 20 May 1720, naming wife Elizabeth and children **Ephraim, Joseph, Margret, Elizabeth, Mary, John, Ebenezer, Othniel, Richard, William** — ten children, matching secondary claims that Joseph "had at least ten children." [Genealogy Trails: Gloucester Co. NJ Will Calendar](http://genealogytrails.com/njer/gloucester/will_calendar.html)
  - Compiled/secondary sources (Huntington Library finding aid for the Tomlinson Family Papers, 1683–1899) describe Joseph Tomlinson as a Quaker immigrant from London who studied law, was appointed sheriff, and became "King's attorney" in 1696, married Elizabeth (surname given elsewhere as Shevier/Shivers) in 1694 in Gloucester, NJ, and settled in Gloucester Township. [OAC finding aid, Tomlinson Family Papers 1683–1899](https://oac.cdlib.org/findaid/ark:/13030/c8n87gff) — this is a compiled archival description, not a primary immigration record; treat the "immigrant from London" detail as secondary/unverified in this pass.
  - The same finding aid states Joseph's son **John Tomlinson married Mary Fairlamb of Chester County, Pennsylvania, in 1736**, and that John & Mary's children included **Anne Tomlinson** (m. Jeremiah Haines) and **Isaac Tomlinson** (m. Elizabeth Shever/Shivers, 1766) — i.e., Eleanor is not named in the (non-exhaustive) finding-aid summary of children, but the summary explicitly says "among other children," leaving room for an unlisted daughter Eleanor.

## Corrections to the tree

- **"Elijah Tomlinson" & "Grace Tomlinson" (gen. 9, parents of John Tomlinson) → likely should be "Joseph Tomlinson" (d. 1719/20) & "Elizabeth [Shevier/Shivers] Tomlinson."**
  Evidence: No period New Jersey record of an "Elijah Tomlinson" turned up in any search of NJ colonial will calendars, Gloucester Co. genealogy compilations, or the Tomlinson Family Papers finding aid; the only "Elijah Tomlinson" found anywhere is a 19th-century Elijah S. Tomlinson (b. 1850), clearly a different era/person. By contrast, an independently documented John Tomlinson of Gloucester Township — matching the tree's John Tomlinson on marriage partner surname (Fairlamb), approximate era, and township of residence — is on record as the son of Joseph Tomlinson (will 1719/probated 1720, naming son John among ten children) and his wife Elizabeth. This is a **plausible correction, not a certainty**: I did not find a document naming Eleanor as a child of this specific John & Mary (Fairlamb) Tomlinson, and there could in principle be more than one "John Tomlinson" active in Gloucester Co. in this period. Recommend treating "Elijah/Grace" as unsupported lore pending a primary source (will, Quaker meeting record, or the "Abstract of Wills 1670–1817" entry the tree already cites but which I could not pull) and treating "Joseph/Elizabeth" as the better-evidenced working hypothesis.
- No correction found for Eleanor's own vital dates or her marriage to Josiah Albertson — both are plausible and partly tree-sourced, but see Open Questions re: which of (at least) two contemporaneous men named Josiah Albertson she married.

## New findings

- **Immigrant candidate identified:** Joseph Tomlinson, said to have immigrated to the Delaware Valley from London, settled in Gloucester Township, Gloucester Co., NJ, married Elizabeth (Shevier/Shivers) in 1694, was a Quaker active in civic life (sheriff; "King's attorney" 1696), and died 1719/20 leaving a will naming ten children. If this is indeed the ancestor of the tree's John Tomlinson (gen. 8), the documented Tomlinson line in America reaches back to Joseph as the immigrant/founding generation — one generation beyond what the tree currently records (the tree stops at the unsourced "Elijah/Grace" pairing).
- **Siblings of John Tomlinson (gen. 8), if the Joseph/Elizabeth identification holds:** Ephraim, Joseph Jr., Margret, Elizabeth, Mary, Ebenezer, Othniel, Richard, William Tomlinson (per the 1719 will).
- **Children of John & Mary (Fairlamb) Tomlinson beyond Eleanor**, per the Huntington/OAC finding aid: Anne Tomlinson (m. Jeremiah Haines; their children Keziah, Aaron, and Amos Haines) and Isaac Tomlinson (m. Elizabeth Shever/Shivers, 1766); the finding aid also traces a documented male-line descent Isaac → Isaac II → John Tomlinson II (m. Elizabeth Hilyard) → Isaac III / Joshua / Evans R. Tomlinson → Evans H. → Evans J. Tomlinson, running well into the 19th century — this is a collateral (not direct-ancestor) branch relative to Eleanor's line but demonstrates the family's continued documentation in Gloucester Co. records and papers held at the Huntington Library.
- **Possible duplicate/confusion risk flagged for the wider tree:** two distinct Josiah Albertsons of the right era turned up in web sources — a "Josiah Alberson Jr." (per Geni, 1738–1782) and a "Josiah Albertson" (per FamilySearch/Find A Grave, 1741–1827, of Blue Anchor Cemetery, NJ, married to Ann Chew, with a daughter Sarah b. 1767). Given Eleanor died in 1777, the 1738–1782 man is the more plausible match for her husband; the 1741–1827/Ann Chew man is very likely a different individual (possibly a relative) whose record should not be merged with Eleanor's husband. I could not confirm either identification with a primary source in this pass — flagging for the tree's Albertson researchers as a likely source of the "many unmerged duplicates" the project is watching for, even though full Albertson profiles are outside this Tomlinson-surname dossier's scope.

## Open questions

1. Does the *New Jersey, Abstract of Wills, 1670–1817* entry the tree cites for John Tomlinson (gen. 8) name his father and his daughter Eleanor? This is the single record most likely to settle the Elijah/Grace vs. Joseph/Elizabeth question and should be pulled directly from FamilySearch or the NJ State Archives calendar of wills.
2. Is there a Quaker (Haddonfield or Newton Monthly Meeting) birth/marriage record for Eleanor Tomlinson, 1741, that would confirm her parentage and connect her to the Joseph Tomlinson family independently of Find A Grave?
3. Which Josiah Albertson did Eleanor actually marry — the 1738–1782 man or another contemporary of the same name? A primary marriage record (Quaker meeting minutes are the standard source for this family/era/region) would resolve this.
4. Was the specific Find A Grave memorial behind the tree's "sourced" death/burial entries for Eleanor ever transcribed from an actual gravestone, or is it a compiled/user-submitted memorial with no photographed stone? (Not verifiable via automated fetch in this pass — findagrave.com blocked the request with HTTP 403.)
5. No records at all — sourced or unsourced — exist in the tree for "Elijah Tomlinson" or "Grace Tomlinson" beyond a bare spousal link and John as their only listed child. If a primary source does surface an Elijah Tomlinson in Gloucester Co. NJ, it would need to be reconciled with the Joseph Tomlinson will (which lists no "Elijah" among its ten named children).
