## Overview

Martha Taber Hedger (b. 1720, Gloucester, NJ; d. 1780) is tree-listed as the
daughter of Benjamin Taber (1704–1737) and Eliza Bolton (b. 1708, Abington,
Montgomery Co., PA), and as the wife of Peter C Cheesman (1704–1788) of Old
Upton, Gloucester Co., NJ, married "Bet 1746 and 1748." Five children are
attached to the couple, spanning 1728–1764: William, Thomas, Mary Ann,
Margaret, and Thomas John. The 1728 birth of William is impossible for an
8-year-old mother, and Eliza Bolton's own 1708 birth makes her impossibly
young (age 12) at Martha's 1720 birth. Both problems trace back to the same
underlying issue: this cluster of records was assembled from generic Ancestry
Family Trees hint-matching (source S446914557) with no primary citations, in
a family that reused given names heavily across generations (Peter, Richard,
Thomas, William, Martha, Margaret all recur across multiple Cheesman, Taber,
and Bolton individuals).

External research — an unpublished but well-regarded local genealogy, "The
Cheesman Family" by Albert Stirling Adams (Gloucester County NJ Historical
Society; transcribed at Long Island Surnames/Long Island Genealogy), Peter
Cheesman's own probated 1786/1788 will, a real 1781 Gloucester Co. land deed,
and a cluster of independently cross-linked WikiTree profiles — resolves most
of this. Peter Cheesman had at least three, probably four, wives: an
unidentified first wife, second wife Ann Hanna Ellis (b. 1710 England, d. 1759
NJ), Martha Taber (this record, a widow — her first husband was Joseph
Hedger), and third/fourth wife Christian Fisher (widow Roe, m. 1781). This
tree collapses all of Peter's children onto Martha alone; the real family was
more complicated, and Martha's own genuine, independently-documented children
(by Joseph Hedger) are not in this tree at all.

## Verified facts

- **Martha Taber Hedger's birth year, 1720, is independently corroborated.** Her own WikiTree profile (Taber-815) documents a real first marriage — to **Joseph Hedger**, 1 Nov 1741, Philadelphia, PA, "at age 21" — which backs out to a birth of ~1720, matching this tree exactly. That profile lists both of her parents as unknown (no link to Benjamin Taber or Eliza Bolton). [documented — WikiTree Taber-815]
- **Martha's real, documented children (by Joseph Hedger) are Deborah (Hedger) Cheeseman and Maribeth (Hedger) Cheeseman**, both b. 16 Mar 1738, Gloucester NJ — neither is in this tree. Maribeth went on to marry **Thomas Cheesman** (Peter's actual son by his second wife, Ann Ellis) on 27 May 1771 — meaning Martha's real documented tie to the Cheesman family runs partly through her own daughter's marriage, independent of her own later marriage to Peter. [documented — WikiTree Taber-815, Cheesman-113]
- **Peter Cheesman's second wife, Ann Hanna Ellis**, was born 16 Apr 1710 in England and died 1759 in Gloucester, NJ; she married Peter in 1732 in Upton, Burlington Co., NJ (per WikiTree) — though a second secondary source (the Adams manuscript) instead implies a 1746 marriage, an unresolved discrepancy in the sources themselves. [documented/inferred — WikiTree Ellis-4456, Cheesman-112]
- **Thomas Cheesman (b. 11 May 1734, d. 9 Feb 1792, Waterford, Gloucester Co., NJ) and Mary Ann Cheesman (b. 16 Sep 1746, d. c.1840s, Ohio) — both exact date-matches to this tree's own records — are independently documented on WikiTree as children of Peter Cheesman and Ann Hanna Ellis**, not of Martha. Three separate WikiTree profiles (Cheesman-112 Peter, Cheesman-113 Thomas, Cheseman-5 Mary Ann Jackson, Ellis-4456 Ann Ellis) cross-link consistently to this effect. [documented — WikiTree, multiple cross-linked profiles]
- **A previously untracked full sibling, Richard D Cheesman** (b. 16 Oct 1749, Old Upton, Gloucester NJ; d. 3 Nov 1799; m. Rachel Williams), is also documented as Peter and Ann Ellis's son (WikiTree Cheesman-110) and named in Peter's will — not currently in this tree at all. [documented — WikiTree, Peter's will]
- **Peter Cheesman's probated will** (dated 19 Apr 1786, proved 25 Aug 1788, Gloucester Twp., Gloucester Co., NJ — reproduced in the Adams manuscript) names: third wife Christian (Fisher, widow Roe) — 100 pounds and goods, "married about 7 years"; sons Richard(4) and Thomas(4) Cheesman (homestead and saw mill, split between them); daughter Mary Ann(4) Jackson (5 shillings, "already" given 400 acres); daughter **Deborah(4) Pierce**, wife of Henry Pierce (60 pounds) — not in this tree at all; daughter Margaret(4) Pierce, wife of William Pierce (10 pounds); grandson Jacob(5) Williams (via an unlisted child, John(4) Williams). No son "William" appears in the will — only a reference to "my brother William(3) Cheesman." [documented — will abstract, Adams manuscript via Long Island Surnames]
- **A real 1781 Gloucester Co. land deed** records "George Sennor... bought land, 80 acres, from Peter (3) and Martha (3) (Hedger) Cheesman" — independently placing Martha (still carrying her first husband's surname) as Peter's wife as late as 1781, well after Ann Ellis's 1759 death. [documented — Adams manuscript deed abstract]
- **Eliza Bolton's parents, Everard Bolton Jr. (b. abt. 1680, Ross, Herefordshire, England) and Mary Naylor, married 2 Apr 1707** — independently corroborated by WikiTree (Bolton-1090), which matches this tree's Everard Bolton Jr./Mary Naylor pairing exactly (same birthplace, same spouse). That profile lists only **one** documented child of the couple, Priscilla (Bolton) Conard — no Eliza. [documented (marriage/birthplace) / notably silent on Eliza — WikiTree Bolton-1090]

## Corrections to the tree

- **William Cheesman (b.1728) is not Martha's son**, and is now doubted as Peter Cheesman's son at all. His birth predates every attested wife/marriage of Peter's found in external sources (Ann Ellis's marriage, whether 1732 or 1746; Martha's own marriage, undated but demonstrably after 1759). He may belong to an unidentified true first wife of Peter's, or — per Peter's own will, which refers to "my brother William(3) Cheesman" (documented elsewhere as b. abt 1713 Hempstead, d. 1766 Gloucester Co. NJ, a son of Richard Cheesman Sr.) — he may be a misfiled/duplicated relative rather than a genuine 1728 child of Peter's. The birth years (1713 vs. 1728) don't match cleanly, so this is offered as a lead, not a confirmed identification.
- **Thomas Cheesman (b.1734) and Mary Ann Cheesman (b.1746) should be reattributed from Martha to Ann Hanna Ellis**, Peter's independently-documented second wife (b. 1710 England, d. 1759 NJ). This is not a borderline call: both children's exact birth/death dates match this tree precisely, and three separate, mutually cross-linked WikiTree profiles agree on the maternal attribution.
- **The "Bet 1746 and 1748" marriage date between Martha and Peter is very likely misattached** — probably copied from the (also uncertain) marriage date given for Ann Ellis in one secondary source ("c.1746"). The real, deed-documented timeline places Martha as Peter's wife by 1781, i.e. a late-in-life marriage after Ann Ellis's 1759 death, consistent with Martha's known status as a widow (of Joseph Hedger) by then.
- **Margaret Cheesman (b. Jan 1764) is the only one of the five tree-listed children still chronologically plausible as Martha's** (Martha would be ~44, and Ann Ellis had been dead five years) — but this is not independently confirmed; Peter's will names her married surname (Pierce) without stating her mother.
- **Thomas John Cheeseman (no birth or death date at all) is wholly unattested** in any external source checked (WikiTree, Find A Grave, FamilySearch, Geni, the Adams manuscript). Given this family's documented habit of repeating given names across generations, this may be a duplicate/ghost entry for Thomas Cheesman (b.1734) rather than a distinct sixth child.
- **The Eliza Bolton Taber → Martha Taber Hedger parent-child link is doubted, not either woman's individual birth year.** Martha's 1720 birth is independently corroborated (see Verified facts); Eliza's parents' 1707 marriage makes any birth year for Eliza compatible with the "she was Everard/Mary's biological daughter" premise incompatible with also being an adult mother in 1720 (at most ~12–13). No external source anywhere links an "Eliza Bolton" to a Benjamin Taber or to this Martha. The likeliest explanation, by analogy with this tree's other resolved cases of the same failure mode (Nancy Davis, gen 7; James McCue, gen 7), is a generic Ancestry Family Trees hint-match conflation rather than a real biological relationship. Martha's true parents are unknown.

## New findings (not in the tree)

- **Peter Cheesman had at least three, probably four, wives**: an unidentified first wife (William's likely mother, if William truly is Peter's son), Ann Hanna Ellis (2nd, m. 1732 or 1746, d. 1759 — mother of Thomas, Mary Ann, and Richard D), Martha Taber, widow Hedger (this record — documented as Peter's wife by 1781), and Christian Fisher, widow Roe (m. 5 Feb 1781, per the will "married about 7 years").
- **Two children of Peter's are entirely missing from this tree**: Richard D Cheesman (b. 16 Oct 1749, d. 3 Nov 1799, m. Rachel Williams — inherited the homestead jointly with Thomas) and Deborah Cheesman Pierce (m. Henry Pierce, 60 pounds in the will). Not added as new person records in this pass — no id assigned and no independently-confirmed exact vitals beyond the will abstract — but flagged for a future research pass.
- **Martha's real documented children were by her first husband, Joseph Hedger** (m. 1 Nov 1741, Philadelphia): Deborah (Hedger) Cheeseman and Maribeth (Hedger) Cheeseman (both b. 16 Mar 1738 — predating the 1741 marriage date on record, itself an unresolved minor inconsistency in the source, but not investigated further here). Maribeth's 1771 marriage to Thomas Cheesman (Peter's son by Ann Ellis) ties the Hedger and Cheesman families together independently of Martha's own marriage to Peter.
- **Peter Cheesman was a Gloucester County NJ Quaker landowner and miller**: he purchased 350 acres from Henry Roe in 1753, owned a saw mill and grist mill inherited from his father Richard Cheesman, served on the Gloucester County committee of observation in 1774, and the area around his holdings (junction of Cross Keys-Berlin Road and Turnersville-Sicklerville Road) became known as "Cheesmanville."
- **Richard Cheesman (Peter's father, I182195863536) married Hannah Stringham** on 10 Jan 1694 at Hempstead, Long Island, NY, and had at least eleven other children besides Peter (Naomi, Thomas, Deborah Anne, Hannah, Benjamin, Margaret, Richard Jr., William, Isabella, and others) — none of this marriage or sibling group is in the current tree record for Richard, which lists no spouse and only one child. [documented — WikiTree Cheesman-22]

## Open questions

- **William Cheesman's real mother/identity.** Is he a genuine, unnamed-first-wife child of Peter's, or a misfiled record of Peter's own brother William (b. abt 1713, d. 1766)? The birth years don't match cleanly (1728 vs. 1713), so neither answer is confirmed.
- **Ann Hanna Ellis's actual marriage date to Peter** — WikiTree says 1732; the Adams manuscript implies 1746. These can't both be right, and no primary marriage record was located to settle it.
- **Martha Taber Hedger's actual parents.** Her own WikiTree profile lists both as unknown; this tree's Benjamin Taber/Eliza Bolton attachment is unconfirmed and now doubted (see Corrections). Genuine Taber and Bolton parish/meeting records for Gloucester Co. NJ or Abington/Cheltenham, PA Quaker meetings were not accessible on the open web and could resolve this.
- **The exact date of Martha's marriage to Peter Cheesman** — bracketed only loosely here as "after Ann Ellis's 1759 death, by 1781" (the deed year). No marriage record itself was found.
- **Margaret Cheesman's mother** — chronologically plausible as Martha's, but not confirmed by any source; a birth/baptism record (if a Quaker meeting kept one) would settle it.
- **Thomas John Cheeseman's existence** — no external record found; possibly a duplicate of Thomas Cheesman (b.1734).
- **Richard D Cheesman and Deborah Cheesman Pierce** are documented in Peter's will but have no records at all in this tree — candidates for addition in a future pass.
- **Eliza Bolton Taber's own marriage to Benjamin Taber** ("bef 1723" in this tree) has no independent source and may simply be a machine-derived placeholder (a few years before Martha's own recorded birth) rather than an actual record — consistent with the broader doubt cast on the Eliza–Martha link above.
