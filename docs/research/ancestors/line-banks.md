# The Banks Line (Line 6) — Surname Survey, Generations 7–12

## Overview

The Banks line as recorded in the tree runs from George Banks (b. 1790, d. 1865, Annandale, Kings County, Prince Edward Island) back through five more generations of Roberts and a Joseph, terminating with a Robert Banks said to be born in Scotland in 1630. Independent research corroborates the line reasonably well for three generations on Prince Edward Island (George Banks, his father Robert Banks b. ~1764, and Robert's wife Mary McKie), largely via a compiled, unsourced-but-detailed descendancy chart on the Island Register (PEI genealogy site). Beyond Robert Banks (b. ~1764), the tree's claimed Scottish ancestry (generations 9–12, supposedly running 1739 back to 1630) could not be corroborated by any record found in this research, and it contains an internal geographic contradiction (Galloway/Dumfries-shire vs. Edinburgh/Lothian) that strongly suggests two unrelated Scottish Banks families have been merged. The documented line effectively ends at generation 8.

## Verified facts

- **George Banks** (gen. 7), b. 1790, d. 1865, buried/resided Annandale, PEI; married **Mary McKenzie** (spelled "McKenzie" in this source; b. St. Peters Bay, PEI, d. 1868). Children listed include Mary Ann, William, Margaret (b. 28 Apr 1816), John, Elizabeth, Ann, Jane, Ada, Sarah — matching the tree's "Mary Ann Banks," "William Banks," and "Margaret Swallow (Banks)" as three of many children, not the full set. — Source: *The Descendants of Robert Banks and Mary McKie*, submitted by Ira Smith, The Island Register (islandregister.com/banks1.html), a compiled/secondary amateur genealogy (no citations given, but internally detailed with dates and cemetery locations).
- **Margaret Banks** (b. 28 Apr 1816, daughter of George Banks) married **Robert Swallow**; their son John (Black John) Swallow appears in the same source — this independently confirms the tree's "Margaret Swallow (Banks)" as George's daughter, and connects to the tree's separately-documented Swallow line (see `gen06-robert-jr-swallow-jr-margaret-swallow-ba.md` already in this repository). — Source: islandregister.com/banks1.html.
- **Robert Banks** (gen. 8), b. ABT 1764, married **Mary McKie** on **8 January 1791**; Mary McKie was b. ABT 1773 "in Cassaend Kelton, Dumfries" [i.e., Kelton parish, Kirkcudbrightshire/Dumfries-shire border area, southwest Scotland], daughter of William McKie and Isabelle Boyce; Mary d. 1823. — Source: islandregister.com/banks1.html.
- Kelton parish (Kirkcudbrightshire) did maintain Old Parish Registers, but historical accounts note the register was kept irregularly and has gaps for parts of the 18th century (1726–1762 largely missing), which is consistent with why no baptism record for Robert Banks (b. 1739 per the tree) or Robert Banks (b. 1764) could be located online. — Source: kirkcudbright.co historical article on Kelton OPR baptisms; oldscottish.com Kelton parish page.
- A wave of emigrants from the Galloway/Solway coast (Kirkcudbright, Kelton, Carsethorn, Annan area) sailed to Prince Edward Island on the ship **Lovely Nelly** in 1774 and again in 1775 — the so-called "Dumfries(-shire) Settlers," landing 23 Aug 1774 and settling largely at Lot 59. This is a real, well-documented emigration wave geographically consistent with a Kelton-area Banks family's origin story. — Source: islandregister.com/nellie1774.html and nellie1775.html (Island Register ship-passenger transcriptions, ultimately drawn from Viola Root Cameron's *Emigrants from Scotland to America, 1774–1775*).

## Corrections to the tree

- **George Banks's wife's name**: tree says "Margaret Mackenzie" (m. 1810) -> compiled PEI genealogy says **Mary McKenzie**, b. St. Peters Bay PEI, d. 1868. First name and marriage year both differ from the tree, and the wife may have been PEI-born (St. Peters Bay) rather than a Scottish immigrant as the tree's phrasing might suggest. Evidence: islandregister.com/banks1.html. Note that a Geni.com profile for "Margaret MacKenzie (1814–)" also turned up in searches, which may be the actual source of the tree's "Margaret Mackenzie" entry — if so, the tree may have conflated two different MacKenzie women (this could not be resolved from available sources and should be treated as an open question, not a confirmed correction).
- **Robert Banks (gen. 8) death year**: tree gives 1823 for Robert Banks himself. The Island Register genealogy gives **1823 as the death year of his wife Mary McKie**, not of Robert Banks (whose death date is not stated in that source). This looks like a duplicate/attribution artifact: the wife's death year appears to have migrated onto the husband's record, and/or been copied onto both Robert Banks records in the tree (see next item). Treat "Robert Banks d. 1823" (gen. 8) as unverified; "Mary McKie d. 1823" is the better-supported fact.
- **Duplicate death year across two generations**: the tree lists BOTH Robert Banks (gen. 8, b. 1764) and his purported father Robert Banks (gen. 9, b. 1739) as dying in **the same year, 1823** — one "in Prince Edward Island, Canada," the other more specifically "at Bay Fortune, PEI." Two men of the same name, one generation apart, dying in the identical year is a classic duplicate-record/merge artifact rather than a documented coincidence. Combined with the previous point (1823 is independently attested only for Mary McKie), this date has likely been copied across three different tree entries. Flag: **duplicate-record artifact, needs disambiguation against original source (Ancestry Family Trees, unsourced).**

## New findings

- The compiled Island Register genealogy extends the family considerably beyond what's in the tree: Robert Banks (b. ~1764) and Mary McKie had at least **seven** children (George, William b. 1 Jan 1794, Isabella b. 16 May 1796, James b. 2 Mar 1803 d. 29 May 1902, Jane b. 22 Feb 1807 m. John Webster, John b. ~1819 d. 1891, and a second Robert who married Catherine Webster), not just the three (Mary Ann, William, Margaret) captured in the tree's "children" list for George Banks's father-generation entry — actually the tree's children list under George Banks (gen 7) shown in the source data ("Mary Ann Banks," "William Banks," "Margaret Swallow (Banks)") corresponds to only 3 of George's own many children, not siblings — this is a labeling/scope note, not a contradiction, but the tree is very incomplete for this generation's sibling set.
- Family concentration in **Annandale, Bay Fortune, Howe Bay, and St. Peters Bay**, Kings County, PEI, with descendants later emigrating to **Massachusetts** ("d. 1916 in USA," "d. 11 Jan 1926 in Mass.") and Boston-area marriages (Robert Chaffey m. Margaret "Maggie" Banks, 1873, Boston) — a common secondary emigration pattern ("Boston States") for PEI families in the later 19th century.
- The 1774/1775 Lovely Nelly passenger-list transcriptions (as posted on the Island Register) were checked directly and **contain no passenger named Banks** in either year. A search-engine-generated summary elsewhere asserted "Robert and Mary Banks came on the Lovely Nellie... in 1774 and made their home in Annandale," but this could not be traced to any primary or even named secondary source, and is inconsistent with the fact that Robert Banks (b. ~1764) would have been a small child in 1774 and did not marry Mary McKie until 1791. This claim should be treated as **unverified internet lore, likely a garbled/AI-synthesized claim, not evidence** — it is flagged here specifically so it is not repeated as fact.

## Open questions

- **Where does the documented line end?** Solidly documented (with a named, if uncited, compiler): George Banks (gen. 7) and his parents Robert Banks (gen. 8) and Mary McKie. Robert Banks's own parentage — the tree's "Robert Banks & Marion Moir" (gen. 9), and everything above them (gens 10–12: Robert Banks/Margaret Gray, Joseph Banks/Helene Lindsay, Robert Banks/Margrat Aiken) — has **no corroborating record of any kind** found in this research (no OPR baptism, no secondary compiled genealogy, no WikiTree/Geni match). These four generations should be treated as **unsupported / likely tree lore** pending someone actually pulling the Kelton OPR film or a ScotlandsPeople search.
- **Geographic contradiction flags likely conflation**: gen. 8–9 are placed in Kelton parish, Kirkcudbrightshire/Dumfries-shire (southwest Scotland, Galloway region) — consistent with the real Lovely Nelly "Dumfries(-shire) Settlers" emigration wave to PEI. But gen. 10 (Robert Banks, b. 1707) is placed in **Edinburgh Parish, Midlothian**, and gen. 11 (Joseph Banks, b. 1666) in **Abercorn, West Lothian** — both in east-central Scotland, ~90+ miles from Kelton, a different historical county and a different social/geographic milieu. There is no known reason a Kelton-area family of gen. 8–9 should have Lothian-area ancestors two generations back with no transitional record. This pattern (an unsourced Ancestry-style tree jumping from a plausible local family to a distant, unrelated same-surname family) is the single most common cause of bogus "deep" lineages in crowd-sourced trees. Recommend treating gens. 10–12 as **very likely a false attachment** unless a specific will, sasine, or OPR record can be produced linking Robert Banks (b. 1707, Edinburgh) to Robert Banks (b. 1739, Kelton).
- Was the family's emigration ship actually identified? Neither of the two known Kelton-area emigrant sailings (Lovely Nelly, 1774 and 1775) lists any Banks passenger. If Robert Banks (b. 1764) and/or his parents emigrated as part of the Galloway wave of Scottish settlement of PEI, the specific vessel and year are not yet established.
- Is "Margaret Mackenzie" (tree) the same woman as "Mary McKenzie" (Island Register)? Given the tree also independently lists a "Margaret MacKenzie (1814–)" showing up in a Geni search, there may be two different MacKenzie wives being conflated, or simply an error in one source's transcription of the given name. Unresolved.
- No death date is recorded anywhere found for Robert Banks (gen. 8, the emigrant); his own death is undocumented beyond the tree's unsourced 1823 (which more likely belongs to his wife).

## Duplicate-record artifact summary

This line shows the pattern flagged in the task brief: **the same death year (1823) has been assigned to three different people** in the tree/source ecosystem — Robert Banks gen. 8 ("d. 1823, PEI"), Robert Banks gen. 9 ("d. 28 Oct 1823, Bay Fortune, PEI"), and (per the Island Register) Mary McKie, wife of Robert Banks gen. 8 ("d. 1823"). Only the last is corroborated by an outside source. Any future merge/cleanup of this tree should treat the two "Robert Banks d. 1823" entries as suspect duplicates or misattributions rather than confirming both independently.
