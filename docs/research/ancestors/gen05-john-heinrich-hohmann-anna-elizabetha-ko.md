# John Heinrich Hohmann (1832–1911) & Anna Elizabetha Kohlhepp (d. 1864)

Generation 5 direct ancestors (parents of Franklin "Frank" P. S. Hohmann, grandparents of Bertha E. Hohmann Albertson). Researched 2026-07-03 from web-accessible primary indexes, digitized city directories, and compiled sources. Builds on the earlier repo dossiers `gen04-franklin-p-s-hohmann-mary-a-crist.md` and `hohmann-line.md`.

## Overview

John Heinrich Hohmann, born 11 August 1832 in the Electorate of Hesse (Hesse-Kassel/Kurhessen), emigrated to America by the mid-1850s and worked as a **cordwainer (shoemaker) in the German districts of Philadelphia**, where five city-directory listings (1859–1867) now place him — consistent with son Franklin's ~1855 Philadelphia birth. His death on 6 April 1911 in Bucks County, PA is now **independently verified in the Pennsylvania official death index (certificate #36432)**. His first wife Anna Elizabetha Kohlhepp remains almost invisible in the records: born in Germany ~1825–1828, dead 6 February 1864 (per the tree, unsourced), most plausibly in Philadelphia — her death certificate is the key unobtained document.

## Verified facts

- **John's death: 6 Apr 1911, Bucks County, PA — VERIFIED against the state death index.** Pennsylvania Death Indices 1911, PA State Archives (free PDF, surnames H–I–J, image p. 143): **"Hohman, John; 36432; Bucks Co.; Apr. 6."** Exact match to the tree's sourced date and place (Falls Twp. is in Bucks Co.). This confirms the tree's citation of *Pennsylvania, U.S., Death Certificates, 1906-1970* and supplies the certificate number: **file #36432 (1911)**.
- **John's residence and trade, Philadelphia 1859–1867 — DOCUMENTED (identity probable, see caveat).** McElroy's Philadelphia City Directory, full-text searched on Archive.org:
  - 1859 (p. 356): "Hohman John H., Nolan's ct"
  - 1860 (p. 496): "Hohmann John H., cordwainer, Nolen's ct near [Coates/Franklin]"
  - 1861 (p. 499): "Hohman John H., cordwainer, 1 Nolen's ct"
  - 1865 (p. 361): "Hohmann John H., cordwainer, Orkney bel Susquehanna av"
  - 1867 (p. 446): "Hohman John, shoemaker, 1728 Callowhill"
  Nolen's/Nolan's Court and Orkney Street lie in the Northern Liberties/Kensington German quarter. *Caveat:* no directory entry names his wife or occupation-independent identifier, so the identification rests on the initials "John H.", the German neighborhood, the shoemaker trade continuity, and the family's documented Philadelphia link (Franklin b. ~1855, "tree says Philadelphia"). Treat as probable, not certain.
- **John is absent from Gopsill's Philadelphia directory 1871** (only "Hohmann Frederick, shoes, 704 St John" appears, p. 747) — consistent with John leaving Philadelphia between 1867 and 1871, presumably toward the Morrisville/Falls Township, Bucks Co. area where he died and where his son's family settled. (Negative evidence; OCR gaps possible.)
- **No Trenton newspaper obituary indexed for him in 1911.** Trenton Historical Society 1911 obituary index contains no Hohman(n) — only a Hoffman, Nicholas (d. 3/27/1911). Consistent with a quiet death in rural Falls Township.
- **Birth 11 Aug 1832, Hesse-Kassel** — accepted from the tree's sourced entry (the death certificate presumably supplied it); not independently re-verified this session. No parish of origin is documented anywhere.

## Corrections to the tree

- **Parent "John Henry Hohman" → REMOVE (refuted splice).** The tree still attaches a father "John Henry Hohman" to John Heinrich. Per this repo's own deep-dive (`hohmann-line.md`), that person is John Henry Hohman of Braddock, PA, **born 1880** — 48 years *after* his supposed son — an accepted-hint chain error involving an unrelated Catholic family (All Saints Braddock cemetery). John Heinrich's parents are **unknown**; no German generation above him is documented.
- **Anna's "birth Abt. 1828 @ Prussia" — the "Prussia" label is an artifact, not a contradiction.** Hesse-Kassel was annexed by Prussia in 1866, so any post-1866 American record for a Hesse-Kassel native routinely says "Prussia." Her three tree birth entries (Germany n.d. / abt. 1828 Prussia / 1825) are one person; the 1825 vs 1828 conflict is unresolved — keep "abt. 1825–1828, Hesse (Germany)".
- No date corrections: the one sourced fact checked (death 6 Apr 1911, Bucks Co.) is confirmed by the state index.

## New findings (not in the tree)

- **Occupation: cordwainer/shoemaker** (McElroy's 1860, 1861, 1865; "shoemaker" 1867). Not recorded in the tree.
- **Philadelphia addresses:** Nolan's/Nolen's Court (1859–1861), Orkney below Susquehanna Ave. (1865), 1728 Callowhill St. (1867, probable).
- **Death certificate number: 36432 (1911, Bucks Co.)** — orderable from the PA State Archives / viewable on Ancestry's *Pennsylvania Death Certificates 1906-1970*; it should name his parents, exact birthplace, informant (likely a child), and burial place.
- **Probable kinsman: Frederich C. Hohmann (Oct 1825, Hessen – 23 Aug 1894, Philadelphia), also a shoemaker** ("Hohmann Frederick, shoemaker, 704 St John" in McElroy's 1867; "shoes, 704 St John" in Gopsill's 1871; WikiTree Hohmann-160, wife Margaret Becker b. 1825 Hessen, daughter Elizabeth b. 1865). Same rare-enough surname, same trade, same Hessian origin, same city and era as John Heinrich — a plausible elder brother or cousin. **Inferred only**; no record yet links the two households.
- **Anna Elizabetha most plausibly died in Philadelphia, 6 Feb 1864.** The directory run places the household in Philadelphia continuously 1859–1865 spanning her death date. Her death should therefore appear in *Philadelphia City Death Certificates, 1803-1915* (FamilySearch, login required) — the single best next record for her, expected to give her age, cause, address, and burial cemetery (likely a German Lutheran/Reformed ground such as Palmer, Hanover St., or Glenwood).
- **Timeline synthesis (new):** b. 1832 Hesse-Kassel → emigration ~1848–1855 (unlocated) → Philadelphia shoemaker, Kensington/Northern Liberties, by 1859 → widowed Feb 1864 → still Philadelphia 1865–1867 → gone from Philadelphia by 1871 → died Falls Twp., Bucks Co. 1911, age 78. Son Franklin died before him (1898); grandchildren were in Morrisville, directly adjacent to Falls Twp. — John's last decades were likely spent near or with them.

## Open questions

1. **Every census 1860–1910 is unchecked** — the indexes (1860/1870 Philadelphia; 1880/1900/1910 presumably Bucks Co.) sit behind FamilySearch/Ancestry logins, which this session could not pass. The 1900 census would give his immigration year and month/year of birth; 1910 would confirm the Falls Twp. household he died in. Highest-value follow-up after the death certificate.
2. **Order death certificate #36432 (1911, Bucks Co.)** — parents' names (tests the whole German origin), burial place, informant.
3. **Anna Elizabetha's death record, Feb 1864** — Philadelphia City Death Certificates 1803-1915 (FamilySearch film/index). Also settles her age (1825 vs 1828) and maybe her burial.
4. **Did John remarry after 1864?** A widower at 31 with small children almost certainly did. Philadelphia marriage indexes 1860-1885 and the 1870 census household would show a second wife and any half-siblings of Franklin.
5. **Where is John buried?** No Find A Grave memorial found for him (searches for Hohman(n) in Bucks Co./Morrisville/Fallsington). Morrisville Cemetery — where daughter-in-law Mary Ann (Crist) Hohmann lies (FG #189161414) — is the natural candidate; the USGenWeb Morrisville survey is only partial and has no Hohman. The death certificate will answer this.
6. **Immigration and naturalization** — not found. Castle Garden's search site was down this session; Philadelphia naturalization index (Filby 1982) is lending-restricted on Archive.org. Hamburg/Bremen lists and the Hessian emigration permits (HesAus/LAGIS, now restructured at lagis.hessen.de) need a parish of origin to be effective.
7. **Parish of origin in Hesse-Kassel** — unknown. The 11 Aug 1832 date is precise enough to find a baptism once a Kreis/parish is suggested (death cert, naturalization, or church burial record may supply it).
8. **Anna's mother "Christina Kohlepp"** (tree) — wholly unverified; no Kohlhepp of this family found in any free index this session. Note the tree's own alias "(Cullip, etc.)" matches the documented American variant chain of the older Philadelphia Kohlhepp/Cullop family (see gen04 dossier, Geni: Peter Caspar Kohlhepp) — possibly the reason the tree links her there, but no evidence connects a Hesse-born ~1825 Anna Elizabetha to that colonial line.
