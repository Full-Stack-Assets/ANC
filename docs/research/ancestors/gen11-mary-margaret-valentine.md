# Mary Margaret Valentine (abt. 1645–abt. 1710/1720) — Generation 11

## Overview

Mary Margaret Valentine was born about 1645 in Hempstead, Nassau Co., Long
Island, New York, daughter of Richard Valentine (of Eccles, Lancashire, and
later Hempstead) and, most likely, Deborah Mott, and died about 1710–1720,
probably still in Hempstead. She married Thomas Cheesman/Cheeseman (b. 1640
Kingston-upon-Thames, England; d. 1713 Long Island), and their son Richard
Cheesman (b. 1674, Hempstead) went on to marry Hannah Stringham in 1694 and
father Peter C. Cheesman (1704–1788) — the same Peter Cheesman already
identified in this tree's research as the husband of **Martha Taber Hedger**
(see `docs/research/ancestors/gen09-martha-taber-hedger-eliza-bolton-taber.md`).
As with several other branches of this tree, Mary Margaret Valentine's entire
family cluster — herself, her parents, and her Cheesman descendants — was
imported twice in parallel, producing two near-identical person records for
her. This dossier identifies which copy is the one that actually connects to
this tree's already-verified Cheesman/Taber material.

## Verified facts

- **Mary Margaret Valentine, b. abt. 1645 Hempstead, d. abt. 1710–1720**, daughter of Richard Valentine and (probably) Deborah Mott, wife of Thomas Cheesman. [documented — WikiTree Valentine-570]
- **Her father, Richard Valentine of Eccles, Lancashire**, is documented as a proven immigrant ancestor of the New England Valentines: he settled first at Wethersfield, Hartford Co., Connecticut (1634), then joined the New Haven colony before crossing Long Island Sound to Hempstead by 1644. He was a proprietor of Hempstead. [documented — rootsweb ~valvz Valentine research]
- **Richard Valentine's unrecorded 1682 Hempstead will** (held in the Long Island Historical Society collection, per the rootsweb Valentine research) explicitly names "his daughter, Mary, wife of Thomas Cheesman" — an independent, will-based confirmation of exactly the marriage recorded in this tree. [documented — rootsweb ~valvz, citing LIHS collection]
- **His wife's name was probably Deborah** (researchers' best reconstruction, also a name that recurs in the following generation); she was still living in 1704/5, when her son Richard made provision for her. [inferred — rootsweb ~valvz Valentine research]
- **Mary Margaret Valentine's son, Richard Cheesman (b. 9 Nov 1674, Hempstead; d. 2 Sep 1744, Cheeseman Plantation, Waterford Twp., Gloucester Co., NJ)**, married **Hannah Stringham on 10 January 1694** at Hempstead — independently confirmed by two separate external sources (WikiTree Cheesman-22, already used in this tree's gen 9 Martha Taber Hedger research, and genealogieonline.nl's "From Castles to America" database) which agree on his parents (Thomas Cheesman + Mary Margaret Valentine), his marriage date, and a twelve-child family including **Peter C. Cheesman (1704–1788)**. [documented — WikiTree Cheesman-22; genealogieonline.nl]
- **Peter C. Cheesman (I182195863485)** — Richard's son — is the same person already researched in this tree's gen 9 dossier as the (multiply-married) husband of Martha Taber Hedger, resolving a *parent-too-young* flag on her record. That dossier independently sourced Richard Cheesman's marriage to Hannah Stringham (per WikiTree Cheesman-22) without at the time needing to resolve Mary Margaret Valentine's own duplication — this dossier closes that loop. [documented — internal cross-reference + WikiTree]

## Corrections to the tree

- **Duplicate-person, whole-family double-import**: `I182197729576` (unsourced, parents `I182541956481`/`I182198586989`, spouse `I182197729555`, child `I182195863536`) and `I182625986323` (unsourced, parents `I182625986581`/`I182625986572`, spouse `I182625986319`, child `I182625986316`) are the same Mary Margaret Valentine. This is not an isolated duplication: her parents (two "Richard Valentine"/"Deborah Mott" pairs with matching vitals), her husband (two "Thomas Cheesman" records, both b.1640/d.1713 Kingston-upon-Thames/Long Island), and her son (two "Richard Cheesman" records, both b.9 Nov 1674/d.2 Sep 1744) are all duplicated in lockstep — the same whole-branch double-import pattern already documented for the Samuel Jacobsson (gen 7) and Britta Gabrielsdotter (gen 8) cases in this tree.
- **Canonical record: `I182197729576`.** Its child, Richard Cheesman `I182195863536`, is the copy whose own son is Peter C Cheesman `I182195863485` — already the subject of substantial, source-cited research in `gen09-martha-taber-hedger-eliza-bolton-taber.md`. Tracing canonicalness to the verified descendant (the same method used throughout this tree's other duplicate-family resolutions) makes this branch the one to keep as primary, even though, like its counterparts in those other cases, it carries no source citation of its own.
- **Duplicate record: `I182625986323`.** Its child, "Richard Cheesman Sr" `I182625986316`, continues to a differently-numbered grandson, "Peter C Cheeseman" `I182625986293` (same name, same 1704–1788 dates as the canonical Peter, but a separate id) — a parallel branch that terminates without connecting anywhere else in this tree's already-established, externally-corroborated material.

## New findings (not in the tree)

- **Richard Valentine's origin story**: Eccles, Lancashire → Wethersfield, CT (1634) → New Haven colony → Hempstead, Long Island (by 1644). None of this migration history is present in either tree copy of his record.
- **Richard Valentine's 1682 will**, naming his daughter Mary as "wife of Thomas Cheesman" — a primary-source-adjacent confirmation of the Valentine–Cheesman marriage not reflected in this tree's (sourceless) records for either spouse.
- **Richard Cheesman's marriage to Hannah Stringham (10 Jan 1694, Hempstead)** and their twelve children — present in the canonical branch's already-existing gen 9 research but confirmed again independently here via genealogieonline.nl.

## Open questions

- **Deborah Mott as Richard Valentine's wife** remains a "best guess," per the rootsweb Valentine research itself, not a certainty — no baptismal or marriage record naming her outright was located on the open web in this pass.
- **Mary Margaret Valentine's exact death date** is inconsistently given across sources: this tree says 1720, while the external Valentine research says "died after 1710, presumably in Hempstead" without a firm end date. Neither figure is independently pinned down by a primary record found in this pass.
- **The duplicate branch's grandson, "Peter C Cheeseman" (`I182625986293`)**, shares Peter C Cheesman's exact birth/death dates (16 Jun 1704 – 14 Jun 1788) with the canonical branch's Peter — suggesting the duplication persists at least one more generation down that parallel branch; not further investigated in this pass since it falls outside the two flagged records.
- **WikiTree's Valentine-570 and Cheesman-22 profile pages** returned blank content to automated fetching in this pass (likely JavaScript-rendered); the information used here comes from search-engine result summaries rather than a direct page read, so the underlying primary citations WikiTree itself relies on were not independently re-verified line-by-line.
