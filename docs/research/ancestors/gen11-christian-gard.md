# Christian Gard (1670–1708) and Peter Coffin Sr — Generation 11

## Overview

Christian Gard was born 31 May 1670 in Boston, Suffolk Co., Massachusetts, and died 15 March 1708 in Nantucket, Massachusetts. She was the first wife of Peter Coffin Sr (b. 5 Aug 1671, d. 27 Aug 1749), a grandson of Nantucket founder Tristram Coffyn. This gen-11 couple exists **twice** in the tree data, in lockstep with their son Bartlett Coffin and his own wife Judith Mary Bunker — a whole-family double-import of the same kind already resolved elsewhere in this tree (John Albertson & Ann Pine, Samuel Jacobsson, Joseph Hibbard III & Hannah Pond, etc.). One copy of the couple carries Christian's parents and continues into the tree's documented, unbroken direct line; the other has no parents and dead-ends two generations down.

## Verified facts

- **Christian Gard was a real historical person: the first wife of Peter Coffin Sr of Nantucket.** External aggregator summaries drawing on WikiTree (Coffin-645), Geni, and FamilySearch consistently describe Peter Coffin (b. 5 Aug 1671, d. 27 Aug 1749, Nantucket) as first married to "Christian (Gard/Guard) Conde/Condey" (marriage intention recorded 18 Jul 1695) and, after her death, to Hope Gardner (daughter of Joseph Gardner and Bethia Macy) on 15 March 1708 — **the same year this record gives for Christian's death**. This is strong independent corroboration: a man does not typically remarry within the same calendar year unless recently widowed. [documented — external aggregator sources; see Sources]
- **Bill Putman's "THE COFFIN FAMILY, England to Nantucket"** (a narrative genealogy tracing Tristram Coffyn's descendants) independently confirms: "PETER COFFIN was born August 5, 1671. He was first married to Christian Condy and then to Hope Gardner, daughter of Joseph Gardner and Bethia Macy. Their children were: Bartlett, Lydia, Abner, Tristram, Robert, Peter, Margaret, Jerusha, Hannah and Joseph." This confirms **Bartlett** as the eldest-named child, matching this tree's son Bartlett Coffin (b. abt. 1699, Nantucket). [documented]
- **Bartlett Coffin's real children include both Rebecca and Uriah.** islandregister.com's "Descendants of Uriah Coffin and Hepsabeth Bunker" independently documents Uriah Coffin (b. 2 Jan 1739, Nantucket) as son of "Bartlett Coffin #581 and Judith Bunker #852," and separately notes a daughter Rebecca Coffin of the same couple (who married a Benjamin Coffin). Both children are genuinely Bartlett's and Judith's — the "duplication" in this tree is not a duplicated child, but the same two real parents imported twice with their real children split across the two copies. [documented]
- **Her exact maiden surname is a genuine, unresolved ambiguity in the source record tradition**, not a tree-specific invention: aggregator summaries render her name variously as "Christian Gard," "Christian Guard," "Christian Condy," and "Christian Conde," with one search snippet naming her mother as "Mary Conde." "Gard/Guard" and "Condy/Conde" are phonetically and orthographically unrelated, so this is not a simple transcription slip — it likely reflects confusion between two early Nantucket/Boston records, or an unresolved discrepancy already present in compiled secondary genealogies (Vital Records of Nantucket to 1850, NEHGS). No primary-source (vital-record-page) confirmation could be reached on the open web. [inferred / open question]

## Corrections to the tree

- No factual correction to Christian Gard's own name or dates is being made — both duplicate copies already agree with each other and with the external record on her birth (1670), death (1708), and husband. The issue is purely the duplication itself (see below).

## New findings (not in the tree)

- **Christian's death and Peter's remarriage are the same year (1708)** — not previously connected in the tree data (neither copy of Christian's record notes Peter's second marriage), but this is the strongest single piece of corroboration that the tree's death year is correct.
- **Ten children total** are attributed to Peter Coffin across both marriages in the Putman genealogy (Bartlett, Lydia, Abner, Tristram, Robert, Peter, Margaret, Jerusha, Hannah, Joseph) — only Bartlett is represented in this tree's data (via both duplicate copies). The other nine are not in this dataset; not fabricated here.
- **Christian's parents (per the canonical tree copy only):** William Gard (b. 1642, Massachusetts, d. 28 Sep 1685) and Mary Mariah Jameson (b. 28 Nov 1647, Boston — baptized at the First Church of Boston, daughter of James and Sarah Jameson, "proven by the deed recording the sale by her mother of a house lot in Boston to her husband in 1677 [SD Vol. 11, p. 216]" i.e. Suffolk Deeds). This parentage is internal to the tree only — no external source was found independently linking a "Christian Gard" to a "William Gard," so it should be treated as unverified/tree-only pending confirmation. [inferred]

## Duplicate-person resolution

Two records share the name "Christian Gard" and identical birth/death data (b. 31 May 1670, Boston; d. 15 Mar 1708, Nantucket):

| Child | Bartlett Coffin `I182381536414` | Bartlett Coffin `I182381538638` (duplicate of the same man) |
| Grandchild(ren) | Rebecca Coffin `I182381536384` → **continues the line** (m. spouse `I182528279309`, child Elisha Coffin Sr `I182381536354`, who continues to the home person) | Uriah Coffin `I182381538618` → **dead end** in this dataset (no spouse or children recorded) |

**Canonical: `I182381536421`.** It is the copy with parents attached, and its descent through Bartlett Coffin → Rebecca Coffin → Elisha Coffin Sr → (onward, already established as unbroken and part of the direct line to the home person, `I182195856751`). The duplicate, `I182381539281`, has no parents and its own descent line (via a different real child, Uriah Coffin) terminates with no further generations recorded in this dataset — consistent with it being the "extra," less-completely-imported copy rather than the branch this tree actually traces.

This is the same double-import pattern documented elsewhere in this tree (John Albertson & Ann Pine, gen 6; Samuel Jacobsson, gen 7; Joseph Hibbard III & Hannah Pond, gen 7/8): an entire small family — here, Christian Gard + Peter Coffin Sr + their son Bartlett Coffin + Bartlett's wife Judith Mary Bunker — was imported twice from the same or overlapping source trees, with different (but both real) grandchildren surviving in each copy.

`manual.notes` were added to all four core records (`I182381536421`, `I182381539281`, `I182381536434`, `I182381539273`) documenting the canonical/duplicate determination. The Peter Coffin Sr duplication (`I182381536434`/`I182381539273`) was already separately flagged in `docs/research/deep_dive_backlog.md` (not in `direct-line-issues.md`'s curated audit list); this write-up resolves it as a byproduct of resolving Christian Gard, since it is the same couple.

## Open questions

- **Christian's true maiden surname** — Gard/Guard vs. Condy/Conde — could not be settled from open-web sources; would require the actual Nantucket or Boston vital-record page (Vital Records of Nantucket, Massachusetts, to the Year 1850, NEHGS 1925-1928; or Suffolk/Essex Co. records) rather than secondary aggregator summaries.
- **Christian's parents (William Gard + Mary Mariah Jameson)** are unverified externally; no source ties a "Gard" family specifically to Christian. Worth checking Suffolk Co. deed and probate records (the "SD Vol. 11 p. 216" citation on Mary Jameson's own record is real and could be traced to confirm or refute the link).
- Whether the "extra" nine Coffin children (Lydia, Abner, Tristram, Robert, Peter, Margaret, Jerusha, Hannah, Joseph) belong anywhere else in this tree's data was not investigated — out of scope for this dossier.
