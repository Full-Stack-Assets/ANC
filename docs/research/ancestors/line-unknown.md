# The "Unknown" Line — Eight Unrelated Brick-Wall Ancestors (Generations 7–12)

## Overview

Unlike the tree's other "surname lines," this group is **not a single continuous paternal lineage**. It is a bucket of eight disconnected women (generations 7–12, roughly 1580s–1780s) whom the tree software filed under the placeholder surname "Unknown" — almost certainly because each is a wife whose own maiden name is unrecorded or unresolved in the source data. Each marries into (or is the mother of) a person from a *different* documented surname line: MacDonald (Scotland), Elwell (Massachusetts/New Jersey), Eliston (England), Bowen (England), Merrick/Myrick (Massachusetts), Rozzi/Guglielmi (Italy), Southgate (England), and Groff (Pennsylvania). There is no genealogical chain running from generation 7 to generation 12 — these are eight independent dead ends at different points in different branches. Only two of the eight (the Elwell and Merrick/Myrick connections) touch families with substantial, checkable published genealogies; the rest could not be corroborated beyond the tree's own (unsourced) entries.

## Verified facts

- The Elwell family of Gloucester, Massachusetts, and Salem County, New Jersey, is documented in two published genealogies: *The Elwell Family in America* (Jacob Thomas Elwell & Charles Henry Pope, 1899, Internet Archive) and *The Elwell Family: Seventeenth and Eighteenth Centuries, Southern New Jersey* (Elmer Garfield Van Name, Internet Archive). Per Van Name's text: "Thomas (3) Elwell" (b. Gloucester 26 Apr 1678, son of Thomas(2)/grandson of immigrant Robert Elwell) moved to Salem County, NJ with his father, purchased land there 19 Jan 1699, married a wife recorded only as "Susanna ———" (surname not given), and made his will 14 Aug 1722 (probated 17 Dec 1722). — [archive.org/stream/elwellfamilyseve00vann](https://archive.org/stream/elwellfamilyseve00vann/elwellfamilyseve00vann_djvu.txt), [archive.org/details/elwellfamilyinam00elwe](https://archive.org/details/elwellfamilyinam00elwe)
- The same source states that "Samuel (4) Elwell... did marry his first cousin, Susanna [Elwell]," daughter of the Thomas (3) Elwell above — i.e., a documented cousin marriage between two Elwells in the Salem County, NJ branch, matching the tree's structure of "Susanna Elwell" (daughter of a Thomas Elwell) marrying a Samuel Elwell. — same source as above
- Thomas Merrick, immigrant ancestor of the New England Merrick/Mirick/Myrick family, was born in Wales about **1620** and emigrated to Massachusetts in **1636** with three brothers, settling at Newbury; he married (1) Sarah Stebbins 14 Jul 1639 and (2) Elizabeth Tilley, and died 7 Sep 1704. He is treated at length in George Byron Merrick, *Genealogy of the Merrick–Mirick–Myrick Family of Massachusetts, 1636–1902* (1902), full text on Internet Archive. — [archive.org/details/genealogyofmerri00merr](https://archive.org/details/genealogyofmerri00merr), [WikiTree Merrick-10](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Merrick-10)
- The town of Newbury, Massachusetts, was not founded/settled until **1635**. — general historical fact, corroborated by the Merrick genealogy's own migration date of 1636.
- Casalgrande (with its frazione Dinazzano) is a real municipality in the Province of Reggio Emilia, Emilia-Romagna, Italy — the place name given for Maria Rozzi's death is geographically valid, even though no record of her specifically was located. — [Casalgrande, Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Casalgrande)
- Elton is a real village/parish in Huntingdonshire, England (Oundle district), consistent with the place given for Elizabeth's death. — [FamilySearch: Elton, Huntingdonshire](https://www.familysearch.org/en/wiki/Elton,_Huntingdonshire,_England_Genealogy)
- Emigration from Benbecula/South Uist to Cape Breton, Nova Scotia and Prince Edward Island was a well-documented mass movement in the early 19th century (~300 people/year at points), the expected context for a MacDonald family recorded there in 1783 — but this is background history, not a record of Mary or Neil MacDonald specifically. — [Western Isles genealogy/emigration blog](https://westernisles.wordpress.com/emigration/), [Island Register (PEI)](https://www.islandregister.com/gindex.html)

## Corrections to the tree

- **Margaret ("Unknown"), b. 1612 Newbury, Essex, Massachusetts → chronologically impossible as written.** Newbury, MA did not exist until 1635, and her stated parents (Thomas Merrick and Mary Griggs) cannot be correct as given: the documented immigrant Thomas Merrick of the Merrick/Mirick/Myrick line was himself born about **1620** in Wales — eight years *after* his supposed daughter Margaret. Evidence: Merrick genealogy (1902) and WikiTree Merrick-10 give Thomas Merrick's birth as c.1620, immigration 1636, and list his known wives as Sarah Stebbins and Elizabeth Tilley — no wife named "Mary Griggs" and no daughter "Margaret" appear in the 1902 genealogy's index of children. This entry looks like a **duplicate/merge artifact**: either a different, unrelated Margaret has been spliced onto the wrong Thomas Merrick, or a Massachusetts-based descendant has been conflated with an English-period ancestor. Recommend: unmerge and re-source, or drop the Newbury birthplace/parentage pending a real record.
- **"Susanna Post" as mother of Susanna Elwell → unsupported by the published Elwell genealogies.** Both the 1899 Elwell genealogy and the 1919-era Van Name New Jersey genealogy record Thomas (3) Elwell's wife only as "Susanna ———," maiden name not given. "Post" does not appear anywhere in either text in connection with this couple. This should be treated as **unsourced tree lore** unless a supporting record (parish register, will, deed) can be produced.
- **"Samuel Jacob Elwell" — middle name "Jacob" not found in the published genealogy.** The Van Name text calls him simply "Samuel (4) Elwell." The middle name may come from a later (uncited) record but could not be verified here.

## New findings

- The cousin marriage in the tree (Susanna Elwell to Samuel [Jacob] Elwell) is independently corroborated as a real, documented event in the Salem County, NJ Elwell family — this is a genuine finding beyond what the bare tree data states, since the tree gives no source for this couple.
- Thomas Elwell (the father, tree's "Thomas Elwell Jr") is identifiable as "Thomas (3) Elwell" of the published genealogy: born Gloucester, MA 26 Apr 1678, migrated with his father to Salem County, NJ, bought land there 19 Jan 1699, willed 14 Aug 1722/probated 17 Dec 1722. This gives a firm anchor date range (fl. 1699–1722) for that branch that is absent from the tree's own (source-less) entry.
- No record could be found anywhere online (WikiTree, FamilySearch, Geni, Google Books, Archive.org) for: Ann/James Eliston beyond the bare FamilySearch index entry already cited in the tree; Grace/George Bowen of Surrey; Maria Rozzi/the Guglielmi family of Dinazzano; Elizabeth/John Southgate of Elton; Hanna/Benjamin Groff of Lancaster County; or Mary/Neil MacDonald of Benbecula. These six of the eight entries currently rest entirely on the tree's own unsourced assertions.

## Open questions

- Is "Margaret" (Newbury, 1612–1708) actually a member of the Merrick family at all, or has a record for an unrelated person been attached to "Thomas Merrick"/"Mary Griggs" parents by mistake? The 1612 birth year predates the family's 1636 immigration and the father's own 1620 birth — this needs to be resolved by finding (or discarding) an actual source record.
- Was Grace (b. 1704, Surrey) a Bowen by marriage into a Welsh/English Bowen family, or into the colonial Rhode Island Bowen line (descended from immigrant Griffith Bowen)? No record connects her specifically to either.
- What is the source, if any, for Maria Rozzi's precise death date (7 Oct 1621) and place (Dinazzano) — the specificity suggests an actual parish record exists somewhere (Antenati/Reggio Emilia state archive), but it was not locatable in this pass and the person's own record is flagged "unsourced" in the tree.
- Is the Elton, Huntingdonshire Southgate family (Elizabeth, d. 1694, m. John Southgate) connected to the American Southgate families of Massachusetts/Maine? Not established here.
- Did Mary (b. abt 1783, Benbecula) and Neil MacDonald emigrate to Nova Scotia/PEI along with the well-documented wave of Uist/Benbecula MacDonalds, or remain in Scotland? Their son Laughlan's very Gaelic name is suggestive but not evidence of emigration.
- Is "Samuel Jacob Elwell" and "Susanna Post" sourced from a specific record the tree owner has seen but not cited, or simply carried over from another online tree?
