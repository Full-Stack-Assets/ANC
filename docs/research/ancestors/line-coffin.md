# The Coffin Line — Nantucket to Prince Edward Island (Generations 7–12)

## Overview

This is a surname-line survey of the Coffin family as it appears in the tree, generations 7–12 (roughly 1652–1805), tracing from Eunice Coffin of Nantucket/Prince Edward Island back through six generations of Nantucket Coffins to Stephen Coffin (b. 1652), son of **Tristram Coffin Sr.**, the immigrant founder of the Nantucket Coffin family and one of the island's original 1659 proprietors. The Nantucket Coffins are among the best-documented colonial New England families (multiple published genealogies, Nantucket vital records, and Quaker meeting records survive), so most links in this chain are solidly documented. The one significant problem area is a generation-11 record ("Peleg Coffin" / "Susannah Bunker") that appears to be a surname-swapped merge artifact rather than real people, and a generation-7 record ("Elisha Coffin," 1763–1851) that is missing its parent link even though it is independently identifiable as a son of Elisha Coffin Sr.

## Verified facts

- **Tristram Coffin Sr.** (immigrant ancestor, referenced as father of Stephen Coffin at gen 12): baptized 11 Mar 1609/10 at Brixton parish near Plymouth, England, son of Peter and Joanna (Kember) Coffin; married Dionis Stevens (1630); emigrated to Massachusetts in 1642 with wife, five children, his widowed mother, and two sisters; lived at Salisbury/Haverhill/Newbury until 1660; led the group that purchased Nantucket from Thomas Mayhew in 1659 for £30 and two beaver hats; died 2 Oct 1681 at Nantucket, survived by 7 children and ~60 grandchildren. — [Wikipedia: Tristram Coffin (settler)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tristram_Coffin_(settler)); [Findagrave 10904410](https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/10904410/tristram-coffin)
- **Stephen Coffin** (gen 12, b. 11 May 1652, d. 1734) is documented as a son of Tristram Coffin Sr. and Dionis Stevens, consistent across multiple compiled genealogies. — WebSearch synthesis of rootsweb/Geni/Wikipedia Coffin genealogies
- **James Coffin Sr. ("Hon. James Coffin," 1640–1720)**, son of Tristram Coffin Sr., married Mary Severance 3 Dec 1663; resided briefly at Dover, NH before returning to Nantucket; died at Nantucket 28 Jul 1720. — [Empty Branches on the Family Tree: James Coffin and Mary Severance](https://emptybranchesonthefamilytree.com/2018/08/james-coffin-and-mary-severance-nantucket-ma/)
- **James Coffin Jr.** (gen 10, 1664–1741), son of James Coffin Sr. and Mary Severance, married (2) Ruth Gardner, daughter of John and Priscilla Gardner, in 1692 — matches the tree's spouse listing. — same source
- **Benjamin Coffin Sen. ("Captain Benjamin Coffin," 1718–1793)**, son of James Coffin Jr., married Rebecca (Rebekah) Coffin ca. 22 Mar 1738/39; their children included Elisha, Rebekah, Benjamin, Lurana, Susanna, Seth, Ruth, Bartlet, Eliakim, and Christopher. — [FamilySearch: Captain Benjamin Coffin (1718–1793)](https://ancestors.familysearch.org/en/L8M1-F8Y/captain-benjamin-coffin-1718-1793); islandregister.com "My Father's Shoes" family register, pp.1–25
- **Elisha Coffin Sr.** (gen 8, 1740–1785) purchased 200 acres on the west side of Savage Harbour, Lot 38, St. John's Island (Prince Edward Island), from Capt. George Burns of Stukely on 5 Sept 1783, and died there 29 Oct 1785, survived by his widow Eunice and nine children aged 7 to 25. — [islandregister.com: My Father's Shoes — Our Coffin Story](https://www.islandregister.com/rcoffin/pg26_50.html); [WikiTree Coffin-277](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Coffin-277)
- **Eunice Coffin** (gen 7, 1766–1805) is documented in a compiled PEI family register as the wife of **John (Jr.) Morrow**, married ca. 1785, with eight recorded children: Abigail, Elisha, John III, James, Andrew, Thomas, Benjamin, and William Morrow. — [islandregister.com: The Descendants of John Morrow](https://www.islandregister.com/morrow1.html)
- **Peleg Bunker** (b. 1 Dec 1676, son of William Bunker and Mary Macy) married Susanna Coffin — daughter of Stephen Coffin and Mary Bunker — on 9 Jan 1700 at Nantucket; he died 1 Jun 1730, she 11 Jun 1740; their children included Judith, Priscilla, Dinah, Mary, and Jonathan Bunker. — WebSearch synthesis of Geni "Peleg Bunker" and Bunker-family compiled genealogy (billputman.com)
- **Bartlett Coffin** (b. ca. 1699, son of Peter Coffin and first wife Christian) married **Judith Bunker** 1 Jan 1718/19; Judith is independently documented as "Judith (Bunker) Coffin (1717–1789)." — [WikiTree Bunker-1437](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Bunker-1437); WebSearch synthesis
- **Peter Coffin (Sr.)** (gen 11, 1671–1749) married first Christian (Gard/Guard) Conde and second Hope Gardner (15 Mar 1708); children included Bartlett, Abner, Joseph, Tristram, Robert, and Peter Coffin, among others — confirming Bartlett Coffin as a genuine son. — [Geni: Peter Coffin (1671–1749)](https://www.geni.com/people/Peter-Coffin/6000000003490605887)

## Corrections to the tree

- **Eunice Coffin's spouse: "William John Morrow (Morrogh)" → "John (Jr.) Morrow."** The compiled PEI Morrow family register names her husband as John (Jr.) Morrow, married ca. 1785. "William" appears to be a conflation with their youngest son, William Morrow (b. ca. 1805). Evidence: [islandregister.com Descendants of John Morrow](https://www.islandregister.com/morrow1.html).
- **"Elisha Coffin" (1763–1851, gen 7, id I182789734710) has no parents listed in the tree, but is identifiable as a son of Elisha Coffin Sr. and Eunice Myrick** (i.e., Elisha Coffin Jr. of Savage Harbour, PEI). The tree should link his parents to Elisha Coffin Sr. (gen 8) and Eunice Myrick, making him a sibling of Eunice Coffin (gen 7) rather than a parentless standalone entry. Evidence: islandregister.com PEI Coffin narrative refers to "Elisha Jr.'s son Benjamin" continuing to hold the original Savage Harbour homestead, and a distinct WikiTree profile "Elisha Coffin Jr (1763–1851)" (Coffin-2357) exists for this person. This is **not** a duplicate of Elisha Coffin Sr. — the birth/death years and generation numbers differ — but a genuine, separate individual whose parent-link is simply missing from this tree file.
- **"Peleg Coffin" (gen 11) and his listed child/spouse data are very likely a surname-swapped merge artifact.** As entered, the tree has "Peleg Coffin" (parent: William Bunker; spouse: Susannah Bunker; child: Judith Mary Bunker) — which is genealogically incoherent, since a son of William **Bunker** would not carry the surname **Coffin**, and a daughter of a male "Peleg Coffin" would not default to the surname "Bunker" unless the tree is recording her by her mother's maiden name. Web sources instead show a documented couple **Peleg Bunker** (son of William Bunker) who married **Susanna(h) Coffin** (daughter of Stephen Coffin and Mary Bunker — i.e., gen 12 in this file). Their daughter was Judith **Bunker** (matching the tree's "Judith Mary Bunker," correctly carrying her father Peleg Bunker's surname). In short: the tree's "Peleg Coffin" should be **Peleg Bunker**, and the tree's "Susannah Bunker" (listed as Stephen Coffin's child) should be **Susannah Coffin** — her birth surname was overwritten by her married surname somewhere upstream in the source data. This does not change the overall lineage (Stephen Coffin → Susanna(h) → Judith → Bartlett/Rebecca still holds), but the person-labels in the tree are swapped. Evidence: WebSearch synthesis of Geni "Peleg Bunker (1676–1730)" and Bunker family genealogy (billputman.com), consistent with the tree's own internal generation-depth math (Peter Coffin Sr. and "Peleg Coffin" are both correctly gen 11, i.e., the same generation-depth as Bartlett Coffin's father-in-law level).
- **Do not confuse this line's "Peleg Coffin" with Congressman Peleg Coffin Jr. (1756–1805) of Nantucket/Portland, ME** — a real but unrelated historical figure who turned up in searches for the name; he belongs to a different branch and generation.
- Elisha Coffin Sr.'s death is recorded twice in the tree's events list under two place-name variants ("Savage Harbor, Prince Edward Island" and "St. John's Island, Savage Harbour, PE"). Both are historically correct for the same event — the colony was named "St. John's Island" until renamed Prince Edward Island in 1799 — so this is a harmless duplicate-event artifact from merging two source records, not a factual conflict.

## New findings

- Eunice Coffin and John (Jr.) Morrow had (at least) eight children on record, not the three ("Elisha," "John," "Benjamin") captured in the tree: Abigail (b. 6 Apr 1788, d. Apr 1798), Elisha (b. 14 Mar 1790), John III (b. 17 Jul 1792), James (b. 20 May 1795), Andrew (b. 19 Aug 1798), Thomas (b. ca. 1799), Benjamin (b. 25 Dec 1802), and William (b. ca. 1805, later a postmaster at East Point).
- Elisha Coffin Sr. bought his Savage Harbour, PEI land (200 acres, Lot 38, west side of the harbour) from Capt. George Burns of Stukely on 5 September 1783 — i.e., the family's move from Nantucket to PEI happened in the early 1780s, in the wake of the American Revolution (a pattern common among Loyalist-leaning or economically displaced Nantucket Quaker families of that era).
- James Coffin Jr.'s father, "Hon. James Coffin" (1640–1720), first married Love Gardner before marrying Ruth Gardner in 1692 — the tree only captures the second marriage (Ruth Gardner), which is the one relevant to this line since Benjamin Coffin Sen. descends from that union.
- Rebecca Coffin's parents (Bartlett Coffin and Judith Bunker) themselves have a documented WikiTree profile for Judith: "Judith (Bunker) Coffin (1717–1789)," married Bartlett Coffin 1 Jan 1718/19 — though her birth year (1717) sits awkwardly close to her marriage date and should be treated cautiously (see Open Questions).

## Open questions

- **Peter Coffin Sr.'s (1671–1749) own parentage is unresolved.** The tree lists no parents for him. Web search results were inconsistent — one summary claimed he was "son of John Coffin" (nephew of Hon. James Coffin), which conflicts with the generation-depth math implied elsewhere in this line (where Peter Coffin Sr. and "Peleg Bunker" both sit at gen 11, parallel to Hon. James Coffin's generation). This needs a primary-source check (Nantucket probate or the published Allen Coffin genealogy, "The Coffin Family," 1962) rather than reliance on secondary web summaries.
- Judith (Bunker) Coffin's birth year (1717 per WikiTree) vs. her marriage date (1 Jan 1718/19) is chronologically tight; a separate calculation from her father Peleg Bunker's 1700 marriage would put her birth closer to ca. 1701–1710. The discrepancy is unresolved and likely reflects an error in one of the secondary compilations rather than the primary Nantucket records.
- No marriage record was located for Elisha Coffin Sr. and Eunice Myrick, and the tree itself does not source it either.
- The exact chain connecting "Tristram Coffin" as named in this tree file to the immigrant Tristram Coffin Sr. is inferred by identity (name, generation depth, and dates all match) rather than confirmed by a direct citation inside this file, since Tristram himself has no person-record in this dataset — only a name reference as Stephen Coffin's father.
