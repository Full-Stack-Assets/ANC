# David Hawkins Crist (c1836/38–1871) & Almina Lucia "Lucy" VanAmburg (1843/44–1904)

Generation 5 direct ancestors. Researched 2026-07-03 from web sources (IAGenWeb Allamakee County record transcriptions, Archive.org county histories, Chronicling America). Note: the tree data supplied for this dossier is the Crist/VanAmburg couple, although the task header named Hohmann/Bornscheuer — this dossier follows the supplied tree data.

## Overview

David H. Crist, said to be born in Montgomery, Orange County, New York, died young on 7 December 1871 at Lansing, Allamakee County, Iowa, and is buried in Oak Hill Cemetery, Lansing Township. His widow Almina Lucia "Lucy" (VanAmburg/VanEmburg) — Vermont-born daughter of James VanEmburg and Lucy — remarried to William P. Dodds of Columbus (a Mississippi-river hamlet in Lansing Township, Allamakee County), had two more sons (Bert and Don Dodds), and died suddenly in her rocking chair on Sunday, 10 April 1904; she is buried, apparently without a marker, in Wexford (Immaculate Conception) Cemetery south of Lansing. The county death register confirms her parents as "James VanEmburg / Lucy (maiden name unknown)", corroborating the tree's parentage.

## Verified facts

- **David H. Crist died 7 Dec 1871 and is buried in Oak Hill Cemetery, Lansing Township, Allamakee Co., Iowa.** Woodmansee burial-grounds compilation, transcribed at IAGenWeb Allamakee: "Crist, David H. — 1836 — 1871 Dec 07 — Obit" (the "Obit" flag means a newspaper obituary was known to the compiler). Same entry in Paul Moritz's merged Oak Hill burial list. This confirms the tree's death date and place (Lansing) exactly; note the cemetery record's birth year of 1836 (see Corrections).
- **Almina died 10 April 1904 in Allamakee County, Iowa, as "Lucia Almina Dodds".** Allamakee Co. death register 1902–1904 (compiled by Richard O'Brien from state & county records, IAGenWeb): "Dodds, Lucia Almina | DOB 1844 Jun 10 | DOD 1904 Apr 10 | Father/Mother: James VanEmburg / Lucy unkn | Burial: St. Mary's, Wexford." Confirms the tree's death date (April 10, 1904) exactly, and the June 10 birthday.
- **Almina's father was James VanEmburg and her mother's given name was Lucy** — stated on her death-register entry (above). This matches the tree's "James C VanEmburgh" and the given name of "Lucy Lucia Hale" (the register did not know the mother's maiden name).
- **Almina is buried in Wexford (Immaculate Conception) Cemetery, Lafayette Township, Allamakee Co.** Woodmansee transcription for Wexford Cemetery: "Dodds, Lucy L — 1844 Jun 10 — 1904 Apr 10 — DR NM" (DR = death record; NM = no marker). Buried near "Dodds, William — 1836 — 1918 Aug 07 — NM."
- **Her 1904 death was sudden, at Columbus (Lansing Twp.).** Allamakee Journal and Lansing Mirror, "Looking Back 25 Years" column of 24 April 1929, recalling April 1904: "Mrs. W. P. Dodds, of Columbus, died suddenly Sunday, while sitting in a rocking chair. A husband and two sons, Bert and Don, survive." (10 April 1904 was indeed a Sunday, tying this squarely to her death-register entry.)
- **Almina used the name "Lucy"** — the tree's nickname is confirmed by her burial entry ("Lucy L") and by her married style; her formal names Lucia/Almina appear reversed in the death register ("Lucia Almina").

## Corrections to the tree

- **David's birth year "abt 1838" → 1836 per his only located record.** The Oak Hill Cemetery burial record (Woodmansee/Moritz transcriptions) gives birth year 1836. No independent record of an 1838 birth was found. Treat as a conflict: cemetery-derived years are themselves often calculated, but 1836 is the documented value.
- **Almina's birth "June 10, 1843" → June 10, 1844 per the county death register.** The register (and the Wexford burial transcription derived from it) says 1844 Jun 10. Month and day agree with the tree; the year differs by one. Death-register years are frequently back-calculated from age, so 1843 may still be right — but the only located record says 1844.
- **Almina's death place "Iowa, USA" → Columbus, Lansing Township, Allamakee County, Iowa**, per the 1929 retrospective news item and the county death register (burial at Wexford, Allamakee Co.).
- **Almina's children: not only Mary A Crist.** She had at least two sons by her second marriage — Bert Dodds and Don Dodds — per her 1904 death notice.

## New findings (not in the tree)

- **Almina remarried after David's 1871 death to William P. Dodds** (c1836 – 7 Aug 1918), of Columbus, Allamakee County. Evidence: her death register as "Dodds, Lucia Almina"; the 1904 notice naming her "Mrs. W. P. Dodds of Columbus"; William Dodds (1836–1918 Aug 07) buried beside her at Wexford Cemetery. The marriage date/place was not found in the (incomplete) Allamakee compiled marriage index — probably mid-1870s.
- **W. P. Dodds's occupations:** he appears in the list of early Allamakee County schoolteachers in Hancock's *Past and Present of Allamakee County* (1913), and the Allamakee Journal of 4 June 1902 reported: "W. P. Dodds has engaged in the land business... He is an old real estate man." Almina's later married life was thus as the wife of a teacher/land agent at Columbus.
- **Half-siblings of Mary A Crist:** Bert Dodds and Don Dodds (born c1870s–1880s, both deceased era; a "Bert E. Dodds" appears in later Iowa state rosters — unconfirmed as the same man).
- **Probable VanEmburg relatives at Lansing (Oak Hill Cemetery, Moritz merged list):** John VanEmberg (1826 – 1 Jul 1892), Frank J. VanEmberg (d. 1881; grave registered as Co. G, 1st Iowa Cavalry, Civil War), Lucretia VanEmberg née Sales (d. 1855), and an unnamed VanEmberg child (b. 15 Mar 1878 – d. c7 Sep 1879). The Lansing Journal of 10 Sep 1879 reported: "Frank Van Emburg, of Columbus, also lost one [child] from the same cause [whooping cough]... in its second year." Note Frank Van Emburg lived at **Columbus** — the same hamlet where Almina (Mrs. W. P. Dodds) lived, strongly suggesting these are her close kin (brother or cousins), though the exact relationships are unproven.
- **Hale family at Lansing:** Almina's mother is given in the tree as Lucy Lucia Hale. Oak Hill Cemetery (Lansing) contains a cluster of Hales — Elisha Hale (1824–1884), Elza Hale (1821–1883), Jane A. Hale (1831–1922), Albert Elisha Hale (1849–1931), etc. — and the 1882 county history names "Elijah Hale, one of Lansing's early settlers" (his house hosted Lansing's first sermon, 1851) and "Alberta Hale," an early female child born there. Possible kin of Almina's mother — inferred only, no record linking them was found.
- **Dodds kin:** Thomas H. Dodds, age 21, of Allamakee County, enlisted Oct 1864 in the Civil War (Hancock 1913 roster); a Thomas Dodds died 1880 and George F. Dodds (1851–1927) has an obit on the Allamakee board — likely relatives of William P. Dodds (unproven).
- **David H. Crist had a newspaper obituary** (the Woodmansee record's "Obit" flag), almost certainly in a Lansing paper (Lansing Mirror/Lansing Journal) of December 1871. Those issues are not in Chronicling America (searched 1871–72; no relevant hits) — a lookup in the microfilmed Lansing papers or the Allamakee obituary board archive could recover it.

## Open questions

- Marriage date and place of David Crist and Almina VanAmburg (likely mid-1860s; not in the compiled Allamakee marriage index — try Winneshiek/Crawford Co. WI/La Crosse records or FamilySearch county marriage films).
- David's parents (Philip Crist & Matilda Hawkins of Montgomery, Orange Co., NY) — unverified. Crist families are well documented at the Brick Reformed Church, Montgomery, Orange Co. (e.g., Cornelius Crist bapt. 1787), so the origin is plausible, but no record for Philip/Matilda or David's c1836–38 birth was located in free web sources. 1850/1860 census of Orange Co. NY should be checked on FamilySearch/Ancestry.
- What brought David to Lansing, Iowa, and what did he die of at ~35? His 1871 obituary (see above) would answer this.
- 1870 census household of David & Almina (presumably Lansing Twp., Allamakee Co.) — IAGenWeb's 1870 transcriptions cover only Center/Fairview/French Creek/Iowa townships; needs a subscription-index lookup.
- 1880 and 1900 census households of Almina as Mrs. W. P. Dodds, Columbus/Lansing Twp. (would give Bert's and Don's birth years and confirm daughter Mary A Crist in the household in 1880).
- Exact relationship of Frank J./John/Frank Van Emburg of Lansing–Columbus to Almina (brothers? father figures? — the tree's father is James C VanEmburgh; note the death register says simply "James VanEmburg").
- No Find A Grave memorial for David H. Crist or Lucy/Almina Dodds surfaced via search-engine queries (Find A Grave blocks direct fetch); memorials may exist unindexed or not at all — worth a manual check, and creating them if absent.
