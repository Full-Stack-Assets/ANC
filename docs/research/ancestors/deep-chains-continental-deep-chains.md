# Continental Deep Chains — Credibility Audit (Generations 13–25)

## Overview

This dossier audits the deep-generation segments (13–23, of a claimed 13–25 range; only 35 individuals were present in the supplied dataset, not the full 79) of the pedigree, spanning Swedish parish/mining-district farm lines, a Swedish clergy-and-mining-nobility line, and two English gentry/peerage clusters. It is a **credibility audit, not exhaustive per-person research**: each segment was checked against period-appropriate secondary compilations (Adelsvapen-Wiki, Svenskt Biografiskt Lexikon, Wikipedia, thepeerage.com, WereRelate, FamilySearch, WikiTree, Geni) rather than primary parish registers, which is the correct evidentiary ceiling for material this deep. Verdict in short: one segment (Angermannus/Svinhufvud clergy line) is a **genuinely documented historical lineage that nonetheless bakes in a known, superseded genealogical error**; one segment (Bruges/Brydges, Barons Chandos) is a **solidly documented English peerage line**; the Olney–Throckmorton marriage is **documented via Visitation-derived pedigree**, but its parent-linkage in this tree is **demonstrably wrong**; and at least two segments (Posse/Bowes; Holt–Durvassal) show the classic fingerprints of **cross-tree conflation or fabrication** and should be labeled legend. All individuals discussed are pre-1656 and safely outside any living-persons concern.

## Verified facts

- **Olaus Andreae Angermannus** (b. c.1522, Sidensjö parish, Västernorrland – d. 10 April 1607, Mora, Dalarna) was a real Swedish clergyman and Riksdag member, vicar of Västerfärnebo (c.1550) then Mora (from 1574), representative at the 1590/1594 Riksdag and the 1593 Uppsala Assembly. — [Olaus Andreæ Angermannus (Swedish Wikipedia)](https://sv.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olaus_Andre%C3%A6_Angermannus)
- Olaus Andreae Angermannus married **Elsa Nilsdotter**, daughter of Nils Hansson ("Stjärna"), a miner at Främsbacka near Stora Kopparberget, and **Gertrud Knutsdotter**, of the older Svinhufvud family at Kopparberget. Their daughter **Justina** married a curate who later became parish priest of Rättvik, and Justina died in Rättvik on 26 January 1626. — [ps12_133.html, Kerstin Ellert's genealogy site](http://nacksta.com/KMFanlista/ps12/ps12_133.html); [Mina Släktanor: Släkten Svinhufvud (af Qvalstad) i Dalarna](https://minaslaktanor.wordpress.com/slakterna-svinhuvud-gatzman-i-dalarna/)
- **Svinhufvud af Qvalstad** is a genuine Swedish noble family, ennobled 1574 and introduced to the Riddarhuset (House of Nobility) in 1627, seated in Stora Kopparbergs parish, Dalarna — ancestral to Finland's president P. E. Svinhufvud. — [Svinhufvud af Qvalstad, Adelsvapen-Wiki](https://www.adelsvapen.com/genealogi/Svinhufvud_af_Qvalstad_nr_145); [Svinhufvud (family), English Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Svinhufvud_(family))
- Modern corrected genealogical scholarship establishes that Elsa Nilsdotter's **father** was of the "Stjärna"/Kopparberg family (six-pointed-star arms, ancestral also to the noble Stiernhielm family), and only her **mother** Gertrud Knutsdotter carried the true Svinhufvud line — correcting older literature that had (wrongly) called Elsa's whole paternal line "Svinhufvud." — [Mina Släktanor](https://minaslaktanor.wordpress.com/slakterna-svinhuvud-gatzman-i-dalarna/); [Geni: Elsa Nilsdotter Svinhufvud](https://www.geni.com/people/Elsa-Svinhufvud/6000000008055849407); [FamilySearch: Elsa Nilsdotter Swinhufvud](https://ancestors.familysearch.org/en/LDKK-J5T/elsa-nilsdotter-swinhufvud-1520-1592)
- **Sir Giles Brugge/Brydges, 6th Baron Chandos** (c.1462–1 Dec 1511), Sheriff of Gloucester, of Cubberley/Coberley, Gloucestershire, fought at the Battle of Blackheath (1497) and against Perkin Warbeck at Exeter; married **Isabel Baynham** (c.1475–1511), daughter of Thomas Baynham of Mitcheldean and Alice Walwyn, in 1484. His parents were **Thomas Brydges (Brugge), 5th Baron Chandos** (c.1427–1493) and **Florence Darrell**. This is a well-attested, multiply-corroborated English peerage lineage. — [Giles Brugge, 6th Baron Chandos, Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Giles_Brugge,_6th_Baron_Chandos); [thepeerage.com p10287](https://www.thepeerage.com/p10287.htm); [FamilySearch](https://ancestors.familysearch.org/en/L2QG-XJV/sir-giles-brydges-6th-baron-of-chandos-1462-1511); [Geni: Isabel Brydges (Baynham)](https://www.geni.com/people/Isabel-Brydges-Baynham/6000000003495291716)
- The marriage of **Margaret Olney**, daughter and heir of Robert Olney of Weston Underwood, Buckinghamshire, and his wife **Goditha Judith Bosum** (daughter and heir of **Sir William Bosum**, Lord of Roxton, Bedfordshire), to **Thomas Throckmorton**, is recorded in Visitation-derived Throckmorton pedigrees. — [WereRelate: Person:Thomas Throckmorton (8)](https://www.werelate.org/wiki/Person:Thomas_Throckmorton_(8))
- **Durvassal** was a genuine armigerous Warwickshire family (Solihull/Coughton area) — e.g. Philip Durvassal (c.1214–aft.1279), John Durvassal (1276–1328), Thomas Durvassal, coroner (d.1329, Coughton) — but every attested member found dates to the 13th–14th century, roughly 150–200 years before the tree's 16th-century "Alianore Durvassal." — [WikiTree: Philip Durvassal](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Durvassal-4); [WikiTree: John Durvassal](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Durvassal-31); [WikiTree: Thomas Durvassal](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Durvassal-3)

## Corrections to the tree

1. **Compound name bakes in a superseded genealogical error** — `" Elsa Elisabeth Nilsdotter Svinhuvud Stierna Kopparbergsätten"` → should read **Elsa Nilsdotter** (patronymic "Nilsdotter" is correct — her father was Nils Hansson of the Stjärna/Kopparberg family, not a Svinhufvud). "Svinhufvud" belongs to her *mother* Gertrud Knutsdotter's line, not to Elsa's own inherited surname/arms. The tree's single fused name repeats a documented 19th/20th-century antiquarian mistake that modern Swedish genealogical scholarship has since corrected. Evidence: Mina Släktanor blog post explicitly discussing and correcting this exact confusion; Geni and FamilySearch entries for Elsa reflect the corrected parentage.
2. **Leaked catalog/register ID in a name field** — `"XV:22144 Gertrud Knutsdotter Svinhufvud af Qvalstad"` → the "XV:22144" prefix is a generation:number citation tag from a Swedish noble-genealogy register (Elgenstierna-style ättartavla / Adelsvapen numbering), not part of her name. It should be stripped to **Gertrud Knutsdotter (Svinhufvud af Qvalstad)** — its presence indicates the branch was copy-pasted wholesale from a compiled online genealogy without cleanup.
3. **Anachronistic source citation** — `Anders Andreas OLOFSSON i LERNBO` (b. c.1500 – d.1564) is cited to **"Sweden, Household Clerical Surveys, 1880–1896."** That record collection covers 1880–1896, roughly 320–390 years after this person's death — a chronological impossibility. This is a textbook Ancestry-hint mismatch (the hint almost certainly belongs to a same-named descendant living in the 1880s) and should be removed as a source for this individual.
4. **Duplicate/contradictory death events** for `Jöns (Jonas) Persson`: one sourced "death: 1 jan 1600 @ Bommersbo, Norrbarke W, Sweden [sourced]" and one unsourced "death: ca 1600 @ Dalarna, Sweden [unsourced]." These should be merged into a single event; keep the sourced one and drop the redundant unsourced duplicate.
5. **Parent misattribution — Robert Olney's parents are actually his wife's parents.** The tree lists `Sir Lord Robert Olney-van Weston`'s parents as **Sir William Bosum** and **Lady Margaret Arden-Bosum**. Per the Visitation-derived Throckmorton pedigree, Sir William Bosum (Lord of Roxton, Bedfordshire) was the father of **Godith Judith Bosum** — Robert Olney's *wife* (correctly listed in the tree as his spouse) — not of Robert himself. Robert Olney's own parentage is not established by any source found in this audit; the Bosum couple should be moved to Godith Judith Bosum's parent slot, and Robert Olney's parents marked unknown pending research. This is a clear in-law/parent duplication bug typical of merged GEDCOM imports.
6. **Corrupted place-name field** for Justina Olofsdotter Angermannus's death event: `"death: 26 Jan 1626 @ 1656459, Vastmanlands, Sweden [unsourced]"` — the numeral "1656459" is plainly a leaked internal place/record ID, not a place name, and the county is likely wrong too: the source located in this audit places her death in **Rättvik, Dalarna** (where her husband was parish priest), not Västmanland.
7. **"19GGM" leaked into a name field** — `"Lady Margaret Arden-Bosum 19GGM"` — "19GGM" is a relationship-degree annotation (19th-great-grandmother, relative to some tree owner) that has been concatenated onto the person's name rather than stored as metadata. Should be stripped to **Margaret Arden**.

## New findings

- The Angermannus/Svinhufvud/Stjärna cluster (gen 13–16) is not merely tree lore — it corresponds to a real, independently documented 16th-century Dalarna clergy-and-mining family with a known, published, and *already-corrected* historiographical error about which noble surname (Svinhufvud vs. Stjärna) actually applies on which side. That correction (see Mina Släktanor) postdates whatever compiled source this tree copied from, explaining the fossilized error.
- The English clergy/gentry cluster around Robert Olney and Godith Judith Bosum connects into a real, traditionally recorded Throckmorton pedigree (Margaret Olney m. Thomas Throckmorton) — this specific marriage is not itself a fabrication, even though the parent-generation linkage feeding into it in this tree is wrong (see Correction 5).
- "Anders Andreas Olofsson i Lernbo" heads a branch (his numerous children: Jöns/Jonas, Lars "Angermannus," Olof, Olaus Andreae Angermanus, Bengt, Mertilia, Knut, Anna, Peder, Erick, Kierstin, Karin) that is consistent with a real large 16th-century Norrbärke/Anundsjö parish family pattern, several of whose sons took the "Angermannus" clerical surname — plausible given Olaus Andreae Angermannus's real, documented clerical career in the same region, though this specific father-son link was not independently confirmed beyond compiled trees.
- The Finn-settler line (Mårten Larsson "Teit," b. Tetom, Pernå parish, Finland; wife's father surnamed "Muurla") fits the well-documented historical Forest Finn (Savo-Finn) migration into the Bergslagen/Dalarna mining district in the 16th century — a real historical phenomenon, though the specific named individuals were not independently verified.

## Open questions

- Does the fuller 79-person dataset (this file held only 35 of the stated 79 individuals, generations 13–23 rather than 13–25) contain the specific "impossible lifespan" / "child born after parent's death" instances our internal audit flagged? None were found as hard violations within this particular subset — the closest analogues are the anachronistic 1880s source on a 1500s person (Correction 3) and a tight-but-not-impossible parental age (Justina Olofsdotter Angermannus's mother's implied age ~15 at her birth, given Hans Jonsson Gröntuv b.1550 and Olof Hansson b.1580). Recommend re-running the lifespan check against the complete 79-person set.
- Is there a documented bridge between the real 13th/14th-century Durvassal family and a purported 16th-century "Alianore Durvassal"? No source found; treat the Holt–Durvassal link as unverified pending a genuine Visitation or IPM (inquisition post mortem) citation.
- What is Robert Olney's actual parentage, now that Sir William Bosum and Margaret Arden have been reassigned to his wife Godith Judith Bosum? Needs original research in Buckinghamshire Visitation pedigrees.
- Was "Cecily Dabridgecourt Mansfield/Mansell, Attendant to Queen Mary" actually a daughter of Giles Brydges, 6th Baron Chandos? Not found among his attested daughters in the sources checked here (thepeerage.com, Wikipedia); could be a lesser-documented daughter, a different Brydges-family Cecily, or a misattachment.
- Is "Per Matsson Posse" a genuine, lesser-documented member of the Posse noble family, or an entirely fabricated identity merged from an unrelated "Per Matsson" farmer plus the Posse surname? No period source located either way in this pass.
