# Ellen Josephine Swanson (1900–1990) — Generation 4

> **Note on assignment:** the orchestrator's task text named "Thomas Larkin & Mary Blackmore" as the subject, but the item file (A15.json) contains Ellen Josephine Swanson and points at this dossier path. This dossier covers Ellen Josephine Swanson as per the item data.

## Overview

Ellen Josephine Swanson was born 4 May 1900 in Easton, Bristol County, Massachusetts, the daughter of Swedish immigrants Simon Anders Svensson (Swanson) of Tegneby, Bohuslän, and Anna Josephina Svenson of Hjo, Västergötland. Her mother died when Ellen was two, and Ellen grew up in the Swedish community of Easton/Brockton (the Ames Shovel Works Swedish colony). She married twice — first a Donahue (father of Howard F. Donahue, the tree owner's line), then George Joseph Lindquist of Brockton — and died 1 August 1990 in Bridgewater, Plymouth County, Massachusetts.
