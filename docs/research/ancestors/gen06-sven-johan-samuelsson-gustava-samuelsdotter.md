# Sven Johan Samuelsson (1839–1877) & Gustava Samuelsdotter (1842–1912) — Generation 6

> **Duplicate-person case.** Flagged: two records for Sven Johan Samuelsson sharing name and birth year 1839 (`I182197443214`, `I182195860593`). Investigation shows the duplication runs through his whole household — wife, mother, son, daughter-in-law — matching the already-resolved John Albertson & Ann Pine (gen 6) and Samuel Jacobsson (gen 7) tree-duplication pattern. This dossier resolves the pair using the same anchor established while researching the adjoining Samuel Jacobsson and Annika Olsdotter cases: which branch's grandson matches an already-independently-verified descendant.

## Overview

Sven Johan Samuelsson was born 7 April 1839 in Långelanda och Torp Parish, Göteborg och Bohus County (Bohuslän), Sweden, and died 24 December 1877, likely still in Bohuslän. He married Gustava Samuelsdotter (b. 11 May 1842, Hjälmvik Nolgården farm, Stala, Bohuslän, daughter of Samuel Jacobsson & Sofia Nilsdotter), who outlived him by 35 years and appears to have followed the family's emigration wave to Massachusetts, dying 28 July 1912 in North Easton, Bristol Co., MA. Their son Simon Swanson (Svensson) emigrated to Easton, Massachusetts; his daughter Ellen Josephine Swanson is the tree owner's ancestor, already documented in `gen05-simon-swanson-anna-josephina-svenson.md` and `gen04-ellen-josephine-swanson.md`. This dossier's job is resolving the tree-import duplication: the Ancestry export split Sven Johan (and his whole household) into two parallel person nodes.
