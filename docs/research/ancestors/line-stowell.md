# The Stowell Line — Newton & Dedham, Massachusetts (Generations 7–12)

## Overview

This is a surname-line survey of the Stowell family as it appears in the tree, generations 7–12 (roughly 1625–1829), tracing from Hannah Stowell (b. 1772, gen 8, Dedham) back through Isaac Stowell Jr. (gen 9), Isaac Stowell Sr. (gen 10), and Israel Stowell (gen 11) to the immigrant **Samuel Stowell of Hingham, Massachusetts** (gen 12, d. 1683) — a colonial Massachusetts family with a full published genealogy, *The Stowell Genealogy: A Record of the Descendants of Samuel Stowell of Hingham, Mass.* (William Henry Harrison Stowell, 1922, on Archive.org). The supplied tree data itself covers only generations 8–9 and contains classic unmerged-duplicate artifacts; web research extended the documented chain a further three generations back to the immigrant ancestor, whose own English origin is explicitly unresolved even in the 1922 genealogy. Every parent-child link from Hannah Stowell (gen 8) back to Samuel Stowell (gen 12) is corroborated by at least one, and usually two, independent published/compiled sources — an unusually well-supported line for this depth, though the last link (Hannah Stowell's marriage into the Pond family) rests more on circumstantial fit than a document naming her father outright.

## Verified facts

- **Hannah Stowell** (gen 8), b. **26 Sep 1772**, d. **21 Aug 1829**, both at Dedham, Norfolk Co., MA — married **Samuel Pond** of Dedham. These exact birth/death dates and the Pond marriage are independently corroborated in Edward D. Harris, *A Genealogical Record of Daniel Pond, and His Descendants* (1873), pp. 100–102, entry #383, which records Samuel Pond's "(2) [marriage] in 1803, Hannah Stowell, b. 26 Sep. 1772, d. Dedham, 21 Aug. 1829" — matching the tree's own (tree-flagged "unsourced") dates exactly. See companion dossier `docs/research/ancestors/line-pond.md`.
- **Isaac Stowell Jr.** (gen 9), b. **1733**, Newton, Middlesex Co., MA; d. **3 Mar 1820**, Dedham, MA — married **Mary Oliver**, **24 May 1753**, Boston. The tree cites "Massachusetts, Town and Vital Records, 1620-1988" for this marriage. This is corroborated by a separate compiled index: AccessGenealogy's "Boston MA Intentions of Marriage 1753" records intentions for "Isaac Stowell and Mary Oliver," dated 2 April 1753 in Boston — consistent with a marriage roughly seven weeks later. — https://accessgenealogy.com/massachusetts/boston-ma-intentions-of-marriage-1753.htm
- **Isaac Stowell Jr. was a son of Isaac Stowell (Sr., b. 1708, Newton) and Abigail Hyde**, not (as the tree's incomplete duplicate record implies) simply of "Abigail (Hyde) Stowell" alone. Two independent secondary compilations agree: (1) a WikiTree/FamilySearch-derived summary giving "Isaac Stowell was born in 1708 in Newton... married Abigail Hyde... 17 Feb 1731/32 at Newton"; (2) the "Stowell Family History" compilation (Grand Lake Historical Society, Colorado, citing the 1922 Stowell genealogy) which lists, under entry "3. Isaac Stowell / Born 1708 / Married Abigail Hyde daughter of Jonathon and Hannah Hyde," a son "Isaac, 1732" as the eldest of eleven children — one year off from the tree's "1733," almost certainly an Old-Style/New-Style dating variance for a birth in the Jan–Mar transitional period. — https://grandlakehistory.org/wp-content/uploads/2013/01/STOWELL-FAMILY-HISTORY-pdf.pdf
- **Isaac Stowell Sr. (gen 10, b. 1708, Newton) was a son of Israel Stowell and Abigail Fuller.** Documented in both the full 1922 Stowell genealogy (Archive.org full text) and the Grand Lake compilation: "2. Israel Stowell / Born Aug 10, 1670 / Married Abigail Fuller daughter of Lt. Samuel & Mary Fuller / Children:... Isaac 1708." Abigail Fuller was b. c.1674, Barnstable, MA; d. 11 Apr 1718, Newton. — https://archive.org/stream/stowellgenealogy00stow/stowellgenealogy00stow_djvu.txt ; https://grandlakehistory.org/wp-content/uploads/2013/01/STOWELL-FAMILY-HISTORY-pdf.pdf
- **Israel Stowell (gen 11), b. 10 Aug 1670, Hingham; d. 7 Aug 1725, Newton, MA, age 55; occupation "weaver"** — was a son of the immigrant Samuel Stowell and Mary Farrow. Israel and Abigail removed from Hingham to Newton by 1716 (land purchase 15 Oct 1716), bringing their two youngest children. — 1922 Stowell genealogy (Archive.org)
- **Samuel Stowell (gen 12), the immigrant ancestor**, b. England c.1625 (date inferred from death record, not directly recorded); d. **Hingham, Mass., 9 Nov. 1683**; occupation "blacksmith." He married **Mary Farrow**, daughter of John and Frances Farrow, on **25 Oct 1649** (per the 1922 genealogy; the Grand Lake compilation gives "Nov. 24, 1649" for the same marriage — a minor date discrepancy between the two secondary sources). Mary Farrow was b. Hingham, England, 22 Sep 1633, and d. Hingham, MA, 24 Oct 1708. They had ten children, including Israel (b. 1670). — 1922 Stowell genealogy (Archive.org); Grand Lake "Stowell Family History" PDF

## Corrections to the tree

- **"Abigail (Hyde) Stowell" listed as sole parent of Isaac Stowell Jr. (record I182541959366) → should be Isaac Stowell Sr. (b. 1708, Newton) *and* Abigail Hyde as a parent pair.** The tree's rendering treats the mother's maiden-name-in-parens notation as if it were the complete parent field, omitting the father entirely. Corroborated by two independent compiled sources (WikiTree/FamilySearch summary and the Grand Lake "Stowell Family History" PDF), both of which name Isaac Stowell Sr. explicitly as the father.
- **Duplicate person records — Hannah Stowell.** `I182541959332` (parents: "Isaac Stowell Jr." only) and `I182541966157` (parents: "Isaac Stowell Jr." and "Mary Oliver") are the same person with identical birth/death dates and the same spouse and child listed. The second record is more complete (correctly adding the mother); the two should be merged, not treated as alternate hypotheses.
- **Duplicate person records — Isaac Stowell Jr.** `I182541959366` (has a parent, "Abigail (Hyde) Stowell," but no spouse/source data) and `I182620334338` (has the spouse — "Mary Oliver m. 24 May 1753 @ Boston" — and the cited source, "Massachusetts, Town and Vital Records, 1620-1988," but an *empty* parents field) are the same person with complementary but incomplete data split across the two records. This is the same unmerged-duplicate pattern already documented across this tree's Pond line (see `line-pond.md`), suggesting it is a systemic import artifact rather than isolated to one branch.
- **Generation gap: the tree data supplied for this line stops at generation 9 (Isaac Stowell Jr.) with no parent record at all for him beyond the incomplete "Abigail (Hyde) Stowell" string.** Generations 10–12 (Isaac Stowell Sr., Israel Stowell, and the immigrant Samuel Stowell) do not exist as person-records in the supplied JSON extract at all, even though a fully documented, published chain to the immigrant ancestor is available. This looks like a straightforward gap in this slice of the tree rather than a factual error — worth adding these three generations as new records if the tree is being expanded.

## New findings

- The immigrant ancestor is **Samuel Stowell, a blacksmith**, first recorded in Hingham at his 1649 marriage to Mary Farrow; none of this (name, occupation, spouse, or immigration) is present anywhere in the supplied tree data.
- The 1922 genealogist's own words on Samuel Stowell's English origin: "Although the compiler has been unable to discover the parentage of our common ancestor, the Samuel Stowell who settled in Hingham, Mass., it is reasonably certain he belongs to the Stowell or Stawell family that settled in County Somerset, England" — but he also notes "his name does not appear on any of the ship's registers or in any of the Hingham records until his marriage in 1649," i.e., a birth c.1625 and any specific emigration date/ship (commonly associated in secondary retellings with the 1635 Hingham migration under Rev. Peter Hobart) is inference/tradition, not a documented fact.
- Isaac Stowell Sr. and Abigail Hyde (m. 1731/32) had eleven children between 1732 and 1755, per the Grand Lake compilation — Isaac (Jr., our line), Jacob, Abigail, Joseph, Mary, Samuel, Ebenezer, Lemuel, two children named Thaddeus (the first died in infancy in 1750), and a second Mary — none of these siblings are in the supplied tree data.
- Israel Stowell and Abigail Fuller had five children (Abigail, Israel [2nd], Elizabeth, Isaac, Temperance) per the same source — again, none appear in the tree.
- Abigail Hyde (Isaac Sr.'s wife) was herself the daughter of Jonathan Hyde and Hannah (Dana) Hyde of Newton, per WikiTree/FamilySearch-derived summaries — a family with its own documented Newton, Massachusetts roots (the Hyde family were early Cambridge/Newton settlers), though this was not independently verified against a primary Newton vital record this session.
- Hannah Stowell's marriage into the Pond family (1803, to Samuel Pond of Dedham) and her exact matching birth/death dates are independently corroborated from the *Pond* side of the family's own published genealogy (Harris, 1873) — a valuable cross-check, since the Stowell-side sources consulted this session do not extend down to Hannah Stowell herself or explicitly confirm Isaac Stowell Jr. as her father.

## Open questions

1. **Is Isaac Stowell Jr.'s birth year 1732 or 1733?** The tree says 1733; the Grand Lake compilation (secondary, drawing on the 1922 genealogy) says 1732. Both are compiled sources, not a direct vital-record quote; the discrepancy is most likely an Old Style/New Style calendar issue for an early-year birth, but this was not resolved against a primary Newton or Boston vital-record entry this session.
2. **Is there a primary source that directly names Isaac Stowell Jr. and Mary Oliver as Hannah Stowell's parents?** The link is highly plausible (correct place, correct generation, no conflicting candidates found) but this session did not locate a specific Dedham or Boston birth-record entry for "Hannah Stowell, 1772" naming her parents outright; the strongest corroboration found was indirect, via the Pond family's own genealogy simply naming her as Samuel Pond's wife.
3. **What was Samuel Stowell the immigrant's parentage and specific origin in England?** The 1922 genealogist states this explicitly could not be discovered beyond a "reasonably certain" (but unproven) association with a Stowell/Stawell family of Somerset. This is the point at which the documented line genuinely ends; any further generations into England would be pure speculation without new primary-record discovery.
4. **The exact marriage date for Samuel Stowell and Mary Farrow is given two ways** by the two secondary sources consulted (25 Oct 1649 per the 1922 genealogy vs. Nov 24, 1649 per the Grand Lake PDF) — worth checking against Hingham's own published vital records (*Vital Records of Hingham, Massachusetts, to the Year 1850*, on Archive.org/HathiTrust) to resolve.
5. **Did Isaac Stowell Sr. (gen 10, b. 1708) have his own death date/place recorded anywhere?** Not found this session; his wife Abigail (Hyde) Stowell reportedly died 12 Aug 1777, per a WikiTree/FamilySearch-derived summary, but Isaac Sr.'s own death was not located.
6. **Findagrave and WikiTree profiles for Samuel Stowell, Israel Stowell, and Abigail Hyde Stowell exist** (identified by URL/title in search results) **but returned blocked/empty responses when fetched directly this session** (403 Forbidden from Find a Grave; empty content from WikiTree) — worth revisiting with a different access method to pick up any additional source citations or Find a Grave memorial/cemetery details.
