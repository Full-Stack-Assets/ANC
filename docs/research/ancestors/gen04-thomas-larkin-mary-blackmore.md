# Thomas Larkin & Mary Blackmore (Generation 4)

Parents of Ellen M. Larkin (1906–1995, m. Frederick Lewis Reagan of Brockton, MA). Tree IDs I182195857640 / I182625288498.

## Overview

The tree's profile for this couple is a heavy conflation of several unrelated people, and this dossier's main contribution is untangling it with the GRO (England & Wales civil registration) indexes. The real ancestor is a Thomas Larkin born about 1860–1864 in Liverpool to Irish Catholic parents (Thomas Larkin of Co. Galway and Catherine Burton), who by the 1911 census was living in Whitehaven, Cumberland with his young daughter Ellen — the Ellen M. Larkin who emigrated via Quebec in 1921, crossed into the U.S. at Bridgewater, Maine in 1929, lived in Presque Isle in 1935, and married Frederick Lewis Reagan. The Maine events on his profile (Benedicta 1877, arrival 1881, Howe Brook 1918, 1920 census, death 1920 Maine) belong to a *different* Thomas Larkin of Aroostook County, and the "Mary Blackmore" profile merges at least four different English women; the woman who died in 1906 cannot be Ellen's mother, because Ellen was born after that death.

## Verified facts

- **A Thomas Larkin family of St Alban's parish, Liverpool, headed by Galway-born Thomas Larkin and Catherine Burton (m. 23 Nov 1851, Liverpool), appears in the 1861, 1871 and 1881 England censuses** — per the tree's own sourced events on the gen-5 couple (1861/1871/1881 England Census citations in the tree data). Our Thomas is one of their children. St Alban's (Athol Street, Vauxhall) was a Roman Catholic parish serving the Liverpool Irish. [Tree sources, accepted as documented at gen 5; not independently re-verified here.]
- **Ellen Larkin's birth was registered in Whitehaven district, March quarter 1907, GRO vol 10b p 689** — verified directly in FreeBMD. This is the *only* Ellen Larkin ever registered in Whitehaven district (checked 1890–1915), and it matches the tree's alternate birth date for Ellen of **13 Nov 1906** (a mid-November birth is normally registered in the following quarter). It is incompatible with the "8 Nov 1905" date and with a birth in "Hamilton, England". [FreeBMD, GRO Births Mar 1907, Larkin, Ellen, Whitehaven 10b 689]
- **GRO Deaths Sep qtr 1906: Mary Larkin, age 54, West Derby district (Liverpool), vol 8b p 428** — verified in FreeBMD. Age 54 = born ~1851/52, which fits the Lynton, Devon baptism of 26 Sep 1852 attached to "Mary Blackmore," and the quarter fits the tree's burial date of 10 Aug 1906 in Merseyside (the tree's "England & Scotland, Select Cemetery Registers" hit is almost certainly a Liverpool municipal cemetery register). This is very likely the record behind the tree's death/burial events — see Corrections for why she cannot be Ellen's mother.
- **No death of any Thomas Larkin was registered in Whitehaven district between 1906 and 1940** — verified in FreeBMD (all Larkin deaths in the district 1905–1925: Catherine 73 and Mary Ellen 42 in Mar 1906, William 60 in Jun 1918, Mary A. 27 in Mar 1924). Consistent with Thomas leaving Whitehaven (emigration or death elsewhere) after 1911.
- **No Larkin–Blackmore marriage exists in the GRO indexes 1868–1910** — verified via FreeBMD spouse cross-matching in two passes (1868–1880 and 1880–1910): zero matches.
- **No Thomas Larkin married in Thanet district (which includes Ramsgate) in 1904–1907.** The complete GRO list of Thomas Larkin marriages Q4 1905–Q4 1907: Nottingham (Thomas Alfred, Dec 1905), Birmingham (Mar 1906), Chesterfield (Jun 1906), Middlesbrough (Sep 1906), Stockton (Sep 1906), Ashton (Sep 1907), Barnsley (Dec 1907). None is at Ramsgate. Additionally, the transcribed St Lawrence Ramsgate marriage register 1905–06 contains no Larkin or Blackmore entries.
- **Benedicta/Silver Ridge, Aroostook County context**: Benedicta was founded 1834 by Bishop Benedict Fenwick as an Irish Catholic colony; the tree's "residence 1877 Benedicta; Silver Ridge" event comes from the 1877 Roe & Colby *Atlas of Aroostook County* land-ownership map (the tree's "U.S., Indexed County Land Ownership Maps, 1860-1918" source). A man who "arrived 1881" cannot be an 1877 landowner — internal evidence of conflation.

## Corrections to the tree

1. **Marriage "25 Dec 1905 @ St Lukes Ramsgate" → no such marriage; remove.** No Thomas Larkin marriage was registered in or near Thanet in 1904–1907 (GRO/FreeBMD, full list above), no Larkin×Blackmore marriage exists in GRO 1868–1910, and the tree's own cited source for the marriage is the *New York, New York Extracted Marriage Index 1866–1937* — a mis-attached hint for a different couple (there are many Thomas Larkins and Mary Blackmores).
2. **Mary Blackmore as died "About 1906, Lancashire" (buried 10 Aug 1906, Merseyside) cannot be Ellen's mother.** Ellen was born ~13 Nov 1906 (birth registered Whitehaven Q1 1907, GRO 10b 689) — three months *after* that burial. Either (a) the 1906 death (GRO: Mary Larkin, 54, West Derby, Q3 1906) belongs to a different, older Mary Larkin of Liverpool, or (b) "Mary Blackmore" is not the biological mother of Ellen. Note the mother-as-"Mary Blackmore" likely derives from Ellen's own U.S. Social Security application (SS Applications & Claims Index is among Ellen's sources), so the *name* is probably right and the *identification* (with the 1852 Devon woman who died 1906) is what's wrong.
3. **The five baptisms on Mary Blackmore (1852 Lynton Devon, 1864 Broadclyst Devon, 1867 Wigan, 1871 England, 1886 England) are five different women; remove all but at most one.** A person cannot be baptized five times across 34 years in three counties. If Ellen's mother reported ~England birth c. 1865–1885, the 27 May 1867 Wigan (All Saints) baptism is geographically the most plausible candidate, but none is verified.
4. **Thomas Larkin's Maine events belong to (at least) one different man and should be split off.** The events "residence 1877 Benedicta/Silver Ridge", "arrival 1881", "military 1914", "residence 8 Apr 1918 Howe Brook" (from the Army Transport Service passenger lists source — a WWI embarkation record of a young soldier, impossible for a man born 1860), "residence Presque Isle", "death 1920 Maine" and the 1920 U.S. census source describe an Aroostook County Thomas Larkin (born Ireland ~1864 per that census, immigrated 1881). Our Thomas was enumerated in Whitehaven, Cumberland in the 2 April 1911 England census — he cannot also be the Maine settler of 1877–1881. Daughter Ellen's own later Aroostook residences (border crossing at Bridgewater, ME 1929; Presque Isle 1935) probably encouraged the merge, and any kinship between the two Larkin groups is unproven.
5. **Birth "1860 @ Liverpool" vs "abt 1864 @ Ireland": keep Liverpool, drop Ireland.** The ~1864-Ireland figure comes from the 1920 U.S. census of the Aroostook Thomas. The 1911 census (Whitehaven) is the record behind "1860 Liverpool". No GRO birth registration of a Thomas Larkin in Liverpool/West Derby exists 1859–1861; the two candidate registrations are **Liverpool Q1 1863 (8b 48)** and **Liverpool Q2 1864 (8b 212)** — so "about 1860–1864, Liverpool" is the honest statement (Irish Catholic births of this era also sometimes went unregistered).
6. **Duplicate parent entry**: Thomas's parents list "Thomas Larkin" twice alongside Catherine Burton — data artifact, deduplicate.
7. **Ellen M. Larkin's birth "8 Nov 1905 @ Hamilton, England" (gen-3 file) → 13 Nov 1906, Whitehaven, Cumberland.** "Hamilton" is not an English place (garbled entry; Hamilton is in Scotland), and the GRO index supports the 1906 date. Her SSA-derived 1905 date is a common year-shift error.

## New findings (not in the tree)

- **GRO Births Mar qtr 1907: Larkin, Ellen — Whitehaven 10b 689** (the anchor record for Ellen; her GRO birth certificate, orderable from GRO with this reference, would name her mother with maiden name and settle the "Mary Blackmore" question).
- **GRO Deaths Sep qtr 1906: Larkin, Mary, 54 — West Derby 8b 428** (the probable source of the tree's 1906 death/burial; born ~1852).
- **GRO Deaths Mar qtr 1906: Larkin, Mary Ellen, 42 — Whitehaven 10b 429**, and Larkin, Catherine, 73 — Whitehaven 10b 430 (same quarter): there was an *older, established Larkin family in Whitehaven*, with births registered there from at least 1890 (Catherine 1890, John 1891, Mary 1891, Simon 1892, Grace Margaret 1893, John William 1894, William 1894 & 1895, Mary Ann 1897, Albert Ernest 1898, Catherine 1899, Hugh 1901, Samuel 1903 — full FreeBMD sweep 1890–1915). Some of these could be Ellen's older siblings if Thomas was in Whitehaven before 1906, or they may be the separate clan whose deaths (Mary Ellen 1906, Catherine 1906) got tangled into this line. The 1901 and 1911 census households would separate them.
- **No Thomas Larkin marriage in Whitehaven district 1890–1915** (only Catherine 1890, William 1894, Annie 1910). If Thomas married Ellen's mother, it happened elsewhere — Liverpool candidates from the GRO marriage pages checked: Q2 1888 Liverpool 8b 331 (bride Anne Cullen or Alice Murphy), Q3 1889 Liverpool 8b 278 (Annie Branigan or Hannah Whitworth), Q2 1891 Toxteth Park 8b 438 (Anne Williams), Q3 1903 West Derby 8b 956 (Margaret Denniss or Elizabeth McCabe). None involves a Blackmore; a Scottish or Irish marriage, or an unregistered union, remain possible.
- **Candidate deaths for Thomas** (if he stayed in England; born 1860–65): W. Derby Q2 1914 age 54; W. Derby Q3 1918 age 55; Chorlton Q1 1920 age 55; Prescot Q2 1913 age 48 and Q2 1915 age 60 (FreeBMD sweep of Thomas Larkin deaths 1911–1925). None verified. Alternatively he emigrated — Ellen's 1921 Canada Ocean Arrivals Form 30A (in her source list) would name the relative she was joining.
- **Family origin detail from the gen-5 tree items**: Thomas's father Thomas Larkin (b. ~1823–25, bapt. 14 Jul 1839 Galway per Catholic parish registers) was son of Laurence Larkin & Winifreda Mulken, with an 1901-census link to Muckanagh, Meelick, Co. Galway; his siblings per the 1861–1881 Liverpool censuses included Patrick James, Margaret, Catherine, Frances Ann, William and Ellen Larkin. Our Ellen was thus named for an aunt.
- **Benedicta, Maine background** (relevant to the conflated Aroostook Larkins): Irish-Catholic colony founded 1834 by Bishop Benedict Fenwick of Boston; Silver Ridge Plantation organized 1863 adjacent; the 1877 Roe & Colby county atlas (digitalmaine.com) is the "land ownership map" source behind the 1877 event.

## Open questions

1. **Who was Ellen's mother?** Order the GRO birth certificate (Ellen Larkin, Mar qtr 1907, Whitehaven 10b 689) or check the GRO online index (mother's maiden name shown for 1907 births). If it reads "formerly Blackmore," the name stands and only the 1852/1906 identification is wrong.
2. **What happened to Thomas after April 1911?** He did not die in Whitehaven. Check: Ellen's Form 30A (May 1921, Quebec — destination/relative field), the 1921 Canada census for Larkins in New Brunswick/Quebec, and the Liverpool-area death candidates above. Did he accompany or precede Ellen across the Atlantic?
3. **The 1911 census household (RG14, Whitehaven)**: pull the full schedule — it will give Thomas's exact age, birthplace, occupation, marital status (widower?), years married, and any other children. This is the single highest-value record not yet examined in full.
4. **Is the Aroostook Thomas Larkin (b. Ireland ~1864, imm. 1881, d. ~1920) a relative** (e.g., an uncle or cousin whose presence explains why Ellen settled in Presque Isle by 1935)? Check the 1920 census image for his household and St. Benedict's cemetery records, Benedicta.
5. **Ellen's 1939 England & Wales Register entry (Cumberland)** conflicts with her 1940 U.S. census enumeration (Weymouth, MA, with 1935 residence Presque Isle). Probably another wrong merge on her profile — verify and detach.
6. Which Whitehaven Larkin births 1890–1903 (if any) are Ellen's full siblings?
