# John Henry Hohman (1880–1935) & Elizabeth M. Hohman

Generation 6 direct ancestor (tree ID I182541955589). Researched 2026-07-03 from the tree's cited census/death/Find A Grave sources plus a Pittsburgh-area newspaper death-notice index and cemetery-transcription search snippets. Note: a same-surname "Hohmann" line elsewhere in this tree (Franklin P.S. Hohmann & Mary Crist of Trenton, NJ, gen04, and Bertha E. Hohmann Albertson, gen03) is a **separate, unrelated Hohmann family** — Franklin was born in 1855 in Philadelphia to parents other than this couple, and predates John Henry Hohman's own 1880 birth, so no lineal connection is possible between the two branches despite the shared surname.

## Overview

John Henry Hohman was born 14 July 1880 in Germany and immigrated to the Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania area, where he and his wife Elizabeth raised at least nine children in the Braddock/North Braddock steel-mill district of Allegheny County. He died 30 June 1935 in North Braddock at age 55; his death was reported in a Pittsburgh-area newspaper the next day. He is buried at what is very likely All Saints (Braddock) Catholic Cemetery, whose Find A Grave location is itself listed under "Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania" — consistent with the tree's burial-place field. His wife, Elizabeth M. Hohman, appears to have survived him by 22 years.

## Verified facts

- **Death date 30 June 1935, North Braddock, Allegheny Co., PA — corroborated by an independent source.** The Allegheny County, PA death-notice index (compiled from Pittsburgh-area newspapers by the USGenWeb Archives project) lists: "HOHMAN, John H, 55yo, North Braddock, notice date 1935-07-01, death date 1935-06-30, Pitt PostGaz" — i.e., a death notice ran in the *Pittsburgh Post-Gazette* on 1 July 1935 reporting a death the prior day. Age 55 is exactly consistent with a 14 July 1880 birth (he would have turned 55 the following two weeks). This independently corroborates the tree's sourced death date and adds the specific newspaper and the more precise locality "North Braddock" (the tree's death record says "Braddock, Allegheny County"). Source: Allegheny County PA Archives, Death Index from Pittsburgh Area Newspapers, letter H (http://files.usgwarchives.net/pa/allegheny/vitals/deaths/pburg-news/death-index-h.txt).
- **Birth 14 July 1880, Germany — as tree states, sourced to PA death certificate.** No independent German birth/baptismal record was located in this session (see Open questions).
- **1920 residence: North Braddock Ward 3, Allegheny Co., PA — as tree states (1920 census), consistent with the 1935 death notice's "North Braddock" locality**, i.e., the family appears to have remained in the same North Braddock neighborhood for at least 15 years.
- **Burial location:** the tree records "Pittsburgh, Allegheny County, PA" with no named cemetery. Web search of cemetery-transcription material for All Saints Braddock Catholic Cemetery (St. Joseph Sections F & G) returned a snippet reading "HOHMAN JOHN H. 1880 1935 ... Father" together with "HOHMAN ELIZABETH M. 1882 1957 ... Mother — JOHN H. HOHMAN stone," i.e., a shared stone/plot for the couple. This is consistent with — and plausibly the specific basis for — the tree's Find A Grave-sourced burial entry, since Find A Grave's own listing for this cemetery is titled "All Saints Braddock Catholic Cemetery in **Pittsburgh**, Pennsylvania." I was not able to load the full rootsweb transcription page directly (site returned HTTP 403 to automated fetches in this session), so this is graded **documented via secondary snippet, not independently re-verified** — see Open questions.

## Corrections to the tree

No outright factual errors were identified in the tree's sourced events for John Henry Hohman himself. One flag for the parents' entry:

- **Possible parent-generation mismatch (unconfirmed, not asserted as a correction).** A Find A Grave memorial for a "Maurice John Hohman (1873–1919)," buried at the same All Saints Braddock Catholic Cemetery, surfaced in search results. If this is the same "Maurice / Morris J. Hohman" the tree names as John Henry's father, the birth year (1873) would make him only 7 years old at John Henry's 1880 birth — impossible as a father. Either (a) this Maurice John Hohman is a different person (e.g., a brother or nephew of John Henry rather than his father), or (b) the tree's father is a different, older Maurice/Morris Hohman not yet located online. I am not changing the tree's parent listing on this basis — it is an open question, not a confirmed correction — but the discrepancy should be resolved before treating "1873–1919" as the father's dates.

## New findings (not in the tree)

- **Wife Elizabeth M. Hohman's birth/death years: 1882–1957** (per the cemetery-transcription snippet described above; the tree lists her only as an undated spouse). If accurate, she outlived John Henry by 22 years.
- **Newspaper death notice:** John H. Hohman's death was reported in the *Pittsburgh Post-Gazette*, 1 July 1935 (see Verified facts).
- **Possible sibling(s) in the same cemetery, same generation:** the same Braddock cemetery-transcription search turned up "HOHMAN ENOS 1878–1919 ... Father" — an Enos Hohman, only two years older than John Henry, dying in 1919 (the influenza-pandemic/post-WWI period). A separate search for the Braddock Hohman family found an "**Ignatz Enos Hohman**," married to **Matilda C. Ruppel**, with a son **William J. Hohman** (b. 28 Nov 1908 Pennsylvania, d. Oct 1984 Braddock/North Braddock, per FamilySearch/Ancestry family-record aggregation). These two "Enos" references likely describe the same man and are plausibly a brother of John Henry Hohman (same surname, same town, same generation, buried in the same Catholic cemetery) — but no direct sibling-relationship record was found; this is **inferred, not documented**.
- **Possible related death, 1936:** the same newspaper death-notice index lists "HOHMAN, Margaret (née Campbell), North Braddock, d. 30 May 1936" — a Hohman woman with an Irish/Scots-Irish maiden name dying in the same neighborhood less than a year after John Henry. She may be a daughter-in-law (wife of one of the sons), but no connecting record was found. Noted for future research, not asserted as a relative.
- **Death-notice index also lists a Frederick Hohman "estate" notice, Allegheny, 1906**, and several other Hohman/Hohmann Pittsburgh-area deaths (1889–1936) that do not obviously connect to this family based on names/ages alone; none matched the tree's named children (Violet, James Bernard, Gertrude, Alice Marie, Adeline/Dougherty, Regus, Elmer, Robert William, John Heinrich) in the same index, so no additional vital records for the children were confirmed in this session.
- **Naming pattern note:** one son is recorded in the tree as "**John Heinrich Hohmann**" — the German-language form of the father's own name "John Henry Hohman." This is consistent with a German immigrant family that used both an anglicized form (Henry) and the original German form (Heinrich) across generations/records, and is very likely why the tree captured two visually similar names as father and son rather than a duplicate/error — but I did not find an independent record isolating this son to confirm his own vital dates.
