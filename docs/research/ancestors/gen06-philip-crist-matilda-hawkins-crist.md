## Overview

Philip Crist (b. 10 Mar 1780, Goshen, Orange Co., NY – d. 29 May 1867, Goshen, Orange Co., NY) and his wife Matilda Hawkins (b. 30 Jun 1793, Blooming Grove, Orange Co., NY – d. 7 Dec 1861, Middletown, Orange Co., NY) were an early-19th-century couple in Orange County, New York, an area long settled by Quaker and Dutch-descended families. Despite a substantial search, almost no independently verifiable period documentation (census, church, cemetery, or newspaper records) could be located for this specific couple through free web sources; a matching entry exists in the crowd-sourced FamilySearch Family Tree, corroborating the tree's birth/death years but adding no primary-source detail accessible without a FamilySearch login. This is consistent with the sibling dossier for their presumed son, David Hawkins Crist (gen05), which independently flagged this couple's parentage of David as "unverified."

## Open questions

- **Age-gap concern with son David Hawkins Crist.** The already-researched descendant dossier (`gen05-david-hawkins-crist-almina-lucia-lucy-va.md`) independently found a Crist family in Lansing, Allamakee Co., Iowa: David H. Crist, cemetery-recorded birth year 1836 (tree said "abt 1838"), died 7 Dec 1871, "said to be born in Montgomery, Orange County, New York." If this David is indeed the son of Philip Crist (b. 1780) and Matilda Hawkins (b. 1793), Matilda would have been about 43–45 years old at his birth — biologically possible but late, and worth double-checking against a full sibling list (the tree currently shows David Hawkins Crist as the only listed child of this couple, which is unusual for a couple married in this era and may indicate missing older children, or that David was a much younger, late-life child, or that the parent-child link is itself an error inherited from a compiled online tree). That dossier explicitly logged the parentage as "unverified" and recommended checking 1850/1860 Orange Co., NY census records — that recommendation is repeated here.
- **Location mismatch: Goshen/Blooming Grove/Middletown (this couple) vs. Montgomery, Orange Co., NY (David's presumed birthplace).** Goshen, Blooming Grove, Middletown, and Montgomery are all towns within Orange County, NY but are geographically and historically distinct communities (Montgomery is in the northern part of the county near the Wallkill River; Goshen/Blooming Grove/Middletown cluster in the central county). No record was found explaining how/when the family might have moved between these towns, or confirming they are the same family at all.
- **Parents of Philip Crist** — tree lists "Sgt. Philippus/Philip Crist" and "Catharina Robberson-Crist." No Revolutionary War pension, muster roll, or genealogical compilation matching a Sergeant Philippus/Philip Crist of Orange or Ulster Co., NY could be found via free search (NY in the Revolution rosters, revwarapps.org, Fold3 index pages, DAR-adjacent sources were checked). The unusual surname "Robberson" for the mother could not be corroborated in any New York genealogical compilation searched (a single unrelated 1718 baptism record mentioning a "Johanna Robberson" as godparent, in an unrelated family, was the only near-hit, and is almost certainly irrelevant).
- **Parents of Matilda Hawkins** — tree lists "David Hawkins Junior" and "Sarah Horton." Both surnames (Hawkins and Horton) are well documented among the Quaker and Long Island-descended families who settled Orange County (the Horton family in particular has a published 1906 genealogy, *Horton Genealogy... Descendants of Barnabas Horton of Southold*, digitized on Archive.org), but that published Horton genealogy's index does not include a Sarah Horton married to a David Hawkins, and no record links a "David Hawkins Junior" of Blooming Grove to this Sarah Horton. This does not rule out the tree's claim — the published Horton book covers specific lines and is not exhaustive — but it means the claim remains uncorroborated by that source.
- **No cemetery or Find a Grave record located for either spouse.** Direct queries to Find a Grave's search endpoint returned HTTP 403 in this environment (the site blocks unauthenticated automated fetches), and search-engine indexing of Find a Grave did not surface a specific memorial page for "Philip Crist" (1780–1867) or "Matilda Hawkins Crist" (1793–1861) in Goshen/Middletown, Orange Co., NY. A manually-run Find a Grave search (logged-in browser) is very likely to succeed where automated search failed, and should be the first follow-up step. Slate Hill Cemetery (Goshen) was checked via a free-standing transcription (interment.net, 132 burials) with no Crist or Hawkins entries — but this is a small subset of Goshen's cemeteries and not the actual burial-record source, so it is inconclusive.
- **No 1800–1860 US Federal Census household could be located** for Philip Crist in Goshen or Blooming Grove, NY, or for the family in Middletown at Matilda's 1861 death. FamilySearch and Ancestry census-index pages are not accessible to unauthenticated automated fetch in this environment; a logged-in genealogy-account search (FamilySearch is free) is the natural next step and would likely resolve several of the open questions above at once (household composition/other children, exact residence, occupation).
- **No obituary was located.** The Goshen Public Library's local "Independent Republican" obituary index (covering 1820–present) is the natural source for both deaths (1867 in Goshen, 1861 in Middletown) but its online finder tool (evanced.info) was not reachable from this environment (DNS resolution failed); a direct visit or phone/email request to the library ((845) 294-6606 ext. 103, per its LibGuide) is recommended.
