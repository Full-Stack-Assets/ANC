# Sarah Wells (1694–1796) and William Bull — Generation 9

## Overview

Sarah Wells was born 6 April 1694 (per the tree; see Corrections/Open questions on the family's own 1694/1696 ambiguity) at Staten Island, Richmond, New York, and died 21 April 1796 at Hamptonburgh, Orange County, New York, aged 100–102 depending on which birth year is used — buried at Campbell Hall (the Bull-Wells family burying ground, later called Hamptonburgh Cemetery). Unlike most flagged records in this tree, she is not a bare stub: Sarah Wells is a locally celebrated figure in Orange County, NY history, reputed to be the first female European settler of the Wawayanda Patent (the Goshen/Hamptonburgh area), sent at about age 16 (c. 1712) by her guardian Christopher Denn to hold Denn's land claim in the wilderness. She married William Bull (b. 1 Feb 1689, Wolverhampton, Staffordshire, England; d. 17 Feb 1755, Hamptonburgh), a stonemason who had come to work the Denn/Crommelin land, in 1718 — reportedly the first marriage between European settlers in the Goshen area. The couple is the subject of a dedicated 1974 book-length genealogy and their homestead, the Bull Stone House, survives as a preserved historic property in Hamptonburgh, NY.

The record carries an audit flag of *parent-too-old* (age ~51 at the birth of her listed child Elenor Weller, 1745). External research confirms this is not a data error: it is the family's own well-documented capstone fact — Elenor was the last of Sarah's 12 children, explicitly said to have been born "just before Sarah's 52nd birthday."

## Verified facts

- **Sarah Wells married William Bull in 1718** at the Wawayanda Patent settlement (Goshen area, Orange County, NY); he was a stonemason from Wolverhampton, Staffordshire, England, who arrived in the colonies c. 1715 to work for patent-holder Daniel Crommelin. [documented — Bull Stone House Association, Find A Grave S516331756, WikiTree, Geni]
- **She and William Bull had 12 children — 5 sons and 7 daughters — born 1721 to 1745, all of whom reportedly lived to adulthood, married, and raised families.** This tree currently carries 10 of the 12 (see New findings for the two missing). The ten in this dataset, in birth order: John Bull (b. 3 May 1721), William Bull (b. 13 Mar 1723), Sarah Bull/Booth (b. 1 Sep 1725, m. a Booth), Thomas Bull (b. 27 Dec 1727, builder of the Hill-Hold stone house), Isaac Bull (b. 17 Nov 1729), Mary Bull/Booth (b. 3 Feb 1733, m. a Booth), Margaret Bull (b. 1 May 1736, m. Silas Horton — the direct-line child, see below), Catherine Bull/Faulkner (b. 24 May 1738, m. a Faulkner), Ann Bull/Eager (b. 3 Nov 1740, m. an Eager), and Elenor/Eleanor Bull/Weller (b. 4 Mar 1745, m. a Weller — the flagged child). [documented, per-child — Find A Grave S516331756; corroborated in aggregate by Bull Stone House Association and multiple Chronicle-Network local-history articles]
- **William Bull died 17 Feb 1755 at Hamptonburgh**; both are buried at Campbell Hall/Hamptonburgh Cemetery, per Find A Grave. [documented — S516331756]
- **The direct-line child in this tree is Margaret Bull (I182567600211)**, who married Silas Horton (b. 27 Dec 1730, Southold, Suffolk Co., NY; d. 1820, Goshen) and had at least one child, Sarah Horton (I182534044137, b. 1764 – d. 1852), continuing the pedigree. Elenor Weller, the flagged child, is a documented sibling off the direct line. [documented/inferred, per underlying per-record sourcing]

## New findings (not in the tree)

- **Two documented children are missing from this dataset**: Esther (Bull) Miller, born 31 May 1731, married John Miller of Montgomery, NY, and had 8 children; and Richard Bull. Neither has a record id in this dataset (no Find A Grave-sourced person was located to attach), so nothing was fabricated — flagged as an open backlog item rather than invented. [documented in secondary sources; not yet reflected in this dataset]
- **Sarah remarried after William Bull's 1755 death.** She married Johannes (John) Miller, a German Lutheran settler of Montgomery, NY, sometime between 1756 and 1770, then separated from him ("essentially divorced") in 1770 and returned to live out her life at the Bull Stone House. No record id exists for Johannes Miller in this dataset, so no spouse link was added. Intriguingly, her daughter Esther (Bull) Miller's husband, John Miller, appears (per secondary sources) to be this second husband's son — i.e., a mother-and-daughter double-marriage into the same Miller family. This deserves independent verification before being added to the graph. [documented in secondary sources, unverified against primary records]
- **She is credited with 335 direct descendants at the time of her death** and an estimated 20,000 living descendants today, per a 2010s Bull family reunion news article — consistent with, and likely the origin of, the extensive secondary documentation available for this ancestor. [documented — Chronicle-Network]

## Open questions

- **Exact birth year: 1694 or 1696?** Descendants' family bibles reportedly disagree (per Bull Stone House Association), giving either "102 years, 15 days" (b. 6 Apr 1694) or "100 years, 15 days" (b. 6 Apr 1696) at her 21 Apr 1796 death. No birth record exists — colonial-era Staten Island predates civil registration, and she was not the Denn family's biological child. This tree keeps the majority-cited 1694, consistent with Find A Grave, WikiTree, and Geni, but the true figure is unresolved and may never be, absent a primary source (a family bible itself, not yet located online).
- **Should Esther (Bull) Miller and Richard Bull be added as new `M`-prefixed records?** Per `data/people/README.md` convention, a person proven by research but absent from the Ancestry export should get a manually-created `M0001`-style record. This dossier flags the need but does not create the records, since their own vital details (Richard Bull's dates in particular) were not independently confirmed here — recommend follow-up research before adding.
- **Is Johannes/John Miller (Sarah's second husband) attachable?** If independently sourced (e.g., Montgomery, NY church or land records), he should be added as a second spouse on I182567600231, and the Esther Bull–John Miller Jr. connection verified.
- **Sarah Wells's own parentage remains a research dead end** (as already reflected by the tree's placeholder parent record, Christopher Denn, tagged "adoptive father," inferred/unsourced) — she was reportedly orphaned and raised/bound out by the Denn family; her biological parents were not identified in this research pass.
