# John Murray (1791–1821) and Honoria (Hierlihy?) Murray — Generation 7

> **Flag investigated:** `child-after-death` — the tree attaches a child, "Michael Murray" (b. 1843), to a father who died in 1821/1822.

## Overview

John Murray was born 21 August 1791 — the tree's "documented" (Geneanet-sourced) vitals place his birth at Saint John, New Brunswick, but the record's own `events` array and every external source instead point to Prince Edward Island, most specifically Bedeque, Prince County. He was the son of John Murray (Jr., d. 1810, Tabusintac) and Eliza/Liza Chambers, a Scots family (from Canonbie, Dumfriesshire) that settled first on PEI and then at Tabusintac, Northumberland County, New Brunswick, by 1799–1803. Around 1813–1815 he married a woman recorded in this tree as "Honoria Beatty" but named in every independent source as **Honoria Hierlihy**, daughter of Philip Hierlihy and Charlotte (Howe) Taylor — members of one of Tabusintac's best-documented founding families. John Murray died about 1821 at Tabusintac; his widow remarried Ephraim Gay in 1822 and was still living, as "Honora Gay, widow," in the 1861 New Brunswick census.

The tree's flagged problem — a child "Michael Murray" supposedly born in 1843, more than two decades after the father's death — turns out to be a straightforward mis-attachment, not a genuine chronological anomaly in the family itself. The other five children in the tree's list line up cleanly, one-for-one, with an independently sourced family reconstruction (WikiTree, citing New Brunswick Genealogical Society archival files, Prince Edward Island baptismal/marriage registers, and 1851/1861 censuses).

## Verified facts

- **Birth 21 Aug 1791**, son of John Murray Jr. (b. 5 Apr 1761 Canonbie, Dumfriesshire, Scotland; d. 1810 Tabusintac, NB) and Eliza (Liza) Chambers (b. ~1752 Scotland). The parents emigrated from Scotland, settling first on Prince Edward Island and then at Tabusintac, New Brunswick, by 1799–1803. [documented — WikiTree Murray-2863, citing NBGS-Miramichi archival files: "A Murray family genealogy" by Sharon Murray, "Murrays of Tabusintac" by Thomas A. Murray, and Hazel Murray's "Descendants of John Murray, United Empire Loyalist"]
- **Marriage to Honoria Hierlihy, ~1813, Tabusintac** — she was daughter of Philip Hierlihy and Charlotte Howe Taylor, of the well-documented pioneer Hierlihy/Taylor family of the Miramichi. [documented — WikiTree Murray-2863, citing NBGS-Miramichi listing for Philip Hierlihy, cross-referencing James A. Fraser's *Loggieville: Child of Miramichi*, Jane L. Webster's *Descendants of Rev. John Urquhart*, and *Loyalist Lineages of Canada 1783–1983*]
- **Death about 1821, Tabusintac, New Brunswick** — corroborated independently: his widow Honoria remarried Ephraim Gay in 1822 (per NBGS-sourced WikiTree bio), and the 1861 New Brunswick census (Northumberland Co., Alnwick, microfilm C-1003/1004) lists "Honora Gay," a widow. [documented — S508118847 Geneanet Community Trees Index, corroborated by WikiTree Murray-2863/NBGS + 1861 census]
- **Five documented children, matching five of this record's six**: James (b. c.1814); Ann Jane (b. c.1815, m. John Blake, son of Robert — 1851 census shows the couple together in Northumberland Co.); Mary (b. 1817, m. James Robertson of Birch Hill, PEI — daughter's 1853 PARO-PEI baptismal record names "Mother's Name Mary Murray"; 1881 PEI census shows the family in Queens Co.); Charlotte Sophia (b. c.1819, m. John Jenkins, 21 Mar 1838, Prince Edward Island — FamilySearch PEI marriage register); and "possibly John" (b. c.1821). [documented — WikiTree Murray-2863, citing PARO-PEI baptismal register, 1851 and 1881 NB/PEI censuses, and FamilySearch PEI marriage registers]
- **Land grant**: "MURRAY, John Jr." received a 290-acre grant at Tabusintac, Northumberland Co., 6 Dec 1810 (Volume E, page 272, grant #568, one of 38 names on the grant). This is very likely this John Murray or his identically-named father, consistent with the family's presence at Tabusintac in this period. [documented — Provincial Archives of New Brunswick, Index to NB Land Grants 1784–1997, RS686, microfilm F16304]

## Corrections to the tree

- **"Michael Murray" (I182625292192, b./bapt. 1843) removed as a child.** His only source is S506435674, "Ireland, Catholic Parish Registers, 1655-1915," with a baptism recorded 14 Dec 1843 at **Galway, Ireland** — a place with no connection to this family, which had been settled in Atlantic Canada for over 40 years by 1843 and traces its immediate ancestry to Scotland (Canonbie, Dumfriesshire), not Ireland. This looks like a classic Ancestry-hint mismatch: an unrelated Michael Murray baptized in an Irish Catholic parish register, attached by name coincidence to a Presbyterian/Church-of-Scotland-descended New Brunswick family. Every independently sourced reconstruction of this family's children (WikiTree Murray-2863, NBGS files, PEI/NB vital and census records) lists five children, not six, and Michael is not among them.
- **Death-date internal conflict resolved in favor of 1821.** The record's `vitals.death` (documented, sourced to Geneanet S508118847) gives 1821; a separate, unsourced `events` array entry gives 18 Jun 1822. Independent corroboration (widow's 1822 remarriage; her "widow" status in the 1861 census) is consistent with either a late-1821 or very-early-1822 death, but no source was found for the specific date "18 Jun 1822," and the documented 1821 year is the better-supported of the two. Recommend treating the 1822 events entry as superseded/lower-confidence rather than an equally valid alternative.

## New findings (not in the tree)

- **Wife's real surname is very likely Hierlihy, not Beatty.** No source — Geneanet, Ancestry family trees, or open-web search — turned up any record of a "Honoria Beatty" connected to this family. Every external source instead documents Honoria **Hierlihy**, daughter of Philip Hierlihy and Charlotte Howe Taylor (Charlotte Taylor being a well-known, extensively researched early Miramichi-area settler; see charlottetaylor.ca). This looks like a tree transcription error (possibly a confusion with another Tabusintac-area family) but the correction properly belongs on Honoria's own person record (I182195863332), not this one.
- **Birthplace is more likely Prince Edward Island (Bedeque, Prince Co.) than Saint John, NB.** The tree's own `events` array (23 Aug 1791, "PE Island, Canada") already disagrees with its "documented" `vitals` entry (Saint John, NB), and WikiTree Murray-2863 independently agrees with the PEI placement, naming Bedeque specifically and describing the family's move from PEI to Tabusintac by 1799–1803. Two independent lines point to PEI; only the single Geneanet-sourced vitals entry supports Saint John.
- **Family network**: John's father-in-law Philip Hierlihy and mother-in-law Charlotte (Howe) Taylor were prominent, well-documented early Tabusintac/Miramichi settlers (Taylor in particular has a dedicated local-history research site, charlottetaylor.ca, and appears in multiple NBGS/PARO-PEI-cited works). This gives the Murray-Hierlihy marriage an unusually strong evidentiary anchor for a family this far back in early-1800s New Brunswick.
- **Land grant "John Murray Jr., Tabusintac, 1810"** raises an open question of whether this specific John Murray (the ancestor, then age ~19) or his father (also confusingly a "John Murray," who died in 1810) is the actual grantee — see Open questions.

## Open questions

- **1820s–1840s New Brunswick record gaps.** No primary birth, marriage, or burial record for John Murray himself (only secondary/compiled sources — Geneanet, Ancestry trees, WikiTree citing NBGS finding aids) was locatable on the open web. Early Alnwick Parish (Tabusintac) church registers from this period are not digitized/indexed at FamilySearch or Ancestry in a way this research could reach; the NBGS-Miramichi manuscript files (MC1/Murray #2, MC80/1535 "Hazel Murray's Descendants of John Murray, UEL") almost certainly contain the primary-source basis for the WikiTree reconstruction, but are only accessible in person or via the Miramichi Genealogical Society, not confirmed here.
- **Exact death date.** Neither "1821" nor "18 Jun 1822" in this record is tied to a primary burial or probate record; both are plausible given the widow's 1822 remarriage. A parish burial register or probate/estate file (if surviving) would settle it.
- **The sixth child, "possibly John" (b. c.1821, I182545330235 in this tree).** WikiTree itself hedges this attribution ("Possibly John, born circa 1821") — if John Sr. died in 1821, a posthumous or very-late birth is plausible but unconfirmed; this is the tree's one legitimately borderline case, unlike the clearly misattached Michael.
- **Who the real "Michael Murray" (bapt. Galway, Ireland, 1843) actually is**, and whether he belongs to some other Murray line entirely in this tree — not investigated here, since the task was to verify (and correct) his attachment to this John Murray, not to trace his real identity.
- **John Murray Jr.'s 1810 land grant** — whether the grantee is this ancestor (then ~19) or his same-named, same-year-of-death father; both are plausible and the record itself is not internally distinguishing.
- **Honoria's maiden name** should be corrected/investigated on her own person record (I182195863332); not done here as it's outside this record's scope.
