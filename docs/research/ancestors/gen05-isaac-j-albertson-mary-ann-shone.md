# Isaac J. Albertson (c. 1823/24–1896) & Mary Ann Shone (c. 1828–1900)

Generation 5 direct ancestors (Camden County, New Jersey). Researched 2026-07-03 from web sources; no paywalled record images were viewable, so census-level detail below the index level remains to be pulled from Ancestry/FamilySearch with a login.

> Note on assignment metadata: the research task header named "Ellen Josephine Swanson," but the item data file (A16.json) and dossier path unambiguously define this couple as the subject. This dossier covers Isaac J Albertson & Mary Ann Shone per the data file.

## Overview

Isaac Albertson (tree: "Isaac J") spent his whole life in the farm country just east and south of Camden, New Jersey — territory that was Gloucester County at his birth, became Camden County in 1844, and (in his neighborhood) Centre Township in 1855. He married Mary Ann Shone about 1850; she was from the Glassboro area of Gloucester County, daughter of John Shone and Catherine Cheesman. They raised at least seven children and are buried side by side in Cedar Grove Cemetery, Gloucester City, Camden County, NJ. The Albertsons descend from a long-established South Jersey Quaker family (Albertson of Newton Township, settled 1680s); the Shone, Pine, and Cheesman families were likewise old Gloucester-County stock, and the Shones and Pines intermarried more than once.

This matches the tree's parent couple and places them in Gloucester County (which then included Glassboro), consistent with Mary Ann's stated birthplace.

So the tree's sourced 1880 residence and the "Centre" death places are the same neighborhood. (Wikipedia, Centre Township, NJ.)
- **Census trail exists**: the Find A Grave memorials cite Ancestry census attachments for **1850, 1860, 1870, 1880** (Isaac) and **1850, 1870, 1880, 1900** (Mary Ann) — i.e., both are findable in every surviving census of their adult lives; the 1870 index spells the family **"Elberson"** (Isaac Elberson, age 47). Record images not viewable without subscription.

## Corrections to the tree

- **Isaac's birthplace "Center Township, Camden, New Jersey" (1824) is anachronistic** → should read (old) **Gloucester County, NJ** (the area of later Centre Twp.). Camden County was not created until 1844, and Centre Township not until 6 Mar 1855. Evidence: Centre Township incorporation history (Wikipedia); Camden County formation 1844. The place is right geographically, wrong jurisdictionally for 1824.
- **Isaac's birth year 1824** → probably **c. 1823/24**. His gravestone/memorial gives 1823; the 1870 census (age 47) implies 1822/23; the 1880 census (age 56) implies 1823/24; Ancestry's compiled record says "abt 1824." Suggest recording "c. 1823–1824." Low-stakes but the tree's flat "1824" is not certain.
- **Mary Ann's birth "20 Oct 1829"** → likely **c. 1827–1828**. Her gravestone reads "Age 72 years" at death on 19 Sep 1900, which back-computes to a birth between Sep 1827 and Sep 1900−72 ≈ Oct 1828 at the latest; Find A Grave carries her as b. 1828. The tree's exact day (20 Oct) may come from an unsourced family record and could still be right with the year shifted to 1827/1828. Flagged, not definitively resolved.
- **Mary Ann's death place "Center Township, Camden, NJ"** → Find A Grave states she died in **Gloucester City, Camden Co., NJ** (where she is buried and where the 1900 census reportedly finds her). Adjacent places; prefer Gloucester City pending her death certificate.
- **Child "Edwin S Albertson"** → compiled Ancestry data for this family lists him as **Edward Shone Albertson (1860–1932)**; the "S" almost certainly stands for Shone (his brother is "John Schone Albertson" in the tree — same maternal surname, variant spelling). Suggest rendering as Edward S(hone) Albertson pending a primary record.

## Open questions

1. **Who exactly were Isaac's parents?** The tree says John Albertson & Ann Pine; Ancestry's machine-compiled page instead gives John Albertson (1783–1857) & **Mary Craig** (1786–1865). Separately, the compiled Albertson genealogy's John (b. 1771) m. Ann Pine would have been 53 at Isaac's birth — possible but strained — and a MyHeritage index shows an **Isaac Pine Albertson (1797–1882)** who looks like a natural son of John & Ann Pine, raising the possibility that our Isaac belongs to a *different* John Albertson (perhaps John b. 1783 m. Mary Craig, with the Ann Pine attachment being a tree conflation of two Johns). Needs Isaac's 1896 NJ death certificate (NJ State Archives, deaths 1878–1900+) which should name his parents.
2. **Exact marriage record, c. 1850** — not in transcribed Camden Book A; check Gloucester County marriage returns and church registers (the family's Quaker background suggests checking Newton/Haddonfield MM records, though by 1850 they may have married in a Methodist/Episcopal church).
3. **Mary Ann's precise birth date** — gravestone age (72 in Sep 1900) vs. tree date 20 Oct 1829 conflict by 1–2 years; her death certificate (NJ State Archives, 1900) and the 1900 census month/year-of-birth column would settle it.
4. **Isaac's occupation and household composition 1850–1880** — census attachments exist on Ancestry (1850, 1860, 1870 "Elberson", 1880 Snow Hill) but images/details were behind login; pull these to get occupation (likely farmer or glass/mill work given the area) and confirm children.
5. **The middle initial "J"** — no record found using it; gravestone and indexes say plain "Isaac." Verify whether "J" has any documentary basis.
6. **Albertson plot record at Cedar Grove** — Camden County Historical Society holds the cemetery's deed/interment books (1852–2001); could reveal additional family burials (e.g., toddler Richard, d. 1858).
