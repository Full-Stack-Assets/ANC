# Douglas Leon Holst & Abigail Elizabeth (Morrow) Holst

## Overview

Douglas Leon Holst (b. 13 Jul 1908) was the fifth of six children of John Oscar
Holst, a Swedish-immigrant machinist of the Dorchester/Boston area, and his second
wife Mina Droulet Stone. He married Abigail Elizabeth Morrow (b. 11 Apr 1910, Little
Pond, Kings County, Prince Edward Island; d. Sept 1958), a Canadian immigrant who
came to the United States as an infant in 1910. The couple lived in the Boston /
Suffolk County area from at least 1930 through 1950. Web research strongly confirms
Douglas's parentage and reveals his full and half sibling set through a published
Dorchester Historical Society / Dorchester Atheneum biography of his half-brother
George Andrew Holst; it added little about Abigail's line beyond the tree.

## Verified facts

- Douglas Holst's father was **John Oscar Holst**, a Swedish immigrant (born
  Gothenburg, Sweden, c. 1858/1859; died 1938), by trade a machinist. Documented via
  the Dorchester Atheneum / Dorchester Historical Society biography of George Andrew
  Holst, plus Geni/WikiTree/FamilySearch profiles.
- Douglas's mother was **Mina Droulet Stone**, John Oscar Holst's second wife, whom
  he married in 1898 after the death of his first wife Augusta Mathilda (Svanson) from
  empyema that same year. (Dorchester Atheneum.)
- Douglas was born **1908**, the fifth of six children of John Oscar and Mina, matching
  the tree's 13 Jul 1908 birth. (Dorchester Atheneum.)
- By 1910 the Holst household lived at **19 Ericsson Street, Port Norfolk section of
  Dorchester** (part of Boston); in 1900 they had lived on Waldo Street in Dedham.
  (Dorchester Atheneum.) This is consistent with the tree's Boston/Suffolk residences.
- Abigail Elizabeth Morrow was born in **Little Pond, Kings County (Lot 56), Prince
  Edward Island, Canada**, and immigrated to the U.S. in 1910 as an infant — consistent
  with the tree's "arrival: 1910." (Tree; PEI/Little Pond context via island genealogy
  sources.)

## New findings (not in the tree)

- **Full siblings of Douglas** (children of John Oscar Holst & Mina Droulet Stone):
  Grace (1900), Olga (1901), Alice (1904), Lorimer (1906), **Douglas (1908)**, and
  John Oscar Jr. (1910). (Dorchester Atheneum.)
- **Half-siblings of Douglas** (children of John Oscar Holst & first wife Augusta
  Mathilda Svanson, m. Boston Nov 1882): Albert (1883), Victoria (1885), George Andrew
  (1 Mar 1888, b. 85 Condor Street, East Boston; 1888–1977; WWI service member), Esther
  (1895), and Florence (1897). (Dorchester Atheneum; Find a Grave memorial 215892126.)
- Father John Oscar Holst's first wife **Augusta Mathilda (Svanson) Holst** died in
  1898 of empyema (a pneumonia-associated pleural infection), which is what precipitated
  his remarriage to Mina Droulet Stone. (Dorchester Atheneum.)
- Brother **Lorimer Addison Holst** (b. 29 Jan 1906) married Anna (Annie) Stinna
  Peterson; he named a daughter "Mina Droulet Holst" (b. 1933, d. 1935) after his
  mother — corroborating the mother's distinctive name. (WikiTree Holst-616; Ancestry
  index.)
