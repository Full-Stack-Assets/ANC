# Garrett Groff (c.1738–1831) and parents Garret Groff Sr. & Susanna Sarah Maus — Generation 9/10

> **Flags being resolved:** `direct-line-issues.md` / `deep_dive_backlog.md` list three linked findings for one family: `I182381535298` Garrett Groff — *impossible-lifespan* (113 years, 1718–1831); `I182381535340` Garret Groff (his father, gen 10) — *parent-too-young* (age 3 at Garrett's 1718 birth); `I182381535311` Susanna Sarah Maus (his mother, gen 10) — *parent-too-young* (age 1 at Garrett's 1718 birth).

## Overview

Garrett Groff is a New Jersey ancestor whose vitals arrive in the tree entirely unsourced: born (per the tree) 1718 in Salem, NJ, died 1831 in New Jersey, son of Garret Groff Sr. (b. 1715, Burlington, NJ; d. 7 June 1797, Salem, NJ) and Susanna Sarah Maus (b. 1717, Burlington Co., NJ; d. 1808, Littlestown, Adams Co., PA). The tree's own numbers are internally impossible: his recorded 1718 birth sits only 3 years after his father's 1715 birth and only 1 year after his mother's 1717 birth, and combined with his recorded 1831 death produces a 113-year lifespan — beyond this repo's own 105-year audit threshold. All three flags trace to one bad number.

The key break in this research is a **genuine, externally-documented marriage**: William Nelson's *New Jersey Marriage Records, 1665–1800* (Archives of the State of New Jersey, First Series, Vol. XXII, 1900) records "Groff, Garret, and Amy Stratton, of Burlington Co., 11-15-1764," independently corroborated from the bride's side by descendant compilations (WikiTree, markstratton.org) naming "Amy (Stratton) Groff," daughter of David Stratton (1714–1771) of Evesham, Burlington Co., NJ, as marrying "Garrot Goff (Groff) of Salem" on that same date. This confirms a real Garret/Garrett Groff of Salem existed and married a real, identifiable Amy Stratton in 1764 — and that marriage date is the anchor that resolves the birth-year problem: at 1764, Amy (b. 1741 per the tree) was an ordinary bridal age of 23; Garrett, if born c. 1738 as internal family logic suggests (see below), would have been a near-contemporary age 26 — not a man of 46 (as the tree's "1718" would imply) marrying a first-time bride 23 years his junior with no evidence of a prior marriage.

## Corrections to the tree

- **Garrett Groff's birth year "1718" is corrected to "ABT 1738."** This is the single data error producing all three flags: only 3 years after his father's birth, only 1 year after his mother's birth, and (with the 1831 death) an impossible 113-year lifespan. The father's 1715 birth is the best-anchored number in the cluster (a normal generation gap below Samuel Joel Groff), so the correction falls on Garrett's own birth year, not either parent's. The now-documented 15 Nov 1764 marriage corroborates this: it makes Garrett an age-appropriate ~26-year-old groom rather than a 46-year-old one. A single-digit transcription slip ("1718" for c. 1738) is the most economical explanation. **Confidence: medium-high** for "the true birth year is materially later than 1718, most plausibly c. late 1730s" (now supported by an independent, age-appropriate marriage record); **low** for the exact year, which remains an estimate, not a sourced fact. Applied via `manual.events` on `I182381535298` (superseding, not deleting, the machine-parsed 1718 figure), plus a new sourced `manual.events` marriage entry (15 Nov 1764) and `manual.notes` on all three originally-flagged records plus Amy Stratton's and Hannah Groff's records.
- **Garrett Groff's 1831 death is left unchanged.** With a corrected c. 1738 birth, 1831 implies an age of about 93 — unusually long-lived for the period, but not a genealogically impossible lifespan under this repo's own 105-year audit threshold. No external record was found to confirm or override the 1831 figure specifically.
- **No Jr./Sr. merge was found or is asserted.** Only one Garret/Garrett Groff record exists anywhere in this tree's data (`I182381535298`); no second, same-named New Jersey Groff of the right generation turned up in FamilySearch, WikiTree, Geni, or Find A Grave searches, and the newly-found marriage record refers to a single "Garret Groff of Salem," not a pair. This is one mis-dated individual, not a two-person conflation of a father and son sharing a name.
- **Both parents' "parent-too-young" flags are resolved as a downstream consequence of the above, not by changing their own vitals.** Garret Groff Sr.'s 1715 birth and Susanna Sarah Maus's 1717 birth are left as recorded; the flags were artifacts of the son's bad birth year, not of anything wrong with the parents' own dates.

## New findings (not in the tree)

- **The 15 Nov 1764 marriage of Garret Groff and Amy Stratton, and Amy's parentage (David Stratton & Mary Elkinton of Evesham), are genuine finds not previously in this tree's data** — see Verified facts and Sources.
- **A new, unresolved conflict surfaced as a side effect of this research: daughter Hannah Groff's recorded birth year, 1758, predates her parents' now-documented 1764 marriage by six years.** Hannah cannot be a marital child of Garrett and Amy as currently dated. No record was found to establish her actual birth year; it is most plausibly sometime after 1765, but this is not asserted as a correction — only flagged, on Hannah's own record (`I182381535051`) and here, as a new open question for a future pass. This conflict was invisible to `tools/audit_tree.py` because that tool only checks parent/child age plausibility against the tree's own (wrong) parent birth years, not against marriage dates.
- **No independent record of the Groff/Dill/Maus side of this cluster (Samuel Joel Groff, Elizabeth Dill, Garret Groff Sr., or Susanna Sarah Maus specifically) was found anywhere in open sources**, despite the Stratton side turning up cleanly documented. Searches covered FamilySearch, WikiTree (Groff genealogy surname page and "Space:Groff Family History"), Geni, Find A Grave, rootsweb Groff pages, and general web, under multiple spelling variants. Only unrelated same-surname people surfaced: the Lancaster County, PA "Hans Graff/Groff" Mennonite line (see "The Groff Book," Groff History Associates, 1985; "Swiss Origins of Groff, Hess, Weber, Landis, and Oberholtzer Families," Jane Evans Best, *Pennsylvania Mennonite Heritage* 13:2, April 1990) — an entirely different family; a "John Groff" b. abt. 1715, d. bef. 10 June 1797, Frederick Co., MD; and an unrelated WikiTree "Elizabeth Groff" (b. 17 Jan 1745, Salem Co., NJ; d. 20 Oct 1786, Evesham, Burlington Co., NJ; m. Samuel Ballinger, 10 Jan 1760) who may or may not be a sibling of this line but could not be connected with confidence.
- **The Groff/Dill/Maus/Stratton surnames all place this family plausibly in the Salem/Burlington County, NJ Quaker milieu** — Evesham (Amy Stratton's birthplace and her father's home) was a historic Quaker settlement, and Salem Friends Meeting marriage-record transcriptions independently confirm a Groff family presence in neighboring Pilesgrove in the same era (e.g., "Abigail Groff of Pilesgrove," married 1783) — consistent with, though not separate proof of, this specific family.

## Open questions

- **Garrett Groff's exact birth date/place remain unrecovered.** "ABT 1738" is an internal-consistency estimate corroborated by the 1764 marriage, but no birth, baptism, or meeting record was found to confirm a specific day, month, or exact year. A Salem or Pilesgrove Monthly Meeting birth record (if the family was Quaker, as the surrounding Stratton/Evesham milieu suggests) would be the natural next place to check, but was not freely accessible on the open web.
- **Whether the 1831 death year is itself accurate is unresolved.** It implies a plausible-but-unusually-old age (~93) under the corrected birth year; no source confirms or contradicts it.
- **Hannah Groff's true birth year is now an open question in its own right** (see New findings) — her recorded 1758 conflicts with her parents' documented 1764 marriage. Not corrected here for lack of any anchoring record.
- **Susanna Sarah Maus's move from New Jersey to Littlestown, Adams County, Pennsylvania by her 1808 death is unexplained** — no record of a relocated Groff/Maus household in south-central Pennsylvania was found, and her own parents are entirely unrecorded in the tree.
- **Whether "Elizabeth Groff" (b. 1745 Salem Co., NJ; d. 1786 Evesham, Burlington Co., NJ; m. Samuel Ballinger)** is a sibling of Garrett Groff could not be confirmed — flagged for a future pass but not added to the tree.
- **No source was found for Samuel Joel Groff and Elizabeth Dill's own marriage or for Garret Groff Sr.'s parentage claim** beyond the tree's own unsourced assertion; that side of the family remains "inferred," not "documented," end to end.
