# The Mansfield Line — Lineage Survey (Generations 11–12)

## Overview

The Mansfield data supplied in this batch (item B21) is very thin: it consists of only
two distinct individuals — Andrew Mansfield of Lynn, Massachusetts (gen. 12, the
line's terminus in this dataset) and his daughter Deborah Mansfield (gen. 11) — each
duplicated once in the tree (four total records, two true people). Andrew Mansfield is a
well-documented early settler of Lynn, Essex Co., Massachusetts, part of the 1630s
"Great Migration," and is the effective immigrant ancestor visible in this data slice,
though published genealogies show his father Robert Mansfield also emigrated (1640) and
is the generation at which the well-sourced documentary trail currently ends. Several
facts in the tree (English birthplace, mother's identity) appear to be conflated or
merged from more than one source record.

## Verified facts

- Andrew Mansfield was baptized 18 March 1620 (some sources give birth c.1623) at Lockington, Leicestershire, England, son of Robert Mansfield and Elizabeth Kettle. — [WikiTree Mansfield-224](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Mansfield-224); [Geni](https://www.geni.com/people/Andrew-Mansfield-of-Lynn/6000000002208859052)
- Andrew (with brother John) was in Lynn, Massachusetts by 1638/39, ahead of their parents; their father Robert arrived in the summer of 1640. Andrew testified in a 1661 Ipswich court deposition (age ~38) that he had lived in Lynn "aboute two or three and twentye yeares." — [Miller-Anderson Histories](http://miller-aanderson.blogspot.com/2011/07/andrew-mansfield-1623-1683.html); [WikiTree Mansfield-221](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Mansfield-221)
- Andrew Mansfield served Lynn as selectman, trial/grand juror (1650–1677), sergeant (from 1663), and deputy to the Massachusetts General Court (1680–1683). — [Miller-Anderson Histories](http://miller-aanderson.blogspot.com/2011/07/andrew-mansfield-1623-1683.html)
- Andrew married three times: (1) Bethiah (surname uncertain — see below), by c.1650, mother of all eight of his children including Deborah; she died 2 July 1672. (2) Mary Neal (widow of John Neal), m. 4 June 1673, d. 27 June 1681. (3) Elizabeth Conant (widow of Lot Conant), m. 10 Jan 1681/82. — [Miller-Anderson Histories](http://miller-aanderson.blogspot.com/2011/07/andrew-mansfield-1623-1683.html); [Geni](https://www.geni.com/people/Andrew-Mansfield-of-Lynn/6000000002208859052)
- Andrew's will was dated 1 June 1679 with a codicil added at Boston 19 November 1683 (witnessed by fellow General Court members after he was "taken suddenly ill" while attending court); the estate inventory was returned 28 November 1683 — the exact date of death is not independently recorded, only bracketed by these documents. — [Geni](https://www.geni.com/people/Andrew-Mansfield-of-Lynn/6000000002208859052)
- Deborah Mansfield (b. 1 Jan 1666, Lynn) married Philip White of Beverly c.1685, per the Mansfield Genealogy (Daland & Mansfield, 1980) as summarized in the Miller-Anderson compilation. — [Miller-Anderson Histories](http://miller-aanderson.blogspot.com/2011/07/andrew-mansfield-1623-1683.html)
- Andrew's father, Robert Mansfield, was baptized 8 November 1590 at Blofield (St. Andrew & St. Peter), Norwich, Norfolk, England; married Elizabeth Kettle at Lockington, Leicestershire, 10 February 1613; emigrated to New England in 1640; died at Lynn 16 December 1666 (will dated 3 Aug 1664, naming wife Elizabeth and sons Andrew, Joseph, John). — [WikiTree Mansfield-221](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Mansfield-221); [Geni Robert Mansfield of Lynn](https://www.geni.com/people/Robert-Mansfield-of-Lynn/6000000002152207890); [Find a Grave — Robert Mansfield](https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/116548927/robert-mansfield)
- Elizabeth Kettle (Robert's wife) was likely the Elizabeth Kettle baptized 19 December 1585 at Loughborough, Leicestershire, daughter of Robert Kettle and Agnes/Alice Burrows; she died 8 September 1673 at Lynn, age ~87. — [WikiTree Kettle-568](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Kettle-568)
- Robert Mansfield's own parents have not been identified by any consulted genealogy — the documented paper trail for this immigrant family ends with Robert. — [WikiTree Mansfield-221](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Mansfield-221)

## Corrections to the tree

1. **Andrew Mansfield's birthplace: "Norfolk, England" -> Lockington, Leicestershire, England.**
   Evidence: WikiTree and Geni consistently place Andrew's baptism (18 Mar 1620) at Lockington, Leicestershire — the location where his father Robert married Elizabeth Kettle in 1613 and where their children were baptized. Norfolk (specifically Blofield, near Norwich) is where **Robert** himself was baptized in 1590, one generation earlier. This looks like a generation-conflation artifact — Robert's birthplace appears to have been copied onto Andrew's record.

2. **Andrew's mother, listed as "Grace Elizabeth (Townsend) Mansfield, Mayfield" -> Elizabeth Kettle.**
   Evidence: Every consulted genealogy (WikiTree Mansfield-221/Kettle-568, Geni, Find a Grave) names Robert Mansfield's wife and Andrew's mother as **Elizabeth Kettle**, married 1613 at Lockington. "Townsend" is a real but *different* person in the record trail — it is one of two competing hypotheses (Gedney vs. Townsend) for the maiden name of **Bethiah**, Andrew's own first *wife* (Deborah's mother), not his mother. "Mayfield" does not appear in any source found and its origin is unclear — this looks like a duplicate/merge artifact combining at least two unrelated identifications (Andrew's mother Elizabeth Kettle + a maiden-name guess for Andrew's wife Bethiah + an unexplained "Mayfield") into one garbled compound name. The tree should split this into: Andrew's mother = Elizabeth Kettle; Andrew's first wife = Bethiah (maiden name uncertain, possibly Townsend or Gedney — not itself an ancestor in this line since Deborah descends from Bethiah, not from a second wife).

3. **Andrew Mansfield's parents listed in one duplicate record only.**
   One of the two "Andrew Lynn Mansfield" tree records lists only "Grace Elizabeth (Townsend) Mansfield, Mayfield" as parent; the other (sourced to "Geneanet Community Trees Index") correctly adds "Robert Mansfield" as a second parent. Per the sources above, **both** Robert Mansfield and Elizabeth Kettle are Andrew's documented parents — the single-parent record is incomplete, not an alternate finding.

## New findings (facts not in the tree)

- Andrew Mansfield's full immigration story: he and brother John arrived in Lynn by 1638/39 as young men, ahead of their parents Robert and Elizabeth, who followed in 1640 — an unusual pattern worth noting in any narrative writeup.
- Andrew's extensive civic record in Lynn: selectman, sergeant, grand/trial juror 1650–1677, deputy to the Massachusetts General Court 1680–1683.
- Andrew's three marriages and the death dates of wives #1 (Bethiah, d. 1672) and #2 (Mary Neal, d. 1681); his third marriage (to Elizabeth Conant) came just before his own death in 1683.
- The circumstances of Andrew's death: taken ill while attending the General Court in Boston in November 1683; a codicil to his 1679 will was witnessed on the spot by fellow court members on 19 Nov 1683, with the estate inventory filed 28 Nov 1683 — the tree's "28 Nov 1683" death date is actually the inventory-filing date, a reasonable proxy but not a confirmed death record.
- Deborah's husband is identified in the secondary literature simply as "Philip White of Beverly," married c.1685 — the tree's "Phillip (Wyeth) White" includes an unexplained middle name/surname element ("Wyeth") not found in any source consulted; this may be another merge artifact and should be checked against Beverly, MA vital records or a White-family genealogy directly.
- Robert Mansfield's wife Elizabeth Kettle is tentatively identified with an Elizabeth Kettle baptized 1585 at Loughborough, Leicestershire, daughter of Robert Kettle and Agnes/Alice Burrows — a possible further-generation lead, but explicitly presented by WikiTree as an identification (not certain vital record) and unverified independently here.

## Duplicate-record artifacts flagged

- **Deborah Mansfield** appears twice in this batch (`I182541959539` and `I182541966183`) with identical data — a straightforward unmerged duplicate.
- **Andrew Lynn Mansfield** appears twice (`I182541959560` and `I182541966193`) with the same birth/death facts but different sourcing and different parent lists (one lists only the garbled "Grace Elizabeth (Townsend) Mansfield, Mayfield"; the other adds "Robert Mansfield" and cites Geneanet). These should be merged into one record with both parents (Robert Mansfield + Elizabeth Kettle) once the "Grace Elizabeth (Townsend) Mansfield, Mayfield" entry is corrected/split per above.
- The name "Grace Elizabeth (Townsend) Mansfield, Mayfield" itself is very likely a merge of at least two, possibly three, distinct source identifications (Andrew's mother Elizabeth Kettle; a maiden-name guess "Townsend" for Andrew's wife Bethiah; an unexplained "Mayfield") into a single garbled tree entry — a strong candidate for a data-cleanup pass across the wider tree, not just this file.

## Open questions

- Is "Andrew WHITE" (Deborah's listed child) actually a documented son of Philip White and Deborah Mansfield? This was not independently verified — Beverly, MA vital/church records or a published White genealogy would be needed.
- What is the actual origin of the "Wyeth" element in "Phillip (Wyeth) White"? Possibly a maiden name of a different White ancestor's mother, mistakenly attached here.
- Was Bethiah (Andrew's first wife, mother of Deborah) a Gedney or a Townsend, or neither? Both attributions are labeled speculative in the secondary sources reviewed (Lynn Historical Register 1913 vs. Warner-Harrington Genealogy) — no primary parish/vital record was found confirming either.
- Robert Mansfield's own parents (the generation before the documented immigrant family) remain unidentified in every source checked — worth a targeted parish-register search in Lockington/Blofield-area Norfolk-Leicestershire records if this line is to be extended further.
- The tree's task framing describes this as spanning "generations 7–12," but the data supplied in this file (B21) contains only generations 11–12. Generations 7–10 of the Mansfield line are not present in this batch and were not researched here.
