# Franklin P. S. Hohmann (1855–1898) & Mary A. (Crist) Hohmann (1861–1946)

Generation 4 direct ancestors (parents of Bertha E. Hohmann Albertson). Researched 2026-07-03 from web-accessible primary indexes and compiled sources. Note: in every period record found, the couple appear simply as **"Frank Hohman(n)"** and **"Mary (Ann) Hohman(n)"** — no record located uses "Franklin P. S."

## Overview

Franklin ("Frank") Hohmann, born about 1855 (tree says Philadelphia), died at age 42 in Trenton, New Jersey on 30 June 1898 — a death now verified in the State of New Jersey's official death-record index. His widow Mary A. (Crist) Hohmann lived on in Trenton for nearly five more decades (41 Heil Ave. in 1919), died 29 March 1946, and is buried in Morrisville Cemetery, Morrisville, Bucks County, PA, just across the Delaware from Trenton. Her obituary reveals the couple had at least **five** children — three more than the tree records — and that **two Hohmann daughters married Albertson men** (Charles L. Albertson and Howard Albertson), tying this family doubly to the Edwin S. Albertson line.

## Verified facts

- **Frank Hohmann's death, 30 June 1898, Trenton, Mercer Co., NJ — VERIFIED.** New Jersey State Archives, *Index to Death Records Recorded by the NJ Department of Health, June 1878 – December 1900* (628,584 records): **"Hohman, Frank | 42y | M | Mercer County : Trenton | 30 Jun 1898 | 1897-98. 62 - H84."** Exact match to the tree's unsourced date. Searched live at wwwdnet-dos.nj.gov/DOS_ArchivesDBPortal/DeathIndex.aspx (surname query `Hohman*`, 28 hits statewide; this is the only Mercer County entry). The death certificate itself (citation 1897-98, vol. 62, H84) is orderable from the NJ State Archives.
- **Frank's age at death: 42 years** → born ~June 1855–June 1856, consistent with the tree's 1855 birth year (birth place Philadelphia remains unverified — see Open questions).
- **Mary's death, 29 March 1946 — VERIFIED.** Find A Grave memorial #189161414, "Mary Ann Hohmann (unknown–29 Mar 1946)," **Morrisville Cemetery, Morrisville, Bucks County, Pennsylvania**. The memorial (created from her Trenton newspaper obituary) states she was "wife of the late Frank Hohmann." Matches the tree's death date exactly. (Tree's death place "Lakeside Park, Mercer County NJ" — a Hamilton Township neighborhood — is plausible as her last residence but is not independently confirmed; the burial across the river in Morrisville is documented.)
- **Mary widowed and living in Trenton, 1919 — VERIFIED.** *Fitzgerald's Trenton and Mercer County Directory* (1919, Archive.org full text, item `fitzgeraldstrent00fitz`): **"Hohman Mary, wid Frank, h 41 Heil av."** Immediately following entry: "Hohmann John, laborer, h 48 Jefferson" (very likely her son John A.).
- **Frank predeceased Mary by decades** — consistent chain: state death index (1898) → "wid Frank" (1919 directory) → "wife of the late Frank Hohmann" (1946 obituary).
- **No Hohmann deaths appear in the Trenton newspapers death indexes 1901–1934** (Trenton Historical Society transcriptions, all 34 yearly files searched) — consistent with Frank dying in 1898 and no children dying in Trenton in that window.

## Corrections to the tree

- **Children list is materially incomplete: 2 listed → at least 5 documented.** Mary's 1946 obituary (quoted on Find A Grave #189161414) names her survivors: *"three daughters — Mrs. Charles L. Albertson, Mrs. Howard Albertson, and Miss Anna Hohmann — and two sons, John A. and Frank Hohmann."* The tree lists only Franklin Pierce Hohmann and Bertha E. Hohmann Albertson. Add: **Anna Hohmann** (unmarried as of 1946), **John A. Hohmann**, and a **second daughter who married an Albertson**. (Bertha is one of the two "Mrs. Albertsons"; the other Mrs. Albertson is a previously unrecorded daughter.)
- **Name as recorded:** "Franklin P. S. Hohmann" is not attested in any located record; period records say **Frank Hohman/Hohmann**. Keep "Franklin P. S." only if the family holds a document (baptism?) supporting it; otherwise treat the middle initials as unsourced tree lore.
- No date corrections needed: death dates for both spouses check out against primary/near-primary sources.

## New findings (not in the tree)

- **Double Hohmann–Albertson marriage.** Two Hohmann daughters married Albertson men — "Mrs. Charles L. Albertson" and "Mrs. Howard Albertson" (1946 obit). The 1919 Trenton directory shows **Howard Albertson, wireworker, residing at 1041 Liberty St. in the household of Edwin S. Albertson, boilermaker** (with siblings Clara E. and Walter M.) — i.e., Howard is a son of the tree's other gen-4 couple, Edwin S. Albertson & Maria Rankin Pedrick. A "Charles Albertson, h 222 Dayton" also appears in 1919. Determining which sister (Bertha vs. the unnamed one) married which Albertson is an open task.
- **Mary's burial place: Morrisville Cemetery, Morrisville, Bucks Co., PA** (Find A Grave #189161414). Not in the tree.
- **Mary's 1919 address: 41 Heil Avenue, Trenton** (Fitzgerald's directory).
- **Probable son John A. Hohmann:** a John A. Hohmann (May 1885 – 23 Mar 1948) is buried in **Riverview Cemetery, Trenton** (Find A Grave #189161203), husband of Margaret (McGovern). Caution: that memorial's obit note calls him "son of the late **John F.** Hohmann," which conflicts with father "Frank" — either the obituary/memorial errs, Frank's formal name was John F(ranklin), or this is a different John A. (see Open questions). A "Hohmann, John" obituary also ran in Trenton papers 27 Jul 1965 (Trenton Historical Society index) — possibly a namesake son.
- **Daughter Bertha E. Albertson's obituary ran in Trenton newspapers on 15 Jan 1965** (Trenton Historical Society obituary index, Jan–Mar 1965 file: "Albertson, Bertha E., 1/15/1965"). This strongly suggests Bertha (Hohmann) Albertson died in mid-January 1965 in the Trenton area. It also implies the AncientFaces/SSDI-derived "Bertha E Hohmann, b. 8 May 1901, d. Jan 1995, Trenton" is a **different person** (a 1901 birth would in any case be impossible for a daughter of Frank, who died June 1898 — likely she belongs to the separate John F. Hohmann family of Trenton).
- **No NJ marriage record for the couple.** The NJ State Archives marriage index (May 1848 – Dec 1900) contains **no Frank/Franklin Hohman(n) groom and no Mary Crist bride** (all 19 statewide Hohman* groom entries reviewed; 5 Crist/Mary bride entries reviewed, none match). Inference: Franklin and Mary married **outside New Jersey**, most plausibly in Philadelphia (his birthplace) in the early 1880s, before settling in Trenton.
- **Possible relative:** Anna E. Hohmann, age 45, died Stockton, Camden Co., NJ, 21 Jun 1898 (NJ death index, 1897-98. 15 - H83) — nine days before Frank; relationship unproven, noted for future checking.

## Open questions

1. **Where is Frank buried?** Not in the Mercer Cemetery (Trenton) 1880–1920 burial index; no Find A Grave memorial found. Candidates: Riverview Cemetery, Trenton (where John A. lies) or Morrisville Cemetery with Mary. His 1898 death certificate (NJSA, 1897-98/62/H84, ~$15 order) would name the undertaker/cemetery, plus parents' names — the single highest-value document to obtain.
2. **Marriage date/place of Franklin & Mary** — not in NJ 1848–1900 index; try Philadelphia marriage indexes (1860–1885, FamilySearch/Ancestry) and Wisconsin pre-1907 marriage index (WHS), ca. 1880–1884 (son John b. May 1885).
3. **Franklin's 1855 Philadelphia birth and parents** (John Heinrich Hohmann & Anna Elizabetha Kohlhepp per tree) — unverified. Philadelphia German church registers (St. Michael's/Zion, Lutheran/Reformed; FamilySearch "Pennsylvania, Philadelphia City Births") and the 1860/1870/1880 Philadelphia censuses are the vein. The tree's Kohlhepp line connects to the well-documented Peter Caspar Kohlhepp (arr. Philadelphia 1750; variants Collep/Cullop/Cullip — cf. Geni profile and History Hub discussion, likely posted by a family researcher).
4. **Mary's birth 23 June 1861, Chippewa Falls, WI, and parents David Hawkins Crist & Almina Lucia "Lucy" VanAmburg** — no free online verification found (Wisconsin registration was weak before 1880; WHS pre-1907 index worth a direct search). How/when a Wisconsin-born Crist met a Philadelphia Hohmann and landed in Trenton is untold.
5. **John A. Hohmann paternity discrepancy** — his memorial says "son of the late John F. Hohmann"; Mary's obit lists son "John A." Was Frank's formal name John Franklin? Or are there two John A. Hohmanns in Trenton? Pull his 23 Mar 1948 Trenton Evening Times obituary and 1948 death certificate.
6. **Which daughter married Charles L. vs. Howard Albertson**, and the given name of the second Albertson-married daughter. NJ marriage index 1901–2016 (Reclaim The Records images; Ancestry index) ca. 1915–1925, Mercer County, would settle it.
7. **Census households 1880–1940** (1880 Philadelphia?; 1900/1910/1920/1930/1940 Trenton — Mary should appear as widowed head or with children) — indexes are behind Ancestry/FamilySearch logins; not verifiable in this session. Expect the 1900 census to list Mary's children and her count of children born/living — the key record for completing the child list (including whether Bertha was born before Frank's June 1898 death).
