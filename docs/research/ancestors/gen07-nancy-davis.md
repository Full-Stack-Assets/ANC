# Nancy Davis (b. 1780, Ireland) — Generation 7

> **Verdict up front:** the `parent-too-old` flag is real and points to a genuine
> tree error, not a typo in her birth year. This Nancy Davis is well-documented as
> the mother of **one** child, Winifreda Mulken (b. abt 1808, Ireland). The two
> flagged children, Thomas McKee (1836) and Curtis M McClelland (1838), almost
> certainly belong to a **second, younger, unidentified woman of the same name**
> who married Robert McClelland in Pennsylvania. Confidence: **moderate-high**
> on the diagnosis (internally coherent, multiply corroborated by age math,
> geography, and a surname mismatch); **low** on the second Nancy Davis's actual
> identity, which remains unrecovered.

## Overview

Nancy Davis was born in 1780 in Ireland. The tree attaches her to two source
threads that, on inspection, describe two different women. The first thread —
Pennsylvania Death Certificates 1906-1968 (S447147251) plus the Geneanet
Community Trees Index (S508118847) — supplies her birth year/place and links
her, via her descendants' 20th-century Pennsylvania death certificates, to a
family headed by Robert McClelland (b. Scotland). The second thread — Ireland
Catholic Parish Registers, the 1860 US Census, and Geneanet — documents her as
the mother of Winifreda Mulken (b. abt 1808, Ireland), whose father was James
"Gems" Carey (Mulkarey) (d. 1867, Balrothery, Co. Dublin, Ireland — he never
emigrated). These two threads are not obviously the same person once the ages
are worked out.

## Verified facts

- **Nancy Davis was born 1780 in Ireland.** [documented — S447147251, S508118847]
- **She married (first, in Ireland) James "Gems" Carey, also recorded "Mulkarey"** (I182587609016), b. 1780 Ireland, d. 1867 Balrothery, Co. Dublin, Ireland — himself documented via Ireland Civil Registration Deaths Index 1864-1958 (S513850796) and Geneanet. [documented]
- **Daughter Winifreda Mulken**, b. abt 1808 Ireland, d. 8 Jan 1874, Barton, Washington Co., Wisconsin, USA — documented via the 1860 US Federal Census (Barton, Washington Co., WI, S506473136) and Geneanet (S508118847); married Laurence Larkin (b. abt 1793, Ireland, d. 1868) and had five children in Wisconsin. Nancy would have been **28** at this birth — an unremarkable, biologically normal age. [documented]
- **Her parents are tree-listed as David Davies (I182613118651, b. 1760 Skerries, Co. Dublin, d. 1836 Balrothery) and Rose Riley (I182613118648, b. 1760, d. 1841, both Balrothery, Co. Dublin)** — but both parent records carry **zero sources** and `"confidence": "inferred"`; a 20-year gap to Nancy's own 1780 birth is plausible but unverified. [inferred, unsourced]

## Corrections to the tree

- **Thomas McKee (I182613118644, b. 11 Jun 1836, Pennsylvania, d. 5 May 1909, Chester, Delaware Co., PA) and Curtis M McClelland (I182613118627, b. 18 Jun 1838, Pennsylvania, d. 19 May 1920, Pittsburgh, Allegheny Co., PA) should NOT be listed as children of this Nancy Davis.** Both children's only source is the Pennsylvania Death Certificates index (S447147251) — i.e., their own 20th-century death certificates, on which an informant reported parents' names. Attaching them to *this* Nancy Davis (b. 1780) requires her to have given birth at ages **56** and **58**, which is not biologically credible. Her one well-documented birth (Winifreda, 1808) shows her at a normal fertile age (28), which argues against her birth year being the error — it argues instead that a **different woman sharing the name "Nancy Davis"** was conflated with her.
- **Corroborating detail:** Thomas McKee's own surname does not match the surname of his supposed father, Robert McClelland (I182613118628, b. Scotland) — while his purported full brother, Curtis M **McClelland**, does. This is consistent with a normal, unremarkable family history that got mis-merged: a younger "Nancy Davis" (not this one) first married a man surnamed McKee (Thomas's father), was later widowed or separated, and remarried Robert McClelland in Pennsylvania (Curtis's father) — two children, two surnames, one mother, both born ~1836-38 in PA. That reconstruction is internally tidy for the *second* Nancy Davis; it has nothing to do with the Ireland-born, 1780 Nancy Davis documented above.
- **No "grandchild" pathway exists to rescue the attachment.** Winifreda Mulken's husband was Laurence Larkin (I182585270548), not a McKee or McClelland, and none of Winifreda's five children carry those surnames — so Thomas McKee and Curtis M McClelland cannot be explained as misfiled grandchildren of *this* Nancy Davis via her one documented daughter. This rules out explanation (a) offered in the research brief (grandchildren mislabeled as children).
- **The birth year 1780 itself is not the error.** Revising it forward (e.g., to ~1800-1805) to make her a plausible mother for the 1836/1838 births would break her well-documented, independently-sourced role as Winifreda's mother (his birth abt 1808 would then predate or coincide with Nancy's own infancy). The 1780 birth year should stand for the Winifreda line; it simply should not be extended to cover Thomas McKee and Curtis M McClelland.
- **Net effect:** this Nancy Davis (I182587609594) has exactly **one** documented child, Winifreda Mulken. Her marriage/children link to Robert McClelland is a mis-attachment inherited from the Geneanet Community Trees Index conflating two same-named women; see manual notes added to I182587609594, I182613118644, I182613118627, and I182613118628.

## New findings (not in the tree)

- **A likely second husband pattern for the *Pennsylvania* Nancy Davis** (the still-unidentified woman who actually mothered Thomas McKee and Curtis M McClelland): the surname mismatch between Thomas (McKee) and Curtis (McClelland), born just two years apart (1836, 1838), is best explained by an earlier marriage to a Mr. McKee followed by remarriage to Robert McClelland — an ordinary, unremarkable Pennsylvania family story, just not this family's story.
- **James "Gems" Carey (Mulkarey), Nancy's first husband**, stayed in Ireland his whole life (d. 1867, Balrothery, Co. Dublin) while his and Nancy's daughter Winifreda emigrated alone or with others and turns up in Wisconsin by 1860. This suggests Nancy herself may also have remained in Ireland (consistent with no Pennsylvania record ever surfacing for *her*, personally, as opposed to for her descendants).

## Open questions

- **Who was the real Pennsylvania "Nancy Davis"** who married Robert McClelland (b. Scotland) and mothered Curtis M McClelland (and possibly Thomas McKee, via an earlier marriage)? No birth/marriage record for her was located on the open web; Ancestry/FamilySearch person pages for Thomas McKee and Curtis M McClelland could not be independently found without paywalled access, so her identity, birth year, and birthplace remain unknown. This is the single biggest open item from this review.
- **Did the elder Nancy Davis (b. 1780) ever emigrate to the United States, or did she remain in Ireland?** No death record, US census entry, or independent US document naming her (as opposed to her descendants) was found. Her husband James Carey/Mulkarey died in Ireland in 1867 without any indication of a spouse predeceasing or accompanying him abroad.
- **Are David Davies and Rose Riley genuinely her parents?** Both records are unsourced and marked "inferred" in the tree; no Irish civil or parish record was located to confirm this generation.
- **The original Pennsylvania death certificates for Thomas McKee (Chester, Delaware Co., PA, 1909) and Curtis M McClelland (Pittsburgh, Allegheny Co., PA, 1920)** were not viewable on the open web (Ancestry-gated). Pulling the actual certificate images would show exactly what each informant wrote for "father" and "mother's maiden name" and could confirm or refute the McKee/McClelland split reconstructed here from surnames alone.
