# The Albertson spine, 1771–1932: John → [Josiah] → Isaac J → Edwin S

Deep-dive verification of the male line between the colonial Quakers and the 20th
century. Sources: Haddonfield MM marriage records (New Horizons transcription), Craig's
*South Jersey Marriages* and Camden Co. clerk's marriages, Clement (1877), Prowell
(1886), *Biographical Review of Camden & Burlington Counties* (1897), NJ Index of Wills
prior to 1901, Fitzgerald's 1919 Trenton directory, Magnolia borough history, HMDB.

## Headline: the tree skips a generation

The export's link **John (1771–1842) → Isaac J (1824–1896) is refuted**: the 1897
*Biographical Review* states Chalkley (b. Jan 1816) was the *youngest* of John and
Ann's **ten** children, and Ann Pine was ~49 in 1824. The probable intermediate is
**Josiah Albertson (1795–1849)**, John's eldest son, who married **Abigail C. Hodson**
at Haddonfield MM on 21 Mar 1822 — an 1824 eldest child fits exactly, the couple
demonstrably had children of Isaac's cohort in this county ("Mary Ann Albertson
(d. Josiah & Abigail)" m. 1853), the geography matches, and Isaac's middle initial "J"
plausibly stands for Josiah. Status: **probable, pending** the 1850 census household of
widow Abigail, Isaac's 1896 death return, or the Camden Co. surrogate file.

## Other findings

- **Pine vs Price settled**: the actual Haddonfield MM *marriage* record (20 Nov 1794)
  reads "Ann **Pine** (d John)"; Craig's "Ann Price" was a misreading of the intentions
  entry. Ann was daughter of John and Rachel (Burrough) Pine; died aged ~89.
- **John kept the plantation** — born in the 1743 brick house; will = Gloucester Co.
  file 4876H (W. 1842, Inv. 1843). Chalkley bought out the co-heirs.
- **Chalkley Albertson** (1816–1880): NJ Assemblyman ×4, rebuilt the homestead 1865,
  and the **"Albertson" locality near Magnolia is named from his farm** — the railroad
  stop was arranged with Samuel Richards over dinner at Chalkley's house in 1877.
- **Edwin S** was a **boilermaker** in Trenton — Fitzgerald's 1919 directory has him at
  1041 Liberty St with children Howard (wireworker — the direct ancestor), Walter M
  (U.S. Army), and Clara E (operator). The branch left Camden County for Trenton
  industry between Isaac's death (1896) and 1910; the homestead had gone down
  Chalkley's branch, not theirs.
- **Isaac J**: wife Mary Ann Shone's family confirmed in Camden Co. records; **no
  credible Civil War service** (identity of the one roster "Isaac Albertson" unproven).
