# Hannah Pond Hibbard (b. 1831, Boston, MA – d. 1905?, Wakefield, MA) — Generation 6

## Overview

Hannah P. Hibbard was born in Boston on 20 June 1831, daughter of Joseph Hibbard (a Maine-born vessel owner and mariner) and his second wife, Hannah Pond of Dedham, Massachusetts. A privately published 1901 Hibbard family genealogy independently confirms her birth date/place and her full birth family — but it also records that she married Charles W. Wright at East Boston in 1850 and had children surnamed **Wright**, not Donahue. This directly corroborates a finding already flagged in the companion dossier for Patrick & Ellen F. Donahue (gen05): the tree's link of "Hannah Pond Hibbard" as Ellen F. Donahue's parent is almost certainly a mis-merge from an unrelated Yankee-Protestant family. Note also that this batch item's accompanying task description named "John Burke & Bridget Manning" as the research subject, but the actual data file (A44.json) contains only Hannah Pond Hibbard — this dossier follows the data file, per instructions, and flags the mismatch under Open Questions.

## Verified facts

- **Birth: 20 June 1831, Boston, Massachusetts.** Tree lists this as "unsourced," but it is independently documented in Augustine George Hibbard, *Genealogy of the Hibbard Family Who are Descendants of Robert Hibbard of Salem, Massachusetts* (Woodstock, CT, 1901), p. 88: entry #1006, "Hannah P., b. June 20, 1831," repeated at p. 89 as "Hannah Pond⁷ (dau. Joseph⁶, Joseph⁵, Capt. Joseph⁴, Jeremiah³, Joseph², Robert¹), b. Boston, June 20, 1831." This upgrades the tree's birth event from unsourced to documented (published genealogy, primary compiled record).
- **Parents: Joseph Hibbard and Hannah Pond.** Per the same source: Joseph Hibbard⁶ (of the Robert Hibbard of Salem line: s. Joseph⁵, Joseph⁴ "Capt. Joseph," Jeremiah³, Joseph², Robert¹), b. Penobscot, Maine, 19 Nov 1787; married (1) Prudence Greely, who d. 14 Nov 1815; married (2) 15 Dec 1824, Hannah Pond, of Dedham, Mass. The couple resided in Boston, where their children were born.
- **Father's occupation and death:** Joseph Hibbard "was the owner of several vessels and a seafaring man. He d. at Hayti [Haiti], W.I., Aug. 13, 1856."
- **Marriage: Hannah P. Hibbard to Charles W. Wright, at East Boston, 5 November 1850.** Documented in the same genealogy (entry #1006, continuation).
- **Children (with Charles W. Wright):** George Henry Wright, b. Candia [likely Candia, NH], 22 Feb 1852; Charlotte Adelaide Wright, b. East Boston, 22 Aug 1854. Documented in the same source.
- **Full sibling set** (children of Joseph Hibbard & Hannah Pond, all b. Boston): Prudence Josephine Hibbard, b. 14 Oct 1826 (m. 27 Jun 1852, Jacob G. Homer of E. Boston); Joseph A. Hibbard, b. 12 Jul 1828, d. 20 Jan 1830 (infant); Hannah P. (subject of this dossier), b. 20 Jun 1831; Joseph Hibbard, b. 22 Jun 1835; Charles Henry Hibbard, b. 8 Mar 1838; John Brown Hibbard, b. 7 Apr 1841.

## Corrections to the tree

1. **"Ellen F Donahue" as Hannah's child → not supported by the documentary record; almost certainly a mis-merge.** The 1901 published genealogy shows Hannah P. Hibbard married Charles W. Wright in 1850 and that her documented children (George Henry Wright, b. 1852; Charlotte Adelaide Wright, b. 1854) bear the surname Wright, not Donahue. This independently corroborates the conclusion already reached in the companion dossier `docs/research/ancestors/gen05-patrick-donahue-ellen-f-donahue.md`, which flagged "Hannah Pond Hibbard" as Ellen F. Donahue's parent as "almost certainly wrong" on separate grounds (Hibbard/Pond are colonial Yankee-Protestant surnames; Ellen was an Irish-Catholic Bostonian married in the Catholic church). Recommend detaching Ellen F. Donahue as a child of Hannah Pond Hibbard in the tree.
2. **Duplicated parent entries.** The tree lists Hannah's parents as "Joseph Hibbard III," "Hannah Pond," "Joseph Hibbard III," "Hannah Pond" (each name twice) — almost certainly a data-entry/import duplication rather than two separate couples. Should be a single parent pair.
3. **"Joseph Hibbard III" is not the form used in the primary source**, but is plausibly a reasonable rendering of it: the genealogy's numbering shows the father was the third consecutive "Joseph Hibbard" in the direct paternal line (Joseph⁶, son of Joseph⁵, son of Joseph⁴/"Capt. Joseph"), which is consistent with (though not identical to) a "3rd/III" suffix convention. Not a correction so much as a clarifying note — worth citing the primary source's own naming instead of "III" if the tree is edited.
4. **Death event ("15 Oct 1905 @ Wakefield, Middlesex, MA") could not be verified or refuted.** If Hannah was still alive in 1905, 55 years after marrying Charles W. Wright, she would ordinarily be recorded under her married name "Hannah Wright," not "Hannah Hibbard." It is worth checking whether the tree's (uncited) source for this death event is actually a record for a Hannah Wright that has been mis-filed under her maiden name, or a genuinely different Hannah Hibbard.

## New findings

- Hannah's complete birth family (parents, both of her father's marriages, and five full siblings) reconstructed from a single published, citable 1901 genealogy — none of this was in the tree.
- Her father Joseph Hibbard's biography: Maine-born (Penobscot, 1787), owner of several vessels, career mariner, died at Hayti (Haiti) in the West Indies in 1856 — presumably while at sea or on a voyage, though the source does not specify cause.
- Her actual, documented marriage (Charles W. Wright, East Boston, 1850) and two children (George Henry Wright b. 1852, Charlotte Adelaide Wright b. 1854), none of which appear in the tree at all.
- No Find a Grave memorial could be located for a "Hannah (Hibbard) Wright" or "Hannah P. Hibbard" with birth 1831/death 1905 in Massachusetts; searches for Charles W. Wright + Hannah in Wakefield, MA cemeteries (Forest Glade, Lakeside) returned no match.
- No Massachusetts death index entry for a Hannah Hibbard or Hannah Wright in 1905 could be located via open-web search; the relevant collections (Massachusetts Death Index 1901-1980, town vital records) are Ancestry/FamilySearch-gated and were not independently accessible this session.

## Open questions

1. **Is the 1905 Wakefield death event genuinely for this woman, and under what name?** Should be checked against a married surname (Wright) as well as Hibbard, and against Massachusetts town vital records for Wakefield, 1905.
2. **What became of Hannah and Charles W. Wright after 1854 (the last documented child)?** Did the family relocate from East Boston to Wakefield at some point? A 1900 or earlier census for "Charles W. Wright" household in Massachusetts, if locatable, would help confirm or refute the 1905 Wakefield death.
3. **If Hannah Pond Hibbard is not Ellen F. Donahue's mother, who is?** This remains the live question already posed in the companion Donahue dossier; resolving Ellen's actual parentage (via her 1858 Boston birth record or 1882 marriage record) would let this tree's erroneous Hibbard/Pond/Wright branch be cleanly detached.
4. **Task-routing mismatch:** the assignment brief accompanying this item named "John Burke & Bridget Manning" as the research subject, with details (Prospero/Prospero, Regan/Reagan, Hohmann/Hohman spelling variants) that belong to entirely different branches of this family tree (visible elsewhere in `docs/research/ancestors/`, e.g. gen04-angelo-prospus-prospero, gen04-dennis-francis-regan-sr, gen04-franklin-p-s-hohmann). The actual source file for this item (`A44.json`) contains only Hannah Pond Hibbard, with no John Burke or Bridget Manning data. This dossier was written from the data file as instructed; a John Burke & Bridget Manning research pass, if still needed, should be run against whichever item file actually contains that couple's tree data.
