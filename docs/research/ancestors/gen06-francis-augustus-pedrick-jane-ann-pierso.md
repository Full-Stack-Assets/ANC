# Francis Augustus Pedrick (1810–1895) & Jane Ann Pierson (1814–1905)

Generation 6 direct ancestors. Pedricktown, Oldmans Township, Salem County, New Jersey — founders of the family's three-generation wheelwright and wagon-building business.

> Note on assignment: the research tasking's subject line named "Johann Adam Hohmann & Anna Elisabeth Doepp," but the supplied tree data file (A33.json) and its dossier_path both identify the couple as **Francis Augustus Pedrick & Jane Ann Pierson**. This dossier follows the data file, per instructions to treat it as authoritative. (A Hohmann/Doepp couple may exist elsewhere in this project's item set; this file does not cover them.)

## Overview

Francis Augustus Pedrick (1 May 1810 – 4 Nov 1895) was a sixth-generation descendant of Roger Pedrick, the Quaker who surveyed the 1682 tract that became Pedricktown, Salem County, New Jersey. As a young man he went to Woodbury, Gloucester County, to learn the wheelwright's trade from his uncle Joseph D. Pedrick, married, and opened a shop near the Woodbury canal before returning to Pedricktown about 1840, where his father bought him a shop and he built the wheelwright/wagon-building business his son and grandson later carried on. He married Jane Ann Pierson (1814 – 12 Nov 1905), about whom independent records are essentially silent — a common gap for 19th-century wives in this genealogy. The couple is documented almost entirely through Hubert B. Shoemaker's 1938 published genealogy of the Pedrick family, cross-checked by its author against Trenton vital statistics and cemetery stones for the next generation; no independent census, cemetery, or vital-record trace of Francis or Jane Ann individually was located in this session.

## Verified facts

- **Francis's birth 1 May 1810** — confirmed exactly: "6 FRANCIS AUGUSTUS PEDRICK was born 5-1-1810" (Shoemaker, *Pedrick Family*, 1938, p. 94), and again in the summary table on p. 93: "6-4 Francis A. 5-1-1810." Tree date matches exactly.
- **Francis's death 4 Nov 1895, age 85** — confirmed: "died 11-4-1895 age 85" (p. 94); table on p. 93 agrees: "11-4-1895 85." Tree date matches exactly (85 = 1895−1810).
- **Marriage to Jane Ann Pierson** — confirmed: "married JANE ANN PIERSON" (p. 94; also p. 93 table, "Jane Ann Pierson"). No marriage date or place is given in any source found.
- **Jane Ann's death 12 Nov 1905, age 91** — confirmed: "died 11-12-1905 age 91" (p. 94, American month-day-year format = November 12, 1905). Tree's "12 Nov 1905" matches exactly.
- **Francis's parents: John Pedrick and Elizabeth Elwell** — confirmed: the summary table lists Francis as child "6-4" of "5-1 JOHN PEDRICK married 9-29-1801 ELIZABETH ELWELL" (p. 93). The tree's parent listing is exactly correct.
- **Occupation: wheelwright and wagon builder** — confirmed: "Here he operated as a wheelwright and wagon builder" (p. 94), on Railroad Avenue, Pedricktown, next to the Methodist Episcopal Church, in a shop rebuilt about 1877 on the same site his father had bought him.
- **Son Joseph Elwell Pedrick** — confirmed as their child (b. 24 Apr 1834, d. 5 Jul 1913), listed first among their issue (p. 94). Joseph inherited the wheelwright business; see the companion dossier `gen05-joseph-elwell-pedrick-mary-e-rankins.md` for his family in full.

## Corrections to the tree

- **Francis's birthplace "Upper Penns Neck Township, Salem, NJ" → probably Lower Penns Neck, Salem County, NJ.** Evidence: Shoemaker states Francis's father John Pedrick "was a farmer in Lower Penns Neck on the land partitioned to him from the estate of his father" (Jacob Pedrick Jr., p. 87–88), and the family's ancestral tract described in the book is consistently in Lower Penns Neck, not Upper. Francis was very likely born on this family farm before going to Woodbury as a young man to learn the wheelwright trade. This is a secondary inference from the genealogy's narrative, not a direct statement of Francis's birthplace — flag, don't silently overwrite. (The same correction was independently flagged for Francis's son Joseph in the companion gen05 dossier.)
- **Jane Ann Pierson's birth "FEB 1813" → possibly 1814.** Shoemaker gives her birth year as 1814 and her age at death (12 Nov 1905) as 91, which arithmetically requires a birth in 1814 (or very late 1813, after ~12 November) — a birth of February 1813 would make her 92 at death, one year off from the book's stated age. The tree's specific month (February) does not appear anywhere in the sources reviewed here, so its origin is unknown; this may come from a source (e.g., a cemetery stone or FindAGrave memorial) not located in this session. Flagged as a discrepancy, not a confirmed correction — see Open questions.
- **Children: tree lists only Joseph Elwell Pedrick → the couple had at least five children.** See New findings below for the other four (John, Charles, Emma L., and Francis Augustus Jr., who died in infancy).

## New findings

4. **Francis Augustus Pedrick** (this dossier's subject).
5. Joseph Pedrick (b. 1811, d. 12 May 1831, age 20) — died single/young.
6. Rebecca Pedrick — m. William Armstrong (brother of Jacob's wife Susan — a double Pedrick–Armstrong marriage).
7. Hannah Ann Pedrick (10 Jul 1819 – 27 Dec 1876, age 57) — m. Joseph T. Newcomb.
8. Elizabeth Pedrick (23 May 1823 – 28 May 1892, age 69) — m. Jacob Whitesell.

**Francis's parents' own vital data** (not in tree, from Shoemaker pp. 87–88, 93): John Pedrick, b. 11 Jan 1781, d. 12 Jan 1857 (age 76), buried Pennsville; married 29 Sep 1801 Elizabeth Elwell, b. 2 Mar 1781, d. 21 Feb 1862 (age 81), buried "old Presb. Cem. at Pennsville." John was a farmer in Lower Penns Neck on land his own father, Jacob Pedrick Jr., had bequeathed; his will was probated 18 Jan 1857, leaving his wife a Pennsville lot and $250/year for life, with **Francis named as one of the two executors** (with Dr. Wm. Vanneman) — direct documentary evidence Francis was literate, trusted, and locally resident in 1857.

**The five children of Francis A. Pedrick & Jane Ann Pierson (Shoemaker p. 94):**
1. **Joseph Elwell Pedrick** (24 Apr 1834 – 5 Jul 1913) — succeeded his father in the Pedricktown wheelwright shop; m. Mary E. Rankins. Full family detailed in the companion dossier `gen05-joseph-elwell-pedrick-mary-e-rankins.md`.
2. **John Pedrick** (1835 – 1910, age 75, buried Hillside Cemetery, Philadelphia) — worked as a wagon builder for his father in Pedricktown, later farmed at Churchtown, then moved to Philadelphia about 1873 and served on the Philadelphia police force under Mayor Stokley (two terms) and then Mayors King and Smith, later doing carpentry work; m. Mary Elwell of Salem County (d. age 62, buried Elwell lot, Woodstown).
3. **Charles Pedrick** (24 Feb 1840 – 22 Jan 1889, age 48, buried Penns Grove) — a vessel pilot who ran the boats "Richmond" and "Anna & Mary" between Pedricktown and Philadelphia carrying produce, lumber, melons and sweet potatoes; m. Danelia Gandy of Tuckahoe, NJ (28 Sep 1849 – 17 Jan 1888). Died intestate; **his brother Joseph administered the estate**.
4. **Emma L. Pedrick** (d. 1912, buried West Laurel Hill Cemetery) — m. James Luke, a stationary engineer who later ran a restaurant business in Trenton.
5. **Francis Augustus Pedrick Jr.** (9 May 1851 – 3 May 1853, age 2, buried Pedricktown) — died in early childhood, born when his father was already about 41.

**Family lineage established by Shoemaker:** Roger Pedrick (c. 1615 England – 1692/founder, 1682 survey) → John → Jacob → Jacob Pedrick Jr. (d. 1802, Lower Penns Neck) → John Pedrick (1781–1857) & Elizabeth Elwell (1781–1862) → **Francis Augustus Pedrick (1810–1895) & Jane Ann Pierson (1814–1905)** → Joseph Elwell Pedrick (1834–1913) → Maria Rankin Pedrick (m. Edward Shone Albertson) → the tree owner's Albertson line.

**Business continuity:** the Pedricktown wheelwright shop Francis built up passed through three consecutive generations of this family — Francis (from c. 1840) → his son Joseph Elwell Pedrick (from c. 1860s) → Joseph's son Henry Lawrence Pedrick (still running it as of the book's 1938 publication) — making it one of the longest-documented single-family trade businesses in the source.

## Open questions

- **Jane Ann Pierson's parents and birthplace** — completely unknown. Shoemaker, a Pedrick-surname genealogy, does not trace incoming wives' families ("no search" is his stock phrase for several other female lines in the same book); no Pierson-family material appears anywhere else in the 362-page volume. A Salem or Gloucester County Pierson family history, or an 1850/1860 census of Oldmans/Upper-or-Lower-Penns-Neck showing a Pierson household, would be needed to identify her parents.
- **Marriage date and place of Francis and Jane Ann** — not found. Given Francis "got married" while in Woodbury (Gloucester County) before returning to Pedricktown "about 1840," and their eldest known child Joseph was born April 1834, the marriage almost certainly occurred in Gloucester County (likely Woodbury) by 1833. A Gloucester County marriage register or Woodbury-area church record for 1830–1834 could confirm.
- **Jane Ann's birth month/year "FEB 1813" (tree) vs. 1814 (Shoemaker, implied by age-at-death)** — unresolved; see Corrections above. A cemetery inscription or FindAGrave memorial, if one exists, would settle it; none was located in this session (no Find a Grave or interment.net entry for either Francis A. Pedrick or Jane Ann Pierson/Pedrick was found — see Sources).
- **Death place of Francis Augustus Pedrick** — not stated in Shoemaker beyond the implication he remained in Pedricktown; the tree's "Pedrickstown, Salem, NJ" is plausible (he lived and worked there his whole adult life) but not independently documented. A Salem County death record or an 1895 *Salem Sunbeam* / regional newspaper obituary would confirm.
- **Burial location(s)** — not stated for either Francis or Jane Ann in Shoemaker (unlike several of their children and grandchildren, whose burial places the book does record). Given the family's long residence in Pedricktown, Pedricktown Friends Cemetery is the likely site, but neither name appears in the interment.net transcription of that cemetery consulted for the companion gen05 dossier, nor could an individual Find a Grave memorial be located for either (Find a Grave blocks automated access to its search endpoint; only a general cemetery-browse page for Salem County was reachable).
- **Census records 1810–1895** were not directly examined (subscription/login-gated indexes). Expected: Francis in his father John's Lower Penns Neck household in 1820/1830; a Woodbury/Gloucester County household 1830s; Pedricktown (Oldmans Twp.) households 1850–1890 with occupation "wheelwright," growing older with Jane Ann and, in later censuses, likely a widowed or elderly-couple household. These would also help resolve Jane Ann's exact birth year.
