# Howard F. "Jack" Donahue Jr. (1943–2013) & Kathleen (Reagan) Donahue (1943–2017)

Generation 2 direct ancestors (parents' generation of the tree owner's parent line). Both born in Brockton, Plymouth Co., Massachusetts, in 1943, four months apart.

## Overview

Howard F. Donahue Jr., known his whole life as **"Jack,"** was a career **Boston Police Department officer who retired as a Sergeant**; he was raised in Brockton, lived successively in Brockton, Dedham, Sharon, and Walpole, MA, and wintered/retired in Bonita Springs, Florida, where he died 7 January 2013. Kathleen (Reagan) Donahue was raised in Brockton, graduated from **Brockton High School**, and worked **more than 19 years at Boston College's John J. Burns Library** before retiring; she died 28 March 2017 in Stoughton, MA, and is buried at **St. Michael Cemetery, Avon, MA**. Their marriage produced three children (Brian, Christine, Jill); the marriage evidently ended before 2013 — Howard's obituary names his wife as Terry A. (Arsenault), and Kathleen's obituary does not mention a husband.

## Corrections to the tree

- **Howard's residence spans "2002–2020 Walpole" and "2009–2020 Bonita Springs" extend seven years past his death (7 Jan 2013).** These come from the U.S. Index to Public Records, which keeps addresses active long after death; the end dates should be capped at 2013. Evidence: both obituaries fix death at 7 Jan 2013.
- **Howard's "burial: 12 Jan" is actually the funeral-service date, not a documented interment.** The Gormley obituary specifies the 12 Jan 2013 funeral and Mass but names no cemetery; the tree's supporting source is the Find A Grave "Burials at Sea and other Select Burial Locations" index, which is the category Find A Grave uses for cremations/unknown burial places. Recommend relabeling the event "funeral, 12 Jan 2013, St. John Chrysostom Church, West Roxbury" until an interment record is found.
- **Kathleen's death place "Brockton" is not what her obituary implies.** The obituary describes her as "of Stoughton" at death (her last residence, matching the tree's Stoughton residence entries); the funeral was held in Brockton. Death may have occurred in Stoughton or a nearby facility — treat "Brockton" as unconfirmed rather than flatly wrong.
- Minor: daughter listed in tree as "Jill E. Donahue" appears in both 2013 and 2017 obituaries under her married name **Jill E. Clifford** (husband Michael F. Clifford Jr.).
