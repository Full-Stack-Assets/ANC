# Howard I. Albertson (1895/96–1973) & Bertha E. Hohmann (b. ~1898/1901)

Generation 3 direct ancestors. Trenton, Mercer County, New Jersey wireworker's family; Howard died in Levittown, Bucks County, Pennsylvania in 1973.

## Overview

Howard Albertson (compiled sources give his middle name as **Isaac**) was born in New Jersey about Dec 1895/1896, son of Edwin S. Albertson (a Trenton boilermaker) and Maria Rankin Pedrick. He worked as a **wireworker** in Trenton and lived his whole working life in the city (Liberty St, then Jersey St), moving in old age to Levittown, PA, where he died 2 Sept 1973. His wife Bertha E. Hohmann, born in Pennsylvania, was the daughter of Franklin P. S. Hohmann and Mary A. Crist; her mother was already a widow living at 41 Heil Ave, Trenton, by 1919. The couple's odd 21 Aug 1957 Richmond, VA marriage record — despite their appearing together as a married couple in the 1930–1940 censuses — remains the biggest unresolved puzzle.

## Verified facts

- **Howard, wireworker, res. 1041 Liberty St, Trenton, 1919** — Fitzgerald's Trenton and Mercer County Directory 1919 (Archive.org, items `trentoncountynew1919fitz` and `fitzgeraldstrent00fitz`): "Albertson Howard, wireworker, res 1041 Liberty".
- **Father Edwin S. Albertson, boilermaker, householder at 1041 Liberty St, 1919** — same directory: "Albertson Edwin S, boilermaker, h 1041 Liberty". Confirms the tree's parent Edwin S. Albertson and puts Howard in his father's household at age ~23.
- **Siblings in the same 1919 household**: "Albertson Clara E, operator, res 1041 Liberty" and "Albertson Walter M, U S A [U.S. Army], res 1041 Liberty" — previously unrecorded siblings Clara E. and Walter M. (Walter serving in the Army in 1919). A "Albertson Wilbert, machinist, h 121 Ward av" is very likely the brother Wilbert named as a sibling in compiled records (MyHeritage).
- **Death: Sept 1973, Levittown, PA** — SSDI-derived compiled index (via MyHeritage aggregate page for "Howard Albertson"): Howard Albertson, born **10 Dec 1895**, last residence **Levittown, Pennsylvania**, died **September 1973**, age 77. Corroborates the tree's (unsourced) death of 2 Sept 1973 at Levittown, Bucks Co., PA.
- **Residence 1935/1940: 156 Jersey St, Trenton** — compiled census-index data (MyHeritage aggregate) gives the street address behind the tree's bare "Trenton" 1935/1940 residences: 156 Jersey St, Trenton, Mercer, NJ.
- **Bertha's mother widowed by 1919; Franklin P. S. Hohmann dead before 1919** — Fitzgerald's 1919 directory: "**Hohman Mary, wid Frank, h 41 Heil av**" (note single-n *Hohman* spelling variant). This matches the tree's parents Franklin P. S. Hohmann and Mary A. Crist and shows Franklin died before the 1919 directory canvass.
- **No Howard Albertson obituary in the Trenton Times, Jul–Sept 1973** — Trenton Historical Society obituary index for Jul–Sep 1973 has no Albertson entry. Consistent with his having died (and been written up) in Bucks County, PA rather than Trenton; his obituary is most likely in the *Bucks County Courier Times* (not freely indexed online).

## Corrections to the tree

- **Birth "abt 1896" → 10 December 1895** (probable). The SSDI-derived index entry for Howard Albertson of Levittown, PA (d. Sept 1973, age 77) gives an exact birth date of 10 Dec 1895. Census "abt 1896" ages are consistent with a Dec 1895 birth. Moderate confidence — the SSA record should be checked against the tree's existing "U.S., Social Security Applications and Claims Index" citation.
- **Duplicate child entries: "Maregaret Albertson" and "Margaret Louise Albertson"** are almost certainly the same daughter entered twice (one with a typo). Recommend merging.
- **Internal inconsistency in the tree, not resolved here**: a son is recorded as "Howard **Edwin** Albertson **Jr**", which implies the father was Howard Edwin Sr.; but compiled records (MyHeritage) call the father Howard **Isaac** Albertson. Either the father's middle name is Edwin (and "Isaac" is a compiled-tree error), or the son was not a true "Jr." Worth checking the son's birth record/SS application.

## New findings (not in the tree)

- **Occupation: wireworker (1919)** — Trenton was a world center of wire and wire-rope manufacture (John A. Roebling's Sons Co. and several smaller wire mills). That Howard worked for Roebling specifically is *inference only*, but "wireworker" as his trade is documented in the 1919 directory.
- **Address 1041 Liberty St, Trenton (1919)** — the Edwin S. Albertson family home.
- **Address 156 Jersey St, Trenton (1935–1940)** — Howard and Bertha's household (compiled census index).
- **Siblings of Howard documented/likely**: Clara E. Albertson (operator, 1919), Walter M. Albertson (U.S. Army, 1919; a "Walter M. Albertson" obituary appears in the Trenton *Sunday Times Advertiser* index on 9 Mar 1975), Wilbert Albertson (machinist, 121 Ward Ave, 1919; named as a brother in compiled records), and Annie Albertson (compiled records). Compiled sources say Howard had as many as 12 siblings — the 1900 census household of Edwin S. Albertson should be pulled to enumerate them.
- **Mary A. (Crist) Hohmann's 1919 address: 41 Heil Ave, Trenton** — Bertha's widowed mother; the Hohmann family had therefore relocated from Pennsylvania (Bertha b. PA ~1898) to Trenton by 1919 or earlier.
- **Candidate death record for Bertha**: an SSDI-derived entry (AncientFaces) for **"Bertha E Hohmann", born 8 May 1901, died January 1995, Trenton, Mercer, NJ**, age 93. Status: *plausible but unproven.* If this is our Bertha, she (a) outlived Howard by 21 years and returned to/remained in Trenton, and (b) appears in SSA records under her maiden name — not unusual for women whose SS account was opened before marriage and never updated, but it could equally be an unrelated Trenton Hohmann. The 1901 birth date also conflicts with the tree's census-based "abt 1898" (census ages are frequently off by 1–3 years). Do not adopt without checking the SS Claims Index entry (which the tree already cites) for her exact birth date and parents.
- **Other Trenton Albertson obituaries in the Trenton Times index** (possible relatives, unverified): Charles Albertson 4 Jan 1974; Walter E. Albertson 5 Feb 1975; Walter M. Albertson (STA) 9 Mar 1975; Rose Albertson 7 Feb 1977; Florence Albertson 15 Dec 1979; George R. Albertson 29 Dec 1982; Etta May V. Albertson 16 Aug 1990.
- **Spelling variants encountered**: Hohmann / Hohman (the 1919 directory uses "Hohman" for Mary); Albertson occasionally OCRs/appears as Alberson/Elbertson in indexes.

## Open questions

1. **The 21 Aug 1957 Richmond, Virginia marriage.** Howard and Bertha appear as a married couple with children in the 1930, 1935 and 1940 Trenton records, yet the tree's sourced marriage (Virginia Marriage Records, 1936–2014) is dated 1957 in Richmond — when they were ~61 and ~59. Possibilities: (a) remarriage to each other after a divorce or long separation; (b) a late formalization of a long common-law union; (c) the Virginia record belongs to a different Howard Albertson/Bertha Hohmann or to another family member and is misattached. The original Richmond marriage register entry (ages and parents' names on it) would settle this. No trace of the record was findable on the open web.
2. **Burial at Greenwood Cemetery** (tree, unsourced). Greenwood Cemetery, 1800 Hamilton Ave, Hamilton Twp (Trenton), NJ, has ~65,000 interments recorded only on index cards; there is no online index and Find a Grave has no matching memorial for Howard (or Bertha). Verification requires a written request to the cemetery office.
3. **When and where did Bertha die?** The tree has no death for her. Best candidate is the Jan 1995 Trenton "Bertha E Hohmann" SSDI entry (see above), but this needs confirmation.
4. **Howard's WWI draft card.** Born Dec 1895, he was required to register on 5 June 1917; a card for him (probably at Trenton, occupation wireworker) should exist in the Ancestry/FamilySearch WWI collections (not accessible without login during this research).
5. **1900, 1910 (detail), 1920 and 1930 census households.** The tree cites 1910 (Trenton Ward 10) for Howard and 1930 for Bertha, but the household compositions — especially Edwin S. Albertson's large 1900 household (up to 12 siblings) and Howard & Bertha's own 1920/1930 households — were not retrievable from free sources here.
6. **Franklin P. S. Hohmann's death** (before 1919, probably Trenton or PA) and the Hohmann family's move from Pennsylvania to Trenton.
7. **Middle name: Isaac or Edwin?** See Corrections above.

Note on privacy: the children (Michael, William, Margaret Louise, Anna, Howard Edwin Jr.) have no vital dates in the tree and may be living or recently deceased; they were named but not researched.
