## Overview

Thomas Burton was baptised 21 July 1811, almost certainly in Dublin, Ireland, per the
tree's own citation of "Ireland, Catholic Parish Registers, 1655-1915" (the Ancestry-hosted
index of the National Library of Ireland's Catholic-parish microfilms) plus a compiled
"Ancestry Family Trees" hint. The tree records his parents as "Ths Burton" and "Cathe
Burton" — clearly abbreviated forms of Thomas and Catherine as they would appear written out
in a period Latin/English parish register — and two children, Catherine Burton and Frances
Burton, with no spouse identified. This is a common-name, pre-civil-registration Dublin
Catholic family with no free-web footprint beyond the tree's own citation; this pass could
not independently verify the baptism or find any new record for Thomas Burton himself, but
it did surface a real, documented inconsistency in the tree's *downstream* dates (his
daughter Catherine's) that bears directly on how much weight to put on the 1811 date.

## Verified facts

- Nothing about Thomas Burton could be independently re-verified in this pass beyond what
  the tree already asserts. The baptism (21 Jul 1811, Dublin) is plausible on its face: Dublin
  city's Catholic parishes (e.g. St. Andrew's, St. Michan's, St. Mary's/Pro-Cathedral
  parishes, SS Michael & John, Francis Street) generally have surviving baptismal registers
  reaching well back into the 18th century, unlike most rural Irish parishes (whose registers
  mostly start only in the 1820s-1850s), so a genuine 1811 Dublin Catholic baptism record is
  entirely credible in principle. [General background: National Library of Ireland Catholic
  Parish Registers project, registers.nli.ie]
- The parents' names as given, "Ths Burton" and "Cathe Burton," are consistent in form with
  how a period Dublin Catholic baptismal entry would abbreviate "Thomas" and "Catherine" —
  this is internal-consistency support for the record being a genuine period transcription
  rather than a fabricated/garbled entry, though it does not independently confirm the event.

## Open questions

1. **No specific Dublin parish is named** in the tree's baptism event or its cited source —
   only "Dublin, Ireland." The National Library of Ireland's own register site
   (registers.nli.ie) is browse-by-parish/by-date, not name-searchable, so without a parish
   name (or paid access to the Ancestry/Findmypast index that produced the tree's citation),
   the underlying register image cannot be located and the entry cannot be directly
   inspected. Identifying the parish (most likely one of the older, larger Dublin city
   parishes given the survival of an 1811 register) is the single highest-value next step.
2. **Thomas Burton's spouse (the mother of Catherine and Frances Burton) is completely
   unidentified** in the tree ("spouse: unknown"). No record found in this pass supplies a
   name. If Catherine's true birth year turns out to be earlier than 1831 (see above), this
   marriage would need to be dated correspondingly earlier, likely in the 1810s-1820s in
   Dublin.
3. **Reconcile the age-12 flag on daughter Catherine Burton** (already flagged by this repo's
   audit tooling, not new to this pass): was her birth year mis-transcribed as 1831 in the
   tree (more likely candidate: a decade earlier, e.g. ~1811-1821), or is the 1843 child
   Patrick James Larkin wrongly attached to her? Resolving this would materially change how
   the Thomas Burton (b. 1811) → Catherine Burton generational gap should be read.
4. **Frances Burton** (the second child) has no birth/death/marriage information anywhere in
   the tree or in this pass's research; nothing further is known about her.
5. **irishgenealogy.ie's free church-records search** (which does cover Dublin city Catholic
   parish transcripts, including many pre-1830 registers) returned HTTP 503 on every attempt
   in this session and could not be queried; a retry from a different network/session, or a
   manual lookup, is the most promising concrete next step for this specific person.
